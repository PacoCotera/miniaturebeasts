The kit

Three small devices, one job each.

To get in, and to get back out again, the explorer built three things. We're building them too, in the open.

Companion

Goes out with you, everywhere you go.

The Companion is the one in your hand on an expedition: the place, the mibis out with you, the pods underfoot.

Screen
450 × 600colour, upright, 2.41 inches on the reference board
Keys
A pad, ✓ and ←, and Call: it wakes the ground and hears what's buried.
Away
Lift it from the Caddy charged and ready, for about half an hour out.
Inside
An ESP32-S3 board with a motion sensor, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

Station

Stays home. Its vivarium keeps real hours.

Home is where you read your pods, grow them and live with your mibis. Its screen stays on, showing the collection and the vivarium. Hold it in two thumbs, or play it flat on the table.

Screen
1024 × 600colour, 7 inches, wide
Keys
A pad, ✓ and ←, and four room keys: Home, Research, the Library and the Vivarium.
Inside
A Raspberry Pi 4 on Wi-Fi. It draws every mibi from its kind's painted look and its own traits.

Caddy

Where the two meet, and where the crates cross.

It charges both handhelds, carries the crates across when you dock, and prints. Its little grey screen says what came home.

Screen
792 × 272e-paper, four greys. The picture stays with no power.
Printer
A 58 mm thermal printer, for a mibi's slip.
Cradles
Two, one shaped for each handheld, each with a seat sensor and a soft lamp.

Side by side

Three screens, to scale.

The dock

Seat the Companion, and all three answer.

The trip comes home as one moment. The Caddy feels the Companion land, lights its cradle and tells the Station, in about half a second.

Docking never takes anything for you. Your crate waits in the bay until you open it.

Feels it
A small magnet in each handheld, and a magnet sensor under each cradle. It reads true with a flat battery or a full one.
Lights
One soft lamp in each cradle, behind frosted plastic. It lights the moment you seat it, without waiting on Wi-Fi.
No plugs
Everything is wireless. Nothing to line up, nothing to wear out.

Where it stands

Designed, not made yet.

Nothing has run on a board yet. The game comes first: proven in software, then played by people, before any money goes on circuit boards or cases. Today all three devices play in your browser, side by side.

Play all three now

Then, in this order

  1. Fit the pins, the power and the shapes in one model, before any board.
  2. Measure the screens, keys, printing and power, all at once.
  3. Pull it, reseat it, restart it, and see what breaks.
  4. Check the heat, radio and lamps inside real cases.
  5. Write it all down, so anyone can build one.
