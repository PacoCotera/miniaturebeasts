The world

Nobody knows where this world is.

An explorer stumbled on the way in by chance, and the crossing made them small. Step in after them and the grass is over your head.

The fog

A cold mist rises off the sleeping ground. It lies thickest wherever nothing's been seen for the longest time.

Always afternoon

The sun here doesn't move. The world turns over once each time the explorer goes home. While nobody comes, it waits exactly as it was.

Twenty kinds, nine lands

The Loika, in every land.

This is a mibi, about as tall as a clover leaf. You'll meet Loikas wherever you go. Take one along and the wary mibis around you start to relax.
