Expedition

Walk into the fog.
Bring something home.

Every step lifts the fog. Places react to you: shake a bush, wait, see who comes to eat. Storms charge stones with Energy and can break your Probe. Pick up sealed pods.

Station

Open the pod.
Decide what it becomes.

One pod makes one mibi. Hatch it as it is, or study it first and choose from what it already carries. Energy, Data and Essence from the field power it all. The Station build runs in your browser on the device’s own graphics engine. Each section’s main screen is built, and the rest follows one screen at a time. Caddy

Dock the kit.
Print what you find.

The Caddy charges both handhelds, lets them talk to each other and, in time, brings a mibi’s portrait to the Station. Its printer is part of play: a module that answers what you do, stamp by stamp.

Genetics

One species.
Endless families.

Every mibi is new: coat, markings, crown, ears, size. Some traits hide for a generation and come back. Breed for what you love; your family is yours to shape. The rules already run in the species workbench.

Life

Raise partners.
Grow families.

Carry a mibi, tend it, and it bonds with you. Care grows it up; walks do not. Grown, it comes along as your partner, each with its own knack: digging, calming, sniffing out buried pods. It lives from juvenile to elder, and its children remember where their genes came from.

A few fair questions.

Is it real?

The exploration field test and the Station build run in your browser today. ROUGH PLAYABLE pictures come from the field test; IN THE BUILD pictures are screens from the Station build. STATION ART is finished art for places not in the game yet, such as the Vivarium rooms. CONCEPT pictures are generated art; no device exists yet. The game gets proven in software first, then boards and cases.

When can I get one?

There’s no date and no price yet. The roadmap shows what is being built now and what comes next.

Can I build one myself?

Not yet: the hardware isn’t designed. When it is, the boards and cases will be published as open hardware, to print and put together with common tools. The software is open source already.

Do I need to know genetics?

No. You play by looking, comparing and breeding. The real framework underneath is there for the curious: read the genetics design.
