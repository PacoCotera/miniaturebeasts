A few fair questions.

Is it real?

You can play it in your browser today, with the Companion, the Caddy and the Station side by side. The devices themselves aren’t made yet.

When can I get one?

No date and no price yet. The roadmap shows what comes next.

Can I build one myself?

Not yet. The boards and cases will be open hardware, and the software is open source already.

Is anything for sale in the game?

No. Luck is never for sale, and neither is time. The kit plays fully on its own, with no account and no internet.
