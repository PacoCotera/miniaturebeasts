Miniature Beasts

A creature game, a genetics toy and a small family of handheld devices. You explore with a portable Companion, bring samples home to a Station, learn what their inherited traits can do, create a mibi and raise families within each species.

In development. The open-map exploration, the creature roster and the physical kit are being designed in the open. Nothing here is finished.
