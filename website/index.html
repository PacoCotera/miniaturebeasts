How it plays

Families

Every mibi keeps a secret.

Thistle wears Bramble's crest. Underneath, it carries Sable's bare head, where no one can see it.

So what will Thistle's young look like?

Cross Thistle with another Loika that carries a bare head, and about one young in four comes out bare. Every mibi has two copies of everything, one from each parent. The Station shows you what a cross could give, before you choose.

How families work

One trip

Find it. Bring it home. Find out what it is.

  1. Dig up a pod.

    A pod is a mibi asleep in its shell.

  2. Take what the land gives.

    Warm and storm-struck stones hold Energy.

  3. Read it, grow it, keep it.

    At the Station: the vivarium, the incubator and every genome you have opened.

Out there

Three things to gather. Each does its own job.

Energy
From warm stones and storm-struck ones. Lights the way, mends the Shield, and helps a pod grow.
Data
When a wild mibi eats from you, or settles because you kept still. Reads your pods.
Essence
Dew, fruit pressed on a stone, a tuft of fur. Grows a mibi from its pod.

The wild ones are never caught. What they give you, they give because you were patient.

The kit

Why not just an app?

  • Stationis always on. Its vivarium keeps real hours on your shelf.
  • Companiondoes one thing: goes out with you. Keys, not a touchscreen. Nothing pings you.
  • Caddyis the dock. Seat the Companion, and the trip comes home.

No account. It plays offline, for good.

In design now

A lab for what the wild ones leave you.

Feed a wild mibi, watch it often, and it leaves you samples. At the Station's lab bench, each sample proves one hidden trait, and says why. Pictures for a quick look, the full genetics one key away.

What's being built

Before you play

What do I need?
A browser, on a phone or a laptop.
Is there an account or a chat?
No account and no chat. No loot boxes, and no rewards for coming back every day.
What if I'm away?
Nothing gets sick or leaves while you're away.
Can I hold it?
Not yet. The devices are designed, not made. The game plays in your browser.
