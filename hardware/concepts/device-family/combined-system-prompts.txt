Initial Companion

Product concept plate for CRITTER LAB COMPANION, the MAIN everyday interaction device of a genetics creature game. High quality realistic industrial-design exterior mockup, not engineering drawing. Large HERO portable shown front three-quarter at LEFT, three flat SCREEN CONCEPTS at RIGHT with headings PROBE / CARGO / COMPANIONS. Show ONE same hardware shell, no separate Probe physical device. Brand plate title "COMPANION / YOUR FIELD PARTNER".
Physical design: pocketable substantial portrait handheld, broad enough for expressive colored pixel creatures and readable field information. Approximately taller than wide ratio 1.55:1, not skinny remote, not phone thin. Warm sand/ochre finely textured printable plastic, separately looking graphite charcoal protective corner bumpers, restrained orange accents, recessed visible ordinary screws, straight seams, simple round transitions, flat serviceable shell; integrated fabric wrist loop at UPPER LEFT so fingers free. NO fancy compound curves or hollow handles. Large dominant portrait screen in upper 70% front, modest branding COMPANION above. Bottom control area within right-thumb travel: small directional CROSS at left, BACK round dark button to left of orange CONFIRM round button at right. Clearly raised but protected by perimeter lip. No protruding knob, no extra face buttons, no joystick, no touchscreen callouts. Comfortable-looking rounded back edges. This is proposed hardware, NO dimensions/parts/battery claims.
Hero screen is COMPANIONS: lovable strange pixel creature prominently inhabiting a mini environment, distinct expression and pose, bright clear pixel art, no walls of text. Small header COMPANIONS. Across top a modest three-destination strip pictograms sensor/radar, cargo crate, critter, with critter visibly selected; do not use literal screen buttons implying touch.
Right three screen concept vignettes, consistent portrait aspect and coherent crisp pixel art visual vocabulary:
PROBE: field expedition, small landscape/map and gathering progress as THREE resource containers (data card blue, energy crystal amber, essence green). A clear wild critter silhouette encountered, no invented chance percentages or time countdown. Labels only "PROBE", "SIGNAL FOUND", "Inspect". Encounter inspection separate from capture; no automatic catching.
CARGO: organized illustrative resource packs and sample capsule; separate lower temporary-containment compartment with a small wild creature visible through a capsule-window glyph, label "WILD CAPTURE" and "Return to Lab". This creature not shown bonded or genome decoded. Top "CARGO". Do not depict a physical living animal trapped in electronics.
COMPANIONS: owned critter takes most of screen, joyful responsive presence; simple options "Interact" and "Train" with a visible focus halo on one option. Header "COMPANIONS". Show same creature as hero screen. No complicated stats or magical breeding functionality.
Typography legible, visual playful pixel personality, strong object identity, no meaningless buttons. Avoid Pokémon branding or existing characters. Neutral warm-gray studio background with well-spaced generous editorial layout. Screen vignettes are proposed UI sketches, not final native-rendered screens. Title and mode headings only other text. This is for an adult hobbyist small-batch 3D printed product, not a toddler toy or glossy phone.
