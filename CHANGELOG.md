# Changelog

What changed in each release of Miniature Beasts, newest first. The five newest releases are also listed on the GitHub releases page; older ones live only here.

## Release 2026-11-12a: causeways across ponds, depot, beacon and pod as paintings, repainted fog at the map's edge, every picture signed

_2026-11-12_

- Ponds that cut a shore off get one causeway across.
- Wider map colours and the sea-fog colour.
- The depot, the beacon and the plain pod are now paintings.
- The fog at the map's edge is repainted. On the map views it now runs down the two sides only.
- The way-through marker stone is repainted.
- Old cliff pictures and the drifting fog layers are removed; cliffs draw as before.

Shown as stand-ins for now:

- The charged small double stone shows the charged small stone.
- The notched way mark shows the plain marker stone.
- The map's paper grain and deep sea are flat colours.

## Release 2026-11-11a: the first ten minutes, each land its own ground, the Station arrival look-inside

_2026-11-11_

- First ten minutes: Play opens on the Companion until the first pod is home; one teaching line per deed on the walk, signs and hints held until then; an empty walk ends with a direction; the Station goal sits in the bottom line.
- Each land kind has its own painted ground and decor (leaf litter, dry grass, short turf, scree, loose leaves, dry tufts, thorn shrub, wind tufts, rocks), day and night.
- Station arrival: no choice card; a crate opens on a close look inside, the first pod sits in the nest and holds for Take the pod; pods, then supplies, then finds; supplies fly to their counts.
- Bench: the Companion paintings replace the old device; frame refusals say "made on".

## Release 2026-11-10b: the new land and a fruit bush that reads

_2026-11-10_

- Every map view agrees with the walk on the first 200 worlds, and every first trip finds a pod and Data. A new world is made in the background and kept per world in the browser.
- The fruit bush is a painting, plain or mirrored, so it no longer reads as decor on the new ground.

## Release 2026-11-10a: Vivarium day cycle; map decor and night; painted walk props

_2026-11-10_

- The Vivarium follows a real day: night, morning and evening by the clock, and a 75-minute daylight loop (fresh, full sun, warm sun, passing cloud). Time of day is in the Options panel. The room hushes under a cloud and one grown mibi naps in full sun.
- Map: tufts and flowers are thinner; scrub ground sits at the grass's value; cattails, blue flowers and four tall clumps; night grass, sunlit patches and stone follow one table shared by the walk page and the places.
- Walk: painted props (depot, beacon, map icons, six pods, water bits, way-ups, crown and roof) replace the drawn ones.
