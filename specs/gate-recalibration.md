# The whole-screen gate: recalibration

Creative director, 2026-10-15 20:35 (America/Mexico_City). The owner rejected four screens I passed: Cargo, Incubator ("grow"), Probe and Create ((recorded decision), 10-15 20:29). Home was accepted.

## 1. What I missed

The cause I share across all four: I judged each screen against the fix lists and my own earlier review, and passed it when the fixes held and it was "better than main". I did not lay the owner's references beside it, although my contract names them as the bar ((audit record), (recorded decision)).

- **Cargo.** I passed the sage military cases on a painted bay as finished objects. The owner's references for the cargo containers are **(owner reference set) ((recorded decision) 10-10 11:28 and 11:31: "for the containers in the cargo haul you unload"). They show glass bottles with a cord loop and a cap, a yellow sphere with a lens and a rope loop, a matte jar with an orange band, a rugged yellow case with black corners and a handle, and white handheld units with teal accents and a loop handle. The owner also said "the crates may be a bit too rustic" (10-09 17:28) and, at 10-10 11:42 and 11:48, that each container shows what it holds: energy as a battery cell, data as a cartridge, essence as a vial. I never opened the folder. The build's bay shows near-identical green cases, and the haul items are small extras beside them.
- **Incubator ("grow").** I checked the arc, the plaque and the light, and passed it. I missed two things. First, the machinery is drawn flat (sage slabs, vector gauges), while the board's frame 3 and the rest of the Station are rendered, lit objects with depth: "doesn't follow the visual style, looks flat". Second, the margins: the left column of units, the gauges and the plaque sit at uneven distances from the bezel, and the composition does not hold a grid. I did not measure margins at all.
- **Probe.** I passed it because the Mend and Upgrade modules sat in the housing. The owner's Probe is a hand-held detector, held upright ((recorded decision) 10-10 11:33 and 11:57, the target (owner reference set) "a tall antenna"; the approved Probe concept). The bench lays it on its side in a horizontal cradle. I checked the module, not whether the object is shown as it is used.
- **Create.** I passed it under option A, with conditions about the choice reading. I missed that everything floats: the pod on a disc, the founder in a box, the chamber and the stamp case are each placed on a plain brown plane, with no bench, no surface and no contact. I also missed that the screen does not read as a place where you tweak genes. I judged the parts against cd.md's items and never asked the purpose question at screen level.

## 2. What the gate checks from now on

A screen passes only if the answer to every line is yes, judged on the whole 1× capture beside the references. "Better than before" is not a pass; that test belongs to merges (X29), not to the owner gate.

1. **References first.** For each screen I list every owner reference that bears on it ((recorded decision), the quality reference, the (owner reference set) folders, the concept frame) and lay each one beside the capture. Every object named in a reference is judged against that reference. If a reference I need is missing, I ask before passing.
2. **Objects as they are used.** Each device or object is shown in the pose and orientation the owner described: the Probe upright as held, containers that show what they carry.
3. **One rendered style.** Every object is rendered at the board's depth, lit from the top left and shaded. Flat or vector objects beside rendered ones fail.
4. **Grounded.** Every object sits on a surface or in a recess with contact and shadow. Nothing floats on an empty plane.
5. **Margins and grid.** Outer margins are even, and objects align to a grid. I measure the gaps to the bezel and between modules.
6. **Purpose at a glance.** In three seconds a stranger can say what this screen is for (Create: shaping a creature's genes). If not, it fails, whatever its parts.
7. **The owner's own words.** Each screen is checked against every line of the quality reference that names it.
