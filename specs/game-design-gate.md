# The game design gate

Game designer, 2026-10-17. This is one of the two gates every proof passes before the owner sees it (WORKER.md, "Two gates before the owner"). The other is the creative director's UI and art gate. The verdict is signed in results/ next to the creative director's. It judges the proof **inside the device sheet** (T-20261016-167), among the screens before and after it in the flow, never alone.

## The checks

Each check is answered yes or no on the painted proof at 1×. A yes needs the evidence named: what on the screen makes it true. A proof passes only when every check is yes.

1. **What it does.** A first-time player can say what this screen is for, and what pressing ✓ here will do, without reading more than the bottom line. (The purpose sentence from the screen's brief is the reference.)
2. **What each choice costs.** Every choice that spends shows its price beside it, in the material's own shape. Every choice that loses something (a pod, a mibi, a sitting) shows the loss before the press.
3. **What each choice leads to.** Every option shows its outcome as a picture, and two options look different because their outcomes differ (the outcome table in specs/purpose-briefs-b20261017-1.md section 3). The player can tell where they will land.
4. **Why it is worth coming back.** Something on this screen changes with time or play and is worth checking: a wait with its ring, a reveal, a new arrival, a growth. If the screen has nothing to come back for, it says what to do next instead (specs/station-return-signals.md).
5. **True to the rules.** Every mark and number matches the rules and the code: no mark draws data the game doesn't have (the genome engineer's check for genome screens), and no promise of an effect that isn't designed.
6. **In the flow.** In the device sheet, the screen continues from the one before it and hands over to the one after. The same object reads the same everywhere (the pod, the crate, the mibi), and no jump lands the player somewhere unannounced.
7. **The owner's verbatims.** Every owner line on this screen is answered, each with how.

## The verdict

In results/, per proof: PASS or FAIL, the seven checks with their evidence, the fixes routed (to UI/UX, art or build), and the proofs named for the owner. Signed "game designer" with the date.
