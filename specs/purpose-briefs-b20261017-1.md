# Purpose briefs for the four rejected screens (game designer, batch B-20261017-1)

Written 2026-10-17 for T-20261016-163 (Incubator), T-20261016-164 (Cargo), T-20261016-165 (decision panels) and T-20261016-166 (genome screen). UI/UX owns the layouts, the art director the concepts, and the creative director the allegory and the look. This brief gives the game designer's part: what each screen must make a first-time player **understand** and **feel**, what is shown and why it matters, and, for every panel, each option's outcome as a picture. Section 5 is the test the creative director and I gate the concepts on.

The rules are station-gate design/ (game, research and breeding, genomics, world). Where a picture needs data, the data is named.

## 1. Incubator (T-163)

**Purpose, in one sentence:** *the life you designed on Create is being grown here, and each time you look it is further along and you know more about it.*

**What a first-time player must understand at a glance:**
1. **Something alive is growing inside the pod, and the machine is candling it.** The candling idea stays (owner, 00:43: "yes, the candling idea is ok, the execution was terrible"). The creative director sets its new execution in T-20261016-168. The game requires that the candling shows (a) the founder forming inside the pod, read through the light; (b) the genome it grows from; and (c) the machine's light doing the work. It must read as a lab instrument candling life, not as an egg with a shadow on a moss cushion.
2. **How far along it is, and how long is left.** This holds as ruled: the ring of time and "ready in about n min". Its colour is a science colour, never cream (owner).
3. **What has just been revealed about their mibi, and what it means.** This is the part the owner could not read ("just cleared", "character unflappable").

**What is revealed, and how it must read.** While a founder grows, its unread chapters clear one by one (the rules). Each clearing reveals **traits**. A look and a doing read differently:

| Revealed | Show it as | Example (S03 Tuikis) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A look** (Coat, Face, Shape, Legs & tail) | The part itself, coming into focus on the founder seen through the candled pod, and shown large beside it as the founder's own part, never a pixel glyph | Markings appear on its flank |
| **A doing** (Movement, Stamina, Character, Glow) | **A short behaviour moment**, a few seconds of the mibi doing it, because a doing is behaviour, not a look. Two or three words name it in play language | **Nerve: unflappable** is shown as a shadow passing overhead while the mibi stays put and keeps sniffing; a jumpy one would dart into its burrow. **Curiosity: seeking**: it walks toward a new object. **Stamina: thrift**: it keeps going on a long walk while a typical one sits down |

- **The meaning is the behaviour, not the adjective.** "Unflappable" means nothing on its own; a creature not flinching when the others do means everything. Each doing trait needs one moment per look at its ends (and the middle reads as between). These are animation pieces, per species body plan, from the species' own routines. The frames already name the looks (`jumpy`, `between`, `unflappable` for `nerve`).
- **Why it matters to the player:** a revealed doing tells them what kind of companion they are getting (calm, curious, tireless). Partners' abilities are not designed yet (design/game.md), so **the screen must not promise an effect in play.** It shows the character, which is true today.
- **A first** (a look new to the field guide) gets the first mark and a longer reveal, as ruled.

**What it must make them feel:** anticipation (it's coming, and I can see it forming) and ownership (that's the one I made). They should come back because something new is revealed each visit.

**What goes:** the execution of the candled egg (a flat shadow on an egg), the moss cradle, the "just cleared" card with a glyph and an adjective, cream, brown on brown, and the plastic plaque. The **genome code stays, with its text id** (owner).

**Crossed bud:** it starts knowing its forecast-certain chapters, and the rest stay frosted through ready (T-136 fix 12). Its reveals come after it opens, at the vivarium.

## 2. Cargo reception (T-164)

**Purpose, in one sentence:** *what your trip brought home arrives here: you open each crate and see what came back and where each thing goes.*

**What a first-time player must understand at a glance:**
1. **Each crate is one trip.** A crate is sealed at End expedition, one per expedition. Show each crate with the badge of the place it came from (the trip's main place), so "this is the pond trip" reads without text.
2. **What is inside each crate,** shown as the things themselves when it opens (the owner's container rulings):
   - **pods,** each its species' shell; a pod of a species new to the player glints;
   - **Energy** as battery cells, **Data** as cartridges, **Essence** as vials, as many as it holds (count by the objects, up to what fits, then a number beside the icon);
   - **finds:** a crystal, a pearl or a shard of storm glass.
3. **Where each thing goes, so its place is never ambiguous** (owner: "combined, at the Station, or part of a portfolio"):
   - **materials** leave the crate and fly into the top bar's totals, which tick up. Before opening they are in the crate; after, they are the Station's. There is no separate row of materials;
   - **pods** travel to the rack (Pods). The rack is shown **only as their destination, during that flight,** and only the wells they land in;
   - **finds** go to the Station's keep. If the find opens a sealed chapter of a species the player has, its badge shows that species' face.
4. **What changed in the world while they were away:** the world rows (specs/world-events.md), place badge, change mark and face, at most three.

**What it must make them feel:** the reward of a trip coming home: unpacking. One crate at a time, each a small reveal.

**What goes (owner):**
- the colour bars, since every mark must read without a legend;
- the materials row that is in no crate;
- "Fit the Probe upgrade": Probe configuration belongs on the Probe bench. The dock's free mend is shown in the dock arrival (specs/dock-moments.md), not here;
- the six pod wells, except as destination during the flight;
- tiny icons carrying meaning.

**The one choice here:** none until the crates are open. Then the next step is a pair of named choices: **look at the new pods** (picture: the pods in the rack) or **later** (picture: Home). It is not a lone "Next".

## 3. Decision panels (T-165): each option's outcome as a picture

**Purpose, in one sentence:** *at a real decision, the panel shows what each choice will do, as pictures that differ at a glance, and its cost.*

The rule for every panel: **the two options must look different because their outcomes are different.** Each option is its outcome, drawn: where the mibi or pod ends up, what the player gains or gives. Words are at most the verb. A first-time player covering the words should still tell the options apart.

| Panel | Option | Its outcome, as the picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Return to the wild** | **Return** | The mibi walking into **its home place**: the place it (or its line) came from, as that place's scene (a pond edge, a wood). Its bay in the vivarium is empty and +2 Essence (the vial) comes back. A small mark shows that its place remembers it: later that place sheds a pod of its line (the rules). "Gone for good" shows as the mibi walking away, not as a warning sentence |
| | **Keep** | The mibi in **its bay in your vivarium**, with you, as it is now |
| **Grow it** (Create) | **Grow it** | The founder as you made it, beside the founder the pod would have grown (before and after, the changed parts lit). The pod sealing and going to the Incubator, with the time ring and the price |
| | **Keep shaping** | The drums, still open: you stay at the bench |
| **Hatch and name** (Incubator) | **Keep the name** | The young mibi with its name tag, stepping into its bay |
| | **Choose another** | The same mibi, the tag blank, the namer opening |
| **Grow now** (Incubator, large price) | **Grow now** | The ring jumping to full; the vials it costs |
| | **Wait** | The ring at its place, "ready in about n min" |
| **Cross them** (Cross) | **Cross them** | The two parents and the child's forecast seeds; the bud going to the Incubator; the price |
| | **Pick another** | The partner slot open, the other adults beside it |

| **Read it now** (Create, unread gene) | **Read it** | The chapter's frost clearing to its genes, with the drums that become turnable; the Data it costs |
| | **Not yet** | The chapter still frosted, with the Incubator's ring: "it clears while it grows" |

**What it must make them feel:** that they are choosing between two futures, not confirming.

## 4. The genome screen (T-166)

**Purpose, in one sentence:** *you are looking at your mibi's real genome, and you can tell which part of its body or behaviour each row is, what each gene is doing, and what you changed.*

**What a first-time player must understand at a glance:**
1. **Each row is a part of this animal.** Each row is headed by a picture of that part, **on this founder** (its coat, its face, its silhouette for Shape, its legs and tail), and for a doing row, the doing's moment in miniature (its gait for Movement, for example). These are not emblems or letters. The structure stays as verified (T-135, T-136).
2. **The link between the genome and the body.** Focusing a row lights that part on the founder, and focusing a gene lights the part it makes. This is the single strongest legibility fix: the player sees the row and the body answer each other.
3. **What each gene is doing,** by the verified marks, now sculpted at a legible size:
   - one copy shown in front of a hidden one;
   - two copies blended;
   - one look (both copies the same, the commonest state);
   - asleep behind a switch;
   - only breeding changes it;
   - still a surprise.
4. **What you changed:** the changed genes lit, and the founder's parts that changed with them.

**What it must make them feel:** that they are working on something real and deep, with depth visible, and that the depth is legible enough to be curious about rather than confusing.

## 5. The gate: does a first-time player get it?

The creative director and I judge each concept against these, before the lead. A concept passes only if every line is yes, judged on the painted PNG at 1× by someone who has not read the specs.

| Screen | In three seconds, a first-time player can say… |
| --- | --- |
| Incubator | "The machine is candling something alive growing in the pod; it's about this far along; and this is what it just showed about my mibi" (pointing at the part or the behaviour, without reading the trait's name) |
| Cargo | "This crate is from that trip; these came out of it; the energy (data, essence) went to my stock; the pods went to the rack" |
| Decision panel | "If I pick this, that happens; if I pick that, this happens", pointing at two pictures that look different, words covered |
| Genome screen | "This row is its coat (face, legs…); this gene I can change, this one only breeding changes; this is what I changed" |

Plus every owner verbatim on that screen, line by line, as the ticket says.

### 6.2 Cargo (specs/redesign-cargo.md)

- **Reach (explored land): leaves Cargo. Confirmed.** It belongs to the Companion's map, which already shows explored land. On the Station it has no decision to support.
- **The world events: they leave Cargo, split by whose news they are.**
  - **News about the player's mibis** (`grown`, `elder`) go to the Station's return signals: Home's line "Moss has grown up" and the name tag's mark, until the mibi is visited (specs/station-return-signals.md). That is where the player meets their mibis.
  - **News about the world** (`lampLow`, `lampOut`, `herd`, `storm`, `fruit`, `stirring`, `young`, `quiet`) go to **the Companion's map,** where the next trip is planned. Each is shown on its place as a marker the first time the map is opened after the dock: an outpost's lamp low or out; a herd's arrow; a storm's charged stones; fruit; a glint where something new stirs; young.
  - The partner's face on a storm row (specs/world-events.md, the game designer's calls) moves with them, to the Vivarium's step mark.
  - specs/world-events.md keeps the record (the crate's `world` block). Only its reader changes: the Companion's map, not the Cargo card. The game designer's calls there are amended to match.
- **The pods' route: confirmed.** They go out through the Pods hatch, which shows its free wells, as many as land. A pod with no free well **waits on the bay floor, still sealed,** and lands when a well frees, oldest first. That is the rule (design/architecture.md, "A pod with no free well waits"). The hatch shows that it is full, so the player sees why the pods stayed.
- **The materials' route:** into the store tanks, which then tick the top bar's totals. The flow from crate to tank is the answer to "combined, at the Station, or part of a portfolio".
