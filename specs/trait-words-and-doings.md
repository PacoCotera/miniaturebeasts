## 1. Gene tiles as the controls on Create: yes

Agreed, with four conditions:

1. **◀ ▶ walk the genes in the chapters' ring order,** never by where the tiles sit on screen. On G1 the panes sit around the body, so a spatial order would jump unpredictably. Within a pane, left to right.
2. **Every gene is focusable, including the ones that don't turn,** because R opens their detail, and ▲ ▼ on them plays the refusal (the line to the Cross, or the steps and badge). Unread genes take the "read it now" panel on ▲ ▼, as ruled.
3. **The focused tile shows its neighbour faces** (the look before and after) and the change lands on the founder in the same beat: the light on the part, the reaction, the old look fading (specs/slip-reward.md). With the tray gone, this is all the turn has, so it must not be lost.
4. **The price ticks in the bottom line** on each change, as before.

With those, the loop is turn, turn, Grow, with no tray and nothing shown twice.

## 2. Plain words, for the reveal and the detail

**The rule:** `{Trait}: {what it looks like or does, in a few plain words}`. For a look, the picture already says it, so the words are the look's own name ("Markings: stripes"). For a doing, the words say what the animal does, because the look's name alone ("unflappable") means nothing without the behaviour. A **between** look reads "somewhere in the middle" plus the doing's plain noun, or is left to the picture.

**Doings, every trait in the frames** (one line per end; the middle reads as "between"):

**Never promise an effect in play.** What partners do is not designed yet (design/game.md), so a line describes the animal, never "helps you…". The copywriter tunes the words; the meaning above is fixed.

**Looks** keep the look's own name from the frame ("Eyes: big, wide", "Claws: digging wedges"), because the painted part carries the meaning. Two need a word of help:
- **Claws:** "digging wedges" reads; "pads" should read "padded feet".
- **Carriage** (tail-curl): "tail hangs" / "tail straight" / "tail curls up". The trait's name doesn't say "tail".

## 3. A behaviour picture for each doing chapter

A doing is drawn as **a short moment of the founder's own body doing it**: a still for the genome tile, and a loop of a few seconds for the reveal. Each moment is chosen so the two ends look clearly different side by side. All are painted on the founder's rig, never as an icon.

| Chapter | The moment | How the ends differ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Movement** | The mibi crossing a short stretch of ground, with its trail of footprints behind it | Pace: footprints far apart and quick, or close and unhurried. Stride: long or short gaps. Weave: a straight or a curving trail. Turning: a wide or a tight curve. Flap: the wings' blur, fast or slow. Waddle: the body's sway |
| **Stamina** | The mibi on a long walk, or pushing a stone | Reserve: still walking past the far marker, or sitting down halfway. Strength: the stone moves, or barely. Thrift: one berry and it goes on, or it stops to eat. Basking: lying in a sun patch, or tucked into shade |
| **Character** | Something happens near it: a new object appears; a shadow passes overhead | Curiosity: it walks over to sniff the object, or stays back and watches. Nerve: it bolts for its burrow at the shadow, or keeps sniffing as it passes |
| **Glow** | Dusk, the mibi in the dark | Glow: a faint or a bright halo. Duration: a short flicker, or a steady light through the scene |
| **Charge** | A storm's air, or a touch | Charge: a small spark at its tip, or a crackling bolt. Phase: a paw solid on the stone, or the stone showing through it. Pull: a small stone drifting toward it, or snapping to it |

These are the species' routines in miniature (the vivarium already plays routines), so the art director can derive them from the same rig.
