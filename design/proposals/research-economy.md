# The research economy: a day, a week, three currencies

**Proposal** from game design, 2026-10-08. It paces the approved [research loop](research-loop.md) and [Station loop](station-loop.md) against the [world](../world-and-exploration.md) as tuned in [round 3](exploration-round-3.md), with the [species frames](species-frames.md) and [taxonomy](taxonomy.md) tiers as content and the [art pipeline](art-pipeline.md) v2 as what incubation pays for. **Decided** marks owner decisions; *tuned* marks numbers changed here to make the week hold (§3).

**Decided 2026-10-08:** a day is one walk that yields a pod and a partial read; full knowledge of a species takes about a week of pods, so reading is rationed and glints matter; Data comes from the field and from a small, daily-capped bench trickle earned by residents being watched and compared; a bud grows about twenty minutes plus one minute per shaped trait; the vivarium has a capacity of six or eight at first, grown by Station upgrades; returning a mibi to the wild has meaning.

## 1. The day and the week

**A day** is one **walk** and one **bench sitting**.
- **The walk** (10–20 minutes, Companion lifted): one expedition over one or two places in reach, bringing home about **one pod**, the day's Data, Energy and Essence, and now and then a find. Head home seals it in a crate; the dock hands it over.
- **The sitting** (5–15 minutes): identify the pod, read the chapter or two the day's Data pays for, shape and grow when something is worth growing, watch and compare the residents (the bench trickle), open yesterday's bud. A pod is rarely read whole on its day; the player picks the chapter that glints.

**A week** is the unit for a species: about seven pods of it, hunted on purpose once a chapter is read (its pods wear the species' shell colour on the map). By the week's end every chapter is read, the guide shows nearly every look, two or three members live in the vivarium, and a first child is crossed. The last looks pull into the next week.

## 3. Worked tuning: a week with Pip and a week with a mid species

Worked by drawing random pods from the species' pools (every look equally common), one a day, reading only chapters that glint, best value first, while Data lasts; 400 weeks each, medians shown. Pip is the real Loika frame: 5 traits, 4 chapters, 13 looks. The mid species is Tuikis-like: 24 traits, 7 chapters, 38 loci, 139 looks (species-frames §5).

**Pip, at a starter place: 2 Data a walk + bench 2 a day.**

| Day | Pod | Reads (Data) | Looks seen | Data after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Loika 1 | Coat (free), Face (2), Movement (1) | 9 of 13 | 1 |
| 2 | Loika 2 | Stamina (1, first time); Face glints, half price (1) | 10 | 3 |
| 3 | Loika 3 | Movement and Stamina glint (1 + 1); grow Loika 1 shaped *only pale* (+1) | 12 | 4 |
| 4 | Loika 4 | Face glints (1); **field guide complete** | 13 | 7 |
| 5–7 | Loika 5–7 | no glints: one grown unshaped, two returned (+2 Essence); Probe tier 2 (4 Data); the first cross once both are adults | 13 | 15 |

Seven pods, 8 reads, 8 Data spent of 28 earned, complete on day 4; the rest of the week is tinkering. The surplus of about 15 is the second shelf and the next species' first pod (10 Data whole). A starter is meant to be a short week.

**A mid species, at a mid place: 6 Data a walk + bench 2 = 8 a day.**

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 (Coat 4, Face 3, full price) | 7 | 29 | 1 |
| 2 | 3 | 8 | 51 | 2 |

Seven pods, 56 Data earned and 56 spent on 24 reads; every chapter read by day 4; 96 percent of the looks by day 7; the guide complete on day 9 to 11 (96 percent of weeks by day 14). On days 5–7 four or five pods wait with a glint in a tray of six: the ration, and returning a pod is a real choice. From day 8 Data piles up (about 40 by day 14) for shelves, shaping and the next species.

**What was changed so that neither week starves nor saturates:**
- **Data per walk is tiered, 2 / 4 / 6 / 8,** in place of the flat "about 3 per expedition" the research loop assumed (the r7 tester got 1). At 3 a walk a mid species completes in only 68 percent of two-week runs, with 50 pod-days of glints waiting: r7's starvation. At 5 it is day 10, a day short. At 8 nothing waits and Data piles up from day 7. Six is the number.
- **The bench trickle is capped at 2.** Three behaves like one more from the field; two keeps it a quarter of a mid day.
- **The walk's own +1 Data** (station-loop §3) folds into the field yield, since the walk now is the expedition.
- **Prices unchanged.** Half price on later pods turns the week: the first mid pod would cost 24 whole and gets two chapters on its day; by day 4 a glinting chapter costs 2.
- At the other tiers: an early species (10 traits, 54 looks) at 4 a walk completes on day 6 for 27 Data; a late one (30 traits, 164 looks) at 8 a walk on day 10 to 12, right for a species behind a gate.

**Watch for:** a look rarer than one in five in the wild stretches the guide past two weeks; a rarer look is a deliberate long pull named in the frame.

## 4. The bench trickle

**What earns it.** Data is a record of creatures doing things (**Decided**), and residents do things:
- **Watch:** keep a resident in focus for a minute of its routine: +1, once per resident per day.
- **Compare:** two residents side by side (the free Compare of taxonomy §4), held until the differences have pulsed through: +1, once per pair per day.

**The cap:** 2 a day in all, on the Station's clock (a new day at local midnight; without a clock, 20 hours). The Home line says "bench full for today". Nothing is earned while the chrome is faded; waking never rewards (**Working rule**).

**Why it cannot replace the field:** 14 a week against the 56 a mid species needs; it needs residents, which come from pods, which come only from walks; it never glints or shows a new look, so it only pays for reading what the field found; the find, the next species, Energy and Essence are field only. It exists so a short day still ends with a read, and so the vivarium is looked at.

## 5. Incubation: the bud

**Decided:** a bud grows **twenty minutes plus one minute per shaped trait**, replacing the rule by body size and chapter count (station-loop §1); a cross incubates the same.

**Meanwhile** the sitting carries on: other pods are read and residents watched (the trickle's minutes and the bud's are the same minutes), and the bud's unread chapters clear across the wait, one every few minutes. The Station is always on: a player who leaves finds the bud ready next sitting; Open waits for a press (**Decided**); a cargo never shortens it; one incubator (**Decided**), so one bud at a time.

**The generation budget.** Twenty minutes is the art pipeline's latency budget (v2 §2): at 20–105 seconds a call the full unique set of four calls fits with retries at one and five minutes, so a connected kit opens a unique juvenile almost every time. One incubator, one or two buds a day in practice and six to ten bays bound the spend at about 40–60 mibis a kit-year, the pipeline's cost assumption. Returning a mibi and growing another costs twenty minutes, 2 Energy and a net 2 Essence, so a render is never free to churn; the Caddy's monthly call cap is the backstop.

## 6. Vivarium capacity and the return

**Six bays at the start**, six beds in the terrarium; the one with you keeps its bed. The pod tray stays at six. **Upgrades add bays:** a **second shelf** (+2, after the first cross; 8 Energy, 8 Data, 6 Essence) and a **third shelf** (+2; 12, 12, 10): ten in V1, bought at the bench beside the Probe. The shelves are the sinks the starter week needs. **A full vivarium** turns a bud away before it is paid for ("No bay free · return one, or grow a shelf").

**Return to the wild** (a mibi, from Habitat, two presses) gives back:
- **+2 Essence**, half the body: a founder returned the day it is grown loses 2 Essence and 2 Energy net.
- **A field-guide note** on its species page: name, ring, where and when it was released; its lineage stays as a leaf that ends.
- **A place that remembers it.** It goes to the cell it came from (a founder's pod's place; a child, its mother's) and lives there as a wild individual with its name: met on a later walk it comes to the Probe curious, and it can shed one pod of its own, a world turn later, under the arrival guard. That pod carries its real copies, so a shaped look returned to the wild can be found there again; knowing still grants nothing.

**It costs the mibi:** no longer a partner, never taken back (no wild capture, **Decided**); a **bonded** mibi cannot be returned; a juvenile not until adult.

## 7. What must be built and delivered

**The Station loop build** ([station-loop](station-loop.md) §9) adds: the bench trickle (watch minute, compare hold, per-day ledger, cap and day boundary); six bays and the "no bay free" refusal; the two shelf upgrades; return to the wild for a mibi (Essence, the note, the release handed to the Companion at the next dock, as pods are); the bud at 20 + 1 minutes with chapters clearing across the wait, and decision 2's first-bud rule; the §2 prices in the debug strip.

**Exploration tuning must deliver:** a walk of 10–20 minutes over one or two places that brings home about one pod (reach, place size and pod density sized to the walk, not to the 540–850 actions of a full reach); **Data per walk of 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 by tier of place**, which at the built +1 per moment means about six creature moments in a short mid walk, or a moment worth more at rarer creatures (1, 1, 2, 2 by tier is the simpler lever); Energy and Essence as round 3 set them; the find where the partner's ability reaches it; a returned mibi living in its cell and shedding its pod.

## 8. Decisions for the owner

1. **Six bays, not eight.** Six keeps pods precious (**Decided:** no multitude of mibis) and makes the first shelf a goal inside the Pip week; eight pushes the first return and upgrade to week three. *Recommended: six.*
2. **The first bud ever grows in five minutes, not twenty,** so the first sitting ends with a meet (r7's best moment); it opens plain and swaps later, as the pipeline allows. *Recommended: yes.*
3. **A returned mibi's place can shed a pod of its lineage.** It makes the return a meaning, not a refund, and is the one way a player's shaping reaches the wild. The limits: one pod per returned mibi, a turn later, every read still paid. *Recommended: yes.*
