# Genome extension for the fluffy species: Wickle, Drapet, Curlop

Genome engineer, Fri 30 Oct 2026 (America/Mexico_City), revised after the game and genome design review (the same day, 16:33). Three new species approved, S17–S19 in clans C17–C19 (Reeds, Boulders, Ferns), and S05, S06 and S07 parked. Names are working names; the copywriter names them.

## The reading of the framework, checked

All four statements of the lead's reading hold, with two corrections. (1) The tail bend and the fur length are continuous loci (`copy-mean`), so a third bend value or a longer fur allele would widen the locus range, and the blend step (`stepValue`, 59 steps over the range) moves for every blend already stored and every stamp. That is not backward compatible; the extension is new loci. (2) Neither Wickle nor Curlop stands on its length axis in the rig: a serial body lies along X (only fans have `stand`). Both are tall, so the rig needs one more plan extra, `upright`.

## The rule that keeps every old frame exact

No existing record changes: no allele added to an existing locus, no range, guard, scope or default changed for an existing plan, and no `alsoIn` on an old record (the C03 bulb and the C01 belly records stay exactly as they were). New records are new loci and part switches that only a frame that lists them carries. They are appended at the end of the catalogue and a reader knows them by frame version 6: a frame lists an `absent` line for a record only when the record existed at the frame's version (`sinceFrame`, new, default 0), so S01–S16 keep their bytes (apart from the parked flag on S05–S07). The catalogue pin stays 10: no record of pin 10 changed, and a bump would rewrite `catalogue.version` in all sixteen frames. Adding records under pin 10 matches its rule ("a changed range is a new pin"). The new species are frame version 6.

**The new records are branch records, not trunk.** A generated species or a drop's new clan can pick up any trunk record, and a curled tail on a furred quadruped is a Shiba or a husky, brow tufts on long fur are a terrier. So `tail-curl-presence`, `tail-curl-turn` and `tail-rise` belong to C19; `fur-skirt-presence`, `fur-skirt-drop`, `fur-skirt-flare`, `brow-tuft-presence` and `brow-tuft-length` to C18. `hand-size-ratio` is owned by the arms, which only a mixed plan has.

**Borrowing is the species' list, not the record's.** A species that uses another clan's branch record names it in a list on its spec (`borrows`, written into the frame under `species.borrows`), read where the frame marks other clans' branch records absent. S17 borrows the plume (`tail-curl-presence`, `tail-curl-turn`, `tail-rise` from C19), the apron (`fur-skirt-presence`, `fur-skirt-drop`, `fur-skirt-flare` from C18) and the tip's glow (`tail-tip-bulb` from C03); S19 borrows the belly field and its reach (`belly-field`, `belly-field-extent` from C01). The records of C03 and C01 are unchanged, so S03's and S01's frames stay byte-exact.

## The plans

- **Wickle (S17, C17):** `one|serial|bilateral|contact|zero|one|one|off|fur`, plan extra `upright`. One pair of tiny feet under the body, posture upright, states `hop` and `walk`. Code B1·L2. Not mixed: the bumps at its sides are fur, not arms. Its apron is locked at the knee end of the drop, so the feet show beneath it.
- **Drapet (S18, C18):** `one|serial|bilateral|contact|zero|one|two|off|fur`, not upright. Short legs under the skirt, tucked inside its footprint. `growth.fur-skirt-drop` is locked at its floor end in Drapet's frame (no trait opens the drop in any species), so Hang is the flare alone. Four legs showing under a knee-length coat is a sheepdog (a Komondor, a Puli), the mop dog the art notes flagged; at floor length the legs never show.
- **Curlop (S19, C19):** `one|serial|bilateral|mixed|one|two|one|off|fur`, plan extra `upright`. A free front pair (arms) over one hind pair. Code B1·L2·arms.

## Per need: the smallest change, and how breeding sees it

**(a) Mixed limbs.** The plan key keeps its nine fields; `limbs` gains a fourth value, `mixed` (`none|free|contact` parse as today). `mixed` needs `groups` one and `pairs` one: one free front pair (arms, two links) over one contact hind pair. `limbSet` stays `legs` (the hind pair; posture `upright`, stations on the rear), and a new plan fact `armSet: arms` roots the front pair on the chest. States: those of a one-region legged plan, `hop` and `walk`. `planCode` prints a mixed plan as `B1·L2·arms` (taxonomy §1's limbs cell lists "mixed" too). Arms reuse `growth.free-proximal-ratio`, `free-distal-ratio` and `free-radius-ratio` (their guards also read `arms`, false in every old plan), with no new allele on those records. The hand is not a new allele of `anatomy.contact-terminal-form`: a new allele would enter every generated species' pool and the stamp's allele bins. The rig draws the hand from the arm (a palm and three fingers), sized by one new locus, `growth.hand-size-ratio` (small … plump).

The arms are measured against the body's cross radius (on an upright body the smaller of its front-to-back and side-to-side radii), not against its long axis: the free-chain ranges are fractions of a body's length, and on a tall body they would draw long, thin arms. The rig draws them with fixed volume conventions, as `PAW` does for feet (the short, short, thick arms of the Curlop come out as the stand's short plump arms: links of about half the cross radius, a thickness of about a fifth). The ranges reach the stand at 304 px with these conventions, so no new arm locus is needed. The rest pose is a rig rule: the shoulder on the chest just below the chin, the elbow straight down at the side, the forearm folded in, the three-fingered hands meeting at the chest midline below the face (they stop where they meet and never cross). The hands are locked in Curlop's frame and there is no Hands trait in the vocabulary for now: at 48 px they vanish, and at 96 px they are cream on a cream belly, and a goal look the player cannot see is a bad breeding goal. The locus stays, for a species that will show it.

**(b) Tail curl and a plume from the top.** One part switch, `anatomy.tail-curl-presence` (owner: the tail), and two loci it owns: `growth.tail-curl-turn` (extra turn along the tail: loose, curled, tight; the sweep's curvature grows toward the tip, a fiddlehead) and `growth.tail-rise` (where the tail roots: `rear`, today's, or `crown`, the plume rising from the top). `tail-rise` is a `pair-map` of the two, not a blend: it is a choice, locked per species, and a blend would root the tail in the middle of the back and spend stamp bits on something that never varies. The existing `growth.axial-tail-bend` stays down or up. The tip glow is `anatomy.tail-tip-bulb`, C03's record, borrowed by S17 (see above); the glow's duration stays fixed. Curl turn is open for Wickle over the full range (a loose plume reads as a flame, not an animal) and open for Curlop over the tight half, loose excluded (an upright biped with hands at the chest and a straight tail reads as a kangaroo or a small theropod; the fiddlehead is what keeps Curlop clear of real animals).

**(c) Fur as a range to the floor, and a skirt.** `appearance.fur-length` stays the coat's depth. A new part switch `anatomy.fur-skirt-presence` (owner: fur) with `growth.fur-skirt-drop` (how far the fur hangs from the waist to the floor: knee … floor) and `growth.fur-skirt-flare` (overhang over the body's width). The rig draws a flared fur sheet from the waist, a rigid shell with no cloth simulation; it is not the slug's `skirt.enabled` (a flat foot under the body). The drop is fixed per species (Drapet floor, Wickle knee) and no trait opens it. At floor length the skirt's footprint must cover every leg part from the hem up or the build fails (the guard, in the rig and in validation), and a skirted body keeps its legs tucked under the belly, so a skirt that hides legs does. Drapet: skirt locked on, flare open (Hang). Wickle: the short apron, locked.

**(d) Brow tufts.** Part switch `anatomy.brow-tuft-presence` (owner: the head) and `growth.brow-tuft-length` (short … long). Drapet carries it locked on, length open (Brows). The switch belongs to C18: a species of that clan may open it (a sleeping part when off, as the crest). At `long` a tuft's tip stops above the eye's upper rim (a guard in the rig and in validation), so the eyes and the blinks stay readable.

**The rig's `upright` extra.** Not a key field (it follows `stand`, `wave`, `join`). With it a one-region body's long axis is vertical: height from the size class, front-to-back from the depth ratio, width from the width ratio. Its geometry is written down: the head and the eyes sit on the upper front face (the front is the head side, -X in the rig and the right of a side view), not on the top end; the tail and the plume curl in the sagittal plane, the tip rolling toward the head side (Wickle's plume rises and curls forward over the face, Curlop's tail leaves low on the back and its tip rolls up toward the body); the tail and plume root by `growth.tail-rise`. Legs measured on an upright body may be stubs (a drop of at least 0.06 of the body's depth: the feet of a candle of fur). The plume's height counts toward the stand box, so a size class is read from the body without the plume. Wickle: tapered, one pair of feet. Curlop: tapered, mixed. Drapet is not upright (a wide low body).

## What breeding sees

New loci: `growth.tail-curl-turn`, `growth.tail-rise`, `growth.fur-skirt-drop`, `growth.fur-skirt-flare`, `growth.brow-tuft-length`, `growth.hand-size-ratio`; new part switches: `anatomy.tail-curl-presence`, `anatomy.fur-skirt-presence`, `anatomy.brow-tuft-presence`. Alleles are named ends, blended on the step (`copy-mean`); the switches are dominant-enable like every part switch. Only the S17–S19 frames carry them. Open after the changes, all on the silhouette: Wickle's Curl (full range, clear even at 48 px), Drapet's Hang (the flare) and Brows (the length), Curlop's Curl (the tight half). Everything else new is locked: the rise, the drop, the hands, the arm lengths, the apron, the legs and feet of Wickle and Drapet, the tails' size. The target vocabulary gains Hang, Brows and Curl (working words, for the copywriter); Hands is out until a species shows them.

## For art and movement later

The floor-length skirt is the hardest motion item: keep it a rigid flared shell with fringe lines and one hem-sway parameter driven by the hidden step, with no cloth simulation, and the hem never cuts into the floor. The control set's silhouette is the closed tent, so the hidden legs leave no mark in the controls. Curlop's arms add a pose set (rest at the chest, eat with hands, a wave); its detail waits for the emulated target. Brow tufts sit over the eye landmarks in the controls; the eye guard keeps the painter's eye placement checkable.

## Parked

`parked: true` on the S05, S06, S07 rows (and `species.parked` in their frames and the index). Nothing is deleted: frames, targets, glyphs and the stamp keep them, a save holding one loads. Parked mibis already in a save can still be crossed with each other (no pods and no wild ones, so saves keep playing). The Station's pools skip a species whose frame says `parked` (a follow-up: they are in the Station's code).

## Stopped on

Nothing: every need is met backward compatibly. The arm ranges reach the stand with the rig's volume conventions, so the fallback of new arm loci was not needed.
