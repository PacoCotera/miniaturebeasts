# Proposed changes to world-and-exploration.md after the walk slice

For editorial. This is a list of what the walk slice (chunks C1 to C16) changed in play, written so the design document can be brought to match; it edits nothing. Words in square brackets are for the canon check. Numbers marked (tune) are starting values the playtest may move.

## The survey section: quarters, explored, completion

- **Quarters are gone.** Delete the Quarters bullet, "surveyed", "almost done", the dither of unsurveyed quarters on the map and the reach view, and "half surveyed" in every example line. A place is not measured by how much of it is uncovered.
- **Explored becomes found.** "Explored means visited and Called in at least once; finished means surveyed, Called, nothing left" is replaced by: a place is **found** when its heart is found (below); it is **finished** when it is found and nothing is left to take. The reach grid's dot fills when the heart is found.
- **Completion has no picture.** Delete "When the last one fills, the grid turns bright with a tick and the line says Everything in reach is explored" and Cargo's "Explored N of M". The band reads "Found N of M in reach"; nothing announces the last dot.
- **Call reports lead with the heart**, then what is there to take with a direction: "Water glints in the grass, north-east · a pod north"; after the heart, "Meadow · found · a pod north, fruit west" or "nothing left to take". The Call is a round of 7 tiles in a place (was 11; 4 in a fog bank, 6 with a glowing partner) (tune). It never says "surveyed".
- **Walking lifts about 4 tiles, nothing puts the veil back.** The world turn no longer re-veils a quarter or a place.
- **Wait and place edges** are unchanged.

## Hearts: a new section [canon check: hearts as a kind of thing in the world]

- Every composed place has one **heart**: a moment (a creature at its act, at the heart's spot), a nest (a pod), a spring (a one-cell spring in the grass, Essence +3 once) [canon check], or an old storm stone. Its kind is picked by seed from the places' list.
- **Found = seen**: the heart's tile unveiled and the explorer within 4 tiles (tune). Reaching it is the reward, not the finding. The field's Data is paid when the heart is found, once.
- **Tells** show through the veil within 8 tiles (tune; was 6 in the first build): prints (paws) for a creature, the slow beat for a pod, the shimmer for a spring [canon check], the bolt for a charged stone. They answer for 3 actions after a Call that reaches them and stop when the heart is found.

## Places: size, composition, names [canon check: naming places at all]

- Composed places are **18 × 22 tiles** (the cave and a ford keep 28 × 32). Four templates by land (pond Reedpool, rock the Split Stone, wood the Hollow Tree, meadow Windstep), each with its mirror, each a stage: one landmark (the island stone, the split stone, the hollow tree, the lone tree), a heart at its foot, paths from two to four openings, ground that changes by zone, an edge of trees or boulders. A place carries one of three names from its template's list [canon check].
- Rewrite "Place size": at 18 × 22 a bot walks to the heart in 9 to 17 actions and takes everything in 14 to 28; the first walk to a pod and 2 Data takes 49 actions at the median.

## Seeing the place [canon check: the reveal lines]

- **The arrival look** (first entry only), **Look over the place** (menu, free) and **the reveal** (when the heart is found: the veil dissolves, the place's name and the heart's one line hold, what is still there to take is ringed). All show the whole place at half scale; any press ends them and is consumed; reduced motion shows the last frame.
- The reach view shows a **found place as its own miniature** with its name under it; the full map names only the current cell.

## Signs and map states

- Add the **shimmer** (a spring) to the five signs [canon check]; the bolt stays for a charged stone, and a quiet stone has no sign.
- A place never entered lies under heavier night dots, one entered and not found under light dots, a found place is clear. The pending outline is gone.
- **Start guarantee**: at least one unfound heart's tell lies within one cell of the start, beside the existing Energy guarantee.
- The map line names the current cell and whether it is found: "meadow · found · Ahead: wood".

## The first walk and pods

- The first expedition's pods (one at distance 1, one at 2) lie in the **find slot nearest the heart** of their place, so a Call at the heart lifts them. Tested on seeds 1 to 200: the first walk holds a pod and the 2 Data that identify and read it.
- The bag at the find is a choice with a picture (the found pod beside the ones in the bag); a full bag never ends a walk.

## Open for the playtest

- Whether a person's walk is long enough: the bot's floor is 24 to 30 actions for a place and home and 60 to 81 for four places, against 100 to 130 and 500 to 650 the walk slice aimed at.
- A heart within 4 tiles of an arrival is found on arrival (5 of 32 place-and-side pairs).
- Energy at this scale: 0 to 3 a walk in calm weather, against 12 for Probe tier 2.
