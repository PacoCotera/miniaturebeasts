# Station Create (the review): brief for the "Shape a founder" screen

Brief for the research bench's Create screen, built on the approved [research loop](../../../design/proposals/research-loop.md) (§5 "Shape a founder", §8 row "Shape"), the [Station screens](../../../design/proposals/station-screens.md) ("Create"), the [style guide](../../../design/style-guide/station-screens.md) ("Create (the review)", and the Pods decisions of 2026-10-07) and the [species frames](../../../design/proposals/species-frames.md) (S01's five open traits). Flat screen, 1024×600 at 1×, edge to edge, no bezel. Siblings and quality bar: `../pods-v2/placed/PV-D-r3-a4-stamped-1024x600.png` (Pods, the bench's decided treatment) and `../round3/A-r3-a1-1024x600.png` (approved Home, the device). Pip: `art/miniature-lives/assets/rich-plain-300x310.png` and `rich-marked-300x310.png` (the pale patches). The stamp: the styled C01 face from `../../concept-stamp/`, placed after generation. No image generation is part of this brief.

**Owner rules, carried in.** The dark glass lab is the bench (decided 2026-10-07): deep blue-teal glass, anodised graphite, thin lit edges, one cool beam, exact small readouts; one device with Home and Pods (Inter smooth type, the bolt, diamond and drop counters, the bottom-line grammar, light from the upper left). The genome stamp sits on a **square label of about 220 px**, no plate; it is the prototype's cells placed, never redrawn. Fine pixel grain only on creatures; chrome and type crisp. Labels one engraved word, small, low-contrast, read second; captions on plates, never floating. No species name in the art (names are the copywriter's; species carry codes). Never digits of progress, locus counts, letters or ratios; the only digits are prices and the counters. A shape uses only the copies the pod carries; the code appears only at Grow, so no code is on this screen.

## 1. Purpose and reads first
Shape a founder and see its cost. **Reads first:** the founder, large and lit, within a second at 1×; then the roll of three pictures above it (which one is chosen); then the price in the bottom line and what stays a surprise.

## 2. The scenario drawn
The first S01 pod (research loop §4, expedition 1): Coat read ("shows plain · hides pale"), Movement read ("bursts · hides steady", a doings trait, breed to change), Face and Stamina unread. The player has rolled Markings to **only pale**: the founder redraws with cream pale patches on its charcoal body, the trait wears a "changed" tag, the price reads 2 ⚡ 4 ❀ 1 ◆. Because Face is unread, the founder's head (crown and eye rings) is under a cool frost: the Station draws only what is known. Face and Stamina are the surprises that clear during incubation.

## 7. Pass checklist (yes / no)
1. Founder, changes, surprises and cost are all visible at once.
2. The founder's misty parts match the unread chapters exactly: the head frosted (Face unread), the body clear.
3. The roll offers three pictures from the pod's own two copies and nothing the pod lacks; the chosen one is marked and tagged "changed".
4. The code appears nowhere (it appears only at Grow); the stamp label is empty until the real stamp is placed, and the placed stamp decodes from the finished screen.
5. Same chapter tabs and order as Pods; the doings chapter wears the joined rings.
6. The founder is the warmest, brightest thing; the chrome stays cool; the empty chamber is dark.
7. One device with Home and Pods in the dark glass lab.
8. Never digits of progress, locus counts, letters or ratios; no species name; labels one quiet word; captions on plates.
9. Strings exact (minor fail if garbled, major fail if an object or a light is wrong); flat screen, no bezel.
10. The companion frame shows a shape that won't build with no ✓ cap and the clash marked, nothing spent.
