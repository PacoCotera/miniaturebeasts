# Station Library, the tome's spread of plates: brief for the collection screen

Brief for the Library's collection screen after the owner chose **the tome's spread of plates** from the [two-direction exploration](../library-collection/README.md) (the gallery wall was disliked: hooks, plates, frames and lighting stole the spotlight from the specimens). It follows the rejected [cabinet](../library-cabinet/README.md) and [specimen case](../library-case/README.md), and it opens into the accepted [book](../library-book/README.md). Flat screen, 1024×600 at 1×, edge to edge, no bezel. Siblings: `../round3/A-r3-a1-1024x600.png` (approved Home, the device) and `../library-book/placed/BK-D-r2-a1-stamped-named-1024x600.png` (the book's plate style). No image generation is part of this brief.

**Owner rules, carried in.** The tome must be a **great illustration**: an old botanical-expedition book, the kind kept on a voyage, with life in it: worn board edges, foxed and deckled pages, pressed leaves and seeds between plates, a dried flower, a pencil note in the margin, a ribbon marker, an inked route or a tiny map vignette, the page's own weather. **The specimens are the spotlight.** Found species are tipped-in **framed plates in the book's own plate style** (the Book's mounted portrait at small size), never flat cards; the met species a pencil study; unmet species **empty ruled frames with blank caption rules, no cue**. **Exactly sixteen frames** on the spread, eight a page, growing by a page turn. Clan grouping by a ribbon or an ink rule **per frame group**, not at the page edge. The focus is the cream ring on the frame. No digits, no ledge. The accepted Pip is placed in the Loika frame; the strings are a placed layer; nothing generated carries a name.

## 1. Purpose and reads first
The whole record at a glance as pages of the book. **Reads first:** the specimens (three plates and a pencil study among sixteen frames), within a second at 1×; then which frame is focused; then the book's life around them.

## 2. The scenario drawn
Sixteen species released. Found: Loika (frame 1, the placed Pip, portrayed: a gilt corner on its mat), Untuva (frame 2), Tuikis (frame 3, focused). Met: Hiljan (frame 4), a pencil study of a sitting cat on the page. Unmet: frames 5–16 empty. Clans C01–C04 each marked by a short silk ribbon laid over the top-left corner of its frame (moss green, coral, lagoon, slate violet); unmet frames carry no ribbon and no colour.

## 5. Pass checklist (yes / no)
1. Exactly sixteen frames, eight a page, one dedicated place per species, seen at once.
2. The specimens are the spotlight: framed plates in the book's style, never flat cards; the met cat a pencil study.
3. Unmet frames give no cue: empty ruled frames, blank rules, no ribbon, no colour.
4. Clan marks per frame, not at the page edge; only on met species' frames.
5. The tome is a great illustration: worn boards, foxing, deckle, pressed leaves and seeds, a dried flower, a pencil note, a ribbon marker, a map vignette, without covering any frame.
6. The focus is the cream ring on the frame.
7. No digits, no ledge, no furniture; the Library stays one object, the book.
8. Pip is the placed asset in the Loika frame; names are a placed layer.
9. One device with Home; the bottom line's grammar.
10. Strings exact (minor fail if garbled, major fail if an object or a light is wrong); flat screen, no bezel.
