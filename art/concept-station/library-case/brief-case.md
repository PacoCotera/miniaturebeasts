# Station Library, the Specimen Case: brief for the collection screen

Brief for the Library's collection screen after the owner chose **direction B, the specimen case**, from the [collection display proposal](../../../design/proposals/collection-display.md) (§4 is the brief this one carries; the wireframe is [`B-specimen-case.svg`](../../../design/proposals/collection-display/B-specimen-case.svg)), with the owner's changes of 2026-10-08. It replaces the rejected [cabinet of boxes](../library-cabinet/README.md) and opens into the accepted [book](../library-book/README.md). Flat screen, 1024×600 at 1×, edge to edge, no bezel. Siblings: `../round3/A-r3-a1-1024x600.png` (approved Home, the device) and `../library-book/placed/BK-D-r2-a1-stamped-named-1024x600.png` (the book this opens into). No image generation is part of this brief.

**Owner rules, carried in.** The case is **not dressed in the tome's paper**; paper belongs to the book. The case has its own materials: an ebonised or dark wood frame, one glass lid, brass corners, a linen floor, cards standing on mounts, paper tags on brass pins that **never touch a mibi**. One tray per clan with the clan's colour on the tray's edge. Found species: a card with the face; Loika's card is the placed Pip; the portrayed species wears a gilt corner. A **met** species shows a **pencil field sketch** on its card and a **pencilled name** on its tag, visible on the plate. **Unmet** species are a bare tray: empty mounts, blank tags, nothing to read, no clan colour. Room to grow stays as bare linen floor with faint tray footprints. The synopsis is objects on the brass-edged ledge, never digits. Not basic: depth, material, shadows, a cataloguing hand; still quiet; knowledge, never material. Names are never baked into a generated plate: tags stay blank and the names are placed as a text layer (the met name in a pencil grey). No cue of any kind for an unmet species.

## 1. Purpose and reads first
The whole record at a glance. **Reads first:** how much of the case is filled (three found cards and one pencil sketch among sixteen places), within a second at 1×; then which card is focused; then the ledge.

## 2. The scenario drawn
Sixteen species released. Found: Loika (C01 Lophessa, tray edge moss green; portrayed, gilt corner), Untuva (C02 Kausida, coral), Tuikis (C03 Stilbera, lagoon; focused). Met in the field: Hiljan (C04 Lathreta, slate-violet edge), a pencil sketch of a cat on its card, its name pencilled on the tag. Unmet: twelve bare mounts with blank tags in one plain tray, no clan colour, all alike. The lower half of the floor is bare linen with faint footprints where the next drops' trays will sit.

## 5. Pass checklist (yes / no)
1. Every released species has a place, seen at once; the same slot holds thirty-two.
2. Three states by the object alone: found (face and name), met (pencil sketch and pencilled name), unmet (bare mount, blank tag, nothing to read).
3. Unmet gives no cue of any kind: no silhouette, no shape, no clan colour; the plain tray's twelve places are identical.
4. Clan colour shows only on trays whose member has been met; the tray edge carries it.
5. The portrayed species wears a gilt corner; Loika is the placed Pip; no pin touches a mibi.
6. The ledge reads the record as objects (tags, slider, pins, plate), with no digits.
7. The case is its own object: dark wood, glass, brass, linen; not the tome's paper and not flat tiles; cool light only.
8. Room to grow reads as bare linen with faint footprints.
9. Names are a placed text layer; nothing generated carries a name.
10. Strings exact (minor fail if garbled, major fail if an object or a light is wrong); flat screen, no bezel.
