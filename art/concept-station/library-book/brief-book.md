# Station Library, the Book: brief for the species page at full screen

Brief for the Library's second screen, the book, reworked to the whole screen after the owner kept the book's treatment and rejected the shelf of the [first Library round](../library/README.md). Built on the rewritten [style guide](../../../design/style-guide/station-screens.md) ("Library: Book"), the [research loop](../../../design/proposals/research-loop.md) (§4 "Species", §5 "Wish", §8 "Library"), the [taxonomy](../../../design/proposals/taxonomy.md) (§5 the seventh chapter is Character; §7 the tome page template) and the approved [species names](../../../design/proposals/species-names.md). Flat screen, 1024×600 at 1×, edge to edge, no bezel. Siblings: `../library/placed/LB-D-r3-a2-fit-stamped-named-1024x600.png` (the book's treatment, which stands), `../library-cabinet/placed/CB-B-r2-a1-pip-named-1024x600.png` (the cabinet this opens from) and `../round3/A-r3-a1-1024x600.png` (approved Home, the device). The stamp: the styled C03 tome face `../../concept-stamp/renders/glowtail-c03-tome-300.png`, placed after generation at 120 px. No image generation is part of this brief.

**Owner rules, carried in.** The species page has the whole screen to itself: portrait, places as stamps, the frame as a pressed plate, **as many chapter tabs as the species has** (Tuikis: Coat, Face, Shape, Legs & tail, Movement, Stamina, **Character**), looks with "more?", **the stamp at 120 px on a plain plate**, a **family tree panel** and the pinned wish. The family tree's display is being designed ([family-tree.md](../../../design/proposals/family-tree.md), to come): the book carries a clearly framed, empty placeholder region for it and invents no tree. Knowledge, never material. Names never baked into a generated plate: the name plate and the bottom line's centre stay empty and the names are placed as a text layer. The stamp is the prototype's cells placed, never redrawn, on a plain plate with no frame around it (a nested frame defeats the reader). Loika is Pip wherever it appears (not on this page).

## 1. Purpose and reads first
Each species' field guide. **Reads first:** the portrait and name, within a second at 1×; then the open chapter's specimen plates; then the stamp, the tree's frame and the wish.

## 2. The scenario drawn
Tuikis (S03, clan Stilbera), Coat open with three colours, two leg colours, two markings and one scale look found, each row ending in "more?"; the other six tabs closed; one wish pinned; the stamp of the one mibi grown; the family tree panel empty, framed, waiting for its design.

## 4. Light, materials, type
Cool, even light across the paper; one warm key light on the portrait and the tail-lamp's glow. Paper, ink, pressed specimens, mounted plates, a pin; no wood or felt. Inter, smooth: screen name 3×, the name 3× on its plate (placed), chapter words and trait words 2× in ink, habit line 2× (placed), top bar and bottom line 2×.
**Exact strings, nothing else in the generated art:** top bar "Library  T5", a yellow bolt "7", a blue diamond "3", a green drop "2", "Companion away · with Dot". Tabs "Coat", "Face", "Shape", "Legs & tail", "Movement", "Stamina", "Character". Trait words "Colour", "Leg colour", "Markings", "Scales"; "more?" on each row's last plate. Bottom line "✓ Visit · ← Cabinet" | (empty) | "a pod waits in the tray". **Placed afterwards:** "Tuikis" and "Stilbera · digs burrows · glows at dusk" on the name plate; "Tuikis · Stilbera" in the bottom line's centre.

## 5. Pass checklist (yes / no)
1. The page has the whole screen: portrait, places, frame plate, tabs, looks, stamp, tree panel, wish, all at once.
2. Seven tabs for Tuikis with Character the seventh; never a fixed four.
3. Every look found is a picture on a plate; each row ends in "more?"; no counts.
4. The stamp is the prototype's cells placed at 120 px on a plain plate with no frame around it, and decodes from the finished screen.
5. The family tree panel is a clearly framed, empty region; no tree is invented.
6. Knowledge, never material; never a text page.
7. The portrait is the only warm thing; the archive cool and even; a tome, not a cottage.
8. No species name in the generated art; the names are a placed text layer; no digits but the counters.
9. One device with Home and the cabinet; the bottom line returns to the Cabinet.
10. Strings exact (minor fail if garbled, major fail if an object or a light is wrong); flat screen, no bezel.
