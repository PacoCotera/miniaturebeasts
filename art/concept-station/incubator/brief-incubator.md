# Station Incubator: brief for the "Watch it grow" screen

Brief for the research bench's Incubator screen, built on the approved [research loop](../../../design/proposals/research-loop.md) (§1 step 6, §8 row "Incubate"), the Station screens proposal ("Incubator", decisions 3 "Leaves, not minutes"), the [style guide](../../../design/style-guide/station-screens.md) ("Incubator", and the Pods decisions of 2026-10-07) and the [species frames](../../../design/proposals/species-frames.md) (S01). Flat screen, 1024×600 at 1×, edge to edge, no bezel. Siblings and quality bar: `../create/placed/CR-C2-stamped-1024x600.png` (Create, the same bench and the founder this screen grows), `../pods-v2/placed/PV-D-r3-a4-stamped-1024x600.png` (Pods) and `../round3/A-r3-a1-1024x600.png` (approved Home). The stamp: the styled C01 face from `../../concept-stamp/`, placed after generation. No image generation is part of this brief.

**Owner rules, carried in.** The dark glass lab is the bench; one device with Home, Pods and Create (Inter smooth type, the bolt, diamond and drop counters, the bottom-line grammar, light from the upper left). The genome stamp sits on a **square label of about 220 px**, no plate; it is the prototype's cells placed, never redrawn. Fine pixel grain only on creatures; chrome and type crisp. Labels one engraved word, small, low-contrast, read second; captions on plates, never floating. No species name. **Time reads as leaves, never digits**; no digits of progress, locus counts, letters or ratios. The embryo is the only warm, living thing. Read-only while growing: the bottom line draws no ✓ cap.

## 1. Purpose and reads first
Watch the embryo grow and open it when it is ready. **Reads first:** the embryo glowing in the dome, within a second at 1×; then how many leaves remain; then which chapters have cleared.

## 2. The scenario drawn
The founder shaped on Create (Coat and Movement read, Markings shaped to pale) went into the chamber at Grow. Four leaves is its time. Two leaves are full, the third is filling, the fourth is empty. The embryo is at the bud stage: a pearl bud glowing warm from inside, the shape not yet showing. Face has just cleared (its arc lit, its page known), Stamina is still a surprise. The stamp on the label shows Coat, Face and Movement read, Stamina an empty block; the code appeared at Grow and sits on a small plate under the label (live text).

## 3. Information hierarchy
1. The dome, centred and large, the embryo glowing inside: the one warm thing.
2. The ring of leaves over the dome: two full, one filling, one empty.
3. The chapter arcs above: Coat, Movement and Face full weight, Stamina hairline.
4. The stamp label at the right with its code plate; the status plate under the dome.
5. Top bar and bottom line.

## 5. The states
**Growing (the candidate):** as above.
**Ready (companion frame):** all four leaves full, the dome glowing brighter with the bud opened to the species' shape asleep inside, the bottom line's left part orange "✓ Open · ← Home" and the right part "ready".
**Just started:** all leaves empty, a seed in the nest, every unread arc hairline; not drawn this round.

## 6. Light, materials, type
One key light from the upper left: cool and even on glass and graphite; the only warm light is the embryo's glow inside the dome. Glass, anodised graphite, lit edges, brushed rims; the leaves in leaf green on the cool chrome, the one touch of colour besides the glow. Inter, smooth, tabular figures: screen name 3×, tab words 2× engraved, "growing" 2× on its plate, the line under it in mist, top bar and bottom line 2×. Cream on chrome, context in mist, no orange on this screen (nothing to confirm).
**Exact strings, nothing else:** top bar "Incubator  T5", a yellow bolt "7", a blue diamond "3", a green drop "2", "Companion away · with Dot". Tabs "Coat", "Face", "Movement", "Stamina". Plate "growing" over "a founder · rock field". Bottom line "← Home" | "founder · growing" | "Stamina still a surprise".

## 7. Pass checklist (yes / no)
1. Time reads as leaves, never digits: two full, one filling, one empty.
2. The embryo is the only warm, living thing; chrome, leaves and label cool.
3. Ready would read from across a table: the companion frame's dome glows and every leaf is full.
4. Unread chapters clear as it grows: Face just lit, Stamina still hairline; the placed stamp shows the same (Coat, Face, Movement read; Stamina empty) and decodes from the finished screen.
5. The bottom line draws no ✓ cap while growing.
6. The dome is centred and about 360 px across; the leaves arc over it; the arcs above; the status plate below.
7. One device with Home, Pods and Create in the dark glass lab.
8. No digits of progress, no timer digits, no species name; labels one quiet word; captions on plates; the code only on its plate (live).
9. Strings exact (minor fail if garbled, major fail if an object or a light is wrong); flat screen, no bezel.
10. A still frame shows progress.
