## What was cut and how

- Every picture is the art desk's, read from the pinned desk commit by tools/desk-art.mjs (the pebbles and lily pads are the water bits, art/desk 3323aa85d).
- map palette (art director, mibi-art f1a9fe54e, carried by 633e4d641): every map picture is the desk's recoloured copy in art-jobs/2026-11-10-art-recolour-map-palette/out/applied/<job>/. The pieces the desk has not re-issued there (the style test's route A rock, tufts and flowers, the hollow tree and split stone, the cliff re-cut sheets) take the desk's own colour map (tools/recolour.mjs, tools/recolour-palette.json = its out/palette.json), exact colour to exact colour, no pixel drawn; DESK.json says `recoloured`. The way-up pieces are the desk's own paint in the map palette (art/desk 1158b9710).
- map decor (`desk`: true, tools/desk-art.mjs deskArt; the kit names stay and draw the signed pieces): the tree (cut in two, crown and trunk) and bush of style test 3, the rock, tufts and flowers of route A, the stump and the blue flowers of the map-decor job, the hollow tree (64 x 64) and the split stone, the scrub shrubs; tree-autumn draws the one signed tree, the three bushes and both boulders share one piece, clump-tall is one of four signed tall clumps (26 and 17 rows, each mirrored; the generator picks by hash), the blue flowers are area-reduced to 24 x 22, the scrub ground and the cattail heads take one colour map each, the sweeps of tall grass are the signed tufts-2 and tufts-3 as they are and mirrored. The grass flecks (six the art director keeps) are decals, the reed clumps (four shore looks, two water looks, each mirrored) are props that block nothing; both are laid by the generator's scatter rules (decor.mjs, reeds.mjs).
- terrain layers (`desk`: true, tools/desk-art.mjs deskLayers; set.json `layers` and `tone`): the water, earth, sand, mud, path and grass layers and the grass tone layer. A fill is slot 15 of a signed sheet cropped without a changed pixel (DESK.json `from: <sheet>#15`) or the desk's own fill strip. The water fill has four frames (the ripple job), each a strip of its four variants, 250 ms a frame; deep water stays still. Scrub is the olive flat ground over grass (its ground): the sheet, three variants and the slot-15 strip. Thicket and cliff come with every pixel of alpha under 255 made transparent in their over sheets (art director ruling 2026-11-09: the soft cast shade goes, the opaque undersides and face shading stay); the cliff sheets are the desk's cut run again without the dither specks (tools/recut/README.md); the thicket fill is its full strip, the cliff top is slot 15 of the plain over-cliff sheet, the cliff's tall faces at s = 3 are three variants. The tone layer is the signed lipless grass tone with its dark colour mapped to the sunlit step #82b266 (art director ruling 2026-11-09 20:4x: a patch is lighter than the grass, never a hollow); it is drawn on grass and on the raised top (the cliff terrain, where the cell two rows below is cliff too). The palette keeps its indices (palettePin) and the new colours follow.
