## What was cut and how

- reeds: the pack has no reeds; its bamboo stalk stands in. tall-grass: three of the pack's grass tufts in a row stand in as the frames of a sway (the pack's daisy plant read as flowers). Both are test stand-ins for the art desk's own.
- tree-hollow: the pack's great pair of gnarled trees with the arch between them, cut whole; the Hollow Tree's landmark, its heart at the foot of the arch. split-stone: the pack's three stacked grey boulders with the dark crack between them, cut whole; the Split Stone's landmark.
- desk (`desk`: true, tools/desk-art.mjs): the art desk's way-up pieces, signed in mibi-art abb33a65b (art-jobs/2026-11-09-open-edges/out/), and its meadow decor, signed in mibi-art 5734abc5 (art-jobs/2026-11-09-meadow-decor/out/; small flowers and tufts that replace the kit's sunflowers). Read from those commits, copied byte for byte, hashes in DESK.json; their colours join the palette.
- terrain layers (`desk`: true, tools/desk-art.mjs deskLayers; set.json `layers` and `tone`): the water, earth, sand, mud, path and grass layers and the grass tone layer, signed in mibi-art 4e21bd68f (sand job), 386ed11fa and b60587b26 (meadow), read from those commits. A fill is slot 15 of a signed sheet cropped without a changed pixel (DESK.json `from: <sheet>#15`) or the desk's own fill strip; the reed look of the sand beach is the pixels of the signed reed composites that differ from the plain beach composite. Scrub is drawn as grass. Thicket (mibi-art e43e480ac) and cliff (c335654cc) come with every pixel of alpha under 255 made transparent in their over sheets (art director ruling 2026-11-09: the soft cast shade goes, the opaque undersides and face shading stay); the thicket fill is its full strip, the cliff top is slot 15 of the plain over-cliff sheet, the cliff's tall faces at s = 3 are three variants. The palette keeps its indices (palettePin) and the new colours follow.
