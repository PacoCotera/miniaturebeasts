## What was cut and how

- Every piece is cut whole from the pack's 13-piece blob sets (4 outer corners, 4 edges, centre, 4 inner corners); the two diagonal tiles are pieced from two inner corners. Nothing is repainted.
- grass|sand: the pack has no grass-and-sand blob, so the pack's grass-and-dirt blob is used with its four dirt colours changed to the sand colours of the sand shore (exact colour map in the recipe, `recolour`).
- The sand full tile is the pack's floor sand (TilesetFloor 0,5); the water sheet's own sand cell (0,5) is a lighter peach that does not match the shore pieces and would show a seam.
- mud|water is the pack's dark swamp shore: brown mud and purple water. Its water is not the blue water of the other pairs; the three never meet (an opaque pair refuses a third terrain at a corner).
- reeds: the pack has no reeds; its bamboo stalk stands in. tall-grass: the pack's swaying plant (four frames) stands in. Both are test stand-ins for the art desk's own.

## Pairs

- `grass-water`: grass over water, opaque, 4 frames
- `sand-water`: sand over water, opaque, 4 frames
- `grass-sand`: grass over sand, opaque, 1 frame
- `grass-path`: grass over path, opaque, 1 frame
- `mud-water`: mud over water, opaque, 4 frames
