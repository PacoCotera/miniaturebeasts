# Companion care: the hearts

The bonded heart on the Companion, hand-pixelled on the 48 colours (HiBit): the same enamel heart as the Station's Vivarium card, lit from the top left, `coral` with a `peach` light and a `red` shade, its outline in the red ramp's dark `wine` (never black). No face, no sparkle. Fully opaque pixels on a clear ground. `source/hearts.py` draws both.
