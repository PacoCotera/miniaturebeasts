# Companion care: the hearts

The bonded heart on the Companion: the same enamel heart as the Station's Vivarium card, lit from the top left, `coral` body with a `peach`/`blush` light, one soft `red` shade down the right side and into the tip, its outline in the red ramp's dark `wine` (never black). No face, no sparkle, no centre line or facets: it reads as one whole heart. Fully opaque pixels on a clear ground.

How made: a Retro Diffusion master, then a hand touch-up by the pixel lead (rules.md 18, OWNER 11-16 11:0x: marks up to 32 px and touch-ups of painted art may be hand-pixelled). Master: `rd_plus__low_res`, 24×24 native, text-to-image, input_palette = wine/red/coral/peach/blush from `palette/palette.json`, seed 5201, request `6218b0bd-9338-48a4-aa07-b3dd558ff052` (raw `art-jobs/2026-11-16-companion-heart-2/work/raw/t1-h24-1.png`). The master's outline, silhouette, glint and colours are kept; its split lower half (a light centre stripe with wine wedges, read as a broken heart), the stray dash in the right lobe and the drip under the tip were redrawn by hand as one soft shade. The 16 is drawn by hand from the 24 master. References (rules.md 18d, OpenGameArt): "Pixel Hearts" by TokyoGeisha (CC0), "N64 hearts" 0016 (CC0), "heart 16*16" heart_6 (CC-BY, look only). Script and sheet: `miniaturebeasts-internal/tools/credit-runner/runs/companion-heart-5/`.
