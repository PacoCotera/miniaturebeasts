# Mibi 7×9, the Companion's bitmap face

The master glyph sheet for the face [ui-kit §2](../../../design/proposals/ui-kit.md) and the [style guide](../../../design/style-guide/README.md) (Type) decide for the Companion: cap height 7, x-height 5, descenders 2, rounded bowls, tabular digits, one pixel of tracking, set at **2× for HUD, bottom line and body, 3× for titles and names, never 1×**. No sheet existed until now. The Companion page still sets its 5×7 stand-in (`GLYPHS` in `prototypes/exploration/index.html`; the comment in [`../tools/font.py`](../tools/font.py) that calls it Mibi 7×9 is a misnomer), so nothing in the page changes with this delivery.

**Status: candidate, generated by script from the hand-written source; art director to sign.** Nothing here is shown onward before that.

## The face

- **Cell 7×9.** Columns 0 and 6 are the side bearings and stay empty; ink is in columns 1–5. Caps rows 0–6 (cap height 7), lower case rows 2–6 (x-height 5), ascenders rows 0–6, the baseline under row 6, descenders rows 7–8.
- **Advance** is the ink extent plus one pixel of tracking, so letters sit one font pixel apart as the kit says. Put the cell's column 1 on the pen, draw, then move the pen by the atlas `advance`. Space advances 3. **Digits are tabular**: every digit advances 5 (4 wide ink), so `1111111111` and `0000000000` are the same width. The recommended line pitch is 11 font pixels (22 px at 2×, 33 px at 3×).
- **Width.** Set at 2×, seven of the page's bottom-line and card strings measure 93–97% of what the 5×7 takes, so they fit where they fit now.
- **Rounded, never childish.** One-pixel strokes, clean verticals, bowls with their corners cut, no diagonal noise except where a letter is a diagonal (the V W X Y Z K, the k v w x y and the arrows).

## Where 7×9 did not hold, and what was done

1. **Capital accents.** A seven-row capital plus a two-row mark needs nine rows of its own, and the cell has only two above the x-height line. Rather than clip a mark, `Á À Â Ä É È Ê Ë Í Ì Î Ï Ó Ò Ô Ö Ú Ù Û Ü Ñ Ÿ` are set on a **five-row body** (rows 2–6) under the mark (rows 0–1): the accent keeps its full shape, the capital keeps its width and form, and it stands two rows shorter than an unaccented capital. `Ç` and `Œ` keep the full seven rows (the cedilla hangs in the descender rows). Lower-case accents fit as they are: the mark takes the two ascender rows, touching the letter diagonally as on the classic character-LCD faces; the diaeresis is one row with a gap.
2. **`❀`.** Essence is a green drop and "materials keep one shape" (the style guide), so the `❀` code point is drawn as the **drop**, not a flower. The Station's `ICON_GLYPH` swaps in the icon anyway.
3. **Wide marks.** Em dash, ellipsis and the two chevrons are the full five columns; an em dash cannot be wider than an en dash plus one here (5 and 4).
4. **`1`.** Its ink is three wide in a four-wide tabular slot, so it sits half a pixel left of centre; moving it would break the one-pixel grid.

## Questions for the art director

1. Capital accents sit on a five-row body inside the nine-row cell (above). Accept that, or allow the accented capitals two rows of headroom above the cell, with the full seven-row capital under them? The Companion's line pitch is 11 font pixels, so the leading has the room; the cost is that those glyphs are 7×11, not 7×9.
2. `❀` is drawn as the essence drop (materials keep one shape). Is that right, or should the code point stay a flower and the drop live only as the 16 px icon?
