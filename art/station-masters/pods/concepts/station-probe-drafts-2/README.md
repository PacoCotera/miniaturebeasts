No face, eye, lens ring, screen, needle, glow or gloss. Honest notes. C: the three plates are two on the front-left and left faces and one on the top shoulder under a corner guard (the room for a fourth is the right face); the window panel is small and low and shows a small pod in it; the cord is a tan braided rope with an orange knot. D: the plates are three curved segments around the upper shell (not the middle), a fourth would fit between them; the window is round and framed in a thin yellow ring and shows a small pod inside; the rope is dark and twisted. Neither is at bench size: the Probe is about 60 percent of a 1376x768 picture (about 460 px tall, not 288), and the silhouette beside it is about 270 px tall, not 48 (the 48 px version is cut from it). The silhouettes are filled: C's near black, D's mid grey.
