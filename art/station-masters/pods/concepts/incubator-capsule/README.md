Honest flaws:
- **master-closed:** the pod is the charcoal and russet pod with russet dots and ribs and a russet cap. The arch became a soft warm lamp panel in the top of the capsule, with a falloff over the moss; it reads as a hanging lozenge lamp at the roof, not as a light set into the back wall behind the pod. The moss came out drier, more grass and straw than before. The mint bar is kept. The capsule is a little taller in the frame than the first one.
- **master-open:** same capsule, lamp, pod and moss as the master; the glass door swings on the right with its latch knob, as in the mint-door reference. Clean.
- **incubate:** the moment reads as evening, a tucked-in calm, with a mint ring tracing the whole glass opening. But the door is fully shut, not almost shut, and the base's mint bar is gone (the ring replaces it). The room changed: the sage wall became a blurred warm interior with a curtain and a frame, not the same room as the others.
- **hatch-now:** the pod is open in two shell halves with the russet cap tipped aside on the left, and a young creature stands between them facing right (not mirrored), looking out; joyful and bright. But it is not exactly the reference juvenile: its cap and back came out near-black with a white patch (the reference is dark slate-green and cream), its face is paler with a smaller muzzle, the two leaves are a brighter green, and its body is chunkier. Read it as the same kind of creature, not the same design. The shells' dots and ribs are russet, the mint bar glows.
