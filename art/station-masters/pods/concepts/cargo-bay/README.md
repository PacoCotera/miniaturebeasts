**The cut** (`tools/baycut.py`): a band of plain back wall is taken out (feathered) to reach 3.1:1, then reduced to 992×320. The painted standing pads did not line up with the crate places, so they are taken out: the floor is plain where the crates stand, as the brief asks. On the shut picture the band taken out is a whole number of door slats (period 37 px), so the slats stay even.

**Art director's read: signed.** The bay is the Incubator stage's family and the small Home bay's look at full width. The crates stand at the back of the floor and lift off the dark wall. The lamps (cargo.json, y 304 on the face) fall on the plain front of the floor. One note for placement: the crate slices carry no contact shadow, so they sit slightly light on the floor. A soft shadow under an occupied place is the placement's to add, not the bay's, because an empty place must stay plain.
