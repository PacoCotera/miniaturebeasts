# The Incubator as one object (T-20261017-incubator-object): six paintings, raw

Three directions (A brooder, B bell jar, C household appliance), each closed and then open, from `studio-prompts.md` (work-queue 269727c2). Raw, for the art director's gate; nothing is signed or placed. One request per picture, no retries. Each `*-raw-1264x848.jpg` is the model's 3:2 output as it came; each `*-1024x600.png` is a centred crop of it (resized to 1024 wide, 600 rows from the middle). Each open painting was made from its closed one as the reference (same object, camera, room, light), with the Loika pod reference `ref-loika-pod-sealed-3x.png` also passed. The owner's five reference images were not available to the studio (they are on the internal work-queue only), so they were not used; the prompts are the art director's text. Jobs: `source/work/incubator-object-*.json`; the pod ref is in `source/work/guides/inc/`.

Honest flaws:
- **A (brooder):** closed and open share camera, room and light well; the glass dome in the open one is a big clear sphere hinged back, a little loose as a clamshell, and the lamp's hood glints in it. The lamp hood and neck came out copper-brown (not the amber light alone); it is metal-looking, though matte. The pod is small in the frame (about a quarter of the object's height) and the pod's cap glows orange-amber.
- **B (bell jar):** the painting is more illustrated and flatter than A and C (a different hand), and the pod reads blue-grey with russet, not charcoal. A floor lamp with a lit shade stands at the right edge in both: a second visible light that was not asked for, bright enough to compete with the pod. Closed and open match exactly, the bell set down at the left. The jar's base has a horizontal vent line, as asked.
- **C (appliance):** the pod is clearly charcoal and russet; the cream fabric hose runs left out of the frame; the porthole door in the open one is a real swung-wide door with a hinge. A second lit lamp shade sits at the top right, and the door shows a distorted hose through its glass. The body is bone-white, the light inside the porthole is warm but the object is not the warmest or brightest thing: the room's lamp is as bright.
- **Pod:** in A and C the pod keeps its charcoal shell and russet dots; in all three the pod's russet cap reads more copper-brown than the reference's.
- No text, screens, gauges, cables, chrome (A's and C's metal is matte), pink or creature appears; B's and C's floor-lamp base and the door's hinge are the only metal.
