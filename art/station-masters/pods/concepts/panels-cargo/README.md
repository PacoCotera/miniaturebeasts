Honest flaws:
- The pods' see-through middle is made in the cut, not painted: alpha 0.5 in the middle (an ellipse over the front panel), rising to 1.0 at the rim; the dots in the middle go see-through with it.
- The pod is painted in warm amber (candled), so the charcoal shell reads only at the sides; the cap came out a flat russet-brown, without the signed cap's cream trim and leaf mark.
- Opening pod: the crack runs a little down the front panel as well as along the cap line, and the glow left a faint pink haze on the two cap corners (the magenta ground bled into the soft glow; despilled, a trace stays).
- Crate: the lid hangs slightly over the body on both sides and the light seam is a thin hard-edged line, a little neon; the signed crate's label plate stays on the lid.
- Hatch: a door seen in three-quarter view (hinged left, wide open), the wells are round and shallow and a little larger than 56x64 in proportion at this size; the find strip is a slot with no contents. The sage is greyer than the bay's metal.
