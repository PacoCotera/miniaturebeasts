**Read them honestly:**
- **All three:** the labels came out right and nothing red, no hands or people. The Bay object is a wooden crate (cabinet frame) or a metal-cornered case (the others), not the sage field case of the Cargo master; the Companion is a small handheld, not the exact device.
- **Frame 1 (cabinet):** the plate paper is warm and aged, not bright cream; the specimens are few (a leaf, two eyes, pale patches as outlines); the drawer's portraits are three, joined by thin threads. The slate pane around the cabinet is a plain dark frame.
- **Frame 2 (dome):** the dome is the only dusk and sits well below the vivarium's brightness; the constellation draws a creature's silhouette roughly (a bear-like head and a body), not clearly the Loika; the found-look lights are only two or three tiny images; the portraits at the base are glowing roundels.
- **Frame 3 (jar):** the face "painted like a seed" came out as a smiling bulb with a cartoon face, not the Loika's own face (this one needs a retry if the face matters); five small portraits sit in the roots, two of them teal creatures that are not Loikas; the flowers show no readable looks; the jar's rim is a screw-top jar.
