# The Station's face (LVGL)

The Station's screens are drawn by LVGL 9.6 in C, the same face on the sandbox (WebAssembly), the Raspberry Pi (native Linux) and, with a different display driver, the devices ([technical-architecture.md §8](../../design/proposals/technical-architecture.md#8-assessment-the-real-lvgl-face-in-the-sandbox-now)). The rules, the views, the spec files, the assets, the save and the Caddy client stay where they are: JavaScript and data, in `prototypes/station` and `prototypes/ui`. The face is thin: props in, intents out.

**The plan from here is [lvgl-switch.md](../../design/proposals/lvgl-switch.md)**: the face is props in, intents out, with the vocabulary and the layout rules in C, and every screen moves to it in order (the slots of §3: L2.0 the platform and Pods, L2.2 Home, Cargo and Idle, R, V, L and P, then 7 to 10, then L3). **The face is the only face**: the JavaScript drawing layer is removed (B4a-3), `removed.json` lists it and `tools/guard.mjs` keeps it gone. **The node path** (placing rectangles, text, pictures and clips by hand) is compiled only into the test build (`-DFACE_NODE_API`, `dist/node/`, and the native build): the page's face has none of its exports, and `tests/node-scene.mjs` is how the tests use it. Still to come in L2.0 (B4b): the SDL and DRM builds, the Node host playing Pods against the face, the aarch64 hash and the native goldens.

**Status: R1, Create on the face, after Idle (H3), Cargo (H2), Home (H1) and B4a-3, the JavaScript drawing layer removed.** The Station page (`/sandbox/station/`) draws nothing itself: `main.mjs` is the face's host. It sends the face the palette, the spec files (frame, pods, home, cargo and create), the pictures and each screen's props, copies what the face redrew onto the page's canvas, and turns the keys and the face's intents into the rules (`station/src/intents/`). Pods is drawn whole by the C words (the room, the dish, the pod from its signed layers, the wells, the page, the rail, the rings, the frame) from `views/pods-props.mjs`. **Home** is drawn by the words the living window (the bezel and the glass), the module (a panel, its engraved word, its lamp), the name tag, the rest knob and the leaves word's grid form, in the binding table `screens/home.c`, from `views/home-props.mjs`: the residents are seeded in props and walk in C (each from its own LCG seeded by its props seed, on `home.json` `regions.resident.walk`: the feet in the ground band, the keep-out zone round the bed, 2 px a step in two groups so that at most half of them change in a frame, idle and walk frozen while one is focused; the focused one is drawn last under its feet ring; each node is named by its place in the depth order, so two residents changing places redraw their two boxes and never the glass), the with-you bed holds the carried set asleep or the Companion's mark, the column's five modules hold their objects (a picture the host has not sent is a slot not yet filled: nothing is drawn, nothing moves; the PH plates are pictures of the register), Home's focus is `focus.graph` of `home.json` on the boxes the words drew, `events.crateIn` slides the crates into the Cargo module and `events.rest` settles the knob and dithers to Idle. **Cargo** is drawn by the binding table `screens/cargo.c` from `views/cargo-props.mjs`: the bay (the `panel` word, its inside, shut a PH lid over it), its sealed crates sliding in on the Dock (`events.crateIn`, clipped to the inside), the waiting mark, the rack (the same six wells, in the same order, as Pods' collection), the `ribbon` word, one crate closer opening and its pods travelling to their wells (`events.opening`: the crate and the step in it come from the arrival's own clock, a crate every 3000 ms; with reduced motion the event ends at once and the host stages each step in the props, a cut at its time), and the report card (`reportCard`: its height is `104 + 24 × (crates + Probe row) + (world lines ? 40 + 24 × lines : 0)`); every step shows or hides nodes the face already holds (a hidden sprite is its node with a window of no size), so a refresh redraws only what moved. **Idle** (`props.idle`, `frame.json` `idle`) is the same living-window code on the frame spec's rectangles (`idle_words` in `screens/home.c`: the Vivarium's flat plates until the painting master, the residents walking with their feet in the walk ground and never behind the bed, the bed and the carried set asleep) and the one line on its strip (`props.frame.idle.line`); no frame word, no ring; the first key of any kind says `wake`; its enter and wake dithers are the host's. **Create** (`screens/create.c`, from `views/create-props.mjs` and `create.json`) is the states nothingRead, shape and grow: the bench, the chapter rail (a pip a trait: filled, hollow, a changed diamond or a clash cross, the focused trait's lifted by the tab's `lift` prop (frame.json focus.lift.chrome, 2 px)), the dish and the pod, the small chamber with its busy bud, the work tray with the founder (always one composed picture, the Bayer pick of the old and the new: a `dither` event on the founder carries the old picture's id as its string `from`, 16 levels over 200 ms), the roll (three picture places and two notches always have their nodes; a trait that does not roll shows one picture and no notch; the ring stays on the chosen one: the roll is a stepper), the trait line (the changed tag, the clash ✕ or the breed mark, each with its node in every state), the leaves the bud will take (every leaf empty, the nodes up to the spec's maximum), the stamp label and the code rule; `events.grow` prints the stamp row by row for 300 ms, shows the code at 300 and moves the pod from 300 to 900 in a straight eased line behind the work tray. Every other screen sends `state: "notBuilt"` and the face draws frame.json `notBuilt` (the frame, the stage in the ground and the line `this screen is not built yet`). Room keys, ← and the first press on Idle work on every screen. Each picture the host sends carries its layer policy (`art` or `painted`) and its status (`placeholder` or `master`) from one family table (`ui/asset-policy.mjs`); a sprite or nine-slice takes its layer from its asset, the words choose a layer only for rectangles, text and composed pictures. Text is Inter 16, 20 and 28 px through LVGL's font engine from `lv_font_conv` C files baked from the frozen TrueType files in `ui/fonts/inter/src`. The JavaScript drawing layer is deleted.

## Building

```
# once: the pinned Emscripten SDK (CI does this and caches it)
git clone https://github.com/emscripten-core/emsdk.git ~/emsdk && (cd ~/emsdk && ./emsdk install $(cat <repo>/prototypes/face/emsdk.version) && ./emsdk activate $(cat <repo>/prototypes/face/emsdk.version))
EMSDK=~/emsdk prototypes/face/build.sh all     # needs cmake 3.28+, ninja, gcc for the native build
node --test prototypes/face/tests/*.test.mjs    # the Node checks (after build.sh: some need dist/)
PW_DIR=<dir with node_modules/playwright> node prototypes/face/tools/face-check.mjs
npm install --prefix /tmp/lvfc lv_font_conv@1.5.3 && sh prototypes/face/tools/bake-fonts.sh   # only to regenerate the fonts
```

Open the Station (`/sandbox/station/`; the build's `dist/` must sit beside the page: `prototypes/face/dist`, published at `/sandbox/face/dist`). `?test` puts the face in test mode (the palette passes and the log); `?dev` opens the developer panel.

## What L0 measured (Chromium headless; a development machine and CI's ubuntu-24.04 runner)

| | Development machine | CI runner (first run, cache cold) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emscripten 4.0.23 install and both builds | n/a | 1 min 51 s for the whole step; later runs restore the SDK from the cache |
| Clean build, WebAssembly (LVGL 9.6.0 + the face, `-O2`) | 26.7 s | 35.0 s |
| Clean build, native Linux (gcc) | 19–20 s | 39.1 s |
| `face.wasm` | 221,847 B (89,896 B gzipped) | the same bytes |
| `face.mjs` | 9,251 B (3,101 B gzipped) | the same bytes |
| Page: fetch, compile and initialise | 5 to 7 ms; 15 to 19 ms with the CPU throttled 4× | 7.9 ms; 14.1 ms throttled 4× |
| A frame with nothing changed | 0.003 ms | 0.003 ms |
| A full 1024×600 frame copied to the canvas | 3.3 ms; 15 to 19 ms throttled 4× | 2.7 ms; 13.1 ms throttled 4× (a 60 Hz frame is 16.7 ms) |
| The WebAssembly and native faces' framebuffer hashes | equal (`5fc5fdc5`) | equal (`5fc5fdc5`) |
| The whole job (build, site, smoke, journey, face check, layer checks) | n/a | 5 min 13 s |

The full-frame copy is the worst case (LVGL redraws only what changed, and the page copies only those rectangles); on a phone it fits a frame at 4× throttling but only just, so the copy is done on 32-bit words. The sizes are the empty face: the fonts and images arrive at L1.

## What L1 added

| | Development machine |
| --- | --- |
| `face.wasm` with the three Inter fonts and the scene interpreter | 348,437 B (134,501 B gzipped); the fonts are 137 KB of it |
| Page: fetch, compile and initialise | 6.2 ms; 25.7 ms throttled 4× |
| A full 1024×600 frame copied to the canvas | 3.0 ms; 13.2 ms throttled 4× |
| Where the frame's ink lies, face against canvas renderer | title, materials and subject identical; companion and the need within 4 px of the right edge; the action's last word within 2 px |
| Fixed scene, WebAssembly against native | equal (`3456b1be`) |

LVGL rounds each glyph's advance to whole pixels; the type atlases kept fractions. The views therefore measure through the face (`FACE.measure`), so centring and clipping use the widths LVGL gives, and a string's last letters can sit 1 to 4 px from where the canvas renderer put them.
