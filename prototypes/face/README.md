# The Station's face (LVGL)

The Station's screens are drawn by LVGL 9.6 in C, the same face on the sandbox (WebAssembly), the Raspberry Pi (native Linux) and, with a different display driver, the devices ([technical-architecture.md §8](../../design/proposals/technical-architecture.md#8-assessment-the-real-lvgl-face-in-the-sandbox-now)). The rules, the views, the spec files, the assets, the save and the Caddy client stay where they are: JavaScript and data, in `prototypes/station` and `prototypes/ui`. The face is thin: props in, intents out.

**The plan from here is [lvgl-switch.md](../../design/proposals/lvgl-switch.md)**: the face is props in, intents out, with the vocabulary and the layout rules in C, and every screen moves to it in order (the slots of §3: L2.0 the platform and Pods, L2.2 Home, Cargo and Idle, R, V, L and P, then 7 to 10, then L3). **The face is the only face**: the JavaScript drawing layer is removed (B4a-3), `removed.json` lists it and `tools/guard.mjs` keeps it gone. **The node path** (placing rectangles, text, pictures and clips by hand) is compiled only into the test build (`-DFACE_NODE_API`, `dist/node/`, and the native build): the page's face has none of its exports, and `tests/node-scene.mjs` is how the tests use it. Still to come in L2.0 (B4b): the SDL and DRM builds, the Node host playing Pods against the face, the aarch64 hash and the native goldens.

**Status: M1a, the motion proof on stand-in cells behind the developer switch "Motion sets (stand-ins)" (Station M1: residents with moving sets breathe, hop or walk, curl to sleep, stir and wander at night on Home's glass, Idle and the Vivarium's whole, Greet up close and the hatch's first motion; a set plays without the switch only when every cell is a placed master, so a player sees no change until M1b), after P, the Probe bench on the face, after L (the Library spread), V2 (one mibi up close and the namer), V1 (the Vivarium's whole), the Incubator (R2), Create (R1), Idle (H3), Cargo (H2), Home (H1) and B4a-3, the JavaScript drawing layer removed.** The Station page (`/sandbox/station/`) draws nothing itself: `main.mjs` is the face's host. It sends the face the palette, the spec files (frame, pods, home, cargo, create, incubator, vivarium, habitat, namer, library and bench), the pictures and each screen's props, copies what the face redrew onto the page's canvas, and turns the keys and the face's intents into the rules (`station/src/intents/`). Pods is drawn whole by the C words (the room, the dish, the pod from its signed layers, the wells, the page, the rail, the rings, the frame) from `views/pods-props.mjs`. **The Incubator** is drawn by the binding table `screens/incubator.c` from `views/incubator-props.mjs`: the bench, the rail (a pip is one composed node whichever it is, so a chapter turning read fills pips and adds no node), the chamber's back (signed masters: standby, growing, ready) and the window's front over the nest and the bud, the nest and its front, the bud (the signed bean: early, late, ready, its ready shape and its front over the shape, and the two crack steps over the ready bud; the shape is a slot, empty until a species' is placed), the base master (it carries the plaque plate and the foot light) with the plaque word, the waiting lamp, the juvenile, the stamp, the code and the ribbon. No ring in any state: one subject, ✓ acts on the room and the pad says nothing. Events: `growNow`, `wipe` (a tab's pips fill over 1000 ms; the host plays one for each chapter the bud clears) and `hatch` (a still beat: the chamber stays as it stands and nothing lifts or opens; the bud keeps its ready picture, shape and front until 600, cracks at 600 (crack-1 over the ready bud) and 800 (crack-2 alone over it) and goes at 1000; the juvenile stands from 1000 and steps out at 1400 and 1800 by the meet's hop table; the ribbon shows at 1200; the host hands off at 2600). **Home** is drawn by the words the living window (the bezel and the glass), the module (a panel, its engraved word, its lamp), the name tag and the rest knob, in the binding table `screens/home.c`, from `views/home-props.mjs`: the residents are seeded in props and walk in C (each from its own LCG seeded by its props seed, on `home.json` `regions.resident.walk`: the feet in the ground band, the keep-out zone round the bed, 2 px a step in two groups, one group tick a frame at most (a late frame drops the time past it), so that at most half of them change in any frame, idle and walk frozen while one is focused; the focused one is drawn last under its feet ring; each node is named by its place in the depth order, so two residents changing places redraw their two boxes and never the glass), the with-you bed holds the carried set asleep or the Companion's mark, the column's five modules hold their objects (a picture the host has not sent is a slot not yet filled: nothing is drawn, nothing moves; the PH plates and the hand masters are pictures the host sends), Home's focus is `focus.graph` of `home.json` on the boxes the words drew, `events.crateIn` slides the crates into the Cargo module and `events.rest` settles the knob and dithers to Idle. **The Vivarium's whole** is `vivarium_words` in `screens/home.c`: the living-window code of Home and Idle on `vivarium.json` (the window edge to edge, rows 120 to 560 of Idle's painting of the light through `v_sprite_window`, its plates only while that painting is not on the face; every mibi at home walking, two groups stepping on alternate 40 ms, one group tick a frame at most, so at most half the boxes change in any frame; the with-you bed and the focused one's name tag) and `build_bayStrip` (`screens/home_builds.c`: a region a tile, every tile the same four nodes, the boxes from the `listPitch` rule with forms in `layout/` (`layout_list_pitch`, `layout_list_form`; the Pods rack's `layout_place_rect` is the same core), its oracle `listPitch` in `ui/specs/derive.mjs`, vectors in `tests/vectors/layout.json`); its focus is `vivarium.json focus.graph` (vectors in `focus.json`, the selectors in the props). **One mibi up close** is `habitat_words` in `screens/habitat.c` (with `build_module`, `build_nameTag` and the bay strip in `screens/home_builds.c`), from `views/habitat-props.mjs` and `habitat.json`, in the states rest, meet and out: the stage, the bezel and the glass (the near Vivarium painting of the light, `vivarium-near-<light>`), the resident in its 304 × 312 box with its feet at 392 (lifted 4 px while the ring is on it; the out mark in its place while it is out with the Companion), the name tag or, in the meet, the ribbon in its slot, the card (the heart and the notches, the species line with its mark, the story, the code and the stamp), the chapter plates on `listPitch`, the four modules (door, portrait, cross and wild, with no lamp) and the bay strip with its tiles; the ring and its focus graph (`focus.graph`, the `tile.shown` selector resolved by the host) are `habitat.json`'s. Events: `moment` (1800 ms, hold 300, a cut: the placeholder's two hops lift the creature by the spec's table, the box, the tag and the ring stay), `read` (the stamp prints row by row over 300 ms), `swap` (a dither of the resident from the old mibi's picture id, 200 ms) and the frame's `flash` on the tag when the namer saves a name. **The namer** (`screens/namer.c`, from `views/namer-props.mjs` and `namer.json`) is the face's first overlay: the props key `overlay` ({ id, state, regions, focus }) is accepted only for a screen whose spec lists it (`overlays`), by an overlay spec of `kind` `overlay` that lists the screen (`over`, refused at load when absent) and that has a binding table; anything else is refused whole and the previous props stay. It is drawn after the screen and before the frame's words, with its own focus graph (`namer.json` `focus.graph` over the targets the props list, ids `key.<i>`, `mod.shift`, `mod.space`, `mod.page`, `act.done`, `act.suggest`); while it is open the screen beneath draws no ring and takes no key, and the face's intents carry the screen id `namer`. A key is one composed picture and its label (two nodes), so the namer stays under the object budget (267 objects open). Closing is the host's: props without the overlay, with the habitat focus on the resident after the meet and on the name after a rename. The rack pods in the wells are composed on the host from the art director's 1-bit hand layers (recoloured by the species' pair and A's ramp, 24×32, never scaled; the face takes the finished picture by id, `rackpod:<species>`), not PH plates; the bay, the wells, the chamber, the cradle, the journal and the walk crate are signed masters.

**Cargo** (the Bay, T-20261016-216) is drawn by the binding table `screens/cargo.c` from `views/cargo-props.mjs` and `cargo.json`, built to the build spec's sections 1 to 6: the bay window (one picture, radius 6 with the frame's inner shade), three berths of 256 or, with a portrait crate in, four of 188 (the recess or the slatted shut door, the crate and its shadow, the berth's edge and, on the fourth berth, its gold edge: the edges and the waiting mat are small pictures cut at build time by `ui/tools/cargo-cuts.py`), the Pods hatch (shut with its ring, or open with six wells, the lit wells and the find strip), the waiting mat and the display row. Nothing scales at runtime. The opening plays one crate at a time on the arrival event's own clock (the last entry of `regions.opening.starts` that is <= elapsed): the crate lifts, its contents rise into the display rect by the 180 ms dither (`v_dither_window` over that rect only), the materials flash and travel to the top bar's icons (`frame_material_centre`), the hatch opens, the pods and finds go one at a time and swap size where they land by two dither nodes on one level clock (the travel picture out, the stored picture in), and the crate is done. No Cargo dither is a stage exemption. With reduced motion the event ends at once and the host stages `regions.opening.crate` and `.at`; every travel and dither is a cut. While the opening holds, the face sends `confirm` and `back` on "room" as well as `room:<x>` and ends its own hold; the host applies the current crate's end state. The dock arrival keeps its own bay, crates, rack and ribbon (`regions.dock*`). **Idle** (`props.idle`, `frame.json` `idle`) is the same living-window code on the frame spec's rectangles (`idle_words` in `screens/home.c`: the Vivarium's painting of the light (its flat plates only while the light's slot is empty), the residents walking with their feet in the walk ground and never behind the bed, the bed and the carried set asleep) and the one line on its strip (`props.frame.idle.line`); no frame word, no ring; the first key of any kind says `wake`; its enter and wake dithers are the host's. **Create** (`screens/create.c`, from `views/create-props.mjs` and `create.json`) is the states nothingRead, shape and grow: the bench, the chapter rail (a pip a trait: filled, hollow, a changed diamond or a clash cross, the focused trait's lifted by the tab's `lift` prop (frame.json focus.lift.chrome, 2 px)), the dish and the pod, the small chamber with its busy bud, the work tray with the founder (always one composed picture, the Bayer pick of the old and the new: a `dither` event on the founder carries the old picture's id as its string `from`, 16 levels over 200 ms), the roll (three picture places and two notches always have their nodes; a trait that does not roll shows one picture and no notch; the ring stays on the chosen one: the roll is a stepper), the trait line (the changed tag, the clash ✕ or the breed mark, each with its node in every state), the stamp label and the code rule; `events.grow` prints the stamp row by row for 300 ms, shows the code at 300 and moves the pod from 300 to 900 in a straight eased line behind the work tray. Every other screen sends `state: "notBuilt"` and the face draws frame.json `notBuilt` (the frame, the stage in the ground and the line `this screen is not built yet`). Room keys, ← and the first press on Idle work on every screen. Each picture the host sends carries its layer policy (`art` or `painted`) and its status (`placeholder` or `master`) from one family table (`ui/asset-policy.mjs`); a sprite or nine-slice takes its layer from its asset, the words choose a layer only for rectangles, text and composed pictures. Text is Inter 16, 20 and 28 px through LVGL's font engine from `lv_font_conv` C files baked from the frozen TrueType files in `ui/fonts/inter/src`: Regular 16, Medium 16, Medium 20 and SemiBold 28, named by the font codes 16, 1016, 20 and 28 (`prim.h` `PRIM_FONT_*`). A word sets its text by role (`frame.json` `type.roles`: the font, the colour on each ground, the shadow on a painting; `v_say`, `v_say_in`, `v_say_run`, `v_say_price` and the `v_role_*` measures); the px forms are their internals, and `kit-ratchet.json`'s `text` table holds each screen's px-form calls to today's count. The JavaScript drawing layer is deleted.

## The layout check (report mode)

A mechanical check of the layout over every golden, beside the clearance of text: margins, edges, gutters, overlaps, alignment, empty space, balance, groups, slots, cut pictures and lone marks (the layout system's C1 to C9). Its findings are reviewed and the rules taught from them (a fault is fixed in the screen's pass; "fine, because …" becomes an exception or a threshold or rule change; a fault no rule names becomes a case in the corpus), and screens are then enforced one by one.

```
node prototypes/face/tools/goldens.mjs --check --logs /tmp/layout-logs     # the face's log and the props facts of each golden's frame, beside its hash
node prototypes/face/tools/layout-check.mjs /tmp/layout-logs --out /tmp/layout-check --overlays review   # <out>/index.json, <out>/index.md, the review set and the cards' overlays (all: every finding's; none)
node prototypes/face/tools/layout-check.mjs /tmp/layout-logs --refresh-cases           # after a retake: the case corpus's fixture logs taken again
```

- **The log it reads.** Each node of the face's log carries its solid-ink box (values 14 to 17: alpha ≥ 128, rows and columns with at least 3 such pixels; a text's advance by its cap top to baseline), flag 8 for a window of a larger picture, 16 when that window cuts solid ink and 32 for composed content (the snapshot). While a node shows the snapshot, `log.snapshot` holds the source screen's nodes cut to its rect, and the check measures them where the snapshot shows them. A generated picture's inner boxes are the layout facts its props carry (`layout.facts` in its spec names the path).
- **The rules** (`ui/specs/layout.mjs`, pure): L0 composed content with no inner boxes is not measurable; L1 margins (the outermost component at the margin, or a centred block; 16 under a strip); L2 a box on or across its container's edge; L3 gutters (crowding, or unequal along a row or column); L4 overlaps (and painted props); L5 alignment (declared columns and groups, and near-misses); L6 the largest empty rectangle; L7 the weight of the content (the filled area's centroid, and slack symmetry); L8 groups (a caption apart from what it labels, a component that floats); L9 slots across states; L10 cut pictures; L11 lone marks. A finding of L3, L5, L7, L8 or L10 names its part (a or b).
- **The component model.** The bars and their zones, clips, 1 or 2 px rules and nodes a later solid node covers are not measured; nodes with one rect are the layers of one picture; a picture painted over its whole rect with a text on it is a plate (its rect); a node at least 48 × 48 that holds content is a container when it is composed content (the snapshot, a generated picture), a region `layout.containers` names, or a pane drawn as one (a rect, a nine-slice, a picture painted over its whole rect), measured with its padding (16) and an inner gutter (8); a node covering 90 % of its container and reaching its edges is its ground; a strip hanging from a container's top moves that edge down. In a container, the boxes of one region that meet or lie within 8 px are one component (two pictures of one region that overlap in part stay two), and a region whose spec says `labels`, `under` or `partOf` another joins that one. A component under 16 px is a mark (L2, L4 and L11 only); the focus ring counts for L2 and L4.
- **The data.** Every number is `frame.json` `layout.thresholds` (each `{ value, from }`); each screen's spec has a top-level `layout` block (`stage`: bezel, field, painting or exempt; `opening`, today's measured inner edge; an open decision panel stands on `panel.json`'s). `painted`, `columns`, `groups`, `labels`, `slots` and `bleed` are UI/UX's with the art director. Exceptions are `frame.json` `layout.exceptions` (`{ screen, state?, rule, node, other?, reason, by, date }`, `by` UI/UX, AD or owner; an unused one fails the run); `layout.enforced` lists the rules a screen is held to (`"all"`, or rule by rule), empty in report mode.
- **The output.** `<out>/index.json` (the commit, the time, the thresholds, the counts by rule, by screen and by both, the exceptions used and unused, what is not measurable, every finding with its id `<screen>/<rule>/<n>`, states, container, boxes, golden and overlay), `<out>/index.md` (the rule × screen table, then each screen's findings) and `<screen>/<state>--<rule>-<n>.png`, the golden at 1x, dimmed, with the container dashed, the boxes stroked, the gap as a dimension line with its number, L6's empty rectangle hatched, L7's centroid and centre as crosses and a label plate, drawn on RGBA bytes with the baked 12 px glyph table. Identical findings across a screen's states are one.
- **The review set.** `<out>/review.md` and `review.json`: one card per screen and rule (with its part), the worst instance of that kind (the largest gap between measured and required; instances are its findings grouped ignoring the measure), how many of that kind and the other ids; the nine Station screens first, the Library last under "parked". `--overlays review` draws the cards' overlays only (CI's run); `<out>/index.json` and `<out>/index.md` stay complete.
- **Exit.** 0 in report mode; 1 on a finding of an enforced rule, an unused exception, a malformed layout block or L0 on an enforced screen; 2 when the logs are not the goldens'. CI runs it after the goldens and uploads the output as the `layout-check` artifact.
- **Tests.** `ui/tests/layout.test.mjs` plants logs that make each rule speak and keep it quiet, the exceptions and the enforcement; `ui/tests/layout-cases.test.mjs` holds the check to the known faults (`layout-cases.json`, fixture logs in `ui/tests/layout-cases/`).

## Building

```
# once: the pinned Emscripten SDK (CI does this and caches it)
git clone https://github.com/emscripten-core/emsdk.git ~/emsdk && (cd ~/emsdk && ./emsdk install $(cat <repo>/prototypes/face/emsdk.version) && ./emsdk activate $(cat <repo>/prototypes/face/emsdk.version))
EMSDK=~/emsdk prototypes/face/build.sh all     # needs cmake 3.28+, ninja, gcc for the native build
node --test prototypes/face/tests/*.test.mjs    # the Node checks (after build.sh: some need dist/)
PW_DIR=<dir with node_modules/playwright> node prototypes/face/tools/face-check.mjs
npm install --prefix /tmp/lvfc lv_font_conv@1.5.3 && sh prototypes/face/tools/bake-fonts.sh   # only to regenerate the fonts
```

Open the Station (`/sandbox/station/`; the build's `dist/` must sit beside the page: `prototypes/face/dist`, published at `/sandbox/face/dist`). `?test` puts the face in test mode (the palette passes and the log); `?dev` opens the developer panel.
