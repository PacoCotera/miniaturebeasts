# The Station build: milestones 1 to 4, Read, Grow, Painting and Cross

The first build of the Station loop on the sandbox, per [the Station build plan](../../design/proposals/station-build.md) (owner-approved 2026-10-08). The page simulates the Station's 1024×600 screen at 1:1 device pixels inside a depicted handheld with one row of big keys under it, and shares one save with the [Companion page](../exploration/README.md) in the same browser. **M1 Read** ships: the modules and the state model; the species frames, the resolver, the placeholder and the stamp imported from the workbench and the genome stamp (no genome model of the Station's own); the save migration; the developer panel with seeds; Pods with Identify, reads at the decided prices, glints, Compare and return. **M2 Grow** ships: Create with three-picture rolls per read trait and clash marking, the bud (five minutes the first time, twenty plus one per shaped trait after, instant grow for a price, the developer's timers), Open, six bays, Habitat on the placeholder, return a mibi to the wild. **M3 Painting** ships: the hand-off at Grow to [the Caddy service](../caddy/README.md) (mock painter first, the real one behind the service's own toggle with a daily ceiling), polling, the painting landing at the next fresh draw of the mibi, and the offline lamp ("waiting for the cloud"). At Open the mibi wears the rig placeholder until its painting lands. **M4 Cross** ships: the Cross screen (two adults of one species, the child to be misty between them, their stamps on plates, per trait four seed pictures or a range picture, refusals before cost), the cross itself through the workbench's `cross.mjs` unchanged (blends, Mendelian switches, pedigree kinship with identity as the fallback, the penalty B with A), the child in the bud with its real parents, known only where the switch parents matched, and reading a child on Habitat's card.

What the owner can play at `/sandbox/station/`: a pod from a walk (dock the Companion, open the bay) or from a developer seed, identified and read chapter by chapter at the decided prices, its stamp filling as chapters are read, pictures of this pod's mibi in the placeholder; two pods of a species compared; a pod returned to the wild; then the founder loop end to end: shape a read trait among three pictures, Grow it, watch the bud's leaves and its unread chapters clear (or grow it now for a price), Open, meet the juvenile in Habitat, take it with you; six bays that fill and refuse; a mibi returned to the wild.

## The screen layer (`prototypes/ui`, technical-architecture.md §5)

Between the rules and the pixels sits a small, declarative layer; Pods is built on it (T1), the other screens move as T2 reaches them. Nothing in it reads the save or draws by hand.

| Module | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `specs/station/*.json` | The one home of every number: `frame.json` (top bar, bottom line, message plate, focus ring, type sizes, colour roles, strings) and `pods.json` (the list, wells, hatch, rail, pod by size class, name, origin, stamp label, the open page with its grids, Compare, the focus graph; what the layout document does not give is listed under `derived`). A test compares `pods.json` with the wireframe's boxes |
| `layout.mjs` | The three rules the layout document states and nothing else: the rail's compaction by chapter count, the page grid by trait count, the message plate's place |
| `components/*.mjs` | The vocabulary, each piece once: `frame` (top bar, bottom line, message plate), `focusRing`, `panel`, `stampLabel`, `chapterRail`, `chapterPage`, `list`, `specimen`, `ribbon`. They take props and a context (the spec and the type metrics) and return scene nodes as plain JSON |
| `scene.mjs` | A retained list of nodes on three layers (art, painted, type), diffed by id, with dirty rectangles. The closed set of primitives: `rect`, `sprite`, `nineSlice`, `text` (a glyph run), `clip`; `legacy` is the adapter's node. No canvas path, gradient, shadow, filter, transform or global alpha exists in the set; a test fails the build if the renderer or a component reaches for one |
| `render/station-canvas.mjs` | The layered canvas renderer: `fillRect` and `drawImage` at integer pixels with source rectangles, nothing else. The art layer holds only palette colours; the painted layer the landed paintings; the type layer the glyph runs, tinted from the atlases, with a run log of every string (text, face, size, atlas) |
| `type.mjs`, `fonts/atlas/` | Type from atlases baked from Inter 4.1 (tabular figures frozen in) at 16, 20 and 28 px by `tools/bake-type.mjs` with LVGL's font converter run in Node; a PNG of coverage and a JSON of metrics a face. The page blits from the PNG and measures from the JSON, in the page and in Node alike |
| `assets.mjs` | The manifest: every picture registered by id at its size, with its policy and status (placeholder or master, and what it waits for); a sprite whose slot is not its size is refused and counted. Pictures are built once through the cache by id, the rig's close-ups included |
| `focus.mjs`, `timeline.mjs` | The focus graph (axes, edges to groups or selectors, spatial fallback, arm-then-confirm) and the timeline (events on its own clock; input held while a holding event plays) |

The checks that read it run after the journey (`node prototypes/station/tools/checks.mjs`, a step of the site workflow): the art layer at exactly 0 off-palette pixels; the type log (every string Inter at 16, 20 or 28 px from the bundled atlases, whose hashes match the index, and no text API call); every drawn region's box against its spec file; the stamp label at 120 and the stamp at most 104 px inside it; the rail's tab count against the frame's chapter count; the frame at 1024×600. Their numbers are printed in the job log.

### How a builder adds a screen (§5.4)

1. **The spec.** The UI designer's layout lands in `design/style-guide/station-layouts.md` with its wireframe. The builder transcribes it into `prototypes/ui/specs/station/<screen>.json` (regions as rectangles, the focus graph, strings, colour roles) and lists anything the document does not give under `derived`. No number is typed anywhere else.
2. **The view.** `src/views/<screen>.mjs`: a pure function from the state, the focus and the presentation progress to the props of each region, the focus targets and the bottom line, plus a list of the pictures it needs as plain requests. It imports the rules and the genome bridge, never a canvas. Test it in Node (`tests/<screen>-view.test.mjs`, as `pods-view.test.mjs` does for every species).
3. **The map.** In `src/screens/<screen>.mjs`, each region is drawn by a component of the vocabulary. A piece the vocabulary lacks is a new word: it goes back to the UI designer and the architect, it is not drawn in the screen.
4. **The focus.** The graph is the spec's. The screen resolves the selectors it names (`list.current`, `rail.last`) and fills in nothing else.
5. **The pictures.** Every picture is registered in the manifest at its size (`src/pictures.mjs`), a placeholder flagged with what it waits for. Close-ups are rendered by the rig's camera at their size, never cropped and enlarged.
6. **The intents.** A key on a focused target is one rule call in `state.mjs` (new rules land there with tests); its result becomes events on the timeline (`TL.play({ kind, target, ms, hold })`), which hold input while they play. No global timestamps.
7. **The journey and the sign-off.** Extend `tools/journey.mjs` with the screen's steps and `frameShot` points (1024×600 captures in `img/`), run `tools/checks.mjs`, fill in the sign-off's section 3.

Registering a screen on the layer is `registerScreen(name, { nodes, line, act, enter })`; a screen not yet moved registers `{ draw, line, act, enter }` and is drawn as one legacy node.

## Keys

The Station's depicted keys, mapped to the keyboard. Routine play uses these only; nothing on the screen is clickable.

| Key | Keyboard | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pad | ← ↑ → ↓ | Moves the warm focus ring between drawn things. On Pods the order is fixed: ↑ ↓ walk the wells, then the hatch; → from a well to the pod, ← back; ↑ from the pod to the chapter rail, ◀ ▶ among the chapters, ↓ back to the pod |
| Home (amber) | H | The vivarium and the bench |
| Research (teal) | R | The pods |
| Library (violet) | L | The tome's spread of sixteen frames and a Book per species |
| Habitat (green) | B | Residents, the with-you door, the bond |
| ← | Esc / Backspace | Back one view. On Home: focus back to the room; on the room it only says so |
| ✓ Confirm | Enter / Space | Exactly what the bottom line names, with its price |
| Caddy: Dock / Lift | D | Prototype only, beside the Station: docks or lifts the Companion. It is not a Station key |

The four middle keys switch views and never spend. Every spend is one deliberate press; returning a pod takes two (the first arms).

## The loop, as built in M1

- **Arrival** (as built in the stand-in): dock, then `✓ Open the bay`; one arrival per crate, in order; each crate accepted exactly once; the pods land in the rack's six wells, or wait sealed when no well is free. A crate can also come from the developer panel's seeds.
- **Identify** (1 ⚡, the first ever free): the seal on the cap breaks top-down and the species glyph lights. A new species is learned once (the Library's spread shows its plate; the Companion's `known` index follows for the three species it carries). The stamp appears on its label with every chapter as hairlines.
- **Read a chapter** (1 ◆ per trait in it; half, rounded up, once that chapter was read on an earlier pod of the species; the very first read ever free; a read chapter is free to look at again): the chapter's frost wipes away over two seconds and its page shows each trait as a picture of this pod's mibi. The words are the frame's own looks: *shows X · hides Y* (with a misty seed holding the hidden look), *only X* (a small base), a blend's two halves as two seeds, *asleep* (a switched-off part with sleeping copies), *breed to change* (a doing, two joined rings). The stamp's sector fills; the progress ring in the list fills one arc, sized by the chapter's traits. A sealed chapter is shut with a notch and the picture of what opens it (the developer panel opens sealed chapters).
- **Glint** (per chapter): a four-point star on a chapter the species has had read before on some pod, unread on this one, where this pod carries a look the field guide has not seen. It says "new here", never what. A star on the ring in the list, and on the well.
- **Compare** (free): from a pod's well, walk to another identified pod of the same species; `✓ Compare` lays the two chapter pages side by side; ◀ ▶ change the chapter; traits read on both that differ pulse.
- **Return to the wild** (the hatch under the wells): `✓ Return to the wild · +1 ❀`, ✓ again. The Companion puts the pod back in its place at the next dock (the `returned` record keeps its shape).
- **Create** (✓ on a read pod): the founder large in the placeholder, misty wherever a chapter is unread; the opened pod at the left, the empty chamber and the stamp at the right; ◀ ▶ walk the read traits, ▲ ▼ roll a shapeable look-trait among three pictures drawn from the pod's own copies (as the pod is, only the first copy, only the second; close-ups of the part), +1 ◆ a change; a doing says *breed to change*; a trait the pod carries one look of does not roll; the still-unread chapters are named in one line ("Movement and Stamina stay a surprise"); a shape that will not build marks its clashing traits (reverting one of them alone makes it build, else every changed trait) and withholds Grow. The bottom line reads the total: `✓ Grow it · 2 ⚡ 4 ❀ 1 ◆ · ← Pods` (the first founder ever 2 ⚡ only) and how many leaves it grows in; ← goes back with nothing spent.
- **Grow**: validated whole, then paid; the stamp is pressed and the code appears; the pod leaves the rack; the genome joins `outbox` for the Caddy service (M3). The bud takes five minutes the first time ever (the developer's first-bud rule), then twenty plus one per shaped trait; the developer's bud scale (real, ×10, ×60, instant) recomputes from the start, never a countdown.
- **Incubator**: the dome with the glowing bean (never an embryo), one leaf a minute filling smoothly, the chapter tabs (read, cleared as it grew, still misty: the unread chapters clear one by one across the wait), the stamp filling with them and the code as live text; `✓ Grow now · 1 ⚡ 2 ❀` while growing (a placeholder price under the developer's economy); ready, the shape glows inside and `✓ Open` plays the hatch: the glass lifts, the bud cracks, the juvenile steps out, "Moss · Loika · juvenile", then the meet view on Habitat with the door focused: `✓ Take Moss with you`. A full vivarium refuses Open ("No bay free") and refuses Grow before anything is paid.
- **Vivarium and Habitat**: six bays (eight or ten under the developer's limits); the one with you keeps its bed; the residents in the placeholder at 64 and 96 px with a cool waiting lamp until their painting lands; Habitat's card with the stamp and code, the with-you door, the bond heart and the gate to the wild: `✓ Return Moss to the wild · +2 ❀`, ✓ again. Never a bonded mibi, a juvenile (adult after two world turns, or the developer's setting), or the one with you. A released mibi keeps its record (`released: true`, a leaf that ends in the lineage), a field-guide note is kept per species, and the release is listed in `st.releases` for the Companion to take at the next dock (its place remembers it and may shed a pod of its lineage: exploration's side).
- **The painting** (`src/caddy.mjs`): at Grow the genome joins `outbox` and goes to the Caddy service at once (`POST /caddy-api/v1/grow`, the world and the genome, the page's grow cap beneath the service's); the service answers done when that set is stored, else queues it. The page polls its world's jobs every thirty seconds while any mibi waits and on waking; a finished set (the 300×310 portrait, the Companion size, the 48 px token) is fetched and held, and **lands at the next fresh draw** of that mibi (a screen change, waking), never while it is on screen; the cool lamp goes out. Unreachable, Grow still works: the genome waits in the outbox, the lamp says "waiting for the cloud", and it goes when the service answers. A set that failed its checks leaves the placeholder standing; a capped job says it waits for tomorrow. The landed painting is drawn as it came (the Station may be anti-aliased), its flat ground cleared for the vivarium's sprites; the Habitat resident wears it large. The developer panel's painter setting (`mock`, `real`, `off`) rides along with each job; the service's own mode and ceiling decide.
- **Cross** (✓ on an adult's cross mark in Habitat). *Its layout is a placeholder, marked as such: the style guide's station-layouts.md does not cover the Cross screen yet, so the screen follows station-screens.md's words (parents in the outer thirds, the child and forecast centred) with no composition of the builder's own beyond that; the UI designer's spec replaces it when it lands.* The screen: the two parents left and right (alive; a partner away with you is dimmed, not ineligible), ◀ ▶ pick the partner among the adults of that species, the child to be under frost between them, the kinship line (wild founders · kinship 0, half kin, close kin · a quarter, with the identity share as a caution at kinship 0), both stamps on plates. The forecast, one row per trait (▲▼ page by five): for a switch four seed pictures of this pod's child, quarters by the pairings of the mother's copies against the father's (under the penalty more of the four wear the hidden look; a sleeping bud marks a seed that hides something), "known for sure" where both parents match; for a blend a range picture, the trait drawn at the two ends the child can land in with the stretch between them, "firm" when both ends fall in one bin, "narrowed" under close kin; doings forecast the same way; sealed chapters show shut. Refused before cost with the reason and no ✓ cap: the same individual, another species, not adult, no bay free, the incubator busy. `✓ Cross them · 2 ⚡ 4 ❀ · ← Habitat`: the child is drawn once and built before anything is spent (a child that cannot be built never exists; its clashing traits are named and nothing is paid), then the bud takes it (twenty minutes) with `parents`, two snapshots of id, name, code, sha and genome, the family tree's record.
- **The child**: opens known only where the Station could be sure (every trait of the chapter a switch whose parents matched; a blend is never sure), the rest "one of these until read": Habitat's card shows its chapters as tabs with their price (1 ◆ a trait, half once the chapter was read on the species, the first read ever free) and ✓ on a tab reads it; the card says "of Dot and Nib". Kinship walks the recorded parents three generations deep (parent and child a quarter, full siblings a quarter, half siblings an eighth), with genome identity as the fallback where a parent is unknown.
- **Home, Dock and arrival, the Probe bench, Idle**: as built in the stand-in v2, with residents and pods drawn from the frames.
- **Library** (M5 builds it whole): the spread of sixteen frames (a found plate, a met pencil study, an empty unmet frame), and a Book stub per species: its face, its habit line, a line per chapter with the looks found so far and "more?".
- **Habitat** (M2 adds return): one resident large in the placeholder, its card with the stamp at 88 px and the name-code, the with-you door, the bond heart, the strip of six bays. A cool lamp marks a resident whose painting has not landed (every mibi, until M3).

## Architecture (station-build.md §2)

One page, `index.html` (the device, the screen, the keys, the Caddy, the developer panel's markup), and ES modules under `src/`, loaded by one `<script type="module" src>` (the CI parse check reads inline scripts only).

| Module | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/state.mjs` | The save's Station part and every rule as a pure function: prices and half price, identify, read, glints, compare, return, the dock and the bay, `need`, the migration, the developer seeds. No drawing; the tests run it in Node |
| `src/genome.mjs` | The bridge to the imported model: the frames registry, a pod's genome from its seed (sampled from the species' pools until the body builds), a trait's words from `describe.mjs`, the stamp genome and code from `genome-stamp/src/codec.mjs`, the name-code from the genome's SHA-256 in the Grow service's canonical form (so the Station's `sha` names the set the painter stores) |
| `src/game.mjs` | The live save (the Companion's part read, never written; `st` ours), the developer settings under their own key, the focus state per screen, the presentation events |
| `src/gfx.mjs` | The old pixel primitives (`R`, `blit`, `text`, `panel`) for the screens not yet on the screen layer, now calls into the layered renderer (the adapter, below): the palette as data (`prototypes/ui/palettes/station.json`, the settled 62 colours), indexed buffers for the placeholders and the type as glyph runs from the Inter atlases at the style guide's sizes (16 px body and readouts, 20 titles, 28 names), the material icons inline. 13 px, which the style guide forbids, is set at 16 on the screens not yet moved |
| `src/art.mjs` | The placeholder register (`PLACEHOLDERS`) and every drawn stand-in: the mibi placeholder from the plain renderer, the pod from the frame's four parameters and glyph, trait close-ups, seeds, the stamp raster, the progress ring, chapter emblems, the room |
| `src/screens/*.mjs` | One module per screen (frame, home, pods, create, incubator, cross, library, habitat, bench with idle). Pods is on the screen layer (a controller over a view and a spec file); the others draw as before, through the adapter |
| `src/views/*.mjs`, `src/present.mjs`, `src/pictures.mjs` | The pure views (state and focus to the props of each region, plain JSON, run in Node), the frame's counters and flashes on the timeline's clock, and the registration of each picture a view asks for in the asset manifest at its size |
| `src/caddy.mjs`, `src/dev.mjs` | The Caddy service's client (the hand-off, polling, the landing, the lamp's words) and the developer panel |

**Imported, never copied:** `../workbench/frames/*.json` (fetched beside the page, `mb-species-frame/2` on catalogue 9), `../workbench/framework/{species,describe,catalogue,resolve,rig,raster,plain}.mjs`, `../genome-stamp/src/{codec,stamp,frames}.mjs`. The Companion page's species indexes map 0 to S01 Loika, 1 to S03 Tuikis and 2 to S02 Untuva; a pod or mibi carries its species id (`species`) beside the Companion's index (`sp`, −1 for a species the Companion does not carry).

## The save and its migration (station-build.md §2.3)

The key stays `mb-save-v8`; the Companion's top-level part is untouched; the Station's part `st` carries `schema: 2`. The migration from the stand-in's record runs once on load, forward only, logged: pods keep their seeds and get genomes (`mb-genome/2`, sampled from the frame with the pod's `gs`), their studies start again as chapters (`read: []`); `known` and `met` stay as Companion indexes beside `knownIds` and `metIds`; `readOnce` (chapters read on any pod of a species, for half price) and `guide` (looks seen per trait, rebuilt from mibis and read pods) are new; a mibi keeps `id`, `name`, `sp`, `born`, `from`, `bonded` and becomes a founder from its seed, fully read, with `genome`, `sha`, `code`, `read`, `parents: null`, `bay`, `paint: null`, `released: false`; `inc` becomes `bud`; `bays: 6`; `sitting`, `moments`, `welcomeGiven`, `wish`, `outbox` and `devBay` (the developer's crates) are new. `accepted`, `dockN`, `probe`, `withReq` and `returned` keep their shape.

## Developer panel

Under the device, opened by `?dev` or the "Developer tools" button, never a device key. Settings live under `mb-station-dev`, never in the shared save.

- **Timers:** bud scale (real, ×10, ×60, instant) and the first-bud rule (live); juvenile to adult (live: world turns, or now); the sitting wait and the mock painter's delay (stored; they apply from M3 and M6).
- **Economy:** decided prices; loose (decided plus +2 ⚡ +3 ◆ +2 ❀ per crate opened; the default, per the owner's "loose for testing"); free. The instant-grow price (1 ⚡ 2 ❀ as a placeholder until the real economy sets it; free; 2 ⚡ 4 ❀). Add materials.
- **Limits:** bays (6, 8, 10), rack size, the daily grow cap, the painter mode, sealed chapters open.
- **Seeds:** a crate of pods (any of the sixteen species, count, seed) into the Station's own dev bay, accepted at the dock like any crate; a pod from a pasted genome, checked whole against its frame; two unrelated adults of a species into free bays; two siblings with their parents (four bays). A held sitting comes with M6.
- **Skip to:** the pod under the beam identified, read whole; every pod read; the bud mid-way or ready to open.
- **Inspect:** both copies of every locus of the pod under the beam (bins and values); the Station's record; the placeholder register; the Caddy's status; export the save; import a Station part (this world's only; the Companion's part is never written); reset.
