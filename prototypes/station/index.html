Station Field Test

    
The Station build, milestone 1 (Read): a simulated 1024×600 Station screen on the real species frames. It shares one save with the Companion page in this browser.

    
Explore on the Companion page, Head home, then dock here.

    

      How to play
      
    
        
  • ←↑→↓ or the pad: move the focus ring between things in the picture. On Pods: ↑↓ walk the wells, → to the pod, ↑ to its chapters, ←→ among them.
    • 
        
  • Enter / Space or the orange key: Confirm. It does exactly what the bottom line names after ✓, with its price.
    • 
        
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey ← key: back one view (on Home it only says so). The lamp on Home's bench rests the screen: the vivarium fills it.
    • 
        
  • The four middle keys switch views and never spend: H Home, R Research (the pods), L Library, B Habitat.
    • 
        
  • D or the Caddy's key: Dock or Lift the Companion (prototype only; it is not a Station key).
    • 
        
  • After a minute without a press the vivarium plays alone. Any press wakes it and does what it says.
    • 
      

    

    

      What to try
      
    
        
  1. On the Companion page, explore and Head home: the hold is sealed into a crate in the Companion's bay (three at most), and the world turns.
    2. 
        
  2. Here, press D to dock. The crates slide into the bay door; ✓ Open the bay plays one arrival per crate.
    3. 
        
  3. R Research: ✓ Identify a pod (1 Energy; the first ever is free). The seal breaks, the species shows, its chapters rise as a rail and its stamp appears on the label with every chapter as hairlines.
    4. 
        
  4. ↑ to a chapter, ✓ Read it (1 Data a trait; half, rounded up, once that chapter was read on an earlier pod of the species; the first read ever is free). Its page shows each trait as a picture of this pod's mibi: shows X · hides Y, only X, asleep, or breed to change. The stamp's sector fills.
    5. 
        
  5. A star on a chapter is a glint: this pod carries a look the species has not shown yet. ✓ on another identified pod of the same species compares the two, free. The hatch under the wells returns a pod to the wild for +1 Essence (✓ twice).
    6. 
        
  6. Create, Grow and the incubator come with the next build (M2). Without a Companion world, the developer panel under the device seeds a crate: dock (D) and open the bay at Home.
