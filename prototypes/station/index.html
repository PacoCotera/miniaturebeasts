Station Field Test

    
The Station stand-in v2: a simulated 1024×600 Station screen. It shares one save with the Companion page in this browser.

    
Explore on the Companion page, Head home, then dock here.

    

      How to play
      
    
        
  • ←↑→↓ or the pad: move the focus ring between things in the picture. On Create, ↑↓ roll a window.
    • 
        
  • Enter / Space or the orange key: Confirm. It does exactly what the bottom line names after ✓, with its price.
    • 
        
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey ← key: back one view (on Home it only says so). The lamp on Home's bench rests the screen: the vivarium fills it.
    • 
        
  • The four middle keys switch views and never spend: H Home, R Research (the pods), L Library, B Habitat.
    • 
        
  • D or the Caddy's key: Dock or Lift the Companion (prototype only; it is not a Station key).
    • 
        
  • After a minute without a press the vivarium plays alone. Any press wakes it and does what it says.
    • 
      

    

    

      What to try
      
    
        
  1. On the Companion page, explore and Head home: the hold is sealed into a crate in the Companion's bay (three at most), and the world turns.
    2. 
        
  2. Here, press D to dock. The crates slide into the bay door; ✓ Open the bay plays one arrival per crate.
    3. 
        
  3. R Research: ✓ Identify a pod (1 Energy; the first ever is free). Its trait windows rise, frosted.
    4. 
        
  4. ↑ to a window, ✓ Study it (2 Data; the first study of each species is free): it shows a picture, and a misty seed when the pod hides another look. Its petal on the fingerprint lights.
    5. 
        
  5. ✓ Shape a founder: ↑↓ rolls a studied window through what this pod carries (+2 Data per change). ✓ Grow it (2 Energy + 4 Essence; your first founder costs 2 Energy only).
    6. 
        
  6. The incubator's leaves fill, one per minute. When the dome glows, ✓ Open: you meet the mibi in Habitat, and ✓ takes it with you. The Companion page picks it up from the dock. Press D to lift the Companion before its next expedition.
