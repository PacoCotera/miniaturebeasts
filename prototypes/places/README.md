# Places: the foundation

LDtk levels, the dual grid, tile sets, the baked FlatBuffers format and the indexed renderer, for the walk's 18 × 22 tile places. The design (A§n below) is the place-foundation architecture note kept with the work queue. Built so far (lane A, C1 to C4): the pins, the tile-set contract with the CC0 test kit, the dual grid by terrain with the seam proofs, and the baked format.

## Open edges and the open style (walk content B5)

All four places (Windstep, the Hollow Tree, the Split Stone and Reedpool, since walk content B9) are 24 x 28 and in the open style: no ring of trees, every side runs on into the next cell, an edge closes only where the land has a reason (a cliff, deep water, a thicket) and reads as that thing. The four levels are committed LDtk files (`art/places/ldtk/places/`); the tools that wrote them (`make-*-open.mjs`, `open-level-lib.mjs`) are gone: generator 7 (`prototypes/exploration/src/gen7/decor.mjs`) lays props and the desk's meadow-decor density from world positions, and the bake rules `seam-ground` and `path-short` below are kept only until G4 swaps the page to generator 7. Every level: 8 find slots (a pod, an Essence source and a stone certain, the rest at one in two: a place averages 5.5 finds), three spawn areas (two open to any kind, one by the heart for the moment's species), the heart on its own 3 x 3 clearing of worn earth (on sand for the Split Stone and Reedpool: darker earth, not more sand) 14 or more cells from every opening, no prop within 2 of it but the landmark. The Hollow Tree: groves of broad and autumn trees, a dense thicket in the east, low scrub in the west, the great hollow tree (the kit's, no larger one is signed yet), a road from the south and a footpath from the west. The Split Stone: sun-baked sand flats among the grass with clumps of grey and brown boulders, two trees in the north, a thicket in the south-east, the split stone on its earth clearing and two trodden trails. Reedpool: a pond in the north with a deep pool, a reed bank, a sand shore and an island with the island stone, a sand spit from the west shore, groves west and south, a thicket in the west.

Groves, not an orchard (the lead's look at the Hollow Tree's whole view): the trees and boulders of a place stand in clumps of 3-5 that touch, with open glades between and a few on their own, never an even lattice, rows or a ring. The bake rule `grove-lattice` (`rules.mjs`, `GROVE`) measures the nearest-neighbour spacing of the standing things in a level with 12 or more: at least 40% have a neighbour within 2.3 cells, the spacing varies by at least 0.35 (coefficient of variation) and at least 2 stand alone (4 cells from any other). It fails on the Hollow Tree's old layout and on a ring of stones (`tests/groves-closed.test.mjs`).

Closed looks (walk content B8): every opening of a place in the open style carries a closed look, the set's dense thicket stamped over its span (and one cell either side), two rows deep (`adapter.mjs` `closedLooks`, `CLOSED_DEPTH`); the bake rule `closed-look` holds that every open-style level has a 3 x 3 block of thicket to stamp from (`openedges.thicketDonor`). The page passes `o.closed` (the sides the land refuses: `closedSidesOf` in the exploration page); the adapter then draws that side's look as ground patches, blocks its cells, drops the opening from `lay.openings` and never arrives there. The world's own bands (water, a cliff's lip, the map's thicket) still close the sides they draw. A thicket in every land is a stand-in: each land's own closed look is listed for the art desk.

- Sprites are drawn at 1x, one art pixel to one screen pixel (art director mb-74, mibi-art 75f1ff195, `art-jobs/2026-11-10-art-fix-decor-edges/out/p2/`): the walk's decor, props and trees are the desk's signed files byte for byte, the anchor of each is the manifest's foot, and `bakeset.mjs` no longer scales a sprite by `scale` (only the terrain tiles are 16 x 3 = 48). A tree is a prop (the trunk and its shadow, below the manifest's split row) and a canopy (the crown above it). The pieces bring colours of their own beyond the map palette; the recipe pins the 99 the range haze is keyed to and the new colours follow, and `hazeKit` moves the ramp up after the set and gives each added colour the entries of the keyed colour nearest it. `art/places/map-rules.json` `meadow.noOverlap` drops a meadow piece whole when its rectangle meets another's or a prop's; `tone.enabled` false lays no sunlit patch.
- The art desk's pieces (`art/places/sets/ninja-test/DESK.json` lists each file with its sha256 and signed commit; `tools/desk-art.mjs`, run by `prepare-set` when the recipe says `desk`): the terrain layers (fills, over sheets, the cliff faces `tall`, the way-up pieces `wayup`, the tone), the map decor (the signed tree cut into crown and trunk, bush, rock, tufts, flowers, stump, hollow tree, split stone, the scrub shrubs, the sweeps of tall grass as signed tufts mirrored at random; the kit names stay and draw them), the grass flecks and the reed sprites, and the cliff sheets re-cut at the desk windows (`tools/recut/README.md`). Every map picture is in the map palette (mibi-art f1a9fe54e): the desk's applied copies, or its colour map (`tools/recolour.mjs`) for the pieces it has not re-issued. The pebbles, the lily pad (and the pad with a flower, about one pad in four) and the way-up pieces are the desk's own paint in the map palette. The set has no pair table: `set.json` carries `layers` and `tone` only, and the kit's blob cutting, stitching and the opaque pair sheets are gone.
- Terrain layers (`set.json` `layers` and `tone`; `src/dualgrid.mjs` `terrainLayers` and `packDepth`; `setload` `fillTiles`, `layerTiles`, `layerDescriptors`, `layerRows`): one fill per terrain plus one transparent 16-slot sheet per over kind, drawn in terrain.json z order; the kind is picked by the lower terrain (`lowers`, first kind listed wins), a `drawAs` terrain is drawn as another until its own layers are signed. The bake reads only `layers`. Fills are slot 15 of a signed sheet (DESK.json `from: <sheet>#15`) or the desk's own strip (thicket: its full strip); the reeds are sprites, not a tile look (see Reed beds below). Thicket and cliff come with every pixel of alpha under 255 in their over sheets made transparent (DESK.json `from: <sheet> keyed`; the soft cast shade goes, the opaque undersides and face shading stay). Scrub is the olive flat ground over its `ground` (grass): the sheet, three variants and a full strip. The water fill has four 250 ms frames of four variants each (deep water is still). Tone is drawn on grass, and on a raised top only where its ground is grass. A baked tile set is one row per fill, over kind, cliff face, variant, way-up piece and the tone (`setload.layerRows`; the format's `Pair` table is the row table, `Layer.pair` is deprecated); about 230 unique tiles.
- The baked ground is packed by depth: the k-th piece drawn on every dual tile (its terrain's fill first, then the overs in z order, then the tone) is layer k, so a tile costs only the pieces it has. A place needs at most 4 layers where at most three terrains meet in a 2 x 2.
- Bake: `src/bake/build.mjs` runs `terrainLayers`, `applyOpenEdges`, the code-to-tile-id step and `packDepth`. `src/bake/openedges.mjs` picks each cliff tile by its corners plus the two cells under its bottom corners (s; a list of face variants by hash; a tile of four cliff corners with s > 0 takes the face's tile 15, and with s = 0 the cliff layer draws nothing: the ground under the raised tile shows), the way-up piece for the terrain below the face over a WayUp area, a scrub variant by hash; the cliff's lip and face block (the baked `blockMask`; the adapter blocks them), its top and the way up walk. LDtk: terrains cliff, thicket, scrub; entities WayUp and ReedBank.
- Rules (open style only): `cliff-foot` (no 1-row gully), `wayup`, `two-thirds-open` (every side walkable on at least two thirds), `seam-ground` and `path-short` (the last 2 rows and columns are grass, nothing on them), `heart-far` (10 from every arrival), `heart-clear` (no prop within 2 of the heart), `closed-look` (a 3 x 3 block of thicket to stamp the openings' closed looks from), `grove-lattice` (groves, not an even lattice); up to 8 finds.
- The palette keeps the slots the old range haze was keyed to (the kit's 43, then the open edges' 7); the decor's colours follow.
- The palette merge (signed by the art director): `art/places/sets/ninja-test/palette-merge.json` lists 20 colours that are each replaced by the nearest other colour of the set (every pair within 0.012 in OKLab); `prepare-set` applies it to every picture the desk gives (recipe `paletteMerge`), so the set holds 188 colours and the 48 core and the set fit the 256 indices.
- The adapter closes a side the world closes (the map's end: a thicket stamped over the two outer rows; a cliff: the rows behind its lip), blocked; `tools/views-audit.mjs` holds every open-style side to it.

## Generated places (world generation, G2)

The bake also takes the IR of a generated place (`prototypes/exploration/src/gen7/place.mjs`): `buildPlace` is the same call. An IR may carry `origin` (the world tile of its first tile), `tone` and no heart.
- Reed beds : reed clumps are props with no footprint (`reed-shore-1..4`, `reed-water-1..2`, each with an `m` mirrored copy) laid by `prototypes/exploration/src/gen7/reeds.mjs`: along every shore tile of a stretch, a clump each 8..12 px so neighbours overlap with a ragged base, and 3..5 clumps per stretch in the shallows (shallow water within two tiles of land, never deep). The generator lays them on world tiles (`decor.mjs` `reedClumpsIn`, stretches are its 4 x 4 blocks); the bake lays them in a hand-set place's ReedBank areas (`build.mjs` `reedProps`).
- Grass flecks (the desk's art-paint-grass-flecks): six tiny decals on open grass (`fleck-0..5` and mirrored `m`), laid by the meadow scatter (`decor.mjs`, `meadow.scatter`) with the plants: every plant (flowers, tufts, tall clumps, scrub shrubs, the round bush) in three sizes, young, the signed middle piece and grown, each mirrored (`sc-<slot>[-young|-grown][-m]`; the desk's steps, mibi-art cc712a83e). The boulders are the signed rock family (mibi-art ffa823829): `rock-a`, `rock-b`, `rock-big` and their mirrors; the hand-set places' `boulder-grey` and `boulder-brown` draw the first two.
- With `origin`, the full-tile variants and the scrub variants are picked by `hash32('', world x, world y)` instead of the place id, so a tile is the same in every place that shows it. IR without `origin` (the LDtk places) keeps the place-id hash.
- `tone`: 0 or 1 per tile (the sunlit grass-tone layer), from a noise field at world position, in patches (8-connected blobs of the noise above `tone.noise.threshold` in the map rules) kept whole or dropped whole: every tile of a patch needs the 5 x 5 block round it to be all grass (or all raised top), so a full cell is clear of every edge (path, water, sand, cliff, thicket, scrub); a patch that would come closer is dropped, never clipped. The tone is one step lighter than the grass. The old swirl patches (`groundMix`) are gone: the tone layer replaces them.
- The cliff sheets are the re-cut ones in the map palette (`tools/recut`, `tools/desk-art.mjs` `CLIFF3`; the way-ups are the signed `wayup-cliff-grass` and `wayup-cliff-sand`, `WAYUP`), listed in `DESK.json`; the cliff may meet earth, sand, path and grass (its `lowers`). Raised land has a ground of its own: a place's `top` (the format's last field, one terrain value per tile, 0 = grass; absent on a hand-set place, which then stands on grass) names the ground under each raised tile. `terrainLayers` brings each raised corner's top into the tile's stack and draws the cliff layer last; the cliff layer keeps a fill (`fill-cliff`, the grass it stood on) that the bake blanks on every raised tile that is no face, so the top ground shows alone there.

## Map rules

`art/places/map-rules.json` holds every number and neighbourhood size behind the map's dressing: what stands where, how thick, how far from an edge. The art director owns it; the generator (`exploration/src/gen7/decor.mjs`, `place.mjs`, `reeds.mjs`) reads it through `gen7/rules.mjs` and keeps none of them in code. `map-rules.schema.json` checks it (`tests/map-rules.test.mjs`). Field names follow LDtk's auto-rules where one exists: `chance` (0..1), `clear` (cells; the pattern is `2 * clear + 1` across), `noise` (`scale`, `octaves`, `threshold`).

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `biomes.<id>` | The biome table. Per family (`trees`, `rocks`, `stumps`, `bushes`, `sweeps`): `B` block size in tiles, `used` share of blocks holding a cluster, `size` [smallest, largest] members, `keep` smallest size kept (smaller is dropped whole), `minD2` least squared distance between members. Per biome: `reeds` share of shore stretches with a reed bed, `pads` lily pads, `broad` share of broad-leaf groves, `pebbles` share of sand tiles. The order of the biomes is part of the seeds. |
| `props.clear` | Plain-tile radius: a prop, shrub or meadow decal needs the tile and every tile this near to be one terrain (1 = a full cell off every edge, shore and lip). |
| `props.groves` | How clusters grow: the grove `noise` and how far it swings `used` (`usedSwing`), `reach` of a claim (`base` + `perSize` x size), `touchD2` the farthest next member by family, `minD2` default spacing, `bisector` border kept between two claims, `otherKindShare`, `rockShapes` (the share of an outcrop's rocks that take each signed boulder shape: rock-a, rock-b, rock-big; a mirror by hash). |
| `props.wayZone` | Cells kept clear round a way up (`left`, `right`, `up`, `down` from its rectangle). |
| `props.hollow` | The Hollow Tree: `clearing` of plain grass its site needs, and `keepOff` the other props keep from it (`tree` for crowns, `other`). |
| `meadow.scatter` | The art director's quiet-ground rule: one noise field (`noiseScale` cells, `noiseOctaves`) on the 16 px master cells; its top `patchShare` is the patch zone and a patch is the basin of one peak; `patchDensity` (peak, edge) pieces a cell, `openDensity` between patches, `openKinds`, a `dominants` kind a patch for `dominantShare` of its draws and the `mixed` table for the rest, `kindKeep` the share of a kind's draws kept, `standJitter` for bush and shrub stands, `steps` (grown above, young below, jitter) picking young, the signed middle piece or grown by the field's rank in the patch. `meadow.noOverlap` keeps the pieces' foot boxes apart and off every prop's picture. |
| `shrubs.smallPatchMax` | A scrub patch of at most this many tiles with no inner tile still takes one shrub. |
| `water.padClear` | A lily pad needs this many cells of water round it. |
| `reeds` | `stretch` side in tiles of a reed-bed stretch, `reach` of a reed beyond the area asked for. `shore`: `startSteps`, `spacing` (`min` + 0..`spread`-1 px), `jitter` and `rag` in px, `looks` with their `share`. `water`: `within` (in-water clumps stand on shallow water this near land) and `count` per stretch. |
| `tone` | The sunlit grass tone: `noise` (`threshold` is the level above which a patch grows), `clear` cells that must be one ground round every tile, `patchMax` tiles beyond which a patch is dropped. |

To see a change: edit the file, then `node prototypes/exploration/tools/gen7-goldens.mjs <out dir>` writes the seven stills (day and night windows, three straddling frames) and look at them at 1x beside `prototypes/exploration/tests/golden/gen7-g3/`. A kept change re-records those goldens (`MB_UPDATE_GOLDENS=1`) after the art director has looked at each. The test `map-rules.test.mjs` shows the other way: a copy of the file with `props.clear` 2 instead of 1 moves the props off the ring beside a shore.

## Run

```
npm ci --prefix prototypes/places
node --test prototypes/places/tests/*.test.mjs
```

`format.test.mjs` regenerates the reader with flatc: put the pinned flatc on `PATH` or in `MB_FLATC` (`sh tools/get-flatc.sh <dir>` downloads and checks it); without it that one test skips locally and fails in CI. `set-contract.test.mjs` regenerates the test set from the kit when the kit is at `research/tilesets` (or `MB_TILESETS`) and skips otherwise.

## Adding a tile set

1. Put the source under `research/tilesets/<kit>/` with its licence and `SOURCE.md` (the kit must be on the allow-list in `prepare-set.mjs`).
2. Write `art/places/sets/<id>/recipe.json` (copy `ninja-test`'s).
3. `node prototypes/places/tools/prepare-set.mjs art/places/sets/<id>/recipe.json --tilesets research/tilesets`
4. `MB_UPDATE_GOLDENS=1 node --test prototypes/places/tests/set-contract.test.mjs prototypes/places/tests/seams.test.mjs`, then look at every new golden at 1×. A seam failure is fixed in the recipe or the art, never allow-listed.
