# Companion Field Test (exploration round 3 + Companion mode + the sealed bay, prototype round 8)

Builds exploration round 3 ([`design/proposals/exploration-round-3.md`](../../design/proposals/exploration-round-3.md), its build scope, with the owner's condition that warm stones stay scarce) on top of round 2 ([`exploration-round-2.md`](../../design/proposals/exploration-round-2.md)) and the four-button control scheme ([`companion-controls.md`](../../design/proposals/companion-controls.md)), and Companion mode ([`companion-mode.md`](../../design/proposals/companion-mode.md): the active mibi screen, Energy via the Probe, the ← key), on an honest simulation of the Companion screen: a fogged 16×20 world map, living places under a survey veil, the action clock, storms and fog banks, materials with a purpose, up to three mibis with you and their care, and Head home → the sealed bay → world turn. Since round 8 the Station is its own page, [`prototypes/station/`](../station/README.md) (1024×600), sharing this page's save: cargo reaches it only when the Companion is docked there ([`station-loop.md`](../../design/proposals/station-loop.md) §2, §9).

## Controls

Four buttons. The bottom line always reads in three parts: **✓ what Confirm does** (at most 28 characters), the middle context (the only part that shrinks), and **← what the ← key does** (at most 20 characters, never dropped). The key is engraved with the ← glyph only ("Back" stays its internal name); the bottom line names what it opens on every screen by the menu's top entry: "← Leave" in a place ("← Climb out" in the cave), "← Head home" on the map, "← close" on screens. Wait is not on the ← label: ✓ waits when you face nothing, and Wait sits lower in the menu (after Leave and Head home). The first time in a place a line says "← opens the menu · Leave is first". A screen never offers two ways out: read-only screens (Probe without a patch to make, Cargo at home) leave the ✓ part empty. The middle carries the place and its survey state, with what is left named ("meadow · surveyed 2/4 · 3 to take: fruit · dew · tuft"; the list is cut on a narrow line, never the count), then the **conditions**: storm bolts (1–3) with the storm's heading ◀/▶, and the fog bank's patch with its drift, explained once on first sighting ("Bottom line: bolts are the storm · it moves ◀ west"). It drops the place name first and never drops below the survey words. **Call** has no slot in the top bar and no engraving on the shell (the teal button is told apart by colour and its ring texture); one-time lines explain it: "The teal CALL button pulses: it lifts the veil and finds things" the first time in a place, "CALL here drops a pin for 1 Energy" the first time on the map, and at 0 Energy a Call on the map says "Pins cost 1 Energy · you have 0".

- New world is offered only between expeditions (last menu entry).
- **World turns count from 1** (turn 1 is the first expedition's world) in the top bar, the choice screen, Head home, Cargo, the full map and the Observer. Saves keep the internal count from 0, so no save changes.
- **The bottom line** has three sections: [✓ action · ← back] | where you are | conditions (storm bolts with the heading arrow, fog with its drift), with 1 px muted dividers and even padding. The action text is at most 24 characters; the middle shrinks first.
- **Storm messages** say it in play language: "Strong storm · stray strikes can hit you in the open · shelter under an overhang or in a cave", "Storm peak · strikes more likely · get under an overhang, into a cave or leave". The exact odds are only on the Probe screen's legend (Storm row) and in the play manual.
- **Outpost and beacon signs** use one silhouette at every size (place token, full map at 1×, reach view at 2×): the outpost is a hut with a lamp post beside it (lamp dark, or a flame big/medium/small for the turns left); the beacon is a tall post with a lamp cage (dark, or a flame). Seen unlit beacons are signed too. The bottom line names them on or ahead of you ("outpost (dark)", "outpost · lit 2 turns", "beacon (unlit)", "beacon (lit)"), and the first sighting of each explains it ("An outpost: light it for 1 Energy to head home, mend and shelter there"; "A beacon: light it for 1 Energy to reveal the land around").
- **The pawn** is a small explorer in a hooded orange wetsuit: cream face opening with two eye dots, rounded torso with sleeves in shadow, dark belt with a lantern at the hip, slate gloves and boots. 4 facings × 2 walk frames (plus a crouched creep pose) at 32 px in places and on the tier 1 reach view; a 16 px version of the same figure on the full map and the tier 2 reach view.
- **Expedition choice.** Header: "Expedition N", the turn clock and battery. Under it a status strip ("World · turn 1 · Probe tier 1 · Shield 3/3"). Two cards with 2-frame icons and a one-line hint (Weather: storm cloud, flickering bolt, "Charge stones · pods in the rain"; Deep ground: the burrow mouth with a glint, "Caves · burrow pods"). A partner card (token at 2×, name, stage, "with you", ability; or "No partner yet · a pod hatched at the Station comes along"); ✓ on it opens Mibis. Below, the whole-map inset (fog, seen, visited, last start blinking) and "Explored N of 320 cells". ← opens the menu: Mibis, Full map, New world.
- **Edges and messages.** A held walk stops on a place's edge tile ("Edge of the place · tap again to step off"); stepping off needs a fresh tap. The message box never clips words: notes that don't fit in two lines wait for the next action, and one long note may take three lines.
- One-time pointer messages: first map entry ("Walk with the pad · ✓ goes down · the flag is your start: head home from there"), first place ("The veil lifts as you walk · Call sweeps further"), first edge crossing ("Onto the next cell · ✓ goes down into it"), the storm's arrow on the map ("▶ · the yellow arrow · the storm moves this way"), first pod, first warm stone, hold full, the four creature bubbles, nothing left ("Everything in reach is explored · ← Head home").
- The menu keeps the same rows everywhere: on the map the Leave row shows dimmed ("Leave · in a place") and focus skips it. The tier 2 upgrade also takes two presses.
- The menu shows no action count. Its Head home entry reads "Explored N of M in reach" on the bottom line.
- The depicted shell has Call directly above the ← key (engraved ← only; Call has no engraving), both left of a larger Confirm. Call is teal with a ring texture.
- The bottom line sets its glyphs 1 px apart (still 2× pixels) so the three parts fit 450 px.
- One-time lines count as shown only if they are still in the message box when the action ends; one pushed out by a more urgent line comes back next time.

## The loop

1. Choose Weather (or Deep ground, with a digging partner), pick a start: the first time a glint, later any seen cell. A dotted square shows the Probe's range from that start; the dot grid in the HUD's top-left corner is the same square.
2. Walk the map. Each step lifts the fog one ring around you (not inside a fog bank). Go down into a place: it lies under a veil, which lifts a little around you as you walk. Call to survey it further, quarter by quarter, and gather what the survey shows.
3. Walk off the place's edge onto the next cell, Confirm, and survey that one. Each place you Call in fills its dot.
4. Head back and **Head home**: **it works only on the expedition's start cell (a flag on the map) or on a lit outpost.** Elsewhere the menu's Head home is dimmed and the line names the nearest point ("Start · about 2 cells", or "Outpost · about 1 cell" when one is nearer); Hold full and Everything explored say "head back to the start (about N cells)". Cargo shows "Explored N of M · fully surveyed K", what Head home will seal, and the bay as three crates. Head home seals the hold into the sealed bay and the world turns once (see Head home and the sealed bay). If the Probe breaks instead, nothing is sealed (see Storm and Shield).
5. Dock at the Station page to transfer the crates; lift the Companion there before the next expedition.

## Survey

- **The Call ring is the unveil.** A Call sends a ring that spreads over 300 ms; the veil lifts behind it as it passes, and its final contour (held a moment, dotted) is exactly the Call's range: a round 11 tiles (6 in a fog bank unless a Tuikis partner is along). Hidden finds count only inside that circle. Just outside the ring, a veiled sliver up to 3 tiles deep with unveiled land right behind it (outward) lifts too, so a Call closes thin gaps against land already seen. Survey pace (a player walking a serpentine through the place, rows 8 tiles apart, then Calling where it lifts most): 33 of 33 places fully surveyed with at most 6 Calls, 3–5 each, mostly 4 (seeds 7, 13, 42). "Surveyed" still means 0 veiled tiles (re-checked: 6 places, 0 failures).

- **The veil.** Every place (not the cave under the cliff) starts veiled: the ground shows through, one step darker with a 4×4 dot pattern, so you can see where to walk. Creatures always show (in sight); features, pods, drops (fruit, tufts) and stones on a veiled tile are not drawn. Facing one, Confirm reads "Veiled · Call to see" and spends nothing; bumping into one says "Something is there under the veil · ))) Call to see". Landmarks show anyway: the narrow burrow, the cave exit, the pod on the island. Fruit you put down yourself stays visible.
- **Call lifts it** in a round 11 tiles around you (6 in a fog bank), the ring's own edge. **Walking lifts it too**, in a circle of 4 tiles around the pawn (like the cave light), spreading only through open ground: it lifts the first shore, trunk or cliff tile it meets but nothing behind it, at every step and on arrival; whatever is lifted stays lifted. Quarters and "fully surveyed" count walked tiles, so walking a place thoroughly can survey it; "explored" (the reach dot) still needs one Call there. A Call that adds nothing new reads "· walk or Call further on". Walking does not dig up buried pods or make creatures react: that is still Call's. Lightning striking a veiled stone lifts the 3×3 around it. Tiles no Call could reach (more than 7 tiles from any tile you can stand on) don't count and show plainly.
- **Quarters.** A place has four quarters of 14×16 tiles. A quarter is **surveyed** only when every tile in it that a Call could reach is unveiled, so the words never run ahead of the picture; the overview draws a quarter that is 75% or more unveiled with a lighter "almost" dot tint, and the Call tally names what is left ("3/4 surveyed · a corner north-east still veiled"). Checked by script: on 31 places across seeds 3, 5, 7, 13 and 42 (a bot surveying to 100%), every time the line or the tally said "surveyed" the place had 0 veiled tiles. A cell counts as **explored** (its reach dot fills) once it is visited and at least one Call was made inside it; the overview still draws all four quarters, so a partial and a full survey stay visible. The bottom line names it in words a child reads, one step brighter than the rest of the middle: "rock field · surveyed 2/4 · 3 to take" while things remain, "rock field · surveyed · nothing to take" when done, "rock field · not surveyed" before any quarter is. Walking alone never hears what is buried: a place surveyed by walking reads "surveyed · Call to hear below" until a Call is made there, and a place counts as finished ("nothing to take", the one-press "← Leave", its slow beat hidden on the map) only after a Call there and with no pod still buried.
- **Call reports** how much is surveyed and what is still there to take, each kind with the way to its nearest one ("Half surveyed · a pod east, a warm stone north-west"); announced pods keep the dotted outline until taken. Earlier examples: "Surveyed · 1 pod, 2 dew left", "Half surveyed · 2 fruit", "Survey begun · Call further on · nothing to take yet". It never says "Nothing here". A pod still lying in the place glints again.
- **The overview** draws each visited cell in quarters of 13 px: dotted (night dither) while a quarter is under 75% unveiled, a lighter "almost" dot tint from 75% until every tile is unveiled, whole once surveyed. It marks only what is worth coming back for: a pod you saw and left behind shows the pod sign, a found charged stone the bolt, a found warm stone the hollow bolt. Fruit, dew, tufts and unlit beacons never mark the map (no pips); the Call tally, Cargo and the map line still count them ("Ahead: wood · 3 to take"). The trail is not drawn; the start shows a flag, and a skull marks where the Probe broke until you go back.
- **The world turn** veils again what changed: the quarter of a stone that took fresh storm charge, the whole place where a new pod arrived (its reach dot empties: it needs a Call again). A surveyed place that was not changed stays surveyed across expeditions.

## Completion

- **The reach grid.** One dot per reachable cell in the range square (5×5 at tier 1, 9×9 at tier 2): hollow until explored, filled (mint) once you have been in the place and Called there, your cell framed in amber, no dot for cells you can't reach (water, cliff, the island).
- **Completion is unmistakable.** As the last reachable cell is explored (after the Call that completes it; gated cells don't count), the message box leads with "Everything in reach is explored · ← Head home" (plus "· 1 cave needs a digger" when a gated cell remains) until the next action; the HUD reach grid turns bright (white dots in a mint frame) and gains a small mint tick that stays; the menu opens on Head home (its top entry on the map) for the rest of the expedition; Cargo's totals read "Explored 21 of 21 ✓". Checked by script on seed 13 (18 of 18) and seed 7 with the cave gated (19 of 19 · 1 gated).
- **Cargo** shows "Explored N of M · fully surveyed K" with the same grid beside it; the menu's Head home entry reads "Explored N of M in reach".
- **Wait** (the menu's, or ✓ facing nothing in a place) visibly passes time: a pale dithered band sweeps down the view for about 200 ms (palette only; none under reduced motion), the storm and fog move a step, and if no creature in sight moved, one idle creature takes a visible routine step (toward you when it can). The line says what changed: "The storm edges east · two Loikas wander", "The fog bank rolls in", "The Untuva finished its meal", "Dew gathers in a cup"; or "Nothing is moving here · ← Leave" in a place ("· ← Head home" on the map). In the menu the strip stays: ✓ waits again.
- **Conditions** on the bottom line, after the place words: 1–3 bolts with ◀ or ▶ for the storm's heading; when a fog bank is within two cells, a small fog patch with its own ◀ or ▶. No sun arc, no day-part.
- **Pending and gated cells.** On the map each unexplored cell in reach has a faint dotted outline (fog colour, 1 px, blinking slowly; steady under reduced motion). A **gated** cell (the cell above the cave under the cliff while its burrow is closed, no digger is your partner, and the cave still holds a pod) shows only its gate sign, the reach grid draws it as a hollow dot with a notch, and it is left out of the count: "Explored 8 of 8 · 1 gated". Facing it the line says "Ahead: cave · needs a digger". "Everything in reach is explored · 1 cave needs a digger · ← Head home" fires when only gated cells remain.

## Place edges

- Walking off a place's edge steps onto the neighbouring map cell that way (one map action, so the clock, storm and sweep move), with the map in view and the pawn facing the way you walked. One Confirm goes down into it from the matching side. That is 2 presses per crossing, against 5 before (Back, down, Confirm, a map step, Confirm).
- Past the range edge, off the map, or onto cliff or wide water, the Probe stays in the place with the reason ("Edge of the Probe's range · tier 2 reaches further").
- "Leave this place" is still in the menu. There is no minimap.

## Materials

Every source is an act you choose. Each caps at 20 carried.

- **Stones read at 1×** (the Probe screen shows all four): plain (a grey stone); **warm** (a 2 px amber ring fully inside its tile and a small amber dot on its top, pulsing slowly between two ambers); **charged** (a bright blue-white crackle over the stone, flickering in two frames, and a small bolt above it); a **warned strike** is only the tile's yellow outline (no fill, no icon).
- **Energy**: draw a stone's charge. A storm charge gives +2 (+3 if struck at the storm's peak) and is drawn as a yellow zigzag with a cream ring. A **warm stone** gives +1 and is drawn with a 2 px amber and yellow ring on the ground around its foot and a small glint, blinking slowly until you have been within 3 tiles of it, no zigzag, so it is never confused with a storm charge or with the strike warning (a whole yellow tile with a bolt). Spent in the field on pins (1) and shield patches (3 for one bar, on the Probe screen). Spent at the Station on identifying or logging a pod (1), hatching (2) and the tier 2 Probe (12). Prices are unchanged; Essence never converts to Energy.
- **Warm stones.** At each world turn the sun warms 6 to 8 stones in the Probe's reach (was 3 to 5; tuned 2026-10-09) (seeded, chosen among stones without storm charge when the expedition sets out, leaning toward cells near the start), never more than +1 each and never piling up from turn to turn. The 3×3 around each warm stone is veiled again, so a surveyed place doesn't show it on arrival: a Call finds it. Drawing it spends that stone's warmth until the next world turn. Warmth never arrives in a place you entered on the expedition that just ended (new things arrive away from where you just were), and the start guarantee skips those places too, so Head home can't be spammed for Energy. A found warm stone gets the hollow bolt on the map. No warm stones in the cave.
- **Data** ([`research-economy.md`](../../design/proposals/research-economy.md) §2, decided): **a walk brings home 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 Data by the tier of the place it explored**, starter / early / mid / late. Places have no tier field; the tier comes from the land, as the proposal's table lays it out: **meadow and pond edge are starter places (2)**, **wood and rock field mid places (6)**, **the cave behind the burrow a late place (8)**, behind a gate; no land here is an early place (4) yet. A walk over several places takes the highest tier it entered. Creature moments are still how Data is a record of creatures doing things: a creature eats your fruit, or a Tuikis settles because you kept still, +1 (+2 the first time ever for that species and moment, and for the first moment with each species on an expedition; once per creature per expedition), and they bring the walk's Data in early, up to the walk's yield and never past it ("The Loika ate your fruit · this walk's Data is in"); whatever the moments did not bring arrives at **Head home** as the field yield, so every walk that explored something totals its tier's figure. The Head home screen says it ("+6 Data · mid place · 4 moments"), the Observer's **Data yield** row shows it live, and the events log keeps the split. The walk with the mibi (below) no longer adds +1 of its own: that folded into the field yield. Energy and Essence are unchanged. The yield is `step × tier` with the step at **2**; the Observer's developer field changes the step for a test run (see Persistence and tools).
- **Essence**: dew from a full cup (+1), pressing a fruit instead of feeding it (+2), the tuft a Loika leaves when it shakes dry (+1).

## Storm and Shield

- **Energy never stalls.** Every expedition starts with at least one Energy source in reach: a storm charge or a warm stone. If there is none, the sun warms the stone closest to a place's way in near the start. (The round-2 rule of two lit stones on the first expedition is gone; warm stones cover it. In the scripted runs the guarantee never had to act.) On the first expedition the storm still starts two columns further away.
- The band is 3 columns wide; rain falls under its core. While it is over you it crawls 0.2 columns per action (a pass of rain over you lasts about 15 actions, never more than 20) (elsewhere 0.25), with lulls of 3 actions in every 12. The HUD shows 1–3 bolts and, beside them, the way it heads.
- **Stray strikes**: each action in the open under rain, 1 in 20 (strong) or 1 in 10 (peak); none in shelter (an overhang, a cave or beside a lit outpost; tree canopies and wood cells give **no** shelter) or inside a fog bank, none within 6 actions of any hit, and none before the first warned strike of each pass over you lands (the first hit of a pass is always one you could see coming). **Warned strikes** aim at stones (or open tiles near you, never your own tile), one action ahead. A strike on a veiled stone lifts the veil 3×3 around it, so its fresh charge shows. A stone takes charge at most once per storm pass over the place and isn't aimed at again during it. A stray strike says so as it lands ("A stray strike hit the Probe · Shield 2/3", with the shake and flash) and stops a held walk.
- Stones charge at the world turn where lightning really struck or under one flash in four (60% of the stones there, +2), and that quarter of the place is veiled again.
- **Shield**: 3 bars at tier 1, 4 at tier 2. At 0 the Probe breaks and the expedition ends, and nothing goes home: the pods you carried fall at random free spots in the place where it broke (or the place of the map cell you stood on), carried Energy, Data and Essence are lost, and that cell gets a **skull** on the overview; going down into that cell again clears the skull, and the pods lie there to be picked up (the Observer's events log both); a broken Probe can't set out ("The Probe is broken · dock at the Station: it mends it free"). The Shield is mended only at a lit outpost or at the Station: docking mends up to 2 bars for free (a break in full), then **1 Energy a bar** from the Station's store while its "Mend fully on docking" switch is on; the Companion takes the mended Probe at the dock. The Head home preview says the Station mends it at the dock.

## Range, tiers and the map

- **The reach view.** During an expedition the map shows only the Probe's reach, drawn at its own scale (not a blow-up of the 26 px map): 78 px cells at tier 1 (5×5, 390 px), 48 px at tier 2 (9×9, 432 px; 52 px would not fit 450). Each cell shows its land as the middle 3×3 of its place's real 32 px tiles cropped to the cell (faded when only seen, storm-dark under the storm), its quarters (unsurveyed under the 4×4 dither, 75% lighter, a dotted grid between them), its signs at 1× or 2× (never 3×) in corner slots (start flag first; pod 16 px; bolt, paw, skull, outpost, beacon, gate at 2× at tier 1), the blinking pending outline and the warned-strike highlight; fog cells tile the map's fog texture at 1×; the pawn is the 32 px place pawn (a 16 px one at tier 2); dashes frame the reach. The band below is plain 2× text: the flag with the way home and "Explored N of M in reach", with the whole-world inset to its right (6 px a cell, 4 at tier 2) and nothing on top of either: while a message is up it takes the band's text space instead of covering the map.
- **Full map / Reach view** (a menu entry on the map) switches the map between the reach view and the whole 16×20 map at 26 px; both are fully playable (the pad walks, ✓ goes down, Call pins, the range square and signs draw), and the choice is saved with the game. The reach view is the default. Picking a start uses the whole map, and a beacon's sweep shows on it.
- **Cost:** drawMap (the 26 px map) 2.10–2.23 ms per call; the reach view 1.13–1.32 ms (seeds 7, 13, 42).

The log says "Lit the beacon · −1 Energy · N cells". Lit, it burns with a 2-frame flame in the place and shows a lit-beacon sign on the overview for good.

## Call

- **In a place** (1 action): lifts the veil in a round 11 tiles around you, as its ring spreads (see Survey). Hidden pods and charged or warm stones in the square glint and stay outlined until taken. Curious creatures show "?" and come closer; wary ones show "!" for one action, then hide or run. A settling Tuikis is startled. Your partner comes; a digger near the burrow digs it.
- **On the map**: pins your cell for 1 Energy, or removes your own pin for free.
- **At home** (Mibis and the active mibi screen): a mibi with you hops to the front and chirps; a juvenile looks the wrong way first on two calls in three. Free.
- **Finding Energy.** A Call that leaves any charge or warm stone showing in the place leads its tally with it: "Energy nearby · a warm stone north · Half surveyed". The stone's map cell keeps a bolt until it is drawn, surveyed or not: solid for storm charge, hollow (yellow rim, dark inside, a little fatter) for a warm stone. The Probe screen reads "Energy in reach: N" (Energy in found, undrawn stones in the reach).

## Creatures and feeding

- **Four bubbles**, one at a time, each explained once the first time it shows in view: **!** startled ("stop, or it runs"), **?** curious ("it will come closer"), **fruit** (a red fruit on blush: "it eats what you carry · offer it, or put it down and back off"), **…** settling, with four pips under the creature that fill as it settles ("keep still with ✓ Wait"). The fruit bubble shows on every eater of an identified species in view while you carry fruit. The partner-calm tilde from round 2 is kept.
- **Diet before you offer.** Facing a creature of a species you have identified, the line names it and its diet: "Loika · eats fruit", "Tuikis · doesn't eat fruit · settles when you keep still". An unidentified creature reads "Unknown creature · watch what it does", and Confirm reads only "Unknown creature" (dimmed, nothing offered), whatever it eats. The fruit bubble shows only on eaters of identified species. For an identified eater with fruit in hand, Confirm reads "Offer fruit"; for an identified non-eater "Doesn't eat fruit" (dimmed), and a press names only identified eaters ("Tuikis don't eat fruit · Loikas do") and spends nothing.
- A shaken bush names only identified eaters you can see: "A fruit drops · Loikas eat these"; "no one here eats fruit" when every creature in sight is identified and none eats it; otherwise "watch who comes for it". Hidden creatures and those beyond fog sight don't count. Putting fruit down explains itself once ("Fruit down · eaters come when you back off").
- Creeping never startles a creature unless you step right next to it. A startled creature flees 3 tiles, never off-screen, then watches; after 3 quiet actions it goes back to its routine.

## Fog bank

Cover: creatures notice walking 2 tiles later and creeping never startles; hand-feeding a wary creature works; dew cups refill about every 3 actions; no stray strikes; the map sweep stops and signs are hidden. **Sight is 4 tiles** (6 with a Tuikis partner): beyond it the land takes the fog table and nothing on it is drawn. The veil and the fog look different: the veil is darker with night dots, the fog is washed out to pale, low-contrast colours (the fog table now mixes 62% toward bone) with gentle white puffs drifting over the fogged part only, never over what you see. The edge of sight is soft: each seen tile beside fogged ones fades into the fog table over a whole tile with the 4×4 Bayer dither, from cached edge variants (the cave light's method), so the boundary reads round-ish. Rain stays visible. Call reaches 6 tiles inside. The fog bank drifts with the field clock.

## Head home and the sealed bay

Nothing is teleported (decided 2026-10-07; [`station-loop.md`](../../design/proposals/station-loop.md) §2): the Companion carries its cargo until it is docked at the Station page.
- **Head home** (renamed from Send home; same places and rules) ends the expedition and seals everything in the hold, pods and materials, as one **consignment** (a crate) in the Companion's **sealed bay**, separate from the hold. The bay holds **three** crates, one per expedition, never topped up; each keeps its expedition's id and number so the Station accepts it exactly once.
- **The world turns once per Head home**, on the Companion, as before. Guards: an expedition that explored nothing (no Call in any place) seals nothing and turns nothing ("Nothing explored · the world waits"; the finds stay in the hold); an explored expedition with an empty hold turns the world and seals no crate; with the bay full the expedition ends and the world turns, but the finds stay in the hold, unsealed ("Bay full · your finds stay in the hold"), riding along on the next expedition (at risk on a break, filling pod places) until a dock frees the bay. Nothing forbids setting out: there is no forced return.
- **The Head home screen** (replacing the Station stand-in) is short: Sealed (or Bay full, Nothing explored, The Probe broke), the three crates with the new one sliding in, "N consignments sealed · dock to transfer", the world-turn lines, and ✓ **Next expedition**.
- **Counters** between expeditions show only the hold (what can be spent), never sealed amounts; a crate icon with the count sits beside them, and beside the pod outlines in the expedition HUD. Cargo draws the bay as three crates (expedition number, pods, materials). The choice screen's strip and the active mibi screen's bottom line say "N consignments sealed · dock to transfer".
- **Docking** happens on the Station page (its Caddy key). When the Station opens the bay it marks each crate accepted; this page clears those crates when it reads the save ("The Station has them · 3 pods") and, while docked, takes what the Station hands over: the mended or upgraded Probe, the species identified, the mibis raised (a bond made on the Station before care flows in by OR; no care field is read from the Station), the Station's requests to take a mibi along or bring one home (`st.carryReqs`, below), and pods returned to the wild (back, buried, in the place they came from).
- **Trips for the Station.** Every expedition that turns the world (one that explored something, a Probe break included) adds a record to the save's top-level `trips` list, oldest first, the last 40 kept: `{ v: 1, n, partner, places, storm, carried }`. `n` is the expedition number, which rises by one with each world turn and starts again at 1 only in a new world (a new `wid`), so it never repeats within a world; an expedition that explored nothing keeps its number for the next one. `partner` is the id of the Probe partner on that expedition, or `null` with no partner (a juvenile with you is never the partner). `places` lists, once each, the kinds of place entered, from `meadow`, `pond`, `rock`, `wood` (a place is named by its ground: pond edge and a ford are `pond`) and `cave` (going down the burrow). `storm` is `true` if on any action the storm's band was over the Probe's column while the Probe was outside the cave. `carried` is a copy of the mibis with you at the expedition's end (`[]` allowed); `v` stays 1, and a reader of an older record without it takes `[partner]`. The expedition gathers `places` and `storm` as it goes and keeps them in the save, so a reload mid-expedition loses nothing. An expedition that explored nothing and the Walk add no record. The Station reads the list at the dock.
- **Expeditions start only undocked**: docked, Start reads "Lift to explore" (dimmed) and says "Docked · lift the Companion at the Station to explore". The genome seed of a pod is fixed the first time it is taken (`gs`); the Station reads its genome from it.

- The top bar's right cluster shows the world turn, "T7" with a small sun, left of battery and radio; it flashes for a moment when the world turns. The Station's "Meanwhile, the world turned" and Cargo show "World turn 7 · outpost lights: 2, 0" (the light left on each outpost you have lit).

## Outposts

- **Outposts** (the name is one constant, `DEPOT_NAME`, so it can change): about six per world, never two in one 5×5 area (seeds 1–12: 6 each, none closer than 5 cells), a small hut with a mast lamp in a place. Found like any find (walking or Call); the map then shows an outpost sign, dark until lit.
- **"✓ Light the outpost · 1 Energy"**: it stays lit for **3 world turns** (the expedition you light it on and the next two); the count changes only when the world turns, never during an expedition. Its flame shows the light left, on the place token and the map sign: big (3), medium (2), small (1), then a dark hut. Facing it the line says "Outpost · lit 2 more turns", or "Outpost · dark" with "✓ Relight · 1 Energy" (two presses). The world-turn lines say "The outpost in the meadow burns low" (1 left) and "… has gone out". While lit it offers **Head home** from its cell (which ends the expedition, as at the start: no banking), **Shield mending at 1 Energy a bar** (two presses) and **shelter** (no stray strikes within 3 tiles of it in its place); dark, none of these. No ruins or new outposts. It reveals no land; that is the beacon's job. Checked by script: lit 3 → 2 → 1 → dark over three world turns with the lines above, relit in two presses.

## The mibis with you: the carried set and care

The words are those of [`companion-screens.md`](../../design/style-guide/companion-screens.md) "Care and the carried set". The care screens' own layout (the Lead card, three places, the heart, the bond and grow-up events) is not built yet: the roster and the active screen keep their built look, with the new behaviour and words.

- **The carried set.** Up to three mibis are **with you** (`S.carried`, in the order they were taken); everyone else is **at home**. Undocked or mid-expedition, Mibis and the active screen's pages show only the mibis with you; docked, the mibis with you come first, then the mibis at home in hatch order. A new mibi is never put with you by itself.
- **The partner** (the mibi that joins the Probe) is the lead (`S.lead`) if it is with you and grown; otherwise the first grown mibi with you; otherwise none. Nothing sets the lead on this page yet (the Lead card is not built), so the first grown mibi with you leads. The expedition choice's partner card shows the partner, or the first mibi with you when none is grown.
- **Take and Leave**, docked and between expeditions only: ✓ "Take Moss" on a mibi at home (on Mibis or its own page) adds it at the end of the carried order; a mibi the Station has released is never taken (the shake); with three with you Take is refused, dimmed, with the shake and "Moss stays home: the Companion is full · leave one at home first". ✓ "Leave Dot at home" on Mibis takes Dot off ("Dot stays home"); leaving the lead clears the lead. Away, nothing at home shows, so nothing can be swapped.
- **The Station's requests.** The Station asks for changes through `st.carryReqs` (`{ seq, op: "add" | "home", id }`); this page applies them at the dock (the same dock gate as the rest of the dock), between expeditions only, in seq order, each once (`S.carrySeen`). An add is refused when the mibi is unknown, released, already with you or the Companion is full; each refusal is kept in `S.carryRefused` (`{ seq, id, why }`, the last three) so the Station can say the mibi stayed home, and a full one is said here too. Mid-expedition the requests wait. A Station that sends none (today's) changes nothing.
- **Tend** (replaces Spend time): ✓ "Tend Dot" on a mibi with you, once per Companion day (its local date; `tendDay`), docked or not, never mid-expedition ("Tend Dot when the expedition is over"). The species moment plays (a Loika leans on the glass, a Tuikis glows, an Untuva puffs) and the line reads "Dot leans on the glass · it remembers the meadow" (or "· it hasn't been out yet"); +1 care and +1 Tend. Tended today, ✓ is dimmed ("tended today"), and a press shakes: "Dot was tended today", with no countdown. A clock moved back is refused and resets nothing.
- **The Walk**: once per world turn, undocked, between expeditions, every mibi with you together: +1 outing and +1 care each. ✓ reads "Walk together" ("Walk with Dot" with one); the shown mibi plays its walk moment and the line names them all, "Dot, Moss and Fig walk together" (with one, the built walk line for its species). It brings no Data of its own: the +1 folded into the expedition's field yield ([`research-economy.md`](../../design/proposals/research-economy.md) §2). Then ✓ is Tend until the world turns.
- **The bond** is earned here and nowhere else: after every Tend and Walk, a mibi with three Tends and at least one outing bonds ("Dot is bonded with you"; `bonded`, `bondCare` = its care then). Walks never bond on their own. A mibi already adult by the clock when it bonds stays adult (`grownTurn` = born + 2). The heart is a missing asset (`c-heart-24`, `c-heart-16`, registered in the page's `CARE_ASSETS` as empty): nothing is drawn for it yet.
- **Growing up.** An unbonded juvenile grows on the clock, as built (born + 2 world turns). A bonded juvenile grows only through care: at the first Tend or Walk after its bond, while it is with you (never at the bonding press, never on a world turn, never at home), `grownTurn` = that turn and the line reads "Moss is grown · it leads the Probe now" (or "· it can lead the Probe"). Elder 6 world turns after `grownTurn`. The world turn's lines on Head home skip bonded juveniles and mibis with `grownTurn`; a juvenile grown on the clock reads "Fig is grown · it leads the Probe now", "· it can lead the Probe" or "· at home".
- **Expeditions are not care**: an explored expedition gives an outing to every mibi with you and its memory of the land; a skill notch goes to the partner only if it is bonded and its ability was used, only on an expedition that explored something; a break keeps it (three at most), named on Head home and in the log as "Dot gains a skill notch" (the crate the Station opens keeps the world's lines only). The Station shows notches; this page shows no skill count.
- **Status line** on the active screen, exactly one of: "with you · leads the Probe", "with you · can lead the Probe", "with you · too young for the Probe", "with you · grows with care", "at home", "at home · grows on the Companion". No meter, number or count for care, Tends, the bond, skill or growing up anywhere on the screen.
- **Undocked, a mibi at home is never named**: the expedition choice drops the "at home" list and the count ("No one with you · take one at the Station"), and Deep ground without a digger with you reads "Caves · needs a digging partner".
- **Edge cases.** No mibis: an empty pod outline, "No mibi yet · Bring a pod home, raise it at the Station", Call's slot empty, ✓ Next expedition. Mid-expedition the roster is read-only ("with you for this expedition"). Undocked with no one with you, the active screen falls back to the "No mibi yet" screen until the care screens' "No one with you" layout is built.
- **The active mibi screen** (the Companion's home view, ✓ Visit on Mibis): the token art at 8× (256×256, pixel for pixel) on a stage, a slow two-frame idle (still under reduced motion), name, stage chip, species and ability, and the status line. Page dots below (the mibis with you ringed in amber); ◀ ▶ when there are others.
- **The words** are one table at the top of the care code (`CARE_TEXT`), the approved strings of the screens spec.

## Partners by life stage

New pods hatch as juveniles (at home until taken, too young for the Probe), grow up after 2 world turns counted from the turn after they hatch (can be your partner) unless bonded first (a bonded juvenile grows through care, above), and become elders 6 world turns after growing up (double calming and sniffing radius, feel every stray strike, slow). ## Screen and budget assumptions (as if targeting the ESP32-S3 Companion)

- **Frame.** Offscreen 450×600 at 1:1 device pixels, blitted to the page with `image-rendering: pixelated`. Integer coordinates only; no anti-aliasing, gradients, alpha blending or blur. The page snaps its scale to a whole number of physical pixels per device pixel when that costs ≤15% size.
- **Palette.** 48 colours, kept as data (`PALETTE`) at the top of the script and shown in the Observer. `__mb.offPalette()` confirms every pixel of the frame is a palette entry (checked this round on the map, a veiled and an unveiled place, the storm, a fog bank, the Station, the Mibis roster, the active mibi screen and the Probe screen). Blending effects use only palette lookup tables (storm light, cave dim and dark, fog, the fade table for seen cells and old signs) and the ordered 4×4 Bayer dither (fog bank, warnings, dimmed menus, transitions, the survey veil, unsurveyed quarters on the map). No new tables were added this round.
- **Layout.** HUD 26 px, view 450×540, bottom line 34 px. The HUD holds, left to right: the reach grid (3 px dots at tier 1, 2 px at tier 2, 1 px gaps; 9×9 at tier 2 is the full 26 px), shield bars, pod outlines, the partner token on a 2 px teal ring (its name drops when space runs out), each group 2 px apart, the reach grid in a 1 px frame, shield bars with a 1 px dark outline, empty pod outlines one step brighter; then the three material counters (Energy, Data, Essence: icon and number; a gain counts up one per 90 ms with a 260 ms amber flash on that counter, a spend drops at once), and at the far right a 30 px status slot for battery and connectivity. Text is a 5×7 bitmap font at 2× minimum (14 px caps), 3× for headings; the bottom line sets glyphs 1 px apart.
- **Tiles.** Places are 28×32 tiles of 32×32 px; at most 16×19 tiles in view (≤304 tile blits per full redraw), autotiled by 4-bit masks with 2–4 variants and 2 water frames.
- **The veil.** One bit per tile, stored as 32 row words per place (128 bytes), plus a 4-bit quarter mask per map cell. A veiled tile is drawn from a variant of the same tile baked once per tile and light level (the DARK table plus a 4×4 Bayer dot of night), so it costs no extra blit and no per-pixel work per frame. Canopies and overhang roofs over veiled tiles use the same variant. Inside a fog bank, tiles beyond sight use the existing fog-table variant. Where an unveiled tile meets veiled ones, it fades into the veil's dots over 16 px at the shared sides and corners: a pre-dithered edge variant chosen from an 8-bit side/corner mask, built on first use and cached (the cave light's method; unveiled bits are read once per tile per frame). The walk lift is a 7×7 OR into the row words per step.
- **Map.** 16×20 cells of 26 px. Terrain, its storm-dark copy, its faded copy and the fog cloud layer are baked once per world (4 × 216 KB at 8 bpp, or redrawn from cell tiles). Per-frame overlays: fog cells, faded seen cells, a 13×13 dither sprite per unsurveyed quarter of a visited cell (at most 4 per cell), pips, signs, the storm band, the range square, pins, the start flag and the pawn (no trail).
- **Benchmark** (headless Chromium, mean ms per call over 300 calls, seeds 7, 13, 42, against main): drawMap 2.11–2.38 main vs 2.22–2.30 here (and 2.41–2.45 vs 2.31–2.47 with visited cells); drawPatch 0.89–0.90 vs 0.91–1.00 in a veiled place, 0.92–1.21 vs 0.94–1.03 half surveyed, 0.93–1.02 vs 0.98–1.06 in a fog bank. All within 1.15× of main.
- **Sprites.** Tokens are 32×32; features ≤32×32; canopies 88×76; overhang roofs 104×22. Budget ≤40 sprites per frame. New this round: the fruit bubble (11×13), the warm stone (32×32, a ring of dots baked in), the battery and radio icons (15×9, 9×9). The veil hides sprites, so a veiled place draws fewer. Companion mode adds the mibi at 8× (256×256, baked once per species, pose and frame by integer upscale of the 32×32 token), its floor shadow (180×22), the Tuikis's dithered halo (300×220, checkerboard), the hollow bolt (14×17) and the Loika's paw marks (27×27, the 9×9 icon at 3×); the active screen draws at most about 10 sprites plus text.
- **Redraw.** The browser redraws every animation frame; the device would redraw on input or action, with 110–170 ms slides at full rate and 2–4 Hz idle cycles using dirty rectangles. No per-pixel effects.
- **Motion.** Hold-to-walk starts after 230 ms and repeats every 150 ms. A shield hit gives a 260 ms 3 px shake and a 90 ms bright border; Call sends an expanding ring. With reduced motion: no shake, flash, slides or blinking.

## Persistence and tools

- Saves to localStorage with save format **v8** under `mb-save-v8` (the expedition in flight carries optional fields, `placeTier`, `dField`, `tripPlaces` and `tripStorm`, that default when absent, and a report or history entry carries the yield; the top-level `trips` list is read as empty when a save has none, and a page that does not know it keeps it as stored, so the format stays v8 with no migration; the care fields are additive too: top level `carried`, `lead`, `carrySeen`, `carryRefused`, and per mibi `care`, `tends`, `tendDay`, `bonded`, `bondCare`, `grownTurn`, `notches`, `outings`, defaulted on every load and after every dock read (which also drops from `carried` any mibi the Station has released), with a bond made before care back-filled once (`bondCare` = its care; `grownTurn` = born + 2 if it was already adult). A save with the old one slot (`with`, `withSeen`) is migrated once on load: `carried` = `[with]`, `lead` = `with`, `carrySeen` 0, `with` removed, `withSeen` kept for the Station's step), **shared with the Station page**: this page owns every top-level field except `st`, which the Station owns; each page re-reads the stored save before writing and keeps the other's part as stored, and a `storage` event brings the other page's changes in. A v7 save is migrated (the old Station store becomes the Station's tray and store, mibis move to the Station, the bay starts empty); v1–v6 are discarded. New world gives the world a new id (`wid`), which starts a fresh Station too. Species are saved as indexes, never as names, so renaming the three species to Loika, Untuva and Tuikis changed no save field and the key stays `mb-save-v8` (older event-log lines in a saved game keep the earlier words). Works without storage. `?seed=N` sets the seed of the next New world; a saved world keeps playing until you choose New world.
- **Reset game** (a page button beside the Observer toggle, not a device control) asks once ("Erase this world and start fresh?"), then erases every save version (both pages') and reloads into a new world; `?seed=N` applies to it. Keys go back to the game afterwards.
- The Observer shows expedition, world turn, actions, location, the mibis with you (partner, lead, and each one's care, Tends, last Tend day, bond and `grownTurn`: the developer view only) and who is at home, Probe, carried materials, the bay, the hold and the Station's store as last stored, map counts (visited, surveyed, seen, fog; in reach), **Survey** (the current cell's four quarter percentages, its Calls, and "Explored N of M in reach"), **Energy** (warm and storm-charged stones in reach, how many unveiled, and Energy drawn from warm stones this expedition), **Data yield** (this walk's tier and Data, how much the moments have brought in and how much arrives at Head home, and the ladder by tier; after Head home, the last walk's split), seed, the last 8 events and the palette, plus "Copy playtest notes". `?dev=1` in the address opens it.
- **Developer field** in the Observer (the page's developer panel, never a device key): **Data per tier step**, the tier multiplier; a walk yields step × the tier of the place, so the decided 2 gives 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 and the field shows the ladder it makes. It is kept outside the save (`mb-dev-datastep`), so the save format stays v8 and the Station page sees nothing of it; "Decided (2)" puts it back. Typing in the field keeps the keys; Enter or leaving it applies. **New care day** (developer · care) clears `tendDay` on the mibis with you, logged, so Tend can be played again the same day.
- Test hooks: `window.__mb` (state, `lineFor`, `capture`, `offPalette`, `act`, `press`/`release`, `reachCounts`, `surveyed`, `quarterFracs`, `unveiled`, `leftCounts`, `pipsOf`, `stoneWarm`, `stormCharge`, `sightR`, `bubbleOf`, `crossReason`, `stormLevel`, `sealPreview`, `syncStation`, `isDocked`, `canWalk`, `walk`, `carriedMibis`, `partnerMibi`, `canTend`, `tend`, `localDay`, `startBlock`, `placeTier`, `walkTier`, `walkYield`, `setDataStep`, `dataLadder`, `mapCounts`, `TEST` and others).
- **Tests.** `node --test prototypes/exploration/tests/*.test.mjs`, from the repository root: the trip record runs this page's own functions in Node (a record only when the world turns, a Probe break included, none for an expedition that explored nothing or for the walk; the partner `null` with no partner or a juvenile; the kinds in `places`, once each; `storm` false in the cave; the last 40 kept; an old save without `trips`; `carried` in the record), and `care.test.mjs` the carried set and care (`stageAt` exactly as specified; Tend once a local day, the clock moved back; the group Walk; the bond after three Tends and an outing; growth through care and never on the clock; Take refused at three and swaps docked only; the partner; outings, notches to the bonded partner only, the trip's `carried`; the migration from `with` on the shared fixture `tests/fixtures/save-v8-care-before.json`; the Station's requests applied, refused and kept; the dock's bond OR; no skill count on any line). The agreement tests that need the Station's side (the `stageAt` parity, the fixture through both migrations, the round trip, the merge, `tripCarried`) are skipped until it lands, then run by themselves. CI runs them on every pull request and push to main.
- The build stamp reads `../../build.json` (written by CI); a local copy shows "local build". One self-contained file with no build step.
