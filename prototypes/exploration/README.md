# Companion Field Test (exploration round 3 + Companion mode + the sealed bay, prototype round 8)

Builds exploration round 3 ([`design/proposals/exploration-round-3.md`](../../design/proposals/exploration-round-3.md), its build scope, with warm stones kept scarce) on top of round 2 ([`exploration-round-2.md`](../../design/proposals/exploration-round-2.md)) and the four-button control scheme ([`companion-controls.md`](../../design/proposals/companion-controls.md)), and Companion mode ([`companion-mode.md`](../../design/proposals/companion-mode.md): the active mibi screen, Energy via the Probe, the ← key), on an honest simulation of the Companion screen: a fogged 16×20 world map, living places under a survey veil, the action clock, storms and fog banks, materials with a purpose, up to three mibis with you and their care, and End expedition → the sealed bay → world turn. Since round 8 the Station is its own page, [`prototypes/station/`](../station/README.md) (1024×600), sharing this page's save: cargo reaches it only when the Companion is docked there ([`station-loop.md`](../../design/proposals/station-loop.md) §2, §9).

## Controls

Four buttons. The bottom line always reads in three parts: **✓ what Confirm does** (at most 28 characters), the middle context (the only part that shrinks), and **← what the ← key does** (at most 20 characters, never dropped). The shell's faces carry no words or glyph text (the keys carry their icons; "Back" stays its internal name); the bottom line names what it opens on every screen by the menu's top entry: "← Leave" in a place ("← Climb out" in the cave), "← End expedition" on the map, "← close" on screens. Wait is not on the ← label: ✓ waits when you face nothing, and Wait sits lower in the menu (after Leave and End expedition). The first time in a place a line says "← opens the menu · Leave is first". A screen never offers two ways out: read-only screens (Probe without a patch to make, Cargo at home) leave the ✓ part empty. The middle carries the place and its heart's state, with what is left named ("meadow · found · 3 to take: pod · dew · tuft"; what is left means finds only (a pod, Essence, Data, Energy, a drop: pod, charge, warm stone, dew, tuft), never fruit or an unlit beacon; the list is cut on a narrow line, never the count), then the **conditions**: storm bolts (1–3) with the storm's heading ◀/▶, and the fog bank's patch with its drift, explained once on first sighting ("Bottom line: bolts are the storm · it moves ◀ west"). It drops the place name first and never drops below the heart's state. **Call** has no slot in the top bar and no engraving on the shell (the teal button is told apart by colour and its ring texture); one-time lines explain it: "Call lifts the veil: finds show" the first time in a place, "CALL here drops a pin for 1 Energy" the first time on the map, and at 0 Energy a Call on the map says "Pins cost 1 Energy · you have 0".

- New world is offered only between expeditions (last menu entry).
- **World turns count from 1** (turn 1 is the first expedition's world) in the top bar, the choice screen, Expedition ended, Cargo, the full map and the Observer. Saves keep the internal count from 0, so no save changes.
- **The bottom line** has three sections: [✓ action · ← back] | where you are | conditions (storm bolts with the heading arrow, fog with its drift), with 1 px muted dividers and even padding. The action text is at most 24 characters; the middle shrinks first.
- **Storm signs** say it with the storm's own picture: "More bolts, more strikes · ▶ east" the first time the bottom line shows bolts, and "Overhangs and caves, not trees" the first time you stand in the open under a strong storm. The storm's odds are never on a sign or the bottom line; they live in the code (`STRAY`), the play manual, and one place on the Companion, the Probe, where focusing the storm tile reads them in words ("one in twenty", "one in ten").
- **Outpost and beacon signs** use one silhouette at every size (place token, full map at 1×, reach view at 2×): the outpost is a hut with a lamp post beside it (lamp dark, or a flame big/medium/small for the turns left); the beacon is a tall post with a lamp cage (dark, or a flame). Seen unlit beacons are signed too. The bottom line names them on or ahead of you ("outpost (dark)", "outpost · lit 2 turns", "beacon (unlit)", "beacon (lit)"), and the first sighting of each explains it ("An outpost: light it for 1 Energy to end an expedition, mend and shelter there"; "A beacon: light it for 1 Energy to reveal the land around").
- **The pawn** is a small explorer in a hooded orange wetsuit: cream face opening with two eye dots, rounded torso with sleeves in shadow, dark belt with a lantern at the hip, slate gloves and boots. 4 facings × 2 walk frames (plus a crouched creep pose) at 32 px in places and on the tier 1 reach view; a 16 px version of the same figure on the full map and the tier 2 reach view.
- **Expedition choice (the opening).** Header: "Expedition N", the turn clock and battery. Under it the land you know (the painting the reach view uses, framed round the last start; the first time, the five lights in the dark), one line of what changed (the first line of last turn's world, with an orange pip; the pip also sits on the cells that line names, the storm's charged stones, arrivals and young, until the next set-out), then two tabs (Weather alone and full width until Deep ground is met). The focused tab shows its way on the land: Weather, the storm where it would start from the last start (the land dimmed under it, falling rain, the bolt at its top, the arrow on its leading edge; a small chip on that edge when the band lies outside the frame); Deep ground, the burrow's cell ringed in amber. Deep ground goes with any grown Tuikis along, not only the lead: at set-out it is this expedition's partner and the lead is left alone; while it is focused the partner strip shows it in front and the bottom line says "Deep ground · Moss leads". Under the tabs the partner strip (the partner at 3× on its ring, name, stage chip, ability, the others with you at 1× at the top right; only juveniles carried: the rider, below; with none carried `No one with you`; with no mibi at all the pod and "Your first walk"), see "The mibis with you" below; ✓ on it opens Mibis on the Lead card. Reduced motion: no rain, no twinkle, no pip blink, no breath; every picture holds its first frame. On a world from before height levels (a save without `gen`), the line under the land reads "Flat world · New world has stairs"; it is not focusable, and New world still asks for a second confirm. ← opens the menu: Mibis, Full map, New world.
- **Edges and messages.** A held walk stops on a place's edge tile ("Edge of the place · tap again to step off"); stepping off needs a fresh tap. The message box never clips words: notes that don't fit in two lines wait for the next action, and one long note may take three lines.
- **Signs.** Each first meeting with a thing in the field shows a sign: a card of 48 px in the message box's place, with the thing's own picture and one line of six words or fewer (the start flag, the Call, the first lost Shield bar, a storm, a warned tile, open ground in a storm, the first pod, a full hold, a break that would lose finds, fog, fruit, the four creature bubbles, a warm stone, a tuft, pressed fruit, dew, a well-fed Untuva, a partner that keeps creatures calm, and the first sight of each map sign). Control hints (the menu key in a place, the edge of a place, stepping onto the next cell, Call on the map, removing a pin) are for the bottom line's middle; a hint too long for it shows as a plain note. In a 48 px place the warned tile's card shows the cell's sprite box, columns 8–39 and rows 16–47 of the 48 px cell (what the view draws, in the place's own tables), inside the same 2 px outline.
- On the map the menu has no Leave row: End expedition is its top entry. The tier 2 upgrade also takes two presses.
- The menu shows no action count. Its End expedition entry reads "Found N of M in reach" on the bottom line.
- The depicted shell has Call directly above the ← key (no engravings; Back and Confirm carry the signed cap icons from `shell/`, byte-identical to the masters in `art/device-buttons/caps`), both left of a larger Confirm. Call is teal with a ring texture.
- The bottom line sets its glyphs 1 px apart (still 2× pixels) so the three parts fit 450 px.
- A sign never joins another sign or a note: the best one shows (its priority, hazards first) and the others keep their flags for their next trigger. A one-time line or sign counts as shown only if it is on the screen when the action ends. Every sign met joins the **Probe**, which shows them as pictures in the order met (a 9×4 grid, the pad moves a focus over it and the bottom line names the focused one); there is no legend of words.

## The loop

1. Choose Weather (or Deep ground, with a digging partner), pick a start: the first time a glint, later any seen cell. A dotted square shows the Probe's range from that start; the dot grid in the HUD's top-left corner is the same square.
2. Walk the map. Each step lifts the fog one ring around you (not inside a fog bank). Go down into a place: it lies under a veil, which lifts a little around you as you walk. Call to see further, and find what the place keeps and gather what the survey shows.
3. Walk off the place's edge onto the next cell, Confirm, and look round that one. Each place whose heart you find fills its dot.
4. Head back and **End expedition**: **it works only on the expedition's start cell (a flag on the map) or on a lit outpost.** Elsewhere the menu's End expedition is dimmed and the line names the nearest point ("Start · about 2 cells", or "Outpost · about 1 cell" when one is nearer); Hold full and Everything explored say "head back to the start (about N cells)" on the map, and in a place give a direction instead ("head back south to the start"). Cargo shows the hold as pictures (the three counts, the pods, the creatures met, the reach dots), the first line of what End expedition will seal, and the bay as three crates (an empty place is a dashed outline). End expedition seals the hold into the sealed bay and the world turns once (see End expedition and the sealed bay). If the Probe breaks instead, nothing is sealed (see Storm and Shield).
5. Dock at the Station page to transfer the crates; lift the Companion there before the next expedition.

## The veil and the heart

Quarters are out (walk slice W§6, C10): nothing counts a place as "surveyed", and no quarter is drawn on the map or the reach view. What remains is the veil, a place's heart, and the reach dots.

- **The Call ring is the unveil.** A Call sends a ring that spreads over 300 ms; the veil lifts behind it as it passes, and its final contour (held a moment, dotted) is exactly the Call's range: a round 7 tiles in a place (4 in a fog bank, 6 with a glowing partner; the cave keeps 11). Hidden finds count only inside that circle. Just outside the ring, a veiled sliver up to 3 tiles deep with unveiled land right behind it (outward) lifts too, so a Call closes thin gaps against land already seen.
- **Hold C shows the finds.** While C is held (the whole-place peek) each find you have already uncovered (pod, charged or warm stone, full dew cup, tuft) gets the small take mark (`look-take-<kind>`, the same marks as Look) on its tile, top row first. Fruit, unlit beacons and anything hidden or buried are never marked. Nothing spends; on release the walk is pixel-equal (`tests/peek.test.mjs`, `tests/golden/peek-finds.png`).
- **The veil.** Every place (not the cave under the cliff) starts veiled: the ground shows through, one step darker with a 4×4 dot pattern, so you can see where to walk. Creatures always show (in sight); features, pods, drops (fruit, tufts) and stones on a veiled tile are not drawn. Facing one, Confirm reads "Veiled · Call to see" and spends nothing; bumping into one says "Something is there under the veil · ))) Call to see". Landmarks show anyway: the narrow burrow, the cave exit, the pod on the island. Fruit you put down yourself stays visible.
- **Walking lifts it too**, in a circle of 4 tiles around the pawn, spreading only through open ground: it lifts the first shore, trunk or cliff tile it meets but nothing behind it, at every step and on arrival. Whatever is lifted stays lifted, and **nothing puts the veil back**: the world turn no longer re-veils a quarter or a place (a stone that takes fresh charge just glints; a new pod waits in a found place without veiling it). Lightning striking a veiled stone lifts the 3×3 around it. Tiles no Call could reach (more than 7 tiles from any tile you can stand on) are never veiled (`veilable`).
- **A place is found when its heart is found** (`explored(c)`, kept in `S.called`): see Hearts below. That fills its reach dot. A place counts as finished (the one-press "← Leave", its slow beat hidden on the map) once its heart is found and no pod lies buried.
- **The bottom line and the Call's report** lead with the heart ("found · 2 to take: pod · dew", "a slow beat", "something here to find"); the map line names the cell you are on, "found" when its heart is, and the one you face ("meadow · found · Ahead: wood").
- **Tells on the map (C11).** Every seen, unfound place in the reach shows its heart's tell as a map sign until the heart is found: a **shimmer** for a spring (drawn in code, a stand-in for the art desk's), the **slow beat** for a pod, the **bolt** for a charged stone (a quiet stone has no sign); creatures' paws stay as they were. Nothing shows on an unseen cell. A fog bank hides them like any sign.
- **The band under the reach view** reads three lines: the place you stand in ("Meadow · found · 1 pod waits there", or "Meadow · water glints" / "· not found yet" before), the nearest other tell with its land and way ("Tracks in the wood, north-east"; one row at tier 2, it may wrap at tier 1), and the way home. Names show in the reach view only; the full map names only the cell you are on, in the bottom line.
- **Start guarantee (tells).** `ensureTell` runs when an expedition sets out: at least one unfound heart's tell sign lies within one cell of the start. Springs and beats show on their own; when the neighbours hold only quiet stones the sun strikes the nearest (`ch: 2`, a bolt), before `ensureCharge`, which counts it. Checked on seeds 1 to 200 (`tests/map-tells.test.mjs`): 7 seeds need the struck stone.
- **The overview** marks only what is worth coming back for: a pod you saw and left behind shows the pod sign, a found charged stone the bolt, a found warm stone the hollow bolt. Fruit, dew, tufts and unlit beacons never mark the map. The trail is not drawn; the start shows a flag, and a skull marks where the Probe broke until you go back.

## The reach grid

- **The reach grid.** One dot per reachable cell in the range square (5×5 at tier 1, 9×9 at tier 2): hollow until its heart is found, filled (mint) once it is, your cell framed in amber, no dot for cells you can't reach (water, cliff, the island).
- **No completion push.** The last dot filling says nothing: no "Everything in reach is explored", no bright grid or tick, and the menu does not open on End expedition. The band under the reach view and the menu's End expedition row read "Found N of M in reach" (plus "· 1 gated", "· N need a rambler", "· N need a digger" where ground behind mud, scree or an earth bank is counted apart).
- **Cargo** shows the reach as the same dots at 2× (found filled), with no count line.
- **Wait** (the menu's, or ✓ facing nothing in a place) visibly passes time: a pale dithered band sweeps down the view for about 200 ms (palette only; none under reduced motion), the storm and fog move a step, and if no creature in sight moved, one idle creature takes a visible routine step (toward you when it can). The line says what changed: "The storm edges east · two Loikas wander", "The fog bank rolls in", "The Untuva finished its meal", "Dew gathers in a cup"; or "Nothing is moving here · ← Leave" in a place ("· ← End expedition" on the map). In the menu the strip stays: ✓ waits again.
- **Conditions** on the bottom line, after the place words: 1–3 bolts with ◀ or ▶ for the storm's heading; when a fog bank is within two cells, a small fog patch with its own ◀ or ▶. No sun arc, no day-part.
- **Not found and gated cells.** A place not found is told by the night dots over it (never been in: heavier; been in and not found: light; found: clear), not by an outline. A **gated** cell (the cell above the cave under the cliff while its burrow is closed (a Tuikis is needed on every visit), no Tuikis is your partner, and the cave still holds a pod) shows only its gate sign, the reach grid draws it as a hollow dot with a notch, and it is left out of the count: "Found 8 of 8 · 1 gated". Facing it the line says "Ahead: cave · burrow too narrow". Nothing announces that only gated cells remain: the reach dots just stay hollow for them.
- **Partner abilities beyond the three (L2c).** The partner only; a rider does nothing. `ab` keys: `calm` (Loika), `dig` (Tuikis), `nose` (Untuva), `sure` (Tuliko, sp 19), `bank` (Rupar, sp 7). **Tuliko** (`sure`): crosses *soft ground*, a wade (a water cell that is not a ford, west to east; the map calls it "muddy water") and a scree edge (a one-level cliff edge with rock on a side; "Loose scree"), and walks one ring farther (`ex.range = tier.range + 1`, stacking with the tier 2 Probe: 3 and 5; `firstPods` and `podSite` keep the tier's range). Without a rambler the step bumps "Muddy water · needs a rambler" / "Loose scree · needs a rambler" and pins `soft`. **Rupar** (`bank`): digs an earth bank (a one-level cliff edge with soil on both sides, never the gate's): the first crossing of a bank in a walk costs one extra action (an elder two, "slow but sure"), logged "Fig dug a way up the bank"; it is kept in `ex.dug` (edge indices) and crosses at a normal step for the rest of that walk. Without a digger: "Earth bank · needs a digger", pin `bank`; every other cliff edge keeps "Steep edge · look for stairs". **Tuikis** (`dig`): needed on every visit; `dig()` no longer sets `S.opened` but `ex.burrowDug` (open for that walk, "Follow Fig down"); a save with `S.opened[G.gate]` keeps it open for any partner. **The ground shifts back**: the world turn after a walk that dug a bank or the burrow says "The ground shifted, and the bank Fig dug is whole again." (event `['shift', place, partner id, n]`). The overlays (`softGround(G, seed)`, `bankEdges(G)`, `passableIn(G, c, opts)`, `canStepIn(G, a, dir, opts)` with `opts.sure` and `opts.bank`) live in `src/world.mjs`, are never called in a generator, and the page attaches `G.soft` and `G.banks` after mounting, so the world goldens stay. New place tiles: `T.WADE` 19, `T.SCREE` 20, `T.BANK` 21 (all blocking, none stood on); their pictures, the map marks and the extra range ring are code-drawn stand-ins in the art list. `node tools/ability-sweep.mjs` reports the shares per biome. A ford cell is called a "ford" on screen.

## Place edges

- Walking off a place's edge steps onto the neighbouring map cell that way (one map action, so the clock, storm and sweep move), with the map in view and the pawn facing the way you walked. One Confirm goes down into it from the matching side. That is 2 presses per crossing, against 5 before (Back, down, Confirm, a map step, Confirm).
- Past the range edge, off the map, or onto cliff or wide water, the Probe stays in the place with the reason ("Edge of the Probe's range · the next Probe upgrade reaches further", a [copy] placeholder). In a baked place each opening is classified at entry (`openingKind`: open, range, gate, closed). A range opening shows the map's ice dashes across the gap 3 tiles in from the edge and a plum-dot fade past them; a closed one has its path drawn as grass; the 3 tiles past either are blocked (a step there refuses with the reason and spends nothing); the range words show within 2 tiles of a range line. The line follows `ex.range` (the tier, a Tuliko along).
- "Leave this place" is still in the menu. There is no minimap.

## Height levels, cliffs and stairs (new worlds)

- **New worlds use generator 2** (`NEW_GEN = 2` in `src/world.mjs`): the same meadow, river, island, fords, lake and burrow, on three height levels with one-level steps. A save made before it has no `gen` and keeps generator 1, with no levels, forever; the `?gen=2` developer setting is gone.
- **Edges, not cells.** Where two cells differ in height the edge between them is a cliff (blocks) or a stair (the free way up and down, one per joining edge). On the map a step against a cliff bumps, spends nothing and says "Steep edge · look for stairs"; facing an edge the line reads "Ahead: steep edge", "Ahead: stairs up" or "Ahead: stairs down".
- **In a place** a drop facing south is a two-row face, one facing east or west a side face, one facing north a rim with a walkable shadow row below; the stair is the only way across, and you land on its landing going up or its foot going down. Leaving by the border elsewhere along a cliff says the same refusal. Places draw these from the meadow set when it has loaded (see Screen and budget assumptions): the lawn by height level, a lip and a face by their level's tone, a cast shadow on the lower lawn below and beside a face, the stair, its landing and foot. Caves, and any place the set cannot be built for, keep the code-drawn 32 px stand-ins, listed in the placeholder register.
- **On the map and the reach view** every cliff and stair edge whose two cells are seen (and the pawn's own four edges always) is marked in code: a face on south-facing drops, a light lip on the high side, a 1 px ink line, a checkerboard shadow east of east-facing drops, and a stair wedge centred on the edge. Higher ground is one step lighter per level, up to lime; the lip lightens the ground the same way, so on lime ground it adds nothing and the ink line alone shows the edge (in places too). The burrow's mouth sits on the gate cell's face, drawn each frame once the gate and the cell above it are seen. The marks are drawn each frame, never into the map image, so the inset shows the tint but no marks.
- **Starting.** A start is refused on a cell that no stair joins to the main walkable land ("No stairs out · can’t start"); no world in 10,000 seeds has one.

## Materials

Every source is an act you choose. Each caps at 20 carried.

- **Stones read at 1×** (the Probe screen shows all four): plain (a grey stone); **warm** (a 2 px amber ring fully inside its tile and a small amber dot on its top, pulsing slowly between two ambers); **charged** (a bright blue-white crackle over the stone, flickering in two frames, and a small bolt above it); a **warned strike** is only the tile's yellow outline (no fill, no icon).
- **Energy**: draw a stone's charge. A storm charge gives +2 (+3 if struck at the storm's peak) and is drawn as a yellow zigzag with a cream ring. A **warm stone** gives +1 and is drawn with a 2 px amber and yellow ring on the ground around its foot and a small glint, blinking slowly until you have been within 3 tiles of it, no zigzag, so it is never confused with a storm charge or with the strike warning (a whole yellow tile with a bolt). Spent in the field on pins (1) and shield patches (3 for one bar, on the Probe screen). Spent at the Station on hatching (2) and the tier 2 Probe (12). Prices are unchanged; Essence never converts to Energy.
- **Warm stones.** At each world turn the sun warms 6 to 8 stones in the Probe's reach (seeded, chosen among stones without storm charge when the expedition sets out, leaning toward cells near the start), never more than +1 each and never piling up from turn to turn. The 3×3 around each warm stone is veiled again, so a found place doesn't show it on arrival: a Call finds it. Drawing it spends that stone's warmth until the next world turn. Warmth never arrives in a place you entered on the expedition that just ended (new things arrive away from where you just were), and the start guarantee skips those places too, so End expedition can't be spammed for Energy. A found warm stone gets the hollow bolt on the map. No warm stones in the cave.
- **Data** ([`research-economy.md`](../../design/proposals/research-economy.md) §2, decided): **an expedition that explored a place brings home 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 Data by the highest tier it entered (none if the Probe breaks)**, starter / early / mid / late. Places have no tier field; the tier comes from the land, as the proposal's table lays it out: **meadow and pond edge are starter places (2)**, **wood and rock field mid places (6)**, **the cave behind the burrow a late place (8)**, behind a gate; no land here is an early place (4) yet. An expedition over several places takes the highest tier it entered. Creature acts are how Data is a record of creatures doing things: feeding a creature and a Tuikis settling because you kept still each pay **+1 Data, for certain, once per creature per act per expedition**, said on the creature's line before you act ("Loika · eats fruit · +1 Data"), under a ceiling of **8 a trip** for creature Data ("The Loika ate your fruit · Data for this trip: full"). Nothing is left to chance and there is no first-time bonus. Not built yet: watch, follow, play and the wild sample. The field yield above arrives when you **end the expedition**, counted apart from creature Data. The Expedition ended screen says it ("+6 Data · mid place · 4 creature acts"), the Observer's **Data yield** row shows it live, and the events log keeps the split. The Walk (below) brings no Data. Energy and Essence are unchanged. The yield is `step × tier` with the step at **2**; the Observer's developer field changes the step for a test run (see Persistence and tools). Spent at the Station on identifying or logging a pod (1).
- **Essence**: dew from a full cup (+1), pressing a fruit on a stone, a rock or a tree instead of feeding it (+2; the press mask is `lay.press` from the adapter, 1 stone, 2 tree; cliff faces, cave walls and storm stones count by their own code, never `T.ROCK`; anything else that blocks reads "Press on a stone", dimmed, and spends nothing), the tuft a Loika leaves when it shakes dry (+1).

## Storm and Shield

- **Energy never stalls.** Every expedition starts with at least one Energy source in reach: a storm charge or a warm stone. If there is none, the sun warms the stone closest to a place's way in near the start. (The round-2 rule of two lit stones on the first expedition is gone; warm stones cover it. In the scripted runs the guarantee never had to act.) On the first expedition the storm still starts two columns further away.
- The band is 3 columns wide; rain falls under its core. While it is over you it crawls 0.2 columns per action (a pass of rain over you lasts about 15 actions, never more than 20) (elsewhere 0.25), with lulls of 3 actions in every 12. The HUD shows 1–3 bolts and, beside them, the way it heads.
- **Stray strikes**: each action in the open under rain, 1 in 20 (strong) or 1 in 10 (peak); none in shelter (an overhang, a cave or beside a lit outpost; tree canopies and wood cells give **no** shelter) or inside a fog bank, none within 6 actions of any hit, and none before the first warned strike of each pass over you lands (the first hit of a pass is always one you could see coming). **Warned strikes** aim at stones (or open tiles near you, never your own tile), one action ahead. A strike on a veiled stone lifts the veil 3×3 around it, so its fresh charge shows. A stone takes charge at most once per storm pass over the place and isn't aimed at again during it. A stray strike says so as it lands (the first one shows the Shield sign, later ones "Struck again · a plate lost"; the shake, the hit frame, and the lost plate's fill flashing red in the top bar for 700 ms) and stops a held walk. Plate counts are never written as digits in the field: the plates in the top bar are the Shield.
- Stones charge at the world turn where lightning really struck or under one flash in four (60% of the stones there, +2), and that quarter of the place is veiled again.

The Shield is mended only at a lit outpost or at the Station: docking mends up to 2 bars for free (a break in full), then **1 Energy a bar** from the Station's store while its "Mend fully on docking" switch is on; the Companion takes the mended Probe at the dock. The End expedition preview says the Station mends it at the dock.

## Range, tiers and the map

- **The reach view.** During an expedition the map shows only the Probe's reach, drawn at its own scale (not a blow-up of the 26 px map): 72 px cells at tier 1 (5×5, 360 px, the brackets' arms 9 px), 48 px at tier 2 (9×9, 432 px; 52 px would not fit 450). The reach is **one painted land, not a table of thumbnails** (`landImage`): each pixel takes the land of the cell under it after a soft displacement (integer value noise, ±11 px at 72 px cells plus ±2.5 px fine), so cell borders become coastlines, wood edges and field edges and no grid lines are drawn; every land has a texture at map scale (meadow blades and flowers, packed tree crowns, sand and clay with boulders, a pond's own water ellipse from the place's real water tiles with a sand rim, sea with ripples and a beach, slate bands for cliffs). The fog keeps its three states on the same soft borders (never been in: the dark table plus `FOGV.n` night dots; been in: dots at `FOGV.b`; found: clear); a cell not seen is night with a dithered fringe, not a grey cobble. The painting is made once per change of state (cached; a new one when a cell is found, the storm moves or a cell is seen). A place you have been into wears its **mark** (`placeMark`: meadow a lone tree, wood the hollow tree on a clearing, rock the split stone, pond the island stone; code stand-ins until the art desk paints them) at its landmark's real spot in the cell: a slate silhouette when only been in, full colour once found, none before; 26 px at tier 1, 18 px at tier 2, 12 px on the full map. The names are off the cells: once you have been in a place, band line 1 names the place under the pawn ("Windstep · found · 1 to take"). Signs (tells, pods, Energy) stand at the heart's spot in the cell, the others beside the first, and the start flag is planted at the cell's centre (2× at tier 1; at the upper left on the pawn's own cell). The edge marks (cliff faces, stairs, lips) still run straight along the cell edges; the strike highlight is kept; the pawn is the 32 px place pawn (a 16 px one at tier 2); dashes frame the reach. The band below is plain 2× text: the flag with where to end the expedition and "Explored N of M in reach", with the whole-world inset to its right (6 px a cell, 4 at tier 2) and nothing on top of either: while a message is up it takes the band's text space instead of covering the map.

- **In a place** (1 action): lifts the veil in a round 11 tiles around you, as its ring spreads (see The veil and the heart). A settling Tuikis is startled. Your partner comes; a digger near the burrow digs it.
- **On the map**: pins your cell for 1 Energy, or removes your own pin for free.
- **Between expeditions** (Mibis and the active mibi screen): a mibi with you hops to the front and chirps; a juvenile looks the wrong way first on two calls in three. Free.

## Fog bank

Cover: creatures notice walking 2 tiles later and creeping never startles; hand-feeding a wary creature works; dew cups refill about every 3 actions; no stray strikes; the map sweep stops and signs are hidden. **Sight is 4 tiles** (6 with a Tuikis partner): beyond it the land takes the fog table and nothing on it is drawn. The veil and the fog look different: the veil is darker with night dots, the fog is washed out to pale, low-contrast colours (the fog table now mixes 62% toward bone) with gentle white puffs drifting over the fogged part only, never over what you see. The edge of sight is soft: each seen tile beside fogged ones fades into the fog table over a whole tile with the 4×4 Bayer dither, from cached edge variants (the cave light's method), so the boundary reads round-ish. Rain stays visible. Call reaches 6 tiles inside. The fog bank drifts with the field clock.

## End expedition and the sealed bay

Nothing is teleported ([`station-loop.md`](../../design/proposals/station-loop.md) §2): the Companion carries its cargo until it is docked at the Station page.
- **End expedition**; it ends this expedition only; you can set out again at once. It seals everything in the hold, pods and materials, as one **consignment** (a crate) in the Companion's **sealed bay**, separate from the hold. The bay holds **three** crates, one per expedition, never topped up; each keeps its expedition's id and number so the Station accepts it exactly once.
- **The world turns once when an expedition ends**, on the Companion, as before. Guards: an expedition that explored nothing (no Call in any place) seals nothing and turns nothing ("Nothing explored · the world waits"; the finds stay in the hold); an explored expedition seals a crate whenever the bay has room, carrying at least the place's Data (the Cargo preview counts the Data still to come); with the bay full the expedition ends and the world turns, but the finds stay in the hold, unsealed ("Bay full · your finds stay in the hold"), riding along on the next expedition (at risk on a break, filling pod places) until a dock frees the bay. Nothing forbids setting out: there is no forced return.
- **The Expedition ended screen** (replacing the Station stand-in) is short: Sealed (or Bay full, Nothing explored, The Probe broke), the three crates with the new one sliding in, "2 crates sealed · dock to transfer", the world-turn lines, and ✓ **Next expedition**.
- **Counters** between expeditions show only the hold (what can be spent), never sealed amounts; a crate icon with the count sits beside them, and beside the pod outlines in the expedition HUD. Cargo draws the bay as three crates (expedition number, pods, materials). The choice screen's strip ends "· 2 sealed"; the choice screen's bottom line, Cargo between expeditions and the active mibi screen's "No one with you" and "No mibi yet" layouts say "2 crates sealed · dock to transfer", or "2 crates sealed · ready for the Station" while docked; docked, the choice screen's line shows "Lift to explore" instead.
- **Docking** happens on the Station page (its Caddy key). When the Station opens the bay it marks each crate accepted; this page clears those crates when it reads the save ("The Station has them · 3 pods") and, while docked, takes what the Station hands over: the mended or upgraded Probe, the species identified, the mibis raised (a bond made on the Station before care flows in by OR; no care field is read from the Station), the Station's requests to take a mibi along or bring one home (`st.carryReqs`, below), and pods returned to the wild (back, buried, in the place they came from).
- **Trips for the Station.** Every expedition that turns the world (one that explored something, a Probe break included) adds a record to the save's top-level `trips` list, oldest first, the last 40 kept: `{ v: 1, n, partner, places, storm, carried }`. `n` is the expedition number, which rises by one with each world turn and starts again at 1 only in a new world (a new `wid`), so it never repeats within a world; an expedition that explored nothing keeps its number for the next one. `partner` is the id of the Probe partner on that expedition, or `null` with no partner (a juvenile with you is never the partner). `places` lists, once each, the kinds of place entered, from `meadow`, `pond`, `rock`, `wood` (a place is named by its ground: pond edge and a ford are `pond`) and `cave` (going down the burrow). `storm` is `true` if on any action the storm's band was over the Probe's column while the Probe was outside the cave. `carried` is a copy of the mibis with you at the expedition's end (`[]` allowed); `v` stays 1, and a reader of an older record without it takes `[partner]`. The expedition gathers `places` and `storm` as it goes and keeps them in the save, so a reload mid-expedition loses nothing. An expedition that explored nothing and the Walk add no record. The Station reads the list at the dock.
- **The field freezes while docked mid-expedition** (the game designer's dock-freeze ruling): docking mid-expedition pauses the field. Every press that would move you, spend, act in a place, wait or leave is refused with the bump and changes nothing (`fieldFrozen()`, checked in `act()` before any state changes; the first refusal of each dock says `Field paused while docked · lift to go on · End expedition still works at the start or a lit outpost`, later ones `Docked · lift to go on`). No action is spent, so the storm, fog, creatures and pending strikes wait and the expedition resumes at Lift as it was; the only change is the Shield, mended by the cradle (the mended bars flash at Lift). The views (menu, Full map / Reach view, Probe, Cargo, Mibis), the mibi pages and End expedition (at the start or a lit outpost) still work. A `Docked` chip in the HUD shows the state, refused ✓ labels and the menu's Wait and Leave are dimmed, and ← in a place opens the menu. A Probe break already under way completes before the dock's mend applies; a tier bought while docked applies from the next expedition (the Probe screen reads the expedition's own reach, hold and tier); pods returned to the wild wait, unmarked, and apply at the next sync after the expedition ends, which can be the next dock. No one-time hint shows or is spent while docked: the only message is the dock line, and each hint shows on the first press after Lift if its condition still holds. Screens: [`companion-screens.md`](../../design/style-guide/companion-screens.md#docked-mid-expedition).
- **Expeditions start only undocked**: docked, Start reads "Lift to explore" (dimmed) and says "Docked · lift the Companion to explore". The genome seed of a pod is fixed the first time it is taken (`gs`); the Station reads its genome from it.

### The place layer (world generation, G2; terrain and the dual grid only)

`src/gen7/place.mjs` is layer 2: the terrain of one cell (24 x 28 tiles) with a one-tile apron, as a function of the realm seed and world tile positions (`cellTerrain(seed, X, Y)`, `cellIR`, `windowIR`). - **Shapes come from the fields at every tile.** The world's own fields (`fieldsAt`, the very functions the region samples at cell centres) are sampled per tile, with a light linear pull from the region's cell labels and tile noise at world coordinates. There is no smoothstep between cell centres, so no boundary falls on a cell edge (scheme Rule 1.10). Ground, then sand, scrub, thicket, water; pools under 3 x 3 are dropped; a bit of ground the walker cannot reach (a grass island in a lake) is filled with what rings it, except a swim island.
- **Levels at tile scale, no blocks** (owner, 2026-11-09 14:57; `src/gen7/levels.mjs`). A tile's level is the world's elevation field read at the tile and thresholded, so a cliff is the field's own contour line and wanders, bends and rounds like land. Added to the field, all smooth: tile-scale detail (FastNoiseLite FBm, domain-warped) and a pull toward each cell's own level round its centre, and round the middle of an edge between two cells of one level, as round bumps (flat 5 tiles, a Gaussian skirt, each up to 2.5 tiles off its spot by a hash): an anchor pulls only by the amount the field falls short of its cell's level, so where the field agrees with the cells nothing is added, the cell's level stays a summary of the land and an open edge stays open. Near the realm's rim the raised land fades out over 22 tiles. Then opening and closing by 4 x 4 (nothing narrower than four tiles, so no spike, notch or one-tile ridge), regions under 100 tiles that no cell knows of are dropped, and the rows and columns on either side of a cell edge are read from one sample (a contour cannot run along a cell edge); a run that still lies on a cell edge is moved a tile. A cliff is not straight by construction: runs follow the contour, a diagonal is a staircase of one-tile steps (the dual grid's corner pieces draw it), and the longest runs are where the contour is nearly straight in the field itself.

### Props and decor (world generation, G3; `src/gen7/decor.mjs`)

Pure, no page state: every prop and decal is a function of the realm seed and a **world tile**, never of a cell. A cell asks for the rectangle of the world it shows (`propsIn`, `decalsIn`, `reedsIn`); a prop whose foot is within two tiles sideways or three below a cell's rectangle is in that cell's IR (its crown hangs into it), and the neighbour lists the same prop at the same world position. Nothing snaps to a cell grid.

- **Clusters** (groves, pairs, outcrops, bushes, drifts of tall grass): one seed per world block per kind per biome (the table `TABLE`, the variety scheme's per-biome numbers), jittered inside the block, kept when a hash and a FastNoiseLite field (`g3-grove`, FBm 1/18) allow and when the biome at the seed's own jittered position is that biome (a biome thins into the next over a few tiles, never on a cell edge). A tile belongs to the cluster of the nearest seed of its kind (a Voronoi cell), so clusters of one kind never overlap and none depends on another; a cluster grows trunk by trunk onto tiles of its claim, each touching it (crowns touch) and none beside another, until it has its size; a cluster that cannot reach its smallest size (a grove of 4 in Woods, 6 in Tallwood) is dropped whole, which is how clearings open. Kinds come in an order (trees, rocks, stumps, bushes, tall-grass sweeps); a later kind never stands on a tile an earlier one holds.

## Pacing and the first trip (walk slice, C16)

Three bots play generator 6 through the page's keys (`tools/walker.mjs` holds their moves: Call, walk to a tile by the page's own occupancy, face and ✓, the map's `canStep`, the way home). They are floors, not players: they never hesitate, wander or read, so a person's walk is longer by a factor nobody has measured yet.

- **The first trip** (`tools/first-trip-bot.mjs`, `tests/first-trip.test.mjs`; loop audit 3.18). A fresh save, the first glint, the start place first, then the nearest cell (rings out from the start) whose heart is not found: Call, take what the Call shows, walk to the heart (found already or not: the Call there reaches what lies round it), Call there, take what is shown, leave. It stops when the hold has a pod and 2 Data, the two things the Station asks first (identify a pod 1 Data, read one trait 1 Data; Probe tier 2 at 12 Energy and 4 Data is a later goal and the first walk does not pay it). **Seeds 1 to 200: 200 of 200 hold a pod and 2 Data**; actions to the first pod median 55, to the Data median 38, the whole first trip median 49, 90th percentile 105, longest 200; places entered median 2; Data held median 6 (heart pays `2 × tier`), Energy median 0, one pod. ## Baked places (place foundation, C7; a dev switch)

`index.html?place=<id>` (for example `?place=Test_AllCases&seed=7`) plays a baked place from `prototypes/places/baked/` in every land cell of the world, padded into the 28 × 32 grid as in C7 (the generator 6 places are not padded). Without `?place=` nothing of the dev switch loads (the modules are imported on demand).

- **What draws it.** `places/src/adapter.mjs` turns the baked place into the page's `lay` (terrain to `T`, prop footprints to `TRUNK` or `ROCK`, tall grass to `T.TALL`, the heart, its clue marks, the openings and the find slots from the place's entities). The 18 × 22 place sits centred in the page's 28 × 32 grid with rock round it, because the page's constants stay until places of two sizes land (`lay.baked.ox/oy` say where). `places/src/page.mjs` draws it: the indexed ground and the light and veil pass (`ground.mjs`) are converted once per change to an `ImageData` through the page's core colours and the set's own, props and canopy are cached canvases, the canopy is dithered away in a 22 px disc round the explorer and the partner (`sprites.mjs`), and the camera is `places/src/camera.mjs` (look-ahead, pull to the tell, clamp). Creatures, the partner, the explorer and every overlay draw as before.
- **Where it touches `index.html`** (one block each): the `BAKED` object, `loadBakedPlace` and a two-line branch in `layout()`; `placeTs` (a baked place is 48 px); `camPos` (replaces `camAt` at the four camera sites); in `drawPatch` the `bk` lines before the tile loop (the ground), the prop items before the sort, and the crown pass before the old canopy loop; the off-palette check also accepts the set's colours; `loadBakedPlace()` at boot beside `loadAtlas()`.
- **Test.** `tests/baked-place.test.mjs` (real Chromium, `PW_DIR`): the place draws, `offPalette()` is clean, a step changes the view, two page stills (`tests/golden/baked-walk.png`, `baked-canopy.png`; `MB_UPDATE_GOLDENS=1` rewrites them), the page without `?place=` is unchanged, and the mean `drawPatch` time (300 calls, best of four runs, seeds 7, 13, 42) is within 1.2 times a generated place's.
- **Benchmark.** Standing still a baked place is 0.27–0.72 ms against 0.55–4.5 ms generated on this shared machine (the ground is a cached `ImageData`, put once a frame); a frame in which the camera moves redoes the window cut, the light pass and the colour conversion, measured at 3 ms (no veil) to 5 ms (a veil disc) in Node on this machine, so a walking frame costs more than a generated place's tile blits do (not measured in Chromium). ## The partner in a place

- Your partner stands on a 2 px bright teal ring with a 1 px dark outline (wild creatures never have one) and shows a small name tag above it, with a pointer down to it, for the first 3 actions in each place and for 3 actions after each Call. The tag is drawn after every sprite and takes the first clear of five positions (below, right, left, above, then below slid sideways), refusing other creatures, the player, any find drawn in that frame (a pod, a stone, the crack, a lime pod or fruit on the ground: its cell and 4 px), the warned tile, the message box and any edge tab, so it never covers a find; if none is clear it is not drawn. The HUD token sits on the same ring.

## Partners by life stage

New pods hatch as juveniles (at home until taken, too young for the Probe), grow up after 2 world turns counted from the turn after they hatch (can be your partner) unless bonded first (a bonded juvenile grows through care, above), and become elders 6 world turns after growing up (double calming and sniffing radius, feel every stray strike, slow). A Tuikis digs out the narrow burrow (for that walk only: the ground shifts back at the world turn) and glows (Call keeps its reach and sight widens to 6 tiles in a fog bank); a Loika calms wary creatures; an Untuva sniffs out buried pods and flinches before stray strikes.

- **Palette.** 51 colours (the first 48 and the sage three sageL, sage, sageD at 48–50: S04 Hiljan and S20 Tuliko read sage; the fog, fade and Shade-wash tables of the first 48 are unchanged, a sage colour may land on a sage), kept as data (`PALETTE`) at the top of the script and shown in the Observer. `__mb.offPalette()` confirms every pixel of the frame is a palette entry (in a 48 px place the table in effect is the core 51 plus the meadow's entries; everywhere else the core 51; checked this round on the map, a veiled and an unveiled place, the storm, a fog bank, the Station, the Mibis roster, the active mibi screen and the Probe screen). No new tables were added this round.
- **Layout.** HUD 32 px (rows 0–31), view 450×532 (rows 32–563), bottom line 36 px (rows 564–599). The site copies that folder and nothing else from `art/`.

A bot that places its Calls optimally gains little from walking (its walks run through tiles a Call is about to lift); the gain is for a player who walks a place instead of calling.
- **drawPatch** (mean ms over 300 calls, four runs, before → after the whole round, ratio of means per seed): veiled 0.84–1.33 → 0.86–1.14 (median 1.0×); walked, veil edges in view, 0.81–1.26 → 0.86–1.42 (1.0×); called 0.85–1.27 → 0.92–1.56 (1.2×); fog bank 0.83–1.24 → 0.97–1.57 (1.2×); storm 1.12–1.79 → 1.25–2.02 (1.1×). - **Send-home spam** (a bot that knows where every charged or warm stone is, goes down into the start cell, draws one if it lies there, sends home; 10 expeditions × 5 seeds): before, seed 3 brought 1 Energy on 4 of 10 expeditions; after, each seed brings at most 1 Energy on the first expedition and 0 on all later ones.

- **Slabs** (generator check over every place of seeds 7, 13, 42, 3, 5: 1,319 places with slabs, 1,483 slabs): before, 15 slabs could not be pushed at spawn or had water or cliff on two sides; after, 0. A slab pushed into a spot it can't leave says once "The slab is wedged".

- **Lightning, tuned** (a bot standing in the open through 20 storm passes per seed, seeds 7, 13, 42, 3, 5): stray hits 15 of 860 strong-rain actions and 32 of 640 peak actions in the open; Shield lost per pass 0 or 1 bar in all 100 passes (before the tuning: 0–4 bars, 3–4 on seed 13). ## Known issues

- The outcrop's roof (104 px) is narrower than the 144 px outcrop of a 48 px place; it is centred on it.
- New in round 8: the two pages share one save by ownership (the Companion's fields, the Station's `st`) and re-read it before every write; a write made in both tabs within the same instant could still lose one side's last press. The Companion draws mibis as species tokens, so the looks chosen at the Station don't show here yet. The Walk is one press (the proposal's five-press scene is not built).

- Left from playtest r6: an empty dew cup still looks much like a full one (art); creatures that crowd you after a Call can hem you in for a few actions (tuning); the fog arrow against the Wait words ("drifts south-west" with ▶) was not reproduced: both read the same drift; a second creature meal in an expedition gives no Data and says nothing.

- **Completing a reach is still long** (538–845 actions for a scout; see above): with places at 28×32 tiles crossing each place sets the floor, whatever the Call does.
- Calm Energy scales with places visited (about 1 per place); faster surveys would raise it unless the warm odds drop.
- A found place stays found across expeditions, and the veil is never put back.
- At 1× the map's dotted quarters on dark ground (wood) are subtle.
- Seen meadow cells fade only from grass to a darker green. The map line rarely fits the cell you're on and the one ahead in full.
- Tablet scale factors that don't snap give slightly uneven pixel widths. Some browsers block clipboard access; "Copy playtest notes" then shows the notes selected in a text box.

## Persistence and tools

- Saves to localStorage with save format **v8** under its own Companion's key, `mb-save-v8@<id>` (the id made once and kept under `mb-companion-A`, or `mb-companion-B` for the play page's second Companion, `?companion=B`; the single shared save of earlier builds under `mb-save-v8` moves there with the first write after it loads, and bytes the page cannot load stay where they are, read-only) (the expedition in flight carries optional fields, `placeTier`, `dField`, `tripPlaces` and `tripStorm`, that default when absent, and a report or history entry carries the yield; the top-level `trips` list is read as empty when a save has none, and a page that does not know it keeps it as stored, so the format stays v8 with no migration; the care fields are additive too: top level `carried`, `lead`, `carrySeen`, `carryRefused`, and per mibi `care`, `tends`, `tendDay`, `bonded`, `bondCare`, `grownTurn`, `notches`, `outings`, defaulted on every load and after every dock read (which also drops from `carried` any mibi the Station has released), with a bond made before care back-filled once (`bondCare` = its care; `grownTurn` = born + 2 if it was already adult). - `gen` records the world's generator. A save without it is generator 1, and a save never changes generator. A save from a newer generator (a `gen` this page does not know) is kept read-only: the page writes nothing, ignores the device keys and shows one line, "This page is out of date · reload to play". - **Reset game** (a page button beside the Observer toggle, not a device control) asks once ("Erase this world and start fresh?"), then erases every save version (both pages') and reloads into a new world; `?seed=N` applies to it. Keys go back to the game afterwards.
