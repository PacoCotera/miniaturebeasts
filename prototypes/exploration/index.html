Companion Field Test

Miniature Beasts · exploration rough playable. The screen is drawn as the Companion would draw it: 450×600 pixels, 48 colours. Creatures are tokens, not art.

How to play
  • ←↑→↓ or WASD, or the pad: move. Tap to creep one step; hold to walk. Creeping never startles a creature unless you step right next to it.
  • Enter / Space or the orange button: Confirm. It does exactly what the bottom line says after ✓.
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey button: Back. The right end of the bottom line says where it goes. In the field it opens the menu: Wait, Leave, Send home, Probe, Cargo, Companions.
  • C or the teal button: Call. In a place it sends a pulse (one action): hidden finds glint, curious creatures come, shy ones hide, your partner comes. On the map it pins your cell for 1 Energy. On the Companions screen it calls the mibi riding with you. The top right of the screen says what it will do.
  • Every step, act, Call or Wait is one tick of the field clock. Doing nothing changes nothing.
What to try
  1. Pick a glint to start. The dotted square is how far the Probe reaches.
  2. Go down into a place and press Call. Dig up what glints.
  3. Shake a bush. Press a fruit for Essence, or put it down, back off three tiles and let a creature eat it for Data.
  4. When the storm is over you, charge up from struck stones. Count the bolts top right: each action in the open can hit your Shield. Trees, overhangs and woods are shelter.
  5. Call in every place you visit. The map ticks a place once nothing is hidden or left there.
  6. Send home from the menu before the Probe breaks. The Station spends what you bring: identifying, hatching, the tier 2 Probe.
  7. In a fog bank, creep close to wary creatures and fill up on dew.
