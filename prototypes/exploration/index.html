MINIATURE BEASTSCOMPANION

Companion Field Test

Exploration on a simulated Companion screen. It shares one save with the Station page in this browser: End an expedition here, then dock there and open your crates to read the pods.

How to play
  • ←↑→↓ or WASD, or the pad: move. Tap to creep one step; hold to walk. Creeping never startles a creature unless you step right next to it.
  • Enter / Space or the orange button: Confirm. It does exactly what the bottom line says after ✓.
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey ← key. The key has no word on it: the right end of the bottom line always says what it does ("← Leave" in a place, "← End expedition" on the start cell or a lit outpost, "← Mibis", "← close"). In the field it opens the menu: Leave (in a place) or End expedition on top, then Wait, Probe, Cargo, Mibis.
  • C or the teal button: Call. In a place it sends a pulse (one action): hidden finds glint, curious creatures come, shy ones hide, your partner comes. On the map it pins your cell for 1 Energy. On Mibis and a mibi's own screen it calls the mibi with you, which answers..
  • Every step, act, Call or Wait is one tick of the field clock. Doing nothing changes nothing. Facing nothing in a place, ✓ waits one action; Wait shows time passing and says what moved, or "Nothing is moving here".
  • Each place starts under a veil: you see the ground, not what is on it. It lifts 4 tiles around you as you walk, and stays lifted; Call sends a ring that lifts it as it spreads, 7 tiles round (4 in a fog bank); creatures show through it. Nothing puts the veil back.
  • The dots top left are your reach, one per cell: a dot fills once you have found what that place keeps; your cell is framed.
  • Find what a place holds and the whole place shows itself; its mark goes on your map.
  • Walk off a place's edge to step onto the next map cell; ✓ goes down into it.
  • Up to three mibis are with you in the Companion; the others are at home at the Station. The partner joins the Probe: the lead if it is grown, otherwise the first grown mibi with you. While docked, ✓ Take a mibi at home or Leave one at home on Mibis (the Companion is full at three); the Station's Vivarium can ask for a mibi too, applied at the dock.
  • Between expeditions the Companion shows a mibi with you, large. ))) and it answers; ✓ Walk together once per world turn, undocked, with all of them (a moment together, no Data), then ✓ Tend each one once a day (once a minute with Playtest waits on). Three Tends earn the bond; a bonded juvenile grows up with care. The pad looks at the others; ← goes up to Mibis.
  • Bubbles: ! startled, stop or it runs · ? curious, it comes closer · fruit it eats what you carry · … settling, keep still.
What to try
  1. On an expedition the map shows your reach large, with the whole world small in the corner; "Full map" in the menu switches to the whole map (and "Reach view" back).
  2. Pick a glint to start. The dotted square is how far the Probe reaches; the dot grid top left is the same square.
  3. Go down into a place and Call. The answer says which way to walk; when you find what the place keeps, its dot fills.
  4. Call to find Energy: warm stones (a dim amber ring) give +1 each, only a few each world turn; storm-struck stones (a yellow zigzag) give +2 or +3. A Call that finds one says "Energy nearby", the map marks the cell with a bolt (hollow for a warm stone), and the Probe screen counts "Energy in reach".
  5. Shake a bush. The line says who eats the fruit. Press it for Essence, or put it down, back off three tiles and let an eater have it: each creature pays 1 Data for feeding it and 1 for a settle, once a trip, shown on its line before you act, up to 8 a trip (sometimes a pod too). An expedition that fills a dot also brings home 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 Data by the highest tier it entered (meadow and pond edge 2, wood and rock field 6, the cave 8), none if the Probe breaks, when you end the expedition.
  6. When the storm is over you, charge up from struck stones. Count the bolts top right; the arrow beside them is where it heads. Shelter is an overhang, a cave or a lit outpost; trees are not.
  7. Fill every dot in your reach, then head back to the flag (your start) and End expedition from the menu; away from it the menu says how far. Ending the expedition seals the hold (pods and materials) as a crate in the Companion's bay, three at most, and the world turns. The Companion keeps the crates while you set out again at once, Walk or Tend, or dock when you like. With the bay full, the finds stay unsealed in the hold and ride along: a break would lose them. Nothing reaches the Station until you dock: open the Station page, press D to dock, then open the crates in the Station's Bay, and press R for Pods. Lift it again (D) before the next expedition. If the Probe breaks, nothing is sealed: your pods fall where it broke, under a skull on the map, until you come back for them.
  8. Find an outpost (a hut with a mast) and light it for 1 Energy: for three world turns (its flame shrinks each turn) you can end an expedition there, mend the Shield for 1 Energy a bar, and shelter from stray strikes beside it. Relight it when it goes dark. The top bar's "T" counts world turns.
  9. In a fog bank you see 4 tiles: creep close to wary creatures and fill up on dew.
