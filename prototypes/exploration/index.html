Companion Field Test

Exploration on a simulated Companion screen. It shares one save with the Station page in this browser: Head home here, then dock there.

How to play
  • ←↑→↓ or WASD, or the pad: move. Tap to creep one step; hold to walk. Creeping never startles a creature unless you step right next to it.
  • Enter / Space or the orange button: Confirm. It does exactly what the bottom line says after ✓.
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey ← key. The key has no word on it: the right end of the bottom line always says what it does ("← Leave" in a place, "← Head home" on the start cell or a lit outpost, "← Mibis", "← close"). In the field it opens the menu: Leave (in a place) or Head home on top, then Wait, Probe, Cargo, Mibis.
  • C or the teal button: Call. In a place it sends a pulse (one action): hidden finds glint, curious creatures come, shy ones hide, your partner comes. On the map it pins your cell for 1 Energy. At home it calls the mibi with you, which answers..
  • Every step, act, Call or Wait is one tick of the field clock. Doing nothing changes nothing. Facing nothing in a place, ✓ waits one action; Wait shows time passing and says what moved, or "Nothing is moving here".
  • Each place starts under a veil: you see the ground, not what is on it. It lifts 4 tiles around you as you walk, and stays lifted; Call sends a ring that lifts it as it spreads, 7 tiles round (4 in a fog bank); creatures show through it. A quarter counts as surveyed once every tile in it is unveiled; the overview shows 75% as a lighter "almost".
  • The dots top left are your reach, one per cell: a dot fills once you have been in that place and Called there; your cell is framed. Fill them all and everything in reach is explored. The map's quarters show how fully each place is surveyed.
  • Walk off a place's edge to step onto the next map cell; ✓ goes down into it.
  • One mibi is with you in the Companion. Grown, it is your Probe partner; a juvenile comes along in the Companion but is too young for the Probe. The others live at the Station: choose who goes with you in its Habitat (or, while docked, ✓ on Mibis here).
  • Between expeditions the Companion shows the mibi with you, large. ))) and it answers; ✓ Walk with it once per world turn (a moment together, undocked; its Data is part of the expedition's field yield), then ✓ spends time with it; the pad looks at the others; ← goes up to Mibis.
  • Bubbles: ! startled, stop or it runs · ? curious, it comes closer · fruit it eats what you carry · … settling, keep still.
What to try
  1. On an expedition the map shows your reach large, with the whole world small in the corner; "Full map" in the menu switches to the whole map (and "Reach view" back).
  2. Pick a glint to start. The dotted square is how far the Probe reaches; the dot grid top left is the same square.
  3. Go down into a place and Call in each quarter. Watch the overview: its quarters lose their dots as you survey them.
  4. Call to find Energy: warm stones (a dim amber ring) give +1 each, only a few each world turn; storm-struck stones (a yellow zigzag) give +2 or +3. A Call that finds one says "Energy nearby", the map marks the cell with a bolt (hollow for a warm stone), and the Probe screen counts "Energy in reach".
  5. Shake a bush. The line says who eats the fruit. Press it for Essence, or put it down, back off three tiles and let an eater have it for Data. A walk brings home 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 Data by the tier of the place (meadow and pond edge 2, wood and rock field 6, the cave 8): moments bring it in early, the rest arrives at Head home.
  6. When the storm is over you, charge up from struck stones. Count the bolts top right; the arrow beside them is where it heads. Shelter is an overhang, a cave or a lit outpost; trees are not.
  7. Fill every dot in your reach, then head back to the flag (your start) and Head home from the menu; away from it the menu says how far. Head home seals the hold (pods and materials) as a crate in the Companion's bay, three at most, and the world turns. Nothing reaches the Station until you dock: open the Station page, press D to dock, and open the bay there. Lift it again (D) before the next expedition. If the Probe breaks, nothing is sealed: your pods fall where it broke, under a skull on the map, until you come back for them.
  8. Find an outpost (a hut with a mast) and light it for 1 Energy: for three world turns (its flame shrinks each turn) you can head home there, mend the Shield for 1 Energy a bar, and shelter from stray strikes beside it. Relight it when it goes dark. The top bar's "T" counts world turns.
  9. In a fog bank you see 4 tiles: creep close to wary creatures and fill up on dew.
