Companion Field Test

Miniature Beasts · exploration rough playable. The screen is drawn as the Companion would draw it: 450×600 pixels, 48 colours. Creatures are tokens, not art.

How to play
  • ←↑→↓ or WASD, or the pad: move. Tap to creep one step; hold to walk. Creeping never startles a creature unless you step right next to it.
  • Enter / Space or the orange button: Confirm. It does exactly what the bottom line says after ✓.
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey ← key. The key has no word on it: the right end of the bottom line always says what it does ("← Leave" in a place, "← Send home" on the start cell or a lit outpost, "← Mibis", "← close"). In the field it opens the menu: Leave (in a place) or Send home on top, then Wait, Probe, Cargo, Mibis.
  • C or the teal button: Call. In a place it sends a pulse (one action): hidden finds glint, curious creatures come, shy ones hide, your partner comes. On the map it pins your cell for 1 Energy. At home it calls the mibi with you, which answers..
  • Every step, act, Call or Wait is one tick of the field clock. Doing nothing changes nothing. Facing nothing in a place, ✓ waits one action; Wait shows time passing and says what moved, or "Nothing is moving here".
  • Each place starts under a veil: you see the ground, not what is on it. It lifts 4 tiles around you as you walk, and stays lifted; Call sends a ring that lifts it as it spreads, 7 tiles round (4 in a fog bank); creatures show through it. A quarter counts as surveyed once every tile in it is unveiled; the overview shows 75% as a lighter "almost".
  • The dots top left are your reach, one per cell: a dot fills once you have been in that place and Called there; your cell is framed. Fill them all and everything in reach is explored. The map's quarters show how fully each place is surveyed.
  • Walk off a place's edge to step onto the next map cell; ✓ goes down into it.
  • One mibi is with you in the Companion. Grown, it is your Probe partner; a juvenile comes along in the Companion but is too young for the Probe. On Mibis (in the menu), ✓ on another mibi takes it instead: one press.
  • After the Station, the Companion shows the mibi with you, large. ))) and it answers; ✓ spends time with it; the pad looks at the others; ← goes up to Mibis.
  • Bubbles: ! startled, stop or it runs · ? curious, it comes closer · fruit it eats what you carry · … settling, keep still.
What to try
  1. Pick a glint to start. The dotted square is how far the Probe reaches; the dot grid top left is the same square.
  2. Go down into a place and Call in each quarter. Watch the overview: its quarters lose their dots as you survey them.
  3. Call to find Energy: warm stones (a dim amber ring) give +1 each, only a few each world turn; storm-struck stones (a yellow zigzag) give +2 or +3. A Call that finds one says "Energy nearby", the map marks the cell with a bolt (hollow for a warm stone), and the Probe screen counts "Energy in reach".
  4. Shake a bush. The line says who eats the fruit. Press it for Essence, or put it down, back off three tiles and let an eater have it for Data.
  5. When the storm is over you, charge up from struck stones. Count the bolts top right; the arrow beside them is where it heads. Trees, overhangs and woods are shelter.
  6. Fill every dot in your reach, then head back to the flag (your start) and Send home from the menu; away from it the menu says how far. The Station spends what you bring. If the Probe breaks, nothing goes home: your pods fall where it broke, under a skull on the map, until you come back for them.
  7. Find an outpost (a hut with a mast) and light it for 1 Energy: from then on you can send home there, mend the Shield for 1 Energy a bar, and shelter from stray strikes beside it.
  8. In a fog bank you see 4 tiles: creep close to wary creatures and fill up on dew.
