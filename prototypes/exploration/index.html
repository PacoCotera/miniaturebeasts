Miniature Beasts · exploration rough playable. Creatures are tokens, not art.

How to play
  • ←↑→↓ or WASD, or the pad: move. Tap to creep one step; hold to walk.
  • Enter / Space or the orange button: Confirm. It does what the bottom line says.
  • Esc / Backspace or the grey button: Back, one level out. On the map it opens the mode list.
  • Every step or act is one tick of the field clock. Standing still changes nothing.
What to try
  1. Pick one of the glints to start. Walk toward a sign that interests you.
  2. Go down into a place. Shake a bush, carry fruit to a creature, back off and watch.
  3. Stay near a stone while the storm is overhead. Watch the glowing tile.
  4. Notice where creatures go when it rains, and what happens there.
  5. Find the narrow burrow under the cliff.
  6. Send your cargo home, then come back and look for what changed.
