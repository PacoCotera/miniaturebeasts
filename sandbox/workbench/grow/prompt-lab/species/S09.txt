Species notes: the Belatz, a big bird of open sky.
Colour placement: the body colour on the chest, the hind body, the neck, the head, the crest and the tail; the second colour on the wings and the legs. The crest stays the body colour.
Surface in the drawing: bright, layered feathers: rows of small rounded feather clusters on the breast and back, each row edged with one lighter step, a vivid lit side and a deeper shadow side, never grey; the wings and legs in a clean second colour.
Surface in the painting: bright, layered feathers, vivid in the light and deepening to a rich, darker tone of the body colour in shadow, never grey: small rounded contour feathers over the breast and back, soft down at the throat, a soft sheen along the wing coverts; the wings are second-colour feathers with lighter edges; the legs are smooth scale; the beak is smooth and horn-like.
Parts: the wings are folded fans of feathers, not boards: rounded coverts at the shoulder, long flight feathers stepping out toward the tip, a soft scalloped trailing edge, all inside the drawn wing. The crest is a small plume of pointed feathers. The beak is short, curved and smooth. The legs are slender bird legs with a feathered thigh, a visible knee and a thin scaled shin; the feet are bird feet, three spread toes and a small back toe gripping the ground inside the drawn foot, never blocks or wedges. The tail is a fan of long overlapping feathers.
Posture: it stands tall and upright, chest out, its weight on both feet, its head high and alert.
Character: watchful and lordly, a big bird of the open sky that is still a pet.
