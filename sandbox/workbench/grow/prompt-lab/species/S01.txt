Species notes: the Loika, a round frog-hare.
Colour placement: charcoal on the body, the head and the crest; cream on the belly, the snout and the legs.
Surface in the drawing: a soft charcoal coat, short and velvety, drawn as two or three broad charcoal clusters with one cool lit rim along the back, over a warm cream belly, snout and lower face that stay clean and bright.
Surface in the painting: soft charcoal fur, short and dense like velvet, with a gentle sheen across the back and the top of the head, over a warm cream lower face, snout and belly; the cream is smooth and slightly glowing where the light reaches it; the legs are cream and soft like the belly.
Parts: the body is one round, wide mass like a ripe fruit, with the head fused to it and no neck. The snout is short, cream and rounded, with a tiny curved mouth. The crest is three broad leaves with a soft midrib, standing up from the top of the head; they are leaves, not ears. The four legs are short, plump and padded, ending in rounded feet with small toe bumps.
Posture: it sits square and low on all four feet, its weight settled into its haunches, its head up and a little forward.
Character: gentle and curious, a little shy; a pet you want to pick up.
