Species notes: the Loika, a round frog-hare. Only what differs from the house rendering.
Colour placement: charcoal on the body, the head and the upper face; cream on the belly, the muzzle, the lower face and the legs; the crest leaves are green.
Eyes: the house eye, with an orange iris, inside the cream ring.
Material: smooth skin, matte like suede, no hairs. Its touches: a soft sheen across the back and the crown; the cream belly and muzzle a shade warmer where the light reaches; a soft midrib on each leaf.
Volume: the body is one round mass like a ripe fruit, its back and crown the highest lit form; the head is fused to it with no neck.
Parts: the muzzle is a soft, full-cheeked cream pad that blends into the lower face, with a tiny curved smile; never a bill or a beak. The crest is three broad leaves sprouting together from the crown and fanning outward; leaves, not ears. The four legs are short, plump and padded, ending in rounded feet with small toe bumps.
Posture: it sits square and low on all four feet, its weight in its haunches, its head up and a little forward.
Character: gentle and curious, a little shy; a pet you want to pick up.
