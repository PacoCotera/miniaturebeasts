Species notes: the Belatz, a big bird of open sky. Only what differs from the house rendering.
Colour placement: the body colour on the chest, the hind body, the neck, the head, the crest and the tail; the second colour on the wings and the legs. The crest stays the body colour.
Eyes: the house eye, with a warm amber iris.
Material: feathers in layered groups. The breast, the back, each folded wing and the tail each read as one group, shaded as one form, never feather by feather. Its touches: a lighter scalloped edge along the lower margin of each group; soft down at the throat; a soft sheen along the wing coverts. The beak is smooth horn; the legs are smooth scale.
Volume: the breast is the fullest form and takes the highlight; the head is round; the shadow steps gather under the breast and the tail.
Parts: the wings lie folded along the body exactly as drawn and are never re-laid, spread or raised; inside the drawn wing, rounded coverts at the shoulder and long flight feathers stepping toward the tip. The crest is a small plume of pointed feathers. The beak is short, curved and smooth. The legs are slender bird legs with a feathered thigh, a visible knee and a thin scaled shin; the feet are three spread toes and a small back toe gripping the ground inside the drawn foot. The tail is a fan of long overlapping feathers. The head keeps its drawn size; never enlarged into a chick's.
Posture: it stands tall and upright, chest out, its weight on both feet, its head high and alert.
Character: watchful and lordly, a big bird of the open sky that is still a pet.
