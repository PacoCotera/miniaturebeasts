Species notes: the Loika, a round frog-hare.
Colour placement: charcoal on the body, the head and the upper face; cream on the belly, the muzzle, the lower face and the legs; the crest leaves are green.
Eyes: a large amber-orange iris around a dark pupil, inside the cream ring.
Surface in the drawing: smooth charcoal skin drawn as two or three broad charcoal clusters with one cool lit rim along the back, over a warm cream belly, muzzle and lower face that stay clean and bright.
Surface in the painting: smooth, soft charcoal skin like suede or the bloom on a plum, matte, with a gentle sheen across the back and the top of the head and no visible hairs; the cream belly, muzzle and legs are smooth and slightly glowing where the light reaches them.
Parts: the body is one round, wide mass like a ripe fruit, with the head fused to it and no neck. The muzzle is a soft, full-cheeked cream pad that blends into the lower face, with a tiny curved smile; never a bill or a beak. The crest is three broad, glossy leaves with a soft midrib, sprouting together from the crown and fanning outward; they are leaves, not ears. The four legs are short, plump and padded, ending in rounded feet with small toe bumps.
Posture: it sits square and low on all four feet, its weight settled into its haunches, its head up and a little forward.
Character: gentle and curious, a little shy; a pet you want to pick up.
