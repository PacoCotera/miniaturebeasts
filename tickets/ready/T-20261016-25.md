## Owner verdict (2026-10-16 10:45–10:47, verbatim)
"you're basically decorating a screen and placing an egg in it. terrible." With these points: "tabs make no sense in the incubator"; "the retro mid-century industrial design is not the style for Miniature Beasts"; "there's a lot of wasted space in the layout"; "the egg should be an egg that we collected, not a generic egg"; "the lamp is out of proportion and clipped, and casts no light"; "the fixtures in the walls make no sense"; "the gauges make no sense"; "there's no sense of progress or wait time other than the little leafs, which are tiny and clash with the style"; "it's a screen and experience poorly designed". Screenshot: results/owner/2026-10-16-incubator-tabs.png.

## Goal
Like the Create concept (T-20261015-02), start from purpose: what the player does on the Incubator, what changes because of it, and what the player cares about watching. Every object on screen must exist because of that, or it goes.

## Inputs
- The current screen: station-gate 69ea1d23 `prototypes/face/golden/incubator/*` (what not to do).
- The game rules and economy on station-gate (design/ docs marked current), and how a pod arrives from the Cargo haul (the egg is the one the player collected: its own look and data).
- The Create concept (specs/create-concept.md, when T-20261015-02 is done): the two screens are one flow, Create then Incubator.
- The owner's accepted screens (Home, Pods, Habitat, Vivarium, Idle) for the style; specs/gate-recalibration.md for the references-first gate.
- Device limits: design/proposals/technical-architecture.md (LVGL face, 1024x600, knob and keys).

## Steps
1. One page: the player's purpose here, their actions (knob, keys) and what each does, what they watch change over time (progress and wait time must read at a glance, at the screen's scale and in its style), and when they leave.
2. Two or three distinct options for the owner, one paragraph and one rough 1x wireframe (SVG) each, with a recommendation.
3. For the chosen option: the objects and why each exists, the style note for the art director (not retro-industrial: say what instead, from the accepted screens), and the needs list for art, UI/UX and build.

Art production, layout specs, code.
