## Purpose
The pod is the player's own and the subject of the screen, so it must be drawn crisply at its size, not upscaled. The just-cleared card is the reveal the player comes back for, and it shows the trait as a picture. Both pieces are owed before the Incubator can be judged whole (`results/review-incubator-b-art.md` §4; gaps 1 and 2 of the T-20261016-27 README).
## Style
`design/art-direction.md` ("Pods come from one renderer with species parameters"), `design/style-guide/README.md`, nothing added.
## Inputs
- `specs/station-incubator-tabs-ruling.md` §2 (pod 400, 144, 224, 272) and §5 (the card's picture, (24, 44) 160 × 128, `trait-{species}-{trait}-{look}-160x128`, 1:1).
- Pods' renderer classes: station-gate `prototypes/ui/specs/station/pods.json` `classes.pod` (collection 88 × 112, medium 120 × 152, large 144 × 176) and `podLayers.compose`. The pod masters are the signed `pod-large` layers.
- The trait crop scheme: station-gate `prototypes/station/src/views/pods-props.mjs` :145–150. The trait paintings are in `source/raw`.
## Steps
1. Make a 224 × 272 pod class: the layer set (shade, relief, body, accent, pattern, band) for every species the renderer draws, made at that size, not scaled up. Register it, and name the class key for `pods.json` (the build adds it). Re-run `make-shell-light.py` on its silhouette and seams, so the light overlays (T-20261016-62) fit it exactly.
2. Cut `trait-{species}-{trait}-{look}-160x128` for every look that the card can show, 1:1 from the trait paintings, and list them.
3. Proof: S01 and S03 at 224 × 272 beside today's upscaled stand-in; a sheet of the crops.
