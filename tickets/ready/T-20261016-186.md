## Purpose
Home is the one screen the owner has accepted, and the target picture in the art direction. Challenge it honestly before it becomes the template for everything else.
## How
1. Each of the game designer, the art director, UI/UX and the genome engineer writes their own critique first, from their own craft, without reading the others. Use your judgement on structure and depth; the owner's three questions are the starting point, not a template.
2. The creative director then synthesises: where you agree, where you disagree and why, and a short ranked list of proposals. It gives what Home does for each motivation, and for the person moving between them.
3. Use the device sheet. Judge Home among the screens it leads to.
