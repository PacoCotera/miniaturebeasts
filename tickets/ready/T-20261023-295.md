- **The hold mark (the architect's ruling, named as a kit component per L5):**
  - `void kit_focusRingHold(const char *id, const int box[4])`. It reads frame.json `focus.ring.hold { "bands": [{ "w": 1, "role": "core" }], "gap": 4 }` (spec-only addition in `focus`).
  - It draws one composed node `<id>` at box + gap: 1 px `focus`, with no key bands. The region is the caller's, the layer LAYER_CHROME, and it is drawn before the ring. It is not a focus target.
  - Its rule-8 header; face_test case.
  - The test log marks it `hold`; the checks allow one ring and at most one hold per frame.
- **One compare computation in the logic process** gives the fact, the block light and the path:
  - It compares `traitState(...).shows`, trait by trait, on traits read on both. Whole words count ("charcoal and ash" is not "ash"); heritable traits only; never Glow, Basking or Phase.
  - It never reads the stamps' patterns.
  - A bud is never compared.
- **The fact words** (lower-case trait names from the catalogue, in frame order, joined with commas and "and"):
  - "same {trait list}" (e.g. "same colour", "same colour and eyes", "same colour, markings and eyes");
  - four or more: the first three, then "and more", while the sentence fits 2 lines of 208;
  - "no read look in common" (read on both, none matches);
  - "nothing read on both yet".
  - Never: "got it from", "same genes", "would breed alike", "inherited", "passed down", "genes", "alike", "match", "chance", "odds", or anything about the stamps' patterns.
- **With the ring on the held mibi nothing is compared:** the fact band, the block light and the path are all zero-size.
