## Goal
Purpose: Level 2 answers "what do I know of this one?". Its sheet is one generated picture per species and state: the form, the chapter plates, the colours found and every look. This ticket builds only the logic-process side: the pictures, the facts the face needs to place targets and glints, the cache and the timings. No face file and no golden change here. T-20261023-292 draws the screen.

The layout replaces the earlier four-corner frames for every species, the Loika included; the owner has seen the plates and approved them. Size: about 6 h. Golden-neutral: no face change and no golden retaken.

**Common to every part:**
- Tokens: libGround #211b1a, libPanel #342c29, libHead #3a312c, libWell #1a1514; bone, fog, frostS from the palette.
- **Surfaces are bezel pixels.** Every plate, band and page is a kit_bezel to the pixel:
  - radius 6 by prim/ring.c: pixel (i,j) is inside when `ring_in_round(2i+1, 2j+1, 0, 0, w, h, 6)`;
  - fill libPanel, no edge, no bevel line;
  - top band: libHead on rows 1…b across the inside of the outline (`ring_in_round(…, 1, 1, w−1, h−1, 5)`), then one libWell rule row at b+1. b = 25 for plates and the page, 21 for the two bands;
  - flat and opaque: no shadow, gradient, grain or bloom.
- **Order inside the sheet picture:**
  1. the ground master's own pixels for the rect (flat libGround until `library-ground-1024x522` is placed);
  2. the form (drawForm with finish; bloom, halation and grain belong to the form and the ground only);
  3. the surfaces;
  4. the ruler arcs and ticks (0.6 px, bone 30 %);
  5. the leaders and dots;
  6. type, mosaic cells and swatches.
- **Type:**
  - Inter Regular-tnum and Medium-tnum only, grayscale AA, baselines on whole pixels, drawn after the finish;
  - sentence case as the frames write the words; no italic, no mono, no uppercase transform; floor 12 px;
  - a two-colour look prints in the comma form, `${a}, ${b}` (genome.mjs pairSep). A look whose name holds "and" as a word stays verbatim.
- **Lit variant:**
  - every non-lit chapter's content is drawn at 30 %: its plate type, its mosaic cells, the colours band when Coat is not lit, and its leader, dot, ruler arc and ticks;
  - on a flat surface the 30 % is pre-mixed and opaque, out = round(0.3·ink + 0.7·surface); over the ground it is alpha 0.3;

**Leaders** (plate to sector):
- **The dot:** on radius 164 from (348,286), r 1.8, `bone` 90 %.
- **The line:** 1 px `bone` 42 %, orthogonal only, on pixel centres with square caps. The last segment is horizontal and ends on x 172 (left plates) or 523 (right plates) at plate y + 35.
- **The route.** μ is the sector's centre and h its half-span, (2π/n − 0.2)/2.
  1. Straight: take the point on radius 164 at the anchor's height, on the plate's side. If it lies within 0.75·h of μ, the dot goes there and the leader is one horizontal segment.
  2. Otherwise the dot moves along its arc: φ = μ + clamp(angle(A) − μ, ±h/2).
     - L route: if going from D to the anchor's y moves away from the midline y 286, the leader runs vertical from D, then horizontal into the plate.
     - Z route: otherwise it runs horizontal outward from D to x_E = 348 ∓ ceil(√(168² − (y_A − 286)²)), then vertical to y_A, then horizontal in.
- Expected routes in slot-table order:
  - n 4: L L L Z;
  - n 5: I L L I I;
  - n 6: I L L I I I;
  - n 7: I L L I Z Z I;
  - n 8: L L L I I I Z Z.
- Every leader pixel is ≥ 164 from the centre. Across the 16 species: 0 crossings, and 0 leaders through another plate.
