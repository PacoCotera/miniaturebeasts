## Why
`goScreen` (station/src/game.mjs) sets `st.libSeen` by `S.libOpened(G.st)` when the Library opens full screen, but writes no save: the Home ✓ on the Library bezel goes by intents/home.mjs `toLibrary`, which does not call `h.save()`. The Station has no autosave, so a reload before the next save brings the looks back as new (glints and "{n} new" on Home).
## The change
Save after `libOpened` (the `save()` in game.mjs), or in the intents that open the Library; add a test that the opened state is in the save (`libSeen` equals the guide after the open and a save round trip).
