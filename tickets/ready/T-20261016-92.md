## Purpose
The Caddy is the dock: when the Companion is seated or lifted, its e-paper says so for hours ("Companion out · with Moss"). That is the third device in the dock moment. There is no Caddy view to show it on the play page (`prototypes/caddy` is the painting server), so its lines and icons have nowhere to go.

## Goal
A 792 × 272 four-gray Caddy view on the play page that reads `specs/station-dock-arrival-spec.md` §5's two lines per state and shows the crate and Companion icons, with e-paper behaviour (a partial refresh per change, no animation).

## Inputs (exact commit ids, file paths; everything the worker needs is here or in the repo)
- `specs/station-dock-arrival-spec.md` §5 and its amendments; `specs/dock-moments.md` §4 (the Caddy's limits).
- `design/devices.md` (the Caddy's screen); `prototypes/play/` (the frames).

## Steps
1. The view, beside the Station and Companion frames:
   - its text style (the Caddy's own; rule it if there is none, at 4 grays);
   - two lines, each ≤ 744 px with 24 px margins;
   - a slot for the icons (size ruled here; the art follows).
2. Drive it from the Dock control's `seated` and `lifted` events (T-58) and the bay's opening end.
3. Tests: each state's two lines; one refresh per change.
4. Write the art ask (the crate and Companion icons at the ruled size) into the result for the lead.
5. Run the suites and the public guard. Push the batch branch. Write `results/T-20261016-92.md` on `work-queue`.
