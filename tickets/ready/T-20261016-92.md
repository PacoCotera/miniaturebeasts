## Purpose
The Caddy is the dock: when the Companion is seated or lifted, its e-paper says so for hours ("Companion out · with Moss"). That is the third device in the dock moment. There is no Caddy view to show it on the play page (`prototypes/caddy` is the painting server), so its lines and icons have nowhere to go.

## Goal
A 792 × 272 four-gray Caddy view on the play page that reads `specs/station-dock-arrival-spec.md` §5's two lines per state and shows the crate and Companion icons, with e-paper behaviour (a partial refresh per change, no animation).

## Amendment (T-20261016-109, UI/UX): where the Caddy view sits

**Ruling: option (c), a sheet raised from the cradle.** The Caddy *is* the cradle, so its screen rises from it.
- A fourth tab (b) would hide the Station and the Companion at the one moment the owner wants to see all three react together.
- Moving the shelf (a) would break the journey's pinned rects.

**The sheet (wide tier with the bench shelf, `hasShelf()`, so W ≥ 1878 and H ≥ 1072; 1920 × 1080 is the owner's case):**
- **Outer rect:** **734, 760, 808, 288** in lp. That is an 8 lp bezel in the Caddy's body colour (the shell's sage, the cradle's colour), radius 8.
- **The view:** **742, 768, 792, 272**, at 1 lp per view pixel (`canvasPainter` on a canvas placed there).
- **Fit:** the sheet's right edge (1542) is 8 past the Companion shell's (1534) and inside the row (1558). It sits over the right part of the bench shelf (12, 760, 1522, 308) and ends at 1048, inside it.
- **While raised:** the shelf under it takes no pointer input. Nothing is moved, so every pinned rect stays.

**When it shows:**
- **Automatically:** at each `mb-caddy` change of `docked` (seated or lifted), and at the end of the bay's opening (the "crates home" line). It rises over 160 ms with the seat's ease (a cut under reduced motion), stays 6000 ms, and lowers the same way. Each new line restarts the 6000 ms.
- **Pinned:** a click on the cradle, or the new page key **Shift+C** (caught like the others, never while typing), raises it and keeps it up until the next click or Shift+C. `paper.show(caddy)` updates it while it is up.
- **Hidden by default.** Add a row to the keys panel: "Shift+C · the Caddy's screen".

**Every other tier** (wide without the shelf, standard, short, narrow) has no room for 792 × 272 beside or under the devices. There the bar's note (`note`) shows the Caddy's two lines as one ("Caddy: Companion home · two crates") for 4000 ms at the same events. Shift+C does nothing.

**The journey test (`prototypes/play/tools/journey.mjs`):**
- Every existing pin stays as it is.
- After the journey's dock at 1920 × 1080: the sheet is visible at [734, 760, 808, 288] and the view at [742, 768, 792, 272], and its lines read the docked state.
- At +6000 ms (the test clock, or a wait of 6.2 s) it is hidden.
- Shift+C pins it, and Shift+C again lowers it.
- In the standard tier (1440 × 900): there is no sheet, and the note shows the Caddy line after a dock.
- The play page's screenshots that include the shelf retake only the frames taken in the 6 s after a dock or lift; list them.

The view, the state and the captures you built stand. Mount them per the above (about 20 lines in `shell.mjs`, plus the key and the journey asserts).
