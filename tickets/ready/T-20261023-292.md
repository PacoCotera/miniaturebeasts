## Goal
Purpose: Level 2 answers "what do I know of this one, and what has it given me?". The player reads the sheet, lights one chapter to see its looks and who carries each, changes the wish, visits the species' face, and goes on to a family. This ticket builds the screen `library.species` on T-291's pictures, with its props, keys and words, and narrows "seen" to the species whose sheet was shown.

Size: about 5 h. Goldens: takes the Level 2 goldens below. Golden-neutral for every other screen, Level 1 included.
