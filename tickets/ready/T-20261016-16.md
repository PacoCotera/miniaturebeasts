## Steps
Ruling: `specs/station-stamp-plate-ruling.md` (T-20261016-18). The plate is exempt from the slot's look. It is placed 1:1, and only the stamp glyph takes K 0.20 x WARM.
1. Paint a plate master `stamp-plate-120x120` (120 x 120 at 804, 232) at the machinery's own tones, with no pre-compensation for any look. Give it the same light (key top left), material and edge as the R machinery around it.
   - Make a recess at 8..112 in the plate for the stamp glyph, which code draws centred at up to 104 x 104 (`habitat.json` :194, `incubator-props.mjs` :64-66).
   - The plate paints its own edge; code adds no hairline.
   - Fill the corners opaque (alpha 255 everywhere); the kit's rounded corners leave alpha holes.
   - The code text stays below the plate at (776, 357, 176, 25), and the plate does not hold it.
   - Register the master on masters-pods under the id `stamp-plate-120x120`. The build ticket T-20261016-17 places it.
2. Write a 1x proof: the growing capture with the plate composed 1:1 at (804, 232), NO multiply on the plate, and the glyph drawn on top with its K 0.20 x WARM look (lift the glyph from the capture, as in your compare image).
3. Measure on the proof, over the plate rect (804, 232, 120, 120), the p95 and mean relative luminance. The p95 must be <= 0.34 (ruling section 3.1) and <= the chamber value minus 0.08 (0.574 - 0.08 = 0.494 on today's numbers). If the p95 is over 0.34, darken the paint, not the light direction.
