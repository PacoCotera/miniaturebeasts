"yes, wirh one caveat, the way you are indicating release comes across as negative, a detrimental action. that is not the case. those are options with different outcomes. you most avoid positioning something as negative, unless it indeed is. / also the panel relies too much on text"

## What stands
The two-window pattern is approved for every decision panel: Return, Grow, Fit the upgrade, Grow it now, and the dock arrival. Kept as is:
- the slate pane;
- two windows painting two outcomes;
- Grow's setting, scale and light contrast.

## What the ruling breaks (`results/T-20261016-170/`)
- **Return reads as a loss.**
  - It carries a coral "Gone for good" with a ✕.
  - Its window is cold, empty and small, at dusk: the picture is a farewell.
  - Keep is "the safe option".

  The truth is two outcomes. Kept, Pip lives with you. Returned, Pip goes home to the wild, its place remembers it, and the player gains ◆ +2. Neither is a penalty.
- **Grow's line, "The pod is used.",** is the same loss framing, and it repeats the cost icons beside it.
- **Too much text.** Each window has a title, a verb and a sentence, and the bottom line has a context word on top. The pictures already say most of it.

## Owners
- **Art director, lead:** repaint the Return window as a good outcome in its own right. Pip home in the wild, thriving and in its own light. It differs from Keep by place, not by warmth or loss. Propose how the icons and pictures carry what the sentences carry now. Nothing coral or ✕ unless it is truly detrimental.
- **UI/UX:**
  - Cut the words to the minimum: the verb, the cost or gain icons, and no sentence a picture already says.
  - Decide whether the title question stays.
  - Propose the default focus and how it is shown without marking either option as "safe". Guarding an irreversible press is fair; styling one option as the danger is not.
- **Game designer:** the verbs and the one fact (if any) that no picture can carry. Check that every outcome is stated truthfully: irreversible is a fact, not a threat.

## Deliver
- **Round 1:** the Return and Grow pair as 1× PNGs, in the device sheet's decision row, with the words covered and uncovered.
- **Round 2,** after both gates pass the pair: Fit the upgrade, Grow it now and the dock arrival, in the same language (`specs/station-decision-panel-spec.md`, `specs/station-dock-arrival-spec.md`).

## Gate
Both gates, in the device sheet:
- With the words covered, each panel reads as two different futures in two seconds, and neither reads as the bad one unless it is.
- With the words shown, there is no more text than the pictures need.
