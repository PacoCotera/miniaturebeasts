## Steps
1. List the objects from the concept. For each, write its one-line job as a player would read it. Anything without a job is not drawn.
2. Repaint the stage and the chamber masters so that they keep the ids where the object stays and retire the ids where it goes. Name every id added and removed. Forms, materials and palette follow the ruling's section 4:
   - moulded and rounded, matte polymer, rubber, canvas or braided hose only where it feeds something;
   - a living or soft nest;
   - sage and sand housing on the device's dark matte slate;
   - no rivets, louvres, enamel, chrome, brushed metal, needle gauges or wall hoses from nowhere.
3. The lamp, if the concept has one, must:
   - be in scale (its head at most about a third of the chamber's width);
   - sit wholly inside the frame;
   - show its light landing on the pod and the nest.
4. Growth on the collected pod. The chamber shows the bud's species shell, the same picture as Pods and Cargo, which the build draws (T-20261016-28). Paint the overlays that work on any shell:
   - the warm glow at each stage (early, late, ready);
   - the two crack frames;
   - the nest's front over the shell's foot.
   Give the overlay ids and their rects relative to the shell.
5. Write 1x proofs of empty, growing, ready and hatch-1100, composed with two different species' shells. Lay each beside Pods, Vivarium and hero-kit in one sheet. Measure:
   - the chamber value against every zone (brightness ruling section 2);
   - the stamp plate p95 (stamp ruling section 3).
