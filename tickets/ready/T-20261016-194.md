## Purpose
The game designer's call (A), 2026-10-17: a Tuikis's claws are how it digs, so new Tuikis carry only round feet, padded feet or digging wedges. Today S03's pool holds all six foot forms, and most pods carry hoof, webbed or root. T-20261016-193 gave the old forms words ("hooves", "webbed feet", "roots"). This ticket narrows the pool.
## Conditions (agreed with the game designer)
- A new S03 frame version. New pods draw only rounded, pad and wedge.
- The old forms stay legal for inheritance (a legacy allowance on `anatomy.contact-terminal-form` for S03). Version 3 Tuikis and their lines keep validating and breeding.
- Unopened pods keep the frame version they were found under. Nothing changes under the player, read or not.
- The old forms fade only through breeding. A bred child that shows an old form is described plainly ("hooves"), never as an error.
## Steps
1. Framework: frame versions per species, or a global bump if that is the registry's rule. The pool and a legacy allowance in the S03 frame. `checkGenome` and `cross` accept a legacy copy. `podGenome` samples only the pool. The stamp registry is appended, never rewritten.
2. Station (with a builder, or by the lead's exception):
   - a pod records its frame version and is sampled from it;
   - the genome tree reads the genome's own version (the T-71 note).
3. Tests: version 3 S03 mibis validate and cross under the new frame; new pods carry only the three; a saved pod's genome is unchanged across the bump; the guide counts three for S03 Claws, and "S03:feet" comes off `FRAME_GAPS`.
