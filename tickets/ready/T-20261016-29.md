## Purpose
The Incubator is where the player watches a bud grow and opens it when it is ready. The chamber and the bud are what grows. The leaves say how much of the wait is left, the plaque gives the state across a table, and the stamp shows the bud's chapters clearing (how much is still a surprise). The chapter tabs have no job here: choosing and reading chapters happen on Create and Pods. The owner said so on 2026-10-16 ("tabs make no sense in the incubator"). This ticket removes an object, and adds none.
## Goal
The Incubator draws no chapter rail in any state. The band under the top bar is the bench, as in the empty state, and a founder's chapters clear on the stamp label only. Pods, Create and Cross keep the rail unchanged.
