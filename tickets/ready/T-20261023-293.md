**The model (logic process; the layout never reflows):**
- Founders are pods in row 0. A mibi's row is its lineDepth: a child sits one row below its deeper parent, with both links drawn.
- Families joined by a cross form one block, placed by its earliest founder; blocks go in founding order. Parents go in the order the save records them (never labelled).
- A shed-pod child has one link to its single parent, in the same block. A bud is a pod in its generation. Children of released mibis are normal nodes under a faded parent.
- No parent is ever guessed and no kinship number appears.
- Each node is sent as `{ id, kind (mibi | pod | bud), row, u (in half-sibling units), released }`, with the link segments in grid units.
- CLOSE grid → px (used by T-294): x = 16 + u·48 − cam.x, y = 44 + row·120 − cam.y. Siblings are 96 apart.
- Focus targets are the mibis and the bud. Pods are never targets.
- Tests:
  - two founders and their children;
  - a joined block;
  - a child of parents on different rows;
  - a shed-pod child;
  - a released parent with children;
  - a bud;
  - parent order as saved;
  - the same input giving the same layout after a new birth elsewhere (no reflow of existing nodes).
