## Purpose
The owner tests in the standalone Station and Companion pages and wants to see at a glance how up to date each sandbox and the site are (owner, 2026-10-26).
## Goal
- One always-visible footer line, the same on three pages: website/index.html, prototypes/station (index page) and prototypes/exploration (the standalone Companion):
  "© 2026 Dirty Pawz Press · {short hash} · {dd/mm HH:MM} · save v{schema} · Caddy {online|offline}"
  - {short hash} and the time from the site's build.json (the build's commit and its UTC time shown in Mexico City time, 24-hour, dd/mm), as prototypes/play/src/stamp.mjs already does; "local" when there is no build.json.
  - save v{schema}: the save schema the page writes (the Station's ST_SCHEMA; the Companion's own save version), omitted on the website.
  - Caddy online/offline: whether the Caddy service answers (the Station and Companion already know; omitted on the website).
- One shared module for the text (no copy per page), a unit test for the formatting (UTC to Mexico City, the "local" case, omitted parts).
- Placement: page chrome outside the device frame, in the page's bottom margin, small, muted, never over the game; at phone width it wraps. The Station's existing ?dev-only stamp is replaced by this footer (the Developer tools button stays ?dev only).
- No golden moves (the footer is page chrome, outside the face).
