## Owner direction (2026-10-16 13:23, verbatim)
"interactions must be rich, in the end it's a physical device, for example, when you dock the companion, the station should pop up an arrival screen. that makes it feel like magic, that the companion, caddy and station talk between each other." / "also undocking should have some sort of effect in the station and caddy"

## Purpose
The three devices must feel like one world that talks to itself. Docking is the moment the trip comes home (cargo moves only on docking: design/proposals/technical-architecture.md); undocking is the send-off.

## Inputs
design/devices.md, design/interaction.md, design/architecture.md, design/proposals/technical-architecture.md (what each device can show and sense, and what crosses the dock); specs/station-decision-points.md and the decision panel spec (T-45): the arrival must not become another click-accept. It is a show that then leads into a real choice; specs/station-return-signals.md.

## Steps
1. For dock and for undock, a beat-by-beat script on all three devices at once (Companion, Caddy, Station): what each shows, lights or sounds, in what order and for how long, and what crosses the dock at each beat. Include the cases: a trip with a haul, with a new pod, an empty trip, a mibi coming home or leaving, an undock with an unfinished choice.
2. The Station's arrival screen: what it shows (what came back, who came home), how it hands over to the decision panel or Home, and what the player can skip.
3. Hardware limits per device (what each can do today versus what needs new hardware or firmware), flagged for the architect.
4. One rough wireframe of the Station's arrival screen, rendered to PNG with its label inside the image.

Building, art production.
