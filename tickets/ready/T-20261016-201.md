## Purpose
Today every resident is drawn from its species' stand, so a household of different genomes is pixel-identical. After this, every place that draws a mibi draws it from its own read looks. What a player made in Create and in crossing is visible where the family lives. The owner approved the design (2026-10-17 13:15).

## Style
`design/art-direction.md` on station-gate: the life is painted. Layers are painted cut-outs placed on the stand, never tints.

## Goal
1. **One function, `drawnLooks(frame, mibi)`, used everywhere a mibi is drawn** (Home, the Vivarium, the Habitat, Cross, the Library). Read chapters draw the expressed look. Unread chapters draw the species' most common look on pods. Doings draw nothing on a stand. Hidden copies never show.
2. **A layered stand:** a base plus one layer per locus that a stand can show, chosen by `drawnLooks`. It is generic over loci, so new loci (T-20261016-200's Colour and coat marks) plug in without code changes.
3. **Until the masters land,** the layers are the proof cut-outs, listed in the placeholder register as stand-ins.
