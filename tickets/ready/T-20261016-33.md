## Purpose
The Probe bench is where the player gets the Probe ready for the next trip. The plates are how many strikes it can take out there: the second thing to read after the Probe. The gauge, the wrench, the screwdriver and the cable have no job, so they go. The plates get the space at a readable size. This ticket adds no object; it removes four and redraws two at a new size.

The bench stage without the field tools, and the plates and their strips at the sizes of `specs/station-probe-purpose.md` §4.
