## Purpose
On Create the player edits their founder's genome by turning drums; the owner wants it to feel like "a research/crafting bench. advanced, you're editing life", the one place they glimpse "the underling genome mechanics and framework". The bench's main screen shows the whole genome live: the species' frame, every gene's kind, which copy shows and which hides, what the player changed, what clashes, what is still a surprise. On the Incubator the same instrument, compact, shows the surprises clearing as the bud grows. It replaces the paper slip the owner rejected. What each object is for: the housing holds the screen in the machine; the slate is where the genome shows; the hub and trunk are the species' frame (locked); emblems name chapters; each gene mark tells its kind and state by shape; the pair inside tells which copy shows; the bay isolates the focused gene; the port holds the pod that feeds the drums.
