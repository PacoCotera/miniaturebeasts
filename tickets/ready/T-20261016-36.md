## Purpose
Pods is where the player gets to know each pod they brought home and picks one to create from.
- **The place badge:** each well shows where the pod was found, so two sealed pods of one species can be told apart, and each reads as the player's own find.
- **The caption:** the leaf plate on the overview returns the pod to the wild. A caption, "back to the {place}", says so before it is focused.
- **The cuts:** the dressing with no job (hose, gauge, vent) goes in art T-20261016-35.
