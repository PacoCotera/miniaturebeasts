## Purpose
A returned mibi's place sheds one pod "of its line" (design/game.md, Mechanics, the vivarium). The game designer and the genome engineer agreed on (b′) on 2026-10-17, with the T-20261016-179 check's open question as the starting point. What the game needs:
- the line is real in the pedigree;
- hidden looks can surface;
- a clone never comes out;
- the pod behaves like any pod.

## Facts (measured on station-gate, 16 frames)
- `cross(frame, m, m)` is refused ("one mibi is not a pair").
- At the true selfing kinship (0.5), the decided penalty AB copies blends exactly and surfaces every hidden copy. A selfed bred mibi equals its parent in 2–100% of draws, by species (S04 100%, S14 68%, S01 28%).
- At kinship 1/8, clones fall to 0–3%, and hidden looks surface in about 40–50% of carried traits.

## Steps
1. In `prototypes/workbench/framework/cross.mjs`, add a self cross: one parent, drawn by a given seeded stream.
   - `SELF_KINSHIP = 1/8`, documented as a game rule, not a genetics claim. The draw uses it; the record keeps the true pedigree.
   - origin `{ kind: "shed", parents: [d, d], parentIds: [id, id] }`, so later crosses with its relatives are penalised from the recorded line.
   - Redraw from the same stream, up to 24 times as `podGenome` does, while the child's loci equal the parent's or the body does not build.
   - No mutation. No self-changing mark carried. Sealed chapters are crossed like any others. Built on the current frame version.
   - Leave `cross(m, m)`'s refusal unchanged.
2. Tests in `prototypes/workbench/tests/framework.test.mjs`, over all 16 frames:
   - never equal to the parent;
   - always builds;
   - deterministic per seed;
   - every copy comes from the parent;
   - pedigree kinship to the parent is at least 1/2 when walked from the record.
3. Not in scope:
   - when the place sheds the pod (the game designer's, still open);
   - the Station or Companion wiring.
