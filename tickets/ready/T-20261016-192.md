## Purpose
The column is five equal boxes: a menu, not the machine working for the life (`results/T-20261016-186.md`; the art director's critique). The owner keeps fixed places, so Home is learnt once, and says the column's look is still lacking.

The column must look like the instrument, in fixed places:
- the box that needs you is lit and brought forward within its box;
- quiet boxes recede;
- each holds the object of the screen it opens, in a well, with its contact shadow;
- there are no plate pictures, and lamps are amber or off.
## Owners

- **UI/UX:** focus, states and the fixed geometry.
- **Game designer:** which state brings a module forward.
