"yes, finetune loikas. / also coat marks can be different between individuals."
## Purpose
Four Loikas should look like four individuals more often. Today S01 has 3 look traits and 12 looks, and four random Loikas all differ 36% of the time. Each mibi is drawn as itself (T-20261016-190), so this is now visible.
## Measured candidates (station-gate frames, 400 pods, 4,000 households, all viable 200/200)
| Option | Open loci | Traits | Looks | Four all distinct |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| today | 5 | 5 | 12 | 36% |
| **R (recommended):** + Colour (charcoal / russet / two side by side) + patches vary in extent and size behind the plain/patches switch | 7 | 6 | 78 | about 70% |
| + Colour only | 6 | 6 | 36 | 68% |
| + Colour, patches on every Loika, extent and size | 7 | 6 | 125 | 92% |

R keeps the plain-vs-patches recessive switch, the design doc's worked example of a hidden copy. The 92% option removes "plain", which is the owner's and the game designer's call. Every option stays within the starter tier, which counts traits (6) for generated frames only; no tier rule changes.
## Steps
1. `roster.mjs` S01: open Colour (`appearance.body-palette`, pool charcoal and russet) and the marking extent and scale as sleeping loci of Markings (pools low and high). Leave every other locked locus as it is.
2. A new S01 frame version. Living version-3 Loikas stay valid: their locked copies (charcoal; extent and scale high) lie inside the new pools. Saved pods keep their stored genomes. The stamp registry is appended.
3. `describe.mjs`: Markings' words cover the patches' extent and size (the game designer's words). The stand ranges for the art director: extent 0.15–0.5 of the coat, patch scale 0.06–0.14, each in three bins.
4. Tests: version-3 genomes validate under the new frame; new pods use the new pools; every look has a word; the four-distinct rate is reported.
## Gate
The game designer (words, and R or the 92% option), then the creative director. The art director gets the mark ranges for the stands.
