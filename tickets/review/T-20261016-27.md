## Steps
1. List the objects from the concept. For each, write its one-line job as a player would read it. Anything without a job is not drawn.
2. Repaint the stage and the chamber masters so that they keep the ids where the object stays and retire the ids where it goes. Name every id added and removed. Use the style guide's Station rules as written, and nothing added:
   - materials: sage and sand housing, dark rubber, screws, engraved marks, powder-coat, moulded polymer, canvas, braided hose;
   - dark matte slate panels inside the frame;
   - one key light from the top left;
   - none of the guide's "Never" list.
   Equipment is only of the guide's kinds (feed lines, vents, misters, small gauges, grow lamps), and only where the concept gives it a visible job for the pod (ruling section 4). Every piece of section 1's machinery is cut.
3. The lamp, if the concept has one, is a grow lamp:
   - in proportion to the chamber;
   - wholly inside the frame;
   - its light visibly landing on the pod and the nest.
4. Growth on the collected pod. The chamber shows the bud's species shell, the same picture as Pods and Cargo, which the build draws (T-20261016-28). Paint the overlays that work on any shell:
   - the warm glow at each stage (early, late, ready);
   - the two crack frames;
   - where the pod renderer carries it, the origin dust or moss (art direction: the one thing that varies from pod to pod);
   - the nest's front over the shell's foot.
   Give the overlay ids and their rects relative to the shell.
5. Write 1x proofs of empty, growing, ready and hatch-1100, composed with two different species' shells. Lay each beside Pods and Vivarium in one sheet, and judge it by the style guide's one test (beside the Companion concept and the kit test). Measure:
   - the chamber value against every zone (brightness ruling section 2);
   - the stamp plate p95 (stamp ruling section 3).
