## Purpose
The owner's rule for all screens (2026-10-16 10:51, verbatim): "and this line of thinking applies to all screens. eye candy is ok, but needs to serve a purpose." It follows the Incubator verdict ("you're basically decorating a screen and placing an egg it it. terrible."; "it needs to invite the user to check it"), recorded in T-20261016-25.

## Goal
For every Station screen (Home, Cargo, Idle, Create, Incubator, Vivarium, Library, Probe bench, Pods, Habitat) and the Companion's screens: what the player does there, what pulls them back, what changes because of them, and for each visible object, its job. List the objects with no job, the missing signals (progress, wait time, what needs the player), and any screen that fails the same way the Incubator did.

## Inputs
- station-gate 69ea1d23 captures: `prototypes/face/golden/**`, and the Companion captures on the gate.
- The game rules and economy docs marked current; the owner's accepted screens (Home, Pods, Habitat, Vivarium, Idle accepted on 10-15) also get audited: accepted for look is not accepted for purpose.

1. One section per screen: purpose (one sentence), player actions, return pull, the signals it must show, and an object table (object, job or "none", keep, change or cut).
2. A ranked list of the screens with the biggest purpose gaps, Incubator and Create excepted (they are in T-25 and T-02).
3. For each gap, one line on who fixes it (game designer, UI/UX, art). The lead writes the tickets.
