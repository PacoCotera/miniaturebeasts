## Purpose
The player comes to the Incubator to see more of their own mibi each time they look: the pod lit from below, the shell letting more light through as it grows, and the founder showing through as a shadow (owner's choice, option B). The candling light's job is to make that visible. If the light lights nothing and the steps look the same, the screen is still "an egg on a stand", and nothing invites the player to check.

`design/art-direction.md`, `design/style-guide/README.md` ("painted light on the painted layer"), nothing added. The sage frame, slate ground, moss, cradle form, ring, slip, card ground and stamp plate stay as they are.

## Steps
1. Repaint `incubator-shell-cool`, `-warm` and `-glowing` (and `-seams` if needed) so the light comes from **below**: the shell's foot and underside lit warm by the lens, falling off upward. The top-left key stays for form only.
2. Make the shell transmit. In each step, more of the interior light shows through the shell in the shadow's rect, so a shadow composited at the ruling's opacity reads as a shape. Say in the README which layer the build composites the shadow between (interior light, shadow, shell surface) and give that order.
3. Make the lens light the nest in `incubator-cradle`: a warm pool rising through the moss around the pod's foot, and the cradle's rim catching it. Paint it in the same steps as the shell (cool, warm, glowing), so the pool grows with the shell. If the cradle needs per-step variants, add them and name the ids.
4. Proofs, both species (S01 dark shell, S03 light shell), every step plus ready and hatch-1100:
   - Use a stand-in founder shadow (the juvenile silhouette, blurred) at the ruling's opacity per step.
   - Use stand-in slip and card content at the longest strings of ruling §5 (proof only, not masters), so the columns are judged as the player sees them.
   - Also write each growing step once without the shadow, for the measure below.
5. Measure, and write the numbers into the README (relative linear luminance; pod box 400, 144, 224, 272; top half rows 144–279, bottom half 280–415; moss band 400, 400, 224, 40):
   - **Under-light:** in warm, glowing and ready, the bottom half's mean is at least the top half's, and the bottom half is warmer (R − B) than in cool. Today the bottom is about half the top on S01.
   - **Steps distinct:** cool → warm → glowing each change the pod box by a mean absolute 12/255 or more. Today it is 5–6.
   - **The lamp lights:** the moss band's mean in glowing is at least 1.5 × its mean in cool. Today it is 1.1.
   - **The shadow reads:** in warm, the mean absolute difference between the proof with and without the shadow, inside the shadow's silhouette, is 15/255 or more on both species. It must also read as a creature shape at 1× (say so beside the proof).
   - **Brightness rule kept:** the pod's p95 beats every other region by 0.08 or more (today 0.287).
