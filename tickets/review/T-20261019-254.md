## Why
build-spec §13.2 (UI/UX, game designer confirmed): with more than six mibis at home, the mibi with a pending meet is always in; the rest fill by the newest life event, born or grown up, newest first, ties in bay order. views/home-props.mjs `glassOf` ranks by `born` alone (`rank = id === meet ? Infinity : bornOf`) and breaks ties by the larger id, so a mibi that grew up recently ranks as old as its birth, and ties are not in bay order.
## The change
- The life-event turn of a mibi is the later of `born` and, once it is an adult (`S.mibiStage` is not juvenile), `born + settings.adultTurns ?? JUVENILE_TURNS` (the turn it grew up; there is no stored field).
- Rank: meet first, then the newest life-event turn, ties by bay order (the lower bay first); keep the six, then show them in bay order as now.
- Tests: home-props.test.mjs (nine at home: a mibi born early that has just grown up outranks one born later that has not; two with the same turn keep bay order) and the face test's "at most six" case.
