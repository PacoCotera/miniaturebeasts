## Purpose
A short, principle-led guideline above the detailed style guide. It should say what the Station, the Companion and Miniature Lives feel like, so that any new or changed screen, including the owner's own iterations, can be judged by it. It holds when screens change. The detailed style guide stays below it as the current implementation reference, and it is provisional.
## Who
- The art director writes feel, textures and materials, light, colour mood and motion character.
- The game designer writes information density and tone (voice, how much the device speaks, how it addresses the player).
- The creative director signs the UI/art gate and the game designer the game design gate. Then the owner sees it.
## Shape
- Expand design/art-direction.md. Do not create a new document.
- A few pages of principles, each with one or two reference images: our own accepted art first (Home, Pip, the research-hands concept). No pixel values or sizes; those stay in the style guide.
- Sections:
  - the feel in a paragraph;
  - textures and materials;
  - light;
  - colour mood;
  - information density;
  - tone of voice;
  - motion character;
  - what it is not.
- One line saying the prototypes iterate and the owner's direction overrides any rule below it.
