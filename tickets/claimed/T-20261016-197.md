## What stands
The crate-by-crate idea: one crate opened at a time, its contents shown, and each content going to its place.

## What is redesigned (`results/T-20261016-171/C2-crate-by-crate.png`)
- **The flight** depends on dotted paths and "in flight" captions. A first-time viewer can't read it. The hand-off must read from the objects themselves: where each thing goes, its destination answering, the pod carry from the station experience review's flow section. Never from a drawn line.
- **The space.** About half of 1024×600 is empty grey, and the crate, the pods and the rewards are crammed into the centre. Use the whole screen. The queue of crates, the open crate, its contents and their destinations are one composition across it.
- **The surfaces.** A dark upper slate and a light lower slate split the screen for no reason. Make one surface with a purpose: the bay as a place in the device, with the device's one ground, frame and pane (the station experience review's device-wide rules).

## Owners
- **Art director, lead:** propose and paint the layout. Bring your own structure, not a fix of the three points.
- **UI/UX:** the hand-off motion without lines, focus, and the bottom line (✓ "Open them all" and the queue). Minimal words.
- **Game designer:** what a crate holds and where each content goes; check that nothing reads as a loss.
