## Purpose
The Incubator is where the player's founder grows, and the player wants to come back and check it. It must show, as science and tech, that life is being grown: progress, what is being revealed, and the genome taking shape.
## Owners
- UI/UX: layout and states.
- Game designer: what is revealed and why it matters.
- Art director: concepts.
- Creative director: what it is in the world. Candling stays: see the owner's line below.
## How this round works (lead)
- Purpose first: one sentence on what the screen is for and what the player must understand at a glance. Every element earns its place against that sentence.
- Style: the Miniature Lives look (design/art-direction.md, design/style-guide/README.md): sculpted, rich, warm light, readable. The Station is a lab and a genome-editing machine, never a brown box with text. No brown on brown, no cream panels, no plastic plaques, no tiny icons or pixel glyphs carrying meaning, no floating text.
- Every owner verbatim on this screen is an acceptance test. The owner proof must answer each one, and the result lists them with how each is met.
- Concepts first, as 2–3 options painted enough to judge the look (labelled PNGs), before any build ticket.
- Gate: two signed verdicts, the UI/art gate (creative director) and the game design gate (game designer), each judged in the device sheet against every owner line. Nothing reaches the owner without both.
