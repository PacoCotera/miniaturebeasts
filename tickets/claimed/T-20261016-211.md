### The text to paste

> ## Size and pose
>
> ### Mibis are tiny
>
> Miniature beasts are small in their world, on every screen. The world is large around them, and the camera never zooms a mibi up to fill a view. The table gives a mibi's height at 1×, from crest top to feet.
>
> | View | Adult | Juvenile | Note |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Home, the Vivarium window | **54** | **40** | the base measure. The away frame is a token with its own size |
> | The Vivarium, full screen, and Idle | **92** | **68** | the same world as Home's window, opened |
> | The Habitat and Sitting, a mibi up close | **150** | **110** | the closest world view. The name tag, focus ring and kin marks follow the feet and scale with the mibi |
> | A decision panel's window, close framing (Keep, Sit) | **64** | **48** | |
> | A decision panel's window, wide framing (Release) | **36** | **28** | small in a broad place |
> | Close-ups: the benches (Grow, Create, the Incubator, Cross) and the dock arrival | larger | larger | a mibi held up for work or greeted on arrival. Not settled yet; until it is, these keep the bench scale (up to the 304 × 312 master) |
> | Cargo | none | none | no mibis on screen |
> | Tiles, faces, portraits, the Library's nodes, the Companion | unchanged | unchanged | pictures of a mibi, not the world; the Companion keeps its own pixel scale |
>
> - **The juvenile** is about three quarters of the adult.
> - **Builds** compose each stand at the view's height from the 304 × 312 master (scale = height ÷ the figure's height in the master). The face never scales.
> - **A view not listed** is sized so the world stays large around the mibi. Ask the art director before placing.
>
> ### The Probe is held upright
>
> The Probe is always drawn upright, as a hand holds it: antenna up, screen at the top, grip below. The masters are `bench-probe-upright-t1/t2-288x432`, the cradle is `bench-cradle-upright-empty-288x432`, and the panel cards are `bench-probe-t1/t2-card-128x160`. Smaller Probes are reductions of these, never rotations of a lying picture.
