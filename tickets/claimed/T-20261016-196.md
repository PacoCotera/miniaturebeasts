## Owner (2026-10-17 12:52 local, verbatim)
"you most avoid positioning something as negative, unless it indeed is."

## Purpose
This is a game-wide rule, not a fix for one panel. Coral, ✕, warning marks, loss words ("gone", "lost", "used", "can't"), and cold or bleak painting belong only where the outcome harms the player or a mibi. A choice between outcomes is not harm.

## Owners
- **Game designer:** sweep the words. Search every player-facing string (the Station's strings and notices, the specs' screen words) for loss framing on outcomes that are not detrimental. List each one with its truthful wording.
- **Art director:** sweep the marks and paint. Find every use of coral, ✕ and warning marks, and every outcome painted bleak, in the screens and plates. List each one with whether the outcome truly is detrimental.

## Deliver
One table per owner in `results/T-20261016-196/`, with columns: place, current, detrimental (yes/no), proposed. Nothing is changed in this ticket. Fixes land with each screen's own pass, never piecemeal.

## Gate
