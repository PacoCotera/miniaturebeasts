## Purpose
UI/UX found that the player's pod is the thread of the game, yet no screen hands it on. The creative director accepted the fix and the game designer signed its meaning (specs/station-experience-review.md §6). The goal is to make the Station read as one continuous journey.
## What (decided; the rules are in §6)
1. The pod is carried on the four hand-offs: Cargo→Pods, Pods→Create, Create→Incubator, and hatch→Vivarium. The carry replaces the 2 s arrival ribbon there; the ribbon stays only for jumps with no carry.
2. Notices land on their target, under the game designer's "seen" rule (station-return-signals).
3. ← names where it goes, one step back. Write the rule into the key table.
4. Panels appear only on the existing list. Everything else is one press, with no second confirmation, no toast and no "✓ again".
## Who
- UI/UX: layout, the key table and wireframes in the device sheet.
- Art director: the look, per guide §9's carry row.
- Game designer: meaning.
- The creative director gates it.
