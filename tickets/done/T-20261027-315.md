   - `shedKnown(id)` is true when any of `S.pods`, `S.hold?.pods`, the pods in `S.bay` crates or `S.exp?.cargo.pods` has `shed === id`.
2. **Place.** In `startExpedition` (:1205), after the `firstPods` line, take `shedsDue()[0]` (one an expedition) and push the pod exactly as spec §4:
   `{ id: "s" + r.id, k: String(c), x: -1, y: -1, sp: r.sp, h: 0, bur: 0, g: r.g, how: "shed", shed: r.id, of: r.name, genome: r.genome }`

   - Call `fxGlint` at it when it is revealed, and log `"A pod of " + r.name + "'s line"` (placeholder, C18 owed).
3. **Carry.** The pod enters the hold and the crate like any pod. Make sure `genome`, `shed` and `of` survive the crate's pod copy (check the crate builder where `pods` are copied into `S.bay`).
4. **Words.** Where the Companion names a pod's find (`how` words, :1654), add `shed: "of {of}'s line"` (placeholder until C18).
