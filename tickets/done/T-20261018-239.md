## Why
Review T-20261018-238 (results/review-t235-fix.md). At 68594338, `node prototypes/face/tools/goldens.mjs --check vivarium idle home` fails 45 states (home 24, idle 9, vivarium 12: every state that shows a standing resident) when it runs at 20:29 UTC, and passes when the same check runs under a daytime clock: `TZ=Pacific/Kiritimati ... goldens.mjs --check vivarium idle home` (10:30 local) reports all three sets matching. At 3bdafbb7 and before, the same check passed at 20:29 UTC.

The cause is the commit's own feature. A sleeping resident now draws its curl in place (`p.curl`, set when `restOf` says `asleep`). `restOf` reads the Station time, which the host takes from the real clock (`timeNow` in `host.mjs`: `new Date()`), and the diurnal settle time is 20:00 (`vivarium.json` `rules.rest.settle.diurnal`) with a 30 minute window. Until this commit `rest` only changed a moving resident's picture, so a still resident's golden did not depend on the clock; now it does. The still states of the three golden sets (`vivarium-states.mjs`, `idle-states.mjs`, `home-states.mjs`) set the light (`S.settings.light`) but not the Station time, so a golden taken by day cannot be checked at night, in a UTC runner after 20:00, or before 06:00, and a golden taken at night would fail by day. The builder's goldens were taken awake.

## The change
Pin the Station time in the still states of the three golden harnesses, as the moving household states already do (the Station time noon): every still state that does not name its own time takes noon, and the states that need sleepers (`idle-night-pip-60000ms`, `home-moving-asleep`, the sleeper states) set their own time explicitly. Then retake only the goldens that move, with the repo's tools, and confirm the check passes at any hour of the day.

## Done when
- `goldens.mjs --check vivarium idle home` passes when run under at least three clocks: the default, `TZ=Pacific/Kiritimati` (daytime) and one in the sleep window (for example `TZ=Pacific/Honolulu` or run after 20:00 UTC).
- The diff of the goldens is only what the pinning changes (ideally none, as they were taken awake).
- Station, ui, face, both guards, `checks.mjs`, layer-check and the journey are as at 68594338.
