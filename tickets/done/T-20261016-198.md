## Owner (2026-10-17 12:52 local, verbatim)
"you most avoid positioning something as negative, unless it indeed is."

## Purpose
The T-196 sweep found a true loss that comes from a rule, not from wording. A sitting earned while one is already held "is not given" (`prototypes/station/src/sitting.mjs` lines 30–35, the "use your sitting first" warning). That is use-it-or-lose-it, the pressure the game's pillars refuse (Pillar 4). Softer words would hide it. The rule changes so that nothing earned is lost.

## Owners
- **Game designer, lead:** choose one, with the reason:
  - (a) earned sittings queue;
  - (b) a visible cap where earning pauses, shown before it is reached, so nothing is ever taken away.

  Write the rule and its words.
- **UI/UX:** how a held or queued sitting shows (Home, the Habitat's Portrait). Minimal words; nothing reads as a warning.
- **Genome engineer:** none, unless the save format changes.

## Deliver
The rule in the spec that owns sittings, and 1× layouts of the held and queued (or capped) states in the device sheet.

## Gate
Both gates. The game design gate checks that no earned sitting is ever lost. The UI/art gate checks `specs/gate-recalibration.md` §7: nothing negative, and pictures over words. Then a build ticket.
