## Why
The art direction page's size table says a decision panel's windows show the mibi at 54/40. The signed Hatch now panel frames the Incubator, a close-up, and shows the juvenile at about 126. The rule was written for windows that frame the world. A window inherits the scale of the place it frames. Reconciled with UI/UX (T-20261016-222 review).

## The change (station-gate)
In `design/art-direction.md`, section `## Size and pose`, replace the table row that begins `| A decision panel's windows |` with this row, verbatim. Change nothing else. The text is public: add no ticket ids, dates, names or quotes.

> | A decision panel's windows | **54** | **40** | when a window shows a mibi in its world. A window that frames a close-up (the workbench, the Incubator) takes that close-up's size at the panel's reduction. Either way both windows of a panel share one scale, so neither option reads as the distant or lesser one; the two differ by place and company, not by the size of the mibi |
