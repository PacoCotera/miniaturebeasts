## Why
prototypes/play/tools/journey.mjs fails now and then in its pixel comparisons (seen once at E2, dpr 2: "the Station's differing device pixels: 356, wanted 0"; the builder saw the same at dpr 1.25 and 2). The cause is Home's top bar, which draws the device clock (HH:MM, since H1): when the minute turns between the page screenshot and the Station's `__st.capture()`, the last digit differs (320 device pixels in lp x 996..1006, y 10..23 in my probe, at the minute). The journey opens its contexts with `{"light":"day"}` only, so the clock is live. It is not H4's animation: the contexts run with reduced motion, and a probe of 60 captures that crossed a minute with `stationTime` pinned showed 0 differing pixels.
## The change
In `open()` (and the standalone Station context near the end) set the Station's developer settings to `{"light":"day","stationTime":"12:00","stationDate":"2026-10-17"}` (`settings.stationTime` is what `timeNow` in game.mjs reads), as the Station journey does. Keep one assertion that the bar's clock is the pinned time.
