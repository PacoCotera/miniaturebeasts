## Purpose
The stand (T-20261016-190), the genome screen's bay and the field guide name a look by `traitState(...).shows` (`describe.mjs` `lookOf`). For S03's Claws (`anatomy.contact-terminal-form`, whose pool holds root, pad, webbed, wedge, rounded and hoof), `lookOf` returns the raw allele ids. The frame's looks for the trait are "round feet / pads / digging wedges". The stand, the bay and the guide need one vocabulary.
## Steps
1. Find every trait across the 16 frames whose `lookOf` output falls outside the frame's `trait.looks`. Measure it over 300 pods per species.
2. Fix it in `describe.mjs` or in the trait's looks in `species.mjs`: bin the forms into the trait's looks, or widen the looks to what the pool holds. The game designer chooses the words.
3. Test: for every species and every pod, every shown look is one of `trait.looks`.
