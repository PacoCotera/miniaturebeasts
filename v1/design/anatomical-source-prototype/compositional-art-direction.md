# Creature-source art handoff

The source reference should make a genome-derived creature understandable before illustration: connected organization, part roles, rooted attachments, characteristic proportions, surfaces and pigment owners. It is a construction aid, not finished pixel art. The [compositional contract](compositional-contract.md) holds exact geometry, profile equations and budgets; the [pet art contract](../../prototype/generator-workbench/art-template.md) governs the resulting illustration.

## Organization and silhouette

Resolve the connected region graph before features. Serial regions follow inherited unequal growth, bend, taper and join width. Broad joins can read as continuous tissue; narrow rooted joins can read as articulated lobes. Do not insert a neck between every pair or turn stations into obligatory beads.

Branched fans retain distinct actual parent-local roots and directions. Radial assemblies retain full XYZ frames and can foreshorten or occlude; they are not equally spaced screen spokes. A single volume and a headless body are legitimate sources. Optional typed heads and faces create their own owners rather than repair unfamiliar silhouettes.

Recorded forms include ovoid/barrel/tapered rings and bilateral rounded/rounded-square cross-sections. A camera must fit the source without moving parts or selectively enlarging features. Shared comparisons use common world scale.

## Parts and attachments

| Role | Required visible meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Free chain | Connected tapered links, joints and end taper; no terminal foot or universal ground height |
| Contact chain | Rooted links plus distinct attached terminal with declared dimensions; shape alone establishes no locomotion |
| Thin surface | Rooted sheet with chord/span/orientation, distinct from rods; no automatic foot, support or flight |
| Typed face | Expressed ocular/muzzle/jaw/crown structures on their actual owner; no inferred senses or feeding |
| Ear | Optional head-facet-rooted concave sheet; independent of crown and carrying head pigment |
| Tail | One closed continuous taper with its actual graph-selected owner, length, width and local bend |

Zero appendages is valid. Contact terminals may be rounded, blunt pads or wedges; they are not inferred paws, toes or hoof clefts. Radial body organization with a bilateral local face has mixed local symmetry. A diagnostic eye slice is not exterior tissue.

Required attachments must have actual geometric contact. An unconnected graph cannot be joined by paint. Natural far-side occlusion is permissible; a separate root trace can expose hidden parts without making the primary skin transparent.

## Materials and colour

Solid anatomy and surface material serve different roles. Skin remains a complete opaque base. Scale fields attach to actual local atlases and keep their extent, exclusions and pigment owners; they are neither detached armor nor compulsory stamps.

Fur consumers are separately versioned representations of inherited length/flow and primary-owner fields. Retained ribbons, clustered tufts and the continuous mantle have different geometric operators, while all preserve original copied inputs and exact local pigment boundaries. Non-primary heads, ears, tails, links and thin surfaces remain smooth under these profiles. No material path may invent solids, random extra anatomy, pigment or physiological benefits. Partial coats need their own consumer.

Each region/link/terminal/sheet retains its declared local colour fields. A split repeats per owner, rather than becoming a whole-creature half split or blended hue. Lighting changes displayed shade without rewriting base pigment. No style rule supplies a cream muzzle, belly patch or bright wings absent from expression.

Marking fields are separate from base pigments and material light. Logical masks differ from clipped fragments and separately visible shapes; natural occlusion or weak contrast cannot justify relocating or recolouring them. The exact contract defines provisional diagnostic ink independently of canonical palette.

## Source-to-pet meaning

Use the actual verified source with a short description of expressed shape, counted parts, material owners and named colours. Generic head projections do not become ears; generic rear regions do not become tails. Contact chains can be called legs only under the declared role/frame conditions. Local colour halves are not rings.

The full source audit retains geometry, causes, context and limitations outside the drawing sentence. A word limit cannot delete major anatomy or material ownership. Unsupported semantic labels are inspectable, and wording failure must preserve a valid source.

The source remains constrained in breadth and reference quality. [Anatomical-role evidence](../../prototype/generator-workbench/evidence/anatomical-roles/README.md) supports narrow tail readability while ears/coats remain weak; [mantle evidence](../../prototype/generator-workbench/evidence/coherent-coat/README.md) and [marking evidence](../../prototype/generator-workbench/evidence/marking-fields/README.md) retain their specific craft findings. Working static geometry does not establish a coherent coat, accepted pixel-pet master, animation or movement. The art contract distinguishes source-faithful portrayal from explicit redesign proposals.
