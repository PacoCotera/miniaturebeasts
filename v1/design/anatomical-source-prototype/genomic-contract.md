This implemented static profile preserves exact earlier workbench inputs and their constructed sources. It varies dimensions, pigments and optional modules inside a fixed head/neck/core/support organization. That organization is too narrow for the general creature generator; the [compositional contract](compositional-contract.md) supplies the separate variable-organization experiment. The [genetics framework](../../specs/genetics.md) remains authoritative.

[proposal-content.json](proposal-content.json) supplies exact contributors, allele values, copy operators, imported pigments, presence guards and worked copy sets for `genomic-anatomical-source-experiment@1`, rule `developmental-anatomical-source/1` and profile `anatomical-source/1`. [Actual browser records](../../prototype/generator-workbench/evidence/anatomical-workbench/README.md) retain the evaluated outputs. Existing copies and recipes recover through their pinned versions.

The inspected [compact/lean construction references](comparison.png) and [editable parameters](parameters.json) establish useful anatomical direction. Their fixed example tuples are source-art design inputs, not decoded genes. The new ratio controls below replace those tuples through a shared construction adapter. The two relatives need not reproduce every reference coordinate; their head/trunk/muzzle/support/terminal hierarchy must survive. The third worked copy set adds wing-bearing organization to the same grammar. A working platform must construct the actual resolved input rather than select one of these three drawings.

## Shared composition and inheritance

Every candidate in this bounded package carries the new bilateral source baseline: head, support-bearing core, their neck connection, paired two-link supports, terminal volumes, and the declared surface module references. This fixed baseline is inherited content with a version, not fifty existing loci renamed as anatomical organs. Its presence limits this prototype subset; it does not make heads or terrestrial supports universal Miniature Beasts requirements.

Each listed locus has two ordered copies. Numeric contributors use copy-mean; presence uses the explicit dominant-enable operator; pair count and crown shape use complete pair maps. Pigment loci import the exact version2 candidate records and their existing maps. Do not change imported records to produce a preferred colour. Same-package inheritance passes one exact copy from each parent and preserves donor traces. No copy mutation, desirable-child retry, linked animal bundle or canonical allele frequency is introduced.

Modules compose independently:

- Head and core exist under the inherited baseline. The head carries a genuine local exterior face, rather than the former radial diagnostic eye slice.
- Muzzle presence adds a projecting muzzle and lower-jaw volume on the head. Crown presence adds two head-rooted surface volumes with the independently resolved rounded/pointed form. Neither module supplies physiology.
- Posterior presence adds a distinct posterior region connected behind the core. It does not add legs or wings.
- Support pair count2 yields two core stations, fore and hind, with opposite-side attachments: four complete chains. Count3 yields three core stations: six chains. All three pairs belong to the middle support-bearing core, rather than one pair on each body region. The old0/1/3-group map cannot be reinterpreted to obtain four supports.
- Wing presence adds one paired dorsal surface on the core. It does not force three support pairs, a posterior, a muzzle deletion or a species label. Combinations such as four supports plus wings remain legitimate authored combinations if geometry is constructible.
- Eye presence adds the two head-local ocular mounts. EyeOFF is absence of this modeled module, not a conclusion about every possible sensory ability.

All copies remain in the genome when their owner is OFF. Numeric values may still resolve, but their construction contribution is explicitly inactive with the parent guard in its reason trace. Do not delete dormant wing/crown/posterior contributors, change their copies or report unmodeled physiology as false. Missing a required baseline or required locus is invalid content/genome; a present but unavailable consumer is unsupported, not a repaired creature.

## Construction conventions

These finite conventions are new provisional expression rules. They must be recorded with the constructed output. They are not new inherited copies and must not be hidden renderer improvisation. Coordinates are X longitudinal, head toward negativeX; paired Y; upward Z. Lengths are arbitrary source units.

Let `L` be resolved core half-length. The core radii are `(L, widthRatio*L, depthRatio*L)`. Head radii independently multiply `L`. Terminal radii are `(terminalLengthRatio*L, .73*thatLength, terminalDepthRatio*L)`. Support vertical drop `D` and outward spread `S` independently multiply `L`. Put core centreX=0, centreY=0, and centreZ=`terminalRadiusZ + D - .10*coreRadiusZ`. Thus every support root at core localZ=`.10*coreRadiusZ` has exactly the declared root-to-end vertical span. The overall source is not squeezed into an old body envelope.

**Head and neck.** Head centreX=`-L-.8*headRadiusX`; centreZ=`coreCentreZ+headLiftRatio*L`. Attach a tapered neck volume between the head’s rear surface and the core’s leading surface along their centre-to-centre line. Solve both ellipsoid intersections; overlap each endpoint inside its parent by `.05*L`. Neck section radius is `.70*min(headRadiusY,coreRadiusY)`. Retain the two true root coordinates and the section value. The connected neck is a volumetric connection, not a decorative line or an equal bulb added between regions. Intersections at these declared attachments are intentional.

**Muzzle and jaw.** With muzzleON, muzzle radii are `(projectionRatio*headRadiusX, widthRatio*headRadiusY, .45*headRadiusZ)`. Its centre lies in front of the head at `headFrontX + .15*headRadiusX - .5*muzzleRadiusX`, Y=0, Z=`headCentreZ-.38*headRadiusZ`, so it overlaps its parent rather than floating. Lower-jaw radii are `(.88*muzzleRadiusX,.89*muzzleRadiusY,.28*headRadiusZ)` and centre is `(muzzleCentreX+.07*headRadiusX,0,muzzleCentreZ-.30*headRadiusZ)`. Jaw and muzzle must connect. This defines a lower facial plane; no oral opening, smile, teeth or feeding performance is inferred.

**Exterior eyes.** Resolve eye anchorY=`±spacingRatio*headRadiusY`, localZ=`.20*headRadiusZ`. Solve the leading ellipsoid X surface at those local coordinates. Mount the ocular rim on that surface with its normal thickness overlapping the parent; preserve the calculated exterior root in the trace. Eye radius `r=sizeRatio*min(headRadiusY,headRadiusZ)` gives local radii `(.24*r,.82*r,r)`. Pupil radii are `(.12*r,.44*r,.57*r)` and lie on the leading side of the rim. These are source-defined mounts with a fixed forward-facing local frame, not a claim of tissue, sensing range or radial exterior-eye equivalence. If the root or full rim cannot fit the head-local frame, reject that constructed candidate; do not shrink the inherited size or force eyeOFF. Eye frames and pupils must have the same inherited size/owner in the result and source image.

**Support chains and terminals.** For2 pairs, stationX/L is `[-.65,.65]`; for3 it is `[-.65,0,.65]`. Solve oppositeY roots on the actual core ellipsoid at the declared stationX and rootZ. The root is an exterior attachment, never a pixel displacement. For side `s`, root `(x,s*y,z)` has a joint at `(x-.04*L,s*(y+.60*S),terminalRadiusZ+.50*D)` and distal endpoint at `(x-.10*L,s*(y+S),terminalRadiusZ)`. Connect these exact endpoints with two tapered support volumes. Proximal radius is `supportRadiusRatio*L`; proximal tip is `.86` of that radius; distal root/tip are `.80/.68` of it; a `.90` joint sphere joins them. Store derived XYZ segment lengths and both chains’ rooted frames. The static pose is a construction convention, not a locomotion or physical joint-limit model.

Each terminal is a separate ellipsoid attached to the distal endpoint, centred at endpointX minus `.09*L`. Its radii are independently inherited as above; the attachment must lie inside that terminal. Broad versus narrow terminals must change actual silhouette, not install a painted foot beyond an unchanged rod. No toes, pad patches, hoof cleft or species-specific extremity is supplied. If the smallest terminal cannot contain its attachment, reject the candidate, never extend or clamp a inherited radius. Legitimate far-side occlusion is not a missing support or malformed anatomy.

**Posterior region.** With posteriorON, posterior radii are `(posteriorLengthRatio*L,posteriorWidthRatio*coreRadiusY,posteriorWidthRatio*coreRadiusZ)`. Place it at core centreZ with centreX=`L+posteriorRadiusX-.18*min(L,posteriorRadiusX)`. The retained parent/child overlap connects the two regions. This is an optional abdominal organization in the worked example, not a tail or an extra equal region. It does not replace the core as support/wing owner.

**Crown surfaces.** With crownON, the pair attaches at head localY=`±.67*headRadiusY`, localX=`.06*headRadiusX`; solve the head’s upperZ surface. A rounded volume or a six-sided pointed surface grows from that root; height=`crownHeightRatio*headRadiusZ`, base radius=`.36*min(headRadiusY,headRadiusZ)`. Its base intersects the head at the actual attachment. Form and height are inherited, while the finite primitive convention is versioned. Hearing, horns, armor and signaling powers are unmodeled.

**Wing surfaces.** With wingON, solve two core surface roots at localX=`.15*L`, localZ=`.70*coreRadiusZ`, oppositeY from the ellipsoid. Span=`wingSpanRatio*L`; chord=`wingChordRatio*L`; sweep=`wingSweepRatio*span`. A finite paired thin surface has a root edge running from the root at X±`.5*chord` and an outer edge centred at `(rootX+sweep,rootY+s*span,rootZ+.20*span)` with X±`.30*chord`. Join those edges with a rooted tapered quadrilateral and thickness`.015*L`; the root edge must attach through a finite core-root volume, not merely two floating tips. Retain the root centre, actual edge/attachment intersection, corners, normal and thickness. Rounded corners/faceting may express this envelope, never relocate it. These are bounded wing-bearing surfaces, not flight, vein anatomy, elytra or transparent insect cuticle. Reuse paired volume/surface operators, not a hidden firefly fixture.

## Material and inherited pigment

The exact imported body palette owns head, core, neck, muzzle, jaw, posterior and crown. The exact imported secondary palette owns support links, joints, terminals and wings. It is not a face/belly patch. The initial worked controls are lagoon/lagoon and cream/cream; Generate may use the other existing candidate alleles without another palette decision. Ocular rim `#f1eddc` and pupil `#273036` remain fixed source inks.

The source baseline defines smooth body skin on its volumetric surfaces and smooth thin wing material on the conditional wing surface. The three explicit new Appearance contributors resolve skin/scales, scale-field extent and scale size. These are new anatomical-package records, not a changed interpretation of earlier covering consumers. With scales expressed, each head/core/present-posterior owner receives a surface field from its local longitudinal u=.20 to `.20+extent`, with extent `.35–.70`. Other body modules/supports/terminals/crowns remain smooth; wings remain smooth thin surfaces. This declared coverage is provisional, not a whole-creature coat assumption.

Profile `anatomical-surface-fields/1` lays out six circumferential bands on each eligible ellipsoid. Longitudinal row count is `ceil(extent/scaleSize)` for scale size `.12–.20`; clip the last row to the exact field end and subtract actual ocular mount footprints. At most 6×6×3=108 original material cells are needed before exclusions, within the 128-cell budget. Retain each owner/domain/patch and its inherited pigment; exclusions do not relocate eyes or change the extent. Patches indicate scaled material with no physical tissue depth, detached glyph or anatomy-object meaning. When skin is expressed, extent/size copies remain carried but inactive. Fur, feathers, shell detail, markings/emission and physiology remain unmodeled. Surface light describes base material rather than creating new pigment genes.

Each surface stores a local atlas. Body region/terminal u runs negativeX→positiveX; link/neck u runs proximal→distal; crown u runs base→tip; wing u runs root→outer edge. Use the exact imported palette array order for equal low/high-u ownership. Store resulting masks and geometry fragments when mixed pigments cross a polygon. Neither copy-slot reversal nor camera orientation reverses pigment fields. No averaged hue, whole-body half split, cream muzzle or renderer-selected recolour is allowed. Shading may change displayed light while the base pigment remains recorded.

## Bounds and rejection

Reject unknown alleles, wrong copy counts, missing required records/baseline, unresolvable pair keys, invalid numeric values, incompatible context and cyclic expression dependencies. Construction must reject non-finite/degenerate volumes, impossible surface roots, disconnected required attachments and exceeded declared bounds. Module guards run before geometry, and rejection never replaces copies, clamps inherited sizes, forces featuresOFF or changes an underlying specimen. Parent-child/joint contact is intentional; legitimate depth occlusion or touching projected silhouettes is not automatic anatomical rejection. Unmodeled dynamics are outside the eligibility claim.

Use at most64 graph nodes,128 edges,4096 finite mesh vertices and absolute coordinates below10000. One wing pair, at most3 support pairs and exactly2 links per chain keep this slice bounded. The existing renderer can use coarse finite meshes and its established opaque shallow three-quarter view. Keep a root/part trace auxiliary; do not place x-ray roots, numbered construction or skin glyphs in the model-facing image. A camera is presentation and must not repair anatomical roots or conceal missing source consumers.

Completion evidence is the **working platform**, with an actual Generate action producing retained variable anatomy, plus visible compact/lean and wing-bearing constructions from the declared copies. Inspect the source silhouettes, face/terminal attachment and three-region/six-support/wing ownership at the intended reference size. Compare colour and monochrome to distinguish changed organization from decoration. No new test suite or battery is authorized; architect/coder/independent implementation responsibilities remain separate. Source validity does not certify reference quality, pet appeal, game integration, biomechanical viability or canonical morphology. Stop at this usable static anatomical Generate slice; tail, hoof details, fur, antennae, motion/flight, emission and broader non-bilateral plans remain named gaps.
