# Compositional source contract

This contract turns resolved inherited contributors into a connected static creature source with inspectable part, surface and material causes. It supports varied organization without selecting a species body. The [genetics framework](../../specs/genetics.md) supplies the conceptual model; the [workbench](../../prototype/generator-workbench/README.md) supplies editing and retention.

Current default catalogue6/baseline6/rule6 carries the static innate profile while reusing source7/material5/reference7. Each retained recipe pins its foundation, complete ordered copies, consumers and output digests. These finite host consumers do not supply movement, reproductive physiology or broad viable pet construction.

## Organization and owner applicability


The inherited baseline supplies available volumetric, chain, root and surface operators. It does not install a head, torso, neck, support, abdomen/tail, wing or face. This bounded embodied subset requires one volume; broader non-solid organizations remain outside it.

Independent contributors resolve region depth, serial/fan layout, bilateral/radial primary symmetry, broad/narrow joins, unequal regional taper and bend/branch directions. Serial depth1/2/3 creates1/2/3 regions. Fan creates a root and two bilateral or three radial arms, each with depth minus one child volumes:1/3/5 bilateral or1/4/7 radial regions. Parentage, actual XYZ frames and shared root coordinates determine organization. These are general graph operators, not headless/radial/animal presets. No equal-length straight bulb chain is imposed.

Broad joins deliberately overlap connected volumes and retain a shared inside witness. Narrow joins retain true surface roots and a connector volume. A generic connected creature therefore need not have a separate neck. Serial bend acts in the bilateral XZ plane; radial primary sections use the new circular cross-radius. Fan radial branches rotate full XYZ region/attachment frames around X, rather than inventing equally spaced screen spokes.

Retained source1 uses analytic ellipsoid roots for narrow joins. Source2 intersects the retained visible mesh, preserving inherited copies, dimensions, centers, topology and throat ratio. Each profile keeps its exact builder/reference identity; a corrected output has a distinct source/result identity.

Optional typed head creates the actual owner for the earlier exterior-eye, muzzle/jaw and crown controls. Its absence makes those carried contributions inactive; no face or forehead is repaired onto a generic volume. The inherited join selects a fused connection or explicit neck. The facial module has its declared bilateral local frame even on a radial primary assembly; describe the resulting mixed local symmetry rather than claim the whole creature is rotationally symmetric.

Primary appendage role independently selects none, free-chain or contact-chain. Free-chain has zero to three groups and one/two links, exact lengths, taper and parent-local directions. It has no foot terminal, universal ground height or terrestrial support label. Contact-chain alone adds distinct terminal masses. Bilateral contact pairs retain their original two/three stations and local drop/splay; radial contacts instead use explicit new ray lengths and rotated complete frames. The old bilateral pair/drop/splay facts stay inactive there. Zero appendages is a valid complete result, not a failed creature.

Thin surface presence remains independent of head, region count or chains. Its chord/span/sweep and rooted owner frames describe a finite sheet, rather than another rod with a foot. A surface does not grant flight or install supports/posterior regions. These distinctions must remain in the actual source graph and UI audit.

## Founder sampling policy

Compositional founder support chooses one allele uniformly at each categorical/presence locus, then installs that exact homozygous pair. Thus an independently chosen two-state gate starts with equal founder states, rather than three active genotypes against one inactive genotype. Three-state organization choices have equal authored founder states. This is **state sampling for founders**, not a quota for animals or a rewritten dominance rule. Numeric and pigment copies remain independently sampled. Edited/inherited heterozygotes use their declared maps, with no repair. Keep the first eligible complete draw under the existing1024 total ceiling, including exact copies, actual attempt and input-versus-construction failure reasons. Never rank attractiveness, fill a class quota or silently redraw an unwanted valid outcome.
