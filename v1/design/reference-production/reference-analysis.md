## What the comparison establishes

The quiet rail, single warm focus and centered topic/readout groups make the two states distinguishable. Research is separate from Data; the empty niche does not imply a resident. Changed stock and section facts do not invent active expeditions or incubation. Static images cannot demonstrate navigation or human comprehension.

The destinations still have thick stepped perimeters around small busy interiors. Added refraction, chamber detail and material accents improve recognition, but do not match the reference's confidently modeled silhouettes and surface depth. The cool similar-weight subjects have less material distinction than the reference resources and feature art. Enlarging them or adding outline steps would not establish the missing craft.

These remain bounded source experiments, rather than faithful finished native masters. Reproducibility, dimensions and a readable layout answer different questions from pixel craft, runtime integration and physical performance.
