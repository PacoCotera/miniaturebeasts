# Play the Pip host prototype

This three-device host prototype connects one complete local loop: explore, retain a
haul, research a sample, choose a supported genome, incubate, reveal and visit
the same Pip resident in its habitat. It uses native C state and rendering; the browser
transports the depicted directional, workspace, Confirm and Back buttons and displays native
frames. Screen illustrations are not clickable controls.

## Play

Home opens on a lab-wide status overview. Up/Down previews Explore, Research,
Incubator or Habitat in the large panel; Confirm enters and Back restores the
same Home selection. The preview reads current state without starting anything.
Research shows retained samples and discoveries, because V1 studies resolve
immediately after the existing explicit cost review/commitment.

Press and release Up/Down to choose a row; Confirm acts. Left and Back return
to the parent screen. Right opens supported read-only detail, never a costly
action or reveal. Research, Home, Library and Habitat keys switch views
without stopping activities or spending resources. Library contains recorded
findings; Home opens the Lab overview and Habitat includes the full resident list.
Lab Explore lists received expeditions; it cannot watch the Companion while away.
Start an outing from Companion Probe. The directional pad moves one legal map
tile per press; Confirm at a named place opens its actions. Up/Down chooses an
action and fresh Confirm performs it. Back returns to the same map position;
Back from the map returns to the mode rail.

Camp offers Data2, Energy2 and Essence1. Directions choose a retained offer;
fresh Confirm takes its whole quantity immediately. Single offers at Relay and
Stone shelf take directly. Moss has a supply offer and an independent trace;
read the trace, walk to the Cache and explicitly collect its neutral sample.
A result needs no dismissal before the next directional movement. Back preserves
untaken supplies. Arrival, elapsed time and inspection award nothing. Use Cargo
to review the haul and return.
Connected Cargo
lets you choose a resource class and whole-item quantity, then review the discard.
Back or Keep preserves those items; sealed hauls cannot be discarded. There are
no resource filters.

Research opens a collection-wide Overview; selecting a sample opens its own
workbench. New samples have three investigations with sample-specific discoveries.
A pattern reading may leave one inherited possibility unresolved; comparison can
establish alternatives or how movement and effort are linked. The workbench shows
recorded knowledge and remaining useful investigations. Cost review precedes
spending; known findings and redundant investigations can be inspected freely.
Complete reference knowledge unlocks only the forms supported by that sample.
Legacy samples keep their original five studies and two supported forms. Further
expeditions replenish shared stock without erasing any sample's findings.
Selecting a supported form creates a reversible draft without spending. A separate
Start incubation review shows the five-of-each cost; fresh Confirm commits it.
Back returns to the draft. Busy incubation and the eight-resident limit block
creation without spending. Incubation takes 20 active seconds; opening it is deliberate. Visit the habitat,
spend time with your resident, and explore again.

Supplies are whole items, interchangeable only within their own class. The
internal encoding uses100 per item; new Lab and carried inventory must be
multiples100. Cargo capacity is40 whole supplies, with a separate capsule slot.
Study costs remain4 or5 items; incubation costs5 of each.

The reversible field3 content retains six finite offers: Camp2/2/1, MossEssence12,
RelayData12 and StoneEnergy14. Remote batches yield38; untouched CampData2 fills
40 and leaves Energy2/Essence1. Take26 binds outing, place, source and exact whole
quantity in the saved operation fingerprint. Capacity rejects an oversized batch
unchanged; no partial award or repeated lottery occurs. Sources never refill on
reload. The sample allowance is pinned when an outing starts. Numeric offers are
provisional proof content, not final balance or measured engagement.

Saved field1/2 geometry, cargo, preparation and counters remain readable and
returnable. Their field clock is disabled, and existing preparation never becomes
new supplies. Current field3 reuses field2 geometry without changing the saved
GameStateV4 layout or field/received record sizes. Received validation interprets
old counters as attempts and field3 counters as collection actions/taken units.

Return review defaults to Keep cargo. Cancelling preserves map position and all
untaken offers. Sending seals the haul.
Fresh Lab acceptance ends that expedition; it grants no late sample
or award. The matching receipt closes transport metadata, then a new route starts
a new expedition identity. Cancelling an unsealed send review retains the outing. Legacy preparation stays saved Companion-side without conversion. A completed
route with no transferable items and no shelf room can finish without a phantom
haul, after a consequence review. Home can leave reception without losing a
pending haul or its acceptance/receipt state. Dock keeps its snapshot timestamp
separate from Feed and print-cancellation feedback. Lab keeps the latest16 received
records with accepted supplies, samples and the route actually walked. Two local
map families and three outing labels are bounded fixtures; further events, risks
and distinct drops remain incomplete. The standalone historical timed survey
remains a compatibility fixture, not the current three-device acquisition flow.
Legacy recovery is defined in
[architecture](../../native/selected-lab/README.md#state-and-recovery).

Home, research and temporary haul reception preserve the current workpiece and
reversible draft within the running session. Restart begins at Home and Research
opens the collection Overview; accepted evidence and individuals remain saved,
while transient menu selection and an uncommitted draft are not restored.

## Boundaries

Companion's Companions mode is accessible with an empty list. Its revealed-only
resident snapshot retains each saved ID/source, genome/expression, original art
metadata and visit count, with update time and accepted world revision. Selecting
an ID opens inspection; a fresh `Spend time together` uses the existing Lab care
operation on that same ID. Pending transfers block visits. Lab and connected
Companion share the accepted count; Dock retains an accepted visit total.

Disconnected Companion reads the saved snapshot, labels it cached and disables
visits. It queues no care or reward. Offline restart retains those snapshot bytes
and timestamp. Reconnection refreshes the projection without repeating a visit.
If the world visit saves but its snapshot cannot save, the old snapshot remains
stale and further visits are disabled until refresh. The accepted world operation
is never retried merely to repair a cache. Portraits use recorded original
path/version/hash; unavailable original art is shown as pending, not inferred
from a hidden candidate. The envelope recovery boundary is in
[architecture](../../native/selected-lab/README.md#state-and-recovery).

One local world, eight sample records and eight resident slots. No cloud account,
real sensors, printing, charging, device-to-device transfer or physical display
performance is claimed. Closing or suspending play does not add offline time.
Care is gentle and has no neglect penalty. This is a playable Pip slice, not
the full catalogue, capture/training system or habitat ecology simulation.

The genotype remains engine data. Player screens explain form and expression.
Each individual retains its source sample, founder origin, complete supported
Pip genome, expression and content/art versions. Creation and reveal are separate
durable operations. Gemini source and extraction provenance are in
`design/v1-pip/manifest.json`; the two provisional Pip sprites are playtest art.

## Running and verification

Build `native/selected-lab` with the established CMake toolchain, then run
`selected_lab serve`. Set `BEECHO_V1_SAVE` to an absolute writable save path;
otherwise the program uses a separate `.beecho-v1` save next to its legacy path.
Frozen historical V1/V2/V3 saves are validated before conversion to the appended
V4 field/history format. Already accepted legacy samples retain their five-study
content; fresh accepted samples receive pinned discovery content. Before a live
upgrade, retain the matching world and Kit files: old binaries cannot read V4
world saves. Prototype checks use isolated worlds and do not reset user data. The existing presenter
serves this executable. `ctest` exercises native input and domain behavior;
`native/tests/test_selected_presenter.py BINARY` checks the HTTP adapter;
`native/tests/test_v1_journey.py BINARY FRAME_DIRECTORY` plays a complete
real-time journey and captures native screens, including suspend and restart.

The rollback checkpoint for this round is retained separately. This document
describes the prototype boundary; test execution and review acceptance must be
reported against the actual delivered revision, not inferred from this plan.

## Pinned genetic content

`pip-proof-v1` is the narrow Pip content package, not a generic genome generator.
Its five variable-locus facts are Crown, Eye rings, Markings, Movement and
Efficiency. New samples use `pip-discovery-v1`, which resolves 17 named baseline,
palette and variable references through three investigations with A/B support;
these are provisional authored variants, not new canonical alleles. The nine baseline module references and three fixed-locus references
remain the immutable Pip baseline specified by `prototype/genetics/content.mjs`;
these are package facts, not five new loci or randomly selected traits. The
five-bit native study mask is not the general engine's 17-reference completeness
mask. `pip-rules-v1` evaluates the fixed adult, healthy/rested, firm-ground,
mild-environment reference context. Future environmental expression must vary
state, not mutate the stored genome or change the identity of an existing resident.

## Lab platform and controls

The Lab reference is Raspberry Pi4 Model B with the 1024×600 display and the
[accepted family control layout](../../design/lab-controls/combined-family-materials.png).
Current executable evidence is native C17 compiled with GCC/CMake/Ninja for Linux
x86-64 on the existing VM and CI. This is not an ESP-IDF Lab application or an
emulated Raspberry Pi. ARM compilation, physical display/input drivers and Pi4
performance remain unverified. ESP-IDF builds the separate Companion scaffold.
