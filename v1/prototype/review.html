How to judge physical size later

Use a ruler to calibrate the visible screen rectangle to the vendor active area: 163.2 × 97.92 mm for the monochrome candidate or 160 × 96 mm for Spectra 6. Browser zoom and OS scaling can change the result; verify both dimensions after every adjustment. This is a viewing-distance exercise, not a panel measurement or color proof. A printed, measured layout is another option. Source: the vendor documentation linked above.
