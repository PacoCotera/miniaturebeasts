# From a genomic source to pet art

A pet illustration should turn an inspectable creature source into an inviting character while keeping its recognizable organization, part roles and expressed materials. The source carries inherited meaning; the drawing adds deliberate volume, lighting and pixel craft. Useful creative departures are recorded as proposed redesigns rather than silently becoming genes.

The current compositional handoff attaches one verified source image with this instruction and a short factual description:

> Turn the attached critter into a cute pet, shown alone in rich high-bit pixel art.

The description names actual shape, extremity counts/forms, material owners and colours. It targets 25–45 words without deleting major expressed facts to meet a cutoff. [Source-derived brief rules](../../design/anatomical-source-prototype/compositional-contract.md#source-derived-pet-brief) define exact supported meanings and clause witnesses. Source3/4/5/6 use summary1; source7 uses summary2 with its marking clause. Unrelated retained profiles keep their exact original handoff.

## Renderer and workbench responsibilities

The workbench validates and re-evaluates the source, constructs anatomy and retains causes. Missing semantic construction remains visible upstream. The renderer finishes supplied form and material; local optical relief and curvature can be depiction choices, while new organs, pigment domains or inherited characteristics are explicit changes.

| Subject | What the drawing should retain | What inspection retains separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Connected exterior where modeled, regional proportions, taper and waist relationships | Topology, stations, coordinates and source profile |
| Appendages | Actual roles/counts/root groups, chord/span or linked sections/terminals | Dimensions, edges, joints and unresolved tissue depth |
| Surface | Material kind, field, scale/flow, exclusions and pigment continuity | Root coordinates, contours, sampled elements and operators |
| Colour/marking | Named expressed fields with their actual owners; markings distinct from light | Resolved copies, masks and causal trace |

Fins remain fins; scale samples communicate skin texture rather than detached objects. Sparse coverings retain their actual extent and gaps. A graph without continuous exterior cannot be silently joined by paint.

Name each local pigment owner. “Each body region has…” preserves a repeated local split; it is not one whole-creature split. Uniform pigments stay uniform. Style instructions cannot supply another palette. Candidate pigment vocabulary remains [versioned experimental content](evidence/inherited-pigment-experiment/README.md).

## Portrayal and redesign

**Source-faithful portrayal** preserves semantic organization, part/root relationships, characteristic proportions, features and pigment/material fields. Diagnostic envelopes need not prescribe every final pixel contour; interpretation still needs review against the source.

**Art-first redesign** can propose changed anatomy, proportions, colours or markings. Keep the originating record/reference and a short changed-trait list; do not claim a resulting expression identity until those changes have their own resolved source. A stronger pet image can guide new construction rules without repairing a saved genome.

A useful production sequence is anatomical source/reference, optional outline, illustration/pet treatment, then animation from a selected master. These stages answer different questions and do not require one provider call each. A low-poly reference is a representation option; it cannot add inherited organs.

## Pixel craft and references

Use deliberate contiguous clusters, controlled stepped contours, coherent form-following shadow/highlight groups and selective material edges. Frame the complete subject with readable attachments and let actual facial features carry focus. Keep pigment domains recognizable through light and shade.

[C18](../../design/game-art-proposals/35-vault-composition/18-c-refined.png) supplies interface/contour craft and optical density, not the pet's skeleton. The [pet family](evidence/art-reset/README.md), [supplied transformations](evidence/owner-pet-transformations/README.md) and [image-led example](evidence/owner-simple-pet/README.md) supply character references with their recorded limits. A reference counts as a provider input only when actually attached.

Evaluate actual pixels for recognizable source meaning, coherent appealing character and deliberate craft at intended size. Smooth gradients alone do not establish HiBit pixel craft. Keep exact submitted text, attachments, original bytes/hashes and findings beside the image. The [evidence index](evidence/README.md) distinguishes successful handoff/transport from source drift and unaccepted pet masters.

## Prompt, source and retained-image bindings

The guided workbench initializes an editable render draft from the derived brief. The submitted text is sent unchanged and retained separately from the source-derived text. Manual prompt edits do not mutate the genome. Each image keeps its original source/input/result/scene identities and prompt; grouping versions in one working gallery never rebinds them.

[Rendering and manual retention](rendering-contract.md) define admission, storage, recovery and bounds. No automatic refinement or accepted-art lifecycle is supplied. **Edit this prompt** copies an older submission into the current draft explicitly.

Full semantic exports retain identity, context, anatomy, proportions, surfaces, movement, limitations and causal traces. New module scenes use template4/module-scene-art3; verified module-scene-art2 imports retain original text, while fresh resolution uses art3. Imported output is independently reconstructed before projection. Replaying a source does not reproduce a provider image.

Projection limits remain 64 KiB per subject, 8,192 characters per audit binding and 32,768 characters per assembled prompt; oversized projections reject. Short twelve-hex #G/#E references are lookup fingerprints, not recoverable genomes or guaranteed unique IDs. Full hashes remain exported; [tree codec](codec-contract.md) handles reversible retained data. Scanning or sharing never grants ownership or breeding permission.
