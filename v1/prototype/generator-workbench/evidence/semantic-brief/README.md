artwork. The [form/skin example](../owner-semantic-surface/README.md)
and considered art/pixel/genomic handoff motivate the presentation repair.

The new language replaces counted plate objects and geometric half-width
instructions with integrated skin texture, body-local domain, readable full
element width, alignment, clear roots/features and pigment continuity. Available
fin chord, thickness and taper come from actual construction profiles; generic
fin dimensions alone do not establish taper. Contact tips remain contact tips.

The [measurements](measurements.json) record actual text lengths and unchanged
source/result/scene/identity checks for the retained scenes. The axial brief is
slightly longer because it adds missing appendage form; this increment does not
claim all prompt-length concerns are solved. Detailed construction samples and
coordinates remain inspectable in the unchanged source artifacts. Historical
prompt files are not overwritten. A new presentation has a new prompt identity.

[File hashes](manifest.json), the [active art contract](../../art-template.md)
and source packets linked in the manifest make these examples reviewable.
Source fidelity checks and framework compilation do not certify illustration
quality, pet appeal, animation or physical capabilities. No provider request
was made after the semantic form/skin steering.
