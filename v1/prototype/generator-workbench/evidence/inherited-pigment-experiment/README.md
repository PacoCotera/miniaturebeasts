# Candidate inherited pigment experiment

This host-only candidate experiment uses `genomic-covering-pigment-candidate@2`,
derived from the retained covering foundation at source base
`7b0ccabcb1853b95061da8d7edebff1d64605faa`. Its two
pigment records provide ten body variants and six secondary variants, with 55 and
21 explicit unordered-pair maps. The six legacy map entries and all other records
and construction rules are unchanged. The default workbench packages are not
replaced or promoted. These are provisional pigments and structural diagnostics,
not canonical colours or production pet art.

The initial resolver rejected the candidate because its allele limit was four.
The approved validation amendment permits at most ten alleles only for validated
`partition-map` records with one registered palette output. All other records
retain their four-allele limit. Missing maps, unknown copies and invalid pigments
still reject. No genetic or construction rule is changed.

Run from `prototype/generator-workbench`:

```text
node --test pigment-proof.test.mjs
node pigment-proof.mjs --out evidence/inherited-pigment-experiment
```
