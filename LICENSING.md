Current program sources include prototype `.mjs`, `.html`, `.css`, fixture `.json`, and package metadata. Documentation includes Markdown and the design asset manifest. Separate concept and prototype PNG artwork falls under the artwork license to the extent applicable rights are held. Concept illustrations are artwork, not editable hardware designs.

Pixel masks, glyphs and palette definitions embedded in executable renderer modules remain part of AGPL software. Separately exported artwork is offered under CC BY-SA for applicable project-held rights; generation alone does not establish copyright in an output. Third-party fonts, artwork and code retain their own terms. File-specific third-party notices take precedence over these project defaults. License texts retain their publishers' terms.

The root [LICENSE](LICENSE) contains AGPL version 3 for software discovery. The version is **3 only**, not automatically a later version. It does not replace the other licenses in this scope table.

## Attribution and sharing changes

Preserve required copyright and license notices and identify changes as the applicable license requires. Suggested project credit is **“Miniature Beasts / Dirty Pawz Press and contributors”**, linked to [the source repository](https://github.com/PacoCotera/miniaturebeasts). This suggestion is not an additional advertising condition.

Upstream contributions are encouraged through pull requests. These licenses require sharing under specified conditions; they do not require submitting a pull request, acceptance upstream, or publication of every private experiment.

Software distributors must meet AGPL corresponding-source obligations, including installation information where applicable. Modified network software must provide interacting users the source opportunity required by AGPL section 13. Provide source matching the version people receive or use. Hardware distributions must meet CERN-OHL-S complete-source and source-location requirements, subject to its stated exceptions. Shared adaptations of documentation and eligible artwork follow CC BY-SA attribution and ShareAlike terms.

Product build and installation tools belong with public product source. Separate internal project-management records are outside this repository's scope; that separation cannot substitute for supplying required corresponding source. Credentials and player data are not product source.

## Artwork and branding

Original concept images preserve provenance, including AI-generation provenance. The artwork grant covers only rights the project holds. It does not assert exclusive ownership over non-copyrightable output or license third-party marks or other rights the project does not control.

[v1/BRANDING.md](v1/BRANDING.md) distinguishes official offerings from independent ones without adding restrictions to the copyright licenses. Dependency notices are covered in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The linked license texts govern their actual conditions.
