## The two fixes (both proofs)

1. **Glow is self-changing, not breeding only (check 5).** In the S03 frame, `appearance.emission-brightness` is `changedBy: self`, and only `emission-length` is breeding. Glow's first gene draws the self-changing marks: its steps, always unlit on Create and in the Incubator, with the trigger's place badges, a cave and a wood (specs/genome-screen-calls.md, "What the screens draw"). The joined rings on it say "only breeding changes this", which is false.
2. **The doing rows can't be read (checks 1 and 3).** The Movement, Stamina and Character rows have empty heads and empty wells with only the breeding mark. A player can't say what they are looking at in half the rack. This is the owed art, not a layout change. Each needs a still of this founder doing it, as specs/trait-words-and-doings.md §3 sets out: footprints for Movement, the long walk or the stone for Stamina, the new object or the shadow for Character. Glow's head already has its pose. Until the stills exist, these two proofs can't go to the owner as finished, only as a layout.
