### The four changes
1. **The dotted cross line and the dashed red link must be composed pictures, not runs of rectangles.**
   - As rects, one dotted line can be 94 nodes (S01's lone doing gene to the cross mark: 532 → 720, 1 on 1 off), and a long link is up to about 70 dashes. Eight rows of that breaks "about 40 rectangles" and threatens the 400-object budget.
   - Each line is instead **one composed node** using the existing `dash` op (`["dash", x, y, len, "h"|"v", on, off, colour]`), palette-exact.
   - The 2 px link is two parallel `dash` ops per segment, in one picture over the link's box (at most about 188 × 206 px; at most 155 KB while a clash shows).
   - §6 should read "every solid line is a run of palette rectangles; the dotted cross line and the dashed link are each one composed picture of 1 px palette dashes".
2. **"Changed by you … at most a 1–2 px soft edge" (§2.2) cannot be alpha on the art layer.** The marks are art-layer slices, checked at 0 off palette. The soft edge is either:
   - (a) none;
   - (b) **one ring of the mint ramp's darker step, a palette colour (my recommendation)**;
   - (c) the marks moved to the painted layer, where straight alpha is allowed but the palette check no longer covers them.
   Whichever is chosen goes into T-20261016-130's art brief.
3. **The Incubator's 400 ms `teal` 2 px pulse (§5) is a cut, not a fade.** Chrome has no opacity. It is four rects shown at the clear's frame and removed at 400 ms (or on, off, on at 0/133/266 ms). If a soft rise is wanted, it is a 2-level `bayerPick` composed node, never alpha.
4. **The turn's dirty area is tight.** §3's sum at its worst:
   - the drum 7,680 + the gene and corners 1,024 + the bay 33,280 = 41,984;
   - plus the founder's part box: up to 94,848 if the part is the whole founder (304 × 312);
   - plus the price and the context line's label boxes, about 14,000.
   That is **about 151k of 153,600**. Later dither frames redraw only the founder's box (≤ 94,848). The face test must print the turn frame, as §3 already says. If it passes the ceiling, the bay's cut moves one frame later (16 ms), which the eye won't see.
