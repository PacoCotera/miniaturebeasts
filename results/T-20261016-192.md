## What changed
This is the art director's treatment of Home's module column in fixed places. It follows the owner's 11:27 line ("fixed places, go with 1 and 2, merge both / visually the module column is still lacking") and the game designer's tiers (`results/T-20261016-192/game-designer.md`). It is composited from signed art over the accepted Home captures, with no generated pixels. The script is `results/T-20261016-192/make-column.py`.

**The treatment: one instrument, five fixed places.**
- **The panes:** five one-pane modules (slate `panel`, hairline, bevel) in Home's order (Cargo, Pods, Incubator, Probe, Library), the same place every time. There are no plate pictures, and the lamps are amber or off.
- **The wells:** each pane holds the object of the screen it opens, in a well with a lit lower lip and contact shadows:
  - **Cargo:** the bay, its back wall with the door's slats, and the crates on its floor.
  - **Pods:** the case, three wells with the species' own shells (from the pod renderer).
  - **Incubator:** the candling chamber's ring, with the bud's own pod lit from below.
  - **Probe:** the Probe upright, as held.
  - **Library:** the field journal.
- **The three tiers** (the game designer's call), drawn by light, not by size or position:
  - **Forward** (at most one: the module the bottom line's ✓ acts on): its object a little larger, the warm key light pooled on it in its well, its amber lamp lit.
  - **Live** (a running wait, such as the bud growing): at rest, normal light, its ring showing the state.
  - **Quiet:** its object dimmed in its well, still readable.

  Because nothing moves place, Home is learnt once (the owner's fixed places). The eye still finds what needs you first: the one lit, warm well, with the lamp beside it.

## Round 2: one face (the creative director's fail, 845079f4; the game designer's fix, 1876b827)
Round 1 was cleaner but still five equal framed boxes. This round answers the creative director's four questions:
- **Must the frames be equal?** No. Each module is a recess the shape of what it holds, in a fixed place:
  - the bay door, wide, at the top;
  - three pod sockets cut straight into the face;
  - a tall arched candling chamber;
  - the Probe's clamp;
  - the journal's slot.
- **Labels when the object reads?** None. What the focus is on is named in the bottom line's context, as on every screen.
- **Recesses, not boxes in boxes:** every module is a recess cut into the face, with a dark interior, a shadow under its top edge and a lit lower lip. There are no panes or frames.
- **The same housing as the window?** Yes. The living window is the face's largest recess, behind its rubber bezel, so the column and the window are one slate face of one machine.

**The tiers, by light** (the game designer's call):
- **Forward:** the recess lit from within (a warm pool on its floor), its object at size, its amber lamp. With crates waiting, the bay's shutter is rolled up and the crates sit lit on its floor. With a bud ready, the chamber glows from below and its rim, the ring, is full.
- **Live:** at rest, its ring running (the bud growing: the chamber's rim half lit, the pod faintly candled).
- **Quiet:** at rest, never broken.
  - the bay door shut, slats down;
  - the chamber dark and empty with no ring (the game designer's fix: a ring means a wait is running);
  - the sockets, Probe and journal dimmed in their recesses.
