## What changed
design/game.md, the top expanded in place (the mechanics table below is unchanged). It is principle-led and has no numbers. New or expanded sections:
- **Who it is for:** the page's role as the design guideline, and that the owner's direction overrides it.
- **The feel:** a naturalist's expedition that ends in a home full of life; curious, unhurried and warm. References: Neko Atsume, Creatures.
- **The loop and the arcs:** the core loop (the existing diagram), then six arcs (trip, pod, mibi, species, family, world), each with its question.
- **The hooks we use, each named and justified:** an honest curiosity gap (not a loot box), authorship, ownership and origin, collection with meaning, mastery of a real system, a gentle appointment, goals the player sets, physical ritual.

- **What shows progression:** the guide and Library, the vivarium and growth stages, pod rings, the map's fog, Probe tiers, families and wishes, portraits. Never levels or scores.
- **What makes you come back,** over minutes, a day, and weeks.
- **Three devices, one world,** with the dock as the shared moment.
- **How decisions are communicated:** free browsing; options, cost and outcome before the press; panels only at real decisions.
- **What it is not.**
- **The design principles,** unchanged.
- **Gaps:** partner abilities, the meaning of bonding, the long term, the first session, the Caddy's printing in play, the economy's pacing.

## Update: restructured (owner 16:29, lead's re-brief)
The first version followed the brief's list of topics. The owner asked for the structure a game designer would choose for designers and developers, filling what nobody named; mechanics, for one, were missing from the first cut. So design/game.md is now in this order:
1. A note on how the page is built.
2. Who it is for.
3. **Pillars:** four, each with what it rules out (discover something alive, author life you understand, live with what you made, respect the player's time).
4. The rules every mechanic keeps (the existing principles).
5. **The core loop:** the diagram, then a table of each step's verbs, device and pillar, and the arcs around the loop.
6. **Systems:** ten, each with its purpose, what it takes in and gives, and its rules document; then how they connect, so none is a dead end.
7. **Economy:** Energy, Data and Essence, each with one job, its sources, sinks and the pressure it creates; what is never sold.
8. **Progression:** knowledge, collection, capability, world, lineage and recognition, each as the player sees it.
9. **Engagement:** why players come back over minutes, a day and weeks; the hooks we use and the ones we refuse.
10. **Devices and interactions:** the three devices, the dock, input, and how decisions are communicated.
11. Feel and tone (pointing to art direction), and what it is not.
12. The mechanics reference table (unchanged).
13. **Not designed yet,** with the new gaps merged in (partners' payoff, bonding's meaning, the long term, the first session, printing in play, the economy's pacing).
