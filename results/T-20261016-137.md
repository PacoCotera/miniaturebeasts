## What changed

The frames now carry the owner's kinds of locus. The game designer agreed the triggers (specs/genome-screen-calls.md, and their reply to the proposal: OK as written, not provisional).

- **Every locus row** has `changedBy`, one of:
  - `"species"`: locked;
  - `"player"`: a configurable look;
  - `"breeding"`: inherited-only, which includes S03 Claws and every doing that is not self-changing;
  - `"self"`.

  A sleeping locus takes its switch trait's value. A sealed locus is `"breeding"` or `"self"`.
- **Each self-changing trait** has `selfChanging: { toward, places, storm, stepOf: 16, maxSteps: 4, fadeAfter: 3 }` and `shapeable: false`. `counts.selfChanging` gives the number of such loci.
- **The rules live in the framework** (`species.mjs`: `SELF_CHANGING`, `SELF_MARK`, `changedByOf`):
  - only the partner changes;
  - at most one step a dock, and only when the trigger was met;
  - a step is 1/16 of the species' pool range, up to 4, only toward the end;
  - three partner trips in a row without the trigger take one step back, never below 0;
  - that count restarts at a step up and after each fall;
  - a trip the mibi rode on but did not lead counts for nothing;
  - the mark is kept beside the copies and never inherited.

  The frame build stops if a self-changing trait's end is not in its pool, or if the trait has more than one locus.
- **Unchanged:** genomes, pools, locked pairs, viability (200/200 each), the stamp snapshot (`extract-frames.mjs` writes an identical `frames-data.mjs`), the frame version (3), the digests and the name codes. Apart from the new fields, all 16 frames compare equal to station-gate's (checked by stripping the new fields and comparing the JSON).
