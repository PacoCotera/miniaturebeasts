# Home, art-led: three inspiration frames (the art director's briefs)

The owner, at 16:15 (via the lead): "i meant a genome structure, more in an artistic representation. beautiful to look at. so you see what things you have discovered, slow motion, calm." Family trees also work, as a second module or combined. The loop takes a smaller role.

**The test:** an always-on object in a room, one you'd keep on the shelf for its own sake: peaceful, with wonder.

## Common to all three frames (1024 × 600, the Miniature Lives style)

- **The settled structure:**
  - the living vivarium in the centre, the largest pane;
  - the Library on the right, about a third of the screen;
  - Pods, Incubator, Bay and Companion as small, quiet status on the left, in loop order, each a small painted object with a tiny label.
- **The device:** one calm, matte, dark slate face with soft rounded panes. No bezels, lamps or glowing edges.
- **The light:** one warm daylight from the top left on everything. The life (the mibis, moss, plants and water) carries the colour; the device stays quiet.
- **References to feed:**
  - the vivarium day master (`idle-vivarium-day-1024x568`);
  - the version 4 Loikas (`home-window-four-v4-masters`);
  - the S03 founder;
  - the pods (`pod-collection` renders);
  - the approved concept `station-research-hands` for finish only, not for its blue display.
- **What changes between the frames** is only how the Library shows what the player has discovered of a species' genome, and where your own mibis' family tree lives.

## Direction 1, the specimen cabinet

The Library is a naturalist's glass-fronted cabinet set into the slate. Behind the glass sits one open specimen plate for the species in focus (the Loika): its portrait at the centre, and around it its traits as delicate painted sprigs.
- **Found looks:** each look found is a small, fully painted specimen (the leaf crest, the pale patches, the stripes, the eye rings), pinned and lit by the daylight.
- **Unknown looks:** faint graphite outlines on the plate, a soft mystery waiting to be filled.
- **The family tree:** a slim drawer below the plate, half open, holding small framed portraits of your own Loikas joined by fine painted threads.
- **The paper:** a warm, aged botanical paper, the Library's one place for paper, never cream-bright.

## Direction 2, the night garden in a glass dome

The Library is a glass bell jar on the slate, holding a small dusk garden. The genome is a constellation of soft lights hanging in the jar's air, drawing the species' shape.
- **Found looks:** each look found is a warm point of light with a tiny painted image of the look inside it, like a firefly holding a picture.
- **Unknown looks:** faint dust that has not yet lit.
- **The family tree:** fine light-threads between small glowing portraits of your own mibis, at the jar's base.
- **The light:** the jar is the only dusk on the screen. It is a calm, deep blue-teal, the light inside it soft, and it never outshines the daylit vivarium.

## Direction 3, the living plant in a jar

The Library is a tall glass terrarium jar with a single living, branching plant. The species' genome grows as the plant:
- **The branches:** one stem per trait, rising from the species' face painted into the jar's base like a seed.
- **Found looks:** a bloom, each a small, softly painted flower that shows the look itself.
- **Unknown looks:** closed pale buds.
- **The family tree:** the plant's roots, visible through the glass in the soil below, where your own mibis are small portraits along the root lines, parent to child.
- **The finish:** moss, soil and glass in the vivarium's own warm daylight and finish, so the Library and the vivarium are one world.

## For every frame

- No text other than the small labels on the left.
- No holograms, neon, chrome or glowing outlines.
- No cream panels.
- Nothing red.
