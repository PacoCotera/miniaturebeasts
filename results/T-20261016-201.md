## What changed
Each mibi is drawn as itself, in the logic process (the headless Node side). The face is unchanged and is given the composed picture by id, as it is given the stamp. The deprecated JavaScript drawing layer was not touched.
- `prototypes/station/src/looks.mjs`: `drawnLooks(frame, mibi)`. A read chapter draws the look it shows (`traitState().shows`, so hidden copies never show). An unread chapter draws the species' common look (`ui/specs/station/stand-looks.json`, made by `tools/make-stand-looks.mjs`, 300 pods a species). Doings and sealed chapters draw nothing.
- `prototypes/station/src/layered-stand.mjs`: the layered stand. A base plus layers `{ trait, looks: [...], order, op: over|erase, file }` read from `ui/assets/stands/index.json`. Generic over loci: a new locus is new data.
- **The art is the signed version 4 set (T-20261016-200), not the proof.** `tools/make-stand-layers.py` cuts it, derived down from 304x312 (never up):
  - base: masters-pods `floor/S01/keys` bare base key, anchor pose;
  - crown: the `floor/S01/overlays` crest overlay, for "leaf crest" (a bare head is the base);
  - markings: `layers/` patches (for patches, and for stripes and patches) and stripes (for stripes, and for stripes and patches), patches first, as `make-window-masters.py` does.
  - Adults at 304x312, 144x152 and 48x48; juveniles at 304x312, 104x112 and 48x48. Colour is the charcoal base until T-20261016-203's ramps.
- **Size is composed in the logic, from one config.** `ui/specs/station/stand-sizes.json` gives each view's figure height, adult and juvenile (the art director's table): Home window 54/40, Vivarium full screen and Idle 92/68, Habitat up close 150/110; tiles and the Companion are unlisted, so unchanged. The art is cut once at 304x312; `composeStand` lays the layers on it, then fits the figure down once (Lanczos, premultiplied) into the view's box, bottom-centred in the picture. The picture is the slot's own size, since the face places a picture 1:1 and refuses any other; the figure stands on its foot with transparent around it. The figure box is in the variant, so another figure size is another picture id. The size is never hard-coded in a view: `mibiPictureOf`/`mibiReq` take a `view` (home, vivarium, idle, habitat).
- `views/home-props.mjs` `mibiPictureOf` and `mibiReq` name a layered mibi's picture by its variant (`mibi:S01~<hash>:<juvenile|adult>:WxH[:light]`, the looks it has layers for and the figure box) and carry its looks. Home, the Vivarium (whole and tiles), Idle, the Habitat up close and the Incubator go through it. `pictures.mjs` composes the picture once and caches it by id; a light grades it as it grades a master.
- `ui/asset-policy.mjs`: LAYERED family = painted, for juvenile and adult (a draw policy, not a sign-off).
- Placeholder register entry `stand-layers`: the 16 eye-ring overlays (thin, wide) of `layers/register.json` stay stand-ins (carried in the index as `standIns`); those looks draw the base's "between" rings.
- The page loads layers, looks and sizes (`masters.mjs` `loadStandLayers`, `main.mjs`); Node loads them with `tools/stand-layers-fs.mjs`.
