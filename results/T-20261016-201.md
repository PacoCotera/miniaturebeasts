## What changed
Each mibi is drawn as itself, in the logic process (the headless Node side). The face is unchanged and is given the composed picture by id, as it is given the stamp. The deprecated JavaScript drawing layer was not touched.
- `prototypes/station/src/looks.mjs`: `drawnLooks(frame, mibi)`. A read chapter draws the look it shows (`traitState().shows`, so hidden copies never show). An unread chapter draws the species' common look (`ui/specs/station/stand-looks.json`, made by `tools/make-stand-looks.mjs`, 300 pods a species). Doings and sealed chapters draw nothing.
- `prototypes/station/src/layered-stand.mjs`: the layered stand. A base plus layers `{ trait, look, order, op: over|erase, file }` read from `ui/assets/stands/index.json`. It is generic over loci: a new locus is new data. A look with no layer draws as the base.
- `views/home-props.mjs` `mibiPictureOf` and `mibiReq` name a layered mibi's picture by its variant (`mibi:S01~<hash>:adult:WxH[:light]`, the looks it has layers for) and carry its looks. Home, the Vivarium (whole and tiles), the Habitat up close and the Incubator all go through it. `pictures.mjs` composes the picture once and caches it by id; a light grades it as it grades a master.
- `ui/asset-policy.mjs`: a new LAYERED family (painted in any status). The art director should confirm it.
- S01 adult layers at 304x312, 144x152 and 48x48: the proof's cut-outs (patches; bare head as an erase and a fill; thin and wide rings), cut by `tools/make-stand-layers.py` from the accepted Pip pair. The base is the accepted Pip stand, not the placed `mibi-adult-S01` masters. Listed in the placeholder register as `stand-layers`.
- The page loads the layers and looks (`masters.mjs` `loadStandLayers`, `main.mjs`); Node loads them with `tools/stand-layers-fs.mjs`.
