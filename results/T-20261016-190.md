## What changed
Draw each mibi as itself, S01 first. The art director and the genome engineer worked it together, in `results/T-20261016-190/`.
- **The art (`results/T-20261016-190/art-director.md`):** a species' stand is a base plus one layer for each locus a stand can show. For the Loika those are the crown (a crest layer over a bare-headed base), the markings (a patch layer) and the eyes (ring swaps). Every adult draws at one scale; pose and facing come from the routine, not the genes.
- **The genome (`results/T-20261016-190/genome-engineer.md` and `stand-looks.json`):** one function, `drawnLooks(frame, mibi)`, used everywhere a mibi is drawn:
  - read chapters draw the expressed look;
  - unread chapters draw the stand's default, which is the species' most common look on pods (for the Loika, the base: crest, plain, eyes between);
  - doings draw nothing on a stand.
- **The proof:** `S01-four-stands.png` and `home-window-four-loikas.png` (four real pods, chosen to differ), from `make-stands.py`.
