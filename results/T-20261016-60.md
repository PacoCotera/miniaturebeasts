## What changed
- **specs/face-arc-and-shadow.md** (new).
  - §1, the arc: `word_arcFill` draws the fill as 12 composed pictures, one per 22° segment, each masked by a new composed op `anglePick`. The op is integer-only, using a checked-in sin/cos table and a cross-product test, so WebAssembly, native and JavaScript give the same bytes. It copies the art's pixels and never blends them, so the palette check holds.
    - One degree redraws at most 4,331 px, and growNow at most 133,600, both under the 153,600 ceiling.
    - The node count is fixed at 12 plus the closed window.
    - The art is one picture, `incubator-ring-fill` 400 × 400. This settles the ruling §8 question "degree pieces or a mask" in favour of a mask.
    - Ready shows `incubator-ring-closed` only as a window over its difference box, because placing the whole 400 × 400 picture would redraw 160,000 px, over the ceiling.
    - Rejected forms, with numbers: the whole-sprite swap, a clip per degree, and an LVGL arc with an image source (it anti-aliases on the art layer and would be a new primitive kind).
  - §2, the shadow: a new `shadow` request kind. It is `founderArt` at 160 × 192 for the bud's known chapters, with the existing calm unknown giving one layer per cleared chapter. The alpha is stepped to 25/45/65/80 by integer arithmetic, on the painted layer.
    - The id changes only when the known chapters or the light step change, so it is recomputed at Grow, at each clearing and at each light step: about u + 4 derives per bud.
    - Cost: 123 KB held, a redraw of 30,720 px, and a budget of ≤ 50 ms per derive on the development machine.
    - A crossed bud takes the species' type genome with nothing read (the ghost), so a cross's shadow tells nothing about its own genome.
  - Both sections list their tests.
