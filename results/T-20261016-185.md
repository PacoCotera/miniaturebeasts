## What changed

**The cause:**
- A rig edge case, not a pool problem. The waist (`growth.join-throat-ratio`) sets the gap between two body regions on a narrow join. Thin (0.3) leaves a gap that a connector fills; broad (0.75) overlaps the regions.
- Between them, at about 0.59 to 0.66, the regions only just meet. The rig took them as touching, but no point lay inside both, so the body was "not connected".
- Named alleles never land there. Crossed children do, because their blends sit on the step between the alleles.

**The fix, in its home module** (`framework/rig.mjs`, `connect`):
- Two regions on a narrow join attach directly only when they share a point inside both. This is the same witness test the attachment uses.
- Otherwise they take the connector, as a thin waist does.
- Nothing is repaired in a genome, and no pool is narrowed, so every Waist look stays.

**The check** (`framework/species.mjs`, the frame's viability):
- It now also crosses 24 valid members, drawing about 170–195 children per species. Every child must build, or the frame is rejected, as with member samples.
- `viability` gains `crossed` and `crossedBuilt`.
- Proven sensitive: against the old rig, it rejects all four affected species (S08 4/186, S09 8/191, S13 13/192, S16 13/195).

**Frames:**
- Only the `viability` field changed in the 16 frames. Seeds, pools, loci, chapters and the type specimens are unchanged.
- The frame version stays 3, and the stamp snapshot is unchanged.

**The test** (`tests/framework.test.mjs`):
- every frame's crossed children all build;
- in S08, S09, S13 and S16, every waist value from 0.30 to 0.75 in steps of 0.0075 builds.
