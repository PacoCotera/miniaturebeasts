## The test, corrected

The ticket's gate said "four Loikas read as four individuals". The genome engineer showed that the genome can't promise it.
- With S01's three look traits (Markings, Crown, Eyes), four random Loikas all differ only 35% of the time.
- Deep species separate easily: S03 98%, S12 95%.
- Demanding four distinct Loikas would demand a faked trait, which the art direction forbids ("Art never changes genes").

**The bar, as this gate now holds it:**
1. Each mibi draws exactly what its genome shows, by one rule (`drawnLooks`).
2. A genetic difference reads at a glance where the stand can carry it.
3. Any individuality beyond that comes from non-genetic cues (pose, place, the name on focus), never a trait the mibi doesn't have.

## Judged against it

- **One rule everywhere:** Home, the Vivarium, Habitat and the Companion all draw through `drawnLooks`. A read chapter draws what the mibi shows; hidden or asleep copies never draw; an unread chapter draws the stand's declared default; doings draw nothing. This holds "the same individual everywhere".
- **At a glance:** Pip (crest, plain), Moss (crest, patches), Fig (bare, plain) and Dot (bare, patches) read as four individuals.
  - The silhouette (crest or bare) and the coat (patches or plain) carry it in two seconds.
  - The eye rings read up close, which is honest at Home's scale.
  - Beside today's golden, where every adult is one picture, the difference is the point the owner steered for.
- **The four are real pods** (seeds 12, 13, 14 and 107, with digests), not a staged set.
- **The unread default:** agreed with the genome engineer. The stand as painted (plain, leaf crest, wide pale rings) is the default, declared per trait by the art director and checked against the pool. Using the type specimen would put a look on 78% of Loikas that they don't carry.
- **The plan for other species** (palette, then pattern layers, then parts, proportion and form swaps last; a two-pigment look drawn from its two pigments) is the right order of cost against legibility.
