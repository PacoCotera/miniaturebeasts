## What changed
`specs/station-genome-screen-spec.md` §2.1a: the tree fills its area for every species.
- **The rule:** four tiers chosen by rows R (chapters) and the most genes in a chapter G.
  - **XL**, R ≤ 5 and G ≤ 2: 48 px marks (S01, S04).
  - **L**, R ≤ 5 and G ≤ 4: 40 px (S06, S12, S14).
  - **M**, R ≤ 6 and G ≤ 5: 32 px (S02, S05, S08, S10, S11, S13).
  - **S**, otherwise: today's 24 px (S03, S07, S09, S15, S16).
- In every tier the rows spread to fill the 280 px height, up to a cap per tier, centred. The genes spread across the width up to 2m apart, ending by 716. Integer formulas, so the build can test them on every frame.
- The largest species keep their layout (tier S, 24 px). S09's rows only spread from 34 to 40, and S03's from 34 to 36.
