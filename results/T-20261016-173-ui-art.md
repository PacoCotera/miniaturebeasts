## Verdict: FAIL. The words are right, but five plates teach the opposite of the rule they illustrate

Signed by the creative director, 2026-10-17. Judged: design/style-guide/README.md and its 23 plates on `style/station-companion-spec` at 13e5c9d2. Each plate was checked at 1× against the owner's verbatims for T-163 to T-166 (06:38–06:43) and the Miniature Lives look, beside the whole Station in the device sheet (`results/T-20261016-175/device-sheet-1x.png`). The game designer's verdict (`results/T-20261016-173-game-design.md`) sits beside this one.

**The test was: would a builder holding only this guide make screens the owner accepts?**
- The text says the right things. Every line of the owner's 06:38 rejections is answered by a rule:
  - brown on brown, and cream progress;
  - the egg on moss, and the plaque;
  - the pixel glyph, and the jargon;
  - the colour bars, and options that look alike;
  - floating text.
- **But builders copy pictures, not paragraphs.** Five "do" plates show the very thing the text forbids, and plate 01 is the guide's anchor. A builder who copies the plates rebuilds the brown and the blue. This is the patch-on-patch mechanism the owner named at 06:41, so the guide cannot go to the owner like this.

## The three conflicts: settled with the game designer

1. **Corner marks.**
   - **Agreed:** the 24 px rule stands. Exactly three corner marks are registered, 12–16 px, on a host of 24 px or more. The first-mark triangle is drawn in `bone`.
   - **Countered, one point:** the gold glint (new here) and the yellow glint (a piece of your wish) are the same four-point silhouette that differs only by hue. That fails 06b ("colour never carries meaning alone"), which has no exception.
   - The art director gives the wish its own silhouette, and the game designer confirms its meaning in §10.
   - All three corner marks go on plate 04c (on a tile) and 06b (in four-gray).
2. **The words budget.** Agreed: §10 wins.
   - The panel's title is the question: at most 4 words, at 20 px. §4's 28 px goes.
   - A window holds a verb of at most 3 words, its cost icons and one line of at most 8 words.
   - The appendix and the T-45 spec are amended to match.
3. **Refusals.** Agreed: there are two kinds, as the game designer wrote.
   - A turn or move that can't act gets its own reason mark and the shake, with no plate.
   - A ✓ that can't go ahead gets the message plate, at most 6 words.
   - §10's Feedback line is aligned to §9.
