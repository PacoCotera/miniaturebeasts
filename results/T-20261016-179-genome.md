## Verdict

**PASS, with three wording fixes and one open question.** Nothing in design/game.md contradicts the genome framework or the frames on the points that matter:

- **Loci kinds:** species-locked, player-configurable, breeding-only and self-changing (`changedBy` in the frames).
- **Research:**
  - per chapter;
  - glints say "new here", never what;
  - sealed chapters open with finds;
  - what is read stays read.
- **Creation:**
  - one pod makes one fixed founder;
  - the founder is shaped only among the pod's own copies;
  - no randomness at creation: the pod's genome is fixed by its seed when found.
- **Incubation:** the same individual, never a reroll.
- **Breeding:**
  - only within one species;
  - one child with real parents;
  - no mutation;
  - the forecast shows only what is read.
- **Self-changing traits:**
  - listed under Life, where they belong;
  - never set by the player;
  - only partners change, and partners are adults or elders, so a juvenile's mark never moves;
  - fading counts partner trips, never time, so "nothing is lost by being away" holds.

## Fixes (wording, design/game.md)

1. **Systems table, Creation row:** "A read pod … in" should be "an identified pod". The Mechanics table (Identification) and the code agree that an identified pod can be grown unedited (`growBlockKey` blocks only an unidentified pod). Reading is needed only to shape.
2. **Systems table, Genomics row:** "never shown as notation" contradicts "the real genetics, one press away" (Who it is for) and the detail view (letters, the rule in words: specs/editing-life.md §2a). Suggested: "never required as notation; letters only in the detail view, one press away".
3. **Mechanics table, Upgrades:** research chips "dropped by rare mibis". The framework has no rarity, and Pillar 3 rules out rarity tiers. Say which mibis drop them in the framework's terms (a species, a trait or a find), or leave it open.

## Open question (for the game designer and the genome engineer, not a blocker for this page)

**The vivarium: "its place … later sheds one pod of its line."** The framework has no operation that makes a pod from one mibi. Pods are drawn from the species' pools by their seed (`podGenome`), and children come only from two parents (no mutation, no selfing). The decision behind this line (design/proposals/research-economy.md §6, §8.3) says the pod "carries its real copies". Read literally, that is a clone:

- the same genome digest, name code and stamp as the released mibi;
- so two individuals share an identity that the framework treats as unique ("the same individual everywhere").

Before it is built, one of these must be chosen:

- (a) **The exact genome.** A clone, with the shed pod's origin recording the released mibi, so the record tells them apart. Kinship to the released mibi would count as 1/2 or more.
- (b) **A new genome built from its copies.** One of its two copies at each locus, drawn by the pod's seed. This is a new framework operation (selfing). Its children could show its hidden looks.
- (c) **A wild pod of its species seeded with its looks**, with no claim to be its line.

design/game.md can keep "of its line" until this is decided.
