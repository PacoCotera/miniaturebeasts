Mostly yes, by design: the turn lands on the founder (the light, the reaction, the old look fading into the new: spec section 3, from slip-reward.md), the gene's mark lights with its notch in the same frame, and the bay shows the new look and the copy chosen. ### Findings, routed to UI/UX

1. **One star, two meanings.** The key (07) uses the same star for a glint (an unread chapter hides a look new to the species) and for "a first" (a look no resident has). These are different facts, and the player can't tell them apart. **Fix:** the glint keeps the house's four-point glint (as on Pods and in the rules). "A first" takes a different silhouette, not a star, a diamond (Data) or another material shape. The AD draws it.
2. **The wish mark** (open in the spec). **Settled from game design, for UI/UX to draw:** the house already shows a wish as a lit glint in the wish's accent (the Cross: "the wish in yellow, its one accent"; the rules: a piece of a wish glints). So on the genome screen, a gene whose look is a pinned wish look wears the four-point glint, lit in the wish accent. It shows only when the player has pinned a wish for this species. This also keeps glint and wish in one family ("where", never "what"), and it is distinct from "a first" (finding 1).
3. **Refused edits.** ▲ ▼ on a drum that can't turn (inherited-only, self-changing) does nothing today, with no feedback. **Fix:** the gene's own mark answers once: the joined rings pulse toward the Cross mark, or the step marks pulse toward the place badge, about 400 ms. The bottom line's context already says why ("breed to change"). No plate and no sound. That is "the bench has rules, and a rule shown is a goal suggested" (editing-life.md section 5).
4. **The commit's before and after.** 05 shows the stamp printing but not the before and after of the whole founder. Under T-45 the Grow panel carries it (the animal as the pod would grow it, beside yours). **Fix:** confirm in the spec that ✓ Grow it opens the decision panel first (T-45) and that 05 plays after the panel's ✓, so the reward of the commit is not skipped.
5. **Sealed is not drawn.** Add the sealed chapter to the key (07) as a drawn mark, and to 03 or 01 as a row, so the owner sees every kind.

Game designer's proposal: **make the pod in its port the Grow target, and ✓ on a drum "Look closer".** ◀ from the first drum lands on the pod: "✓ Grow it" with the price, which opens the decision panel. ✓ on any drum opens the detail view over the sample bay, free, and returns to the same gene. The decision is the lead's and UI/UX's; until then the spec keeps ✓ as Grow.
