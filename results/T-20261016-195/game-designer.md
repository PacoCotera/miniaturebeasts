**The words the owner objected to were mine.** "Gone for good" and "The pod is used." came from my scene list (specs/purpose-briefs-b20261017-1.md §6.1). That list is now rewritten under the ruling, for all eight panels, so round 2 starts from it too.

**Why "Release":** it is the owner's own word for the action. It is neutral and true: Pip goes free.

**Why one line on Release, and its words:** the picture shows Pip at home in the wild, but no picture can say "for good". Irreversible is a fact the player needs before pressing, so it is said once, as what happens ("lives in the wild from now on"), not as what is lost ("gone"). It is in the ordinary text colour, with no ✕.

The gain of ❀ +2 is an icon. The place remembering Pip (the shed pod, chosen but its timing not designed) is left to the picture, which can show a pod at the place, or is left out. It is not promised in words until its timing is designed.

**Why no line on Grow:** "the pod is used" restated the price as a loss. The truth is that the pod becomes this mibi, and the window already paints that: the pod, sealed, in the chamber. The icons carry the price.

**Facts to correct in the ticket:** Release gives ❀ +2 (Essence), not ◆ (design/game.md, the vivarium row).

## Truth check (the gate's "irreversible is a fact, not a threat")

- **Release is irreversible.** It is stated once, plainly, and nothing else marks it.
- **Grow uses the pod.** That is the price of the outcome, not a loss. The pod's genome lives on as the mibi.
- **Keep and Keep shaping change nothing.** They get no line and no "safe" styling. Whether focus starts on a guarded option is UI/UX's call.

## UI/UX's calls (f92ddd4a): agreed, with one guard

- **The middle slot empty on every panel:** agreed. The window already paints where the option leads (the pod in the chamber), and the carry confirms it after the press. "To the Incubator" was text a picture carries.

One guard for the irreversible option (Release only): ✓ takes effect only once the panel has settled (its open motion done), so a press that opened the panel can't also pass through it. That is a guard in timing, not in styling.
