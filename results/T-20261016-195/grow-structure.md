# Grow: the structure (the game designer, after the owner's 13:17 ruling)

**The line of reasoning:** at Grow, the player is deciding whether the founder they made is the one to bring to life. So the founder fills both windows, and the windows differ only in what happens to it next: it is sealed in its pod to grow, or it stays open on the bench. Price and wait are facts beside the choice, not its subject.

## The weights, in order

| # | What | Where, and how big |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The founder they made**, with its changed parts lit in the bench's mint scan light, so their edits are visible in both outcomes | The subject of **both** windows. It fills about two thirds of the window's height, at the same scale in each |
| 2 | **What happens to it** | **Grow it:** the founder seen *through* its own pod's shell, which is sealed and candled from below, wrapping the founder at the same scale. The pod is drawn around the founder, not shrunk to a small egg in an empty chamber. **Keep shaping:** the same founder, unwrapped, on the bench, with a column of gene tiles open beside it |
| 3 | **The price** | icons on the verb row of Grow it (⚡2 ❀4 ◆n), and on ✓ |
| 4 | **The wait** | **not in the window.** The minutes belong to the Incubator's bottom line (the owner's ruling on minutes). After ✓, the carry to the Incubator shows the ring there |

## Cut from the current window

- **The dark empty chamber.** It is space spent on emptiness, the owner's Home point.
- **The bare ring.** A wait that hasn't started has nothing to show.
- **The small pod.** The pod's job is to wrap the founder, not to sit beside a void.
