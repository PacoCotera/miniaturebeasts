   - **a piece of your wish** is the four-point glint lit in `yellow`;

   Fix §2 and §4 to match, and give the first-mark triangle its colour role in §2 (`bone` suggested; the register says "cream triangle", and §2 forbids cream as a fill, so name the exact value).

## The three conflicts the lead named: the game designer's positions, for the creative director to settle with me

1. **The "first" triangle against the 24 px rule.**
   - The 24 px rule stands for a mark that carries meaning alone.
   - Corner marks are a registered exception: a mark of 12 to 16 px may sit on the corner of a picture or gene mark of 24 px or more, as a modifier of that thing. There are exactly three corner marks: the gold four-point glint (new here), the yellow four-point glint (a piece of your wish) and the triangle (new to your field guide). Each has a distinct silhouette and reads in four-gray (plate 06b).
   - No other meaning may ride on a corner mark.
   - The triangle is drawn in `bone`, not cream (§2 forbids cream). §10's register should read "the triangle", and §2 should name its role.
2. **The words budget against the appendix and the panel spec.**
   - §10 is the budget, and it wins: §5 already says so, and the appendix is fixed when each screen is redesigned.
   - For the decision panel: the panel's **title is the question, at most 4 words** ("Return Moss to the wild?"), set at the title size of §5's type table (20 px); §4's "28 px" should go.
   - Each window carries the verb (at most 3 words), its cost icons and **one consequence line of at most 8 words**.
   - Where the decision panel spec (T-20261016-45) or the appendix gives more, they are amended to this.
3. **The refusal plate.** There are two kinds of refusal, and the guide should name both:
   - **A turn or move that can't act** (▲ ▼ on a gene only breeding changes, the pad into nothing): the thing answers with its own reason mark and the 240 ms shake, with **no plate**.
   - **✓ on an action that can't go ahead** (it can't be paid, or the bay is full): the dimmed ✓ cap has already warned before the press. On the press, the message plate names what is missing, at most 6 words, with the material's icon where it is a price ("needs ❀ 2 more"), for 4 s. Nothing is spent.

   This is what §9 already says; §10's Feedback line "There is no plate" should read "There is no plate for a turn or move refusal; a ✓ that can't go ahead shows the message plate (§9)".

With these three settled and fixes 1–5 above made, the game design gate passes.
