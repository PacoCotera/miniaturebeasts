## Fixes, routed to the art director

1. **The glint's meaning, §2 and §4.** §2's State row says glint means "a first, something new" in `yellow` and `gold`, and §4's Glint component says "a first; something new". §10's register (binding, agreed with the genome engineer in T-136) gives three different marks:
   - **something new here** is the four-point glint in `gold`, per chapter, never on a gene;
   - **a piece of your wish** is the four-point glint lit in `yellow`;
   - **new to your field guide** (a first) is the triangle, not a glint.

   Fix §2 and §4 to match, and give the first-mark triangle its colour role in §2 (`bone` suggested; the register says "cream triangle", and §2 forbids cream as a fill, so name the exact value).
2. **The minute count, §4 Progress.** "The wait in `mint` words ('ready in about 8 min')" reads as a property of every progress. The ruling (owner, 2026-10-16) allows one rounded minute count, **in the Incubator's bottom-line context only**. Elsewhere a wait is the ring alone (§10: "Numbers … never as progress"). Fix the Progress row to say so.
3. **The genome marks, §4.** The row lists open, changed, one look, breeding only, clash, self-changing and unknown. It misses **asleep, sealed, wish and first**, and it points to a work-queue draft for the tiers. Point it to §10's register for the meanings and keep only the sizes here, so there is one list.
4. **Dock and arrival's body, station-screens.md.** The new purpose line is right, but the body below it still describes the rejected Cargo report:
   - "a report card waits on the vivarium";
   - "how far the land is explored is on the report card";
   - pods "into the rack's wells".

   The rulings for the Cargo redesign (specs/purpose-briefs-b20261017-1.md 6.2) are:
   - reach leaves Cargo (the Companion's map);
   - world events leave (mibi news to the return signals, world news to the Companion's map);
   - pods go out through the Pods hatch, and the ones with no free well wait on the bay floor.

   Rewrite the body to those, or mark it "superseded by the Cargo redesign (T-164)" until that lands, so no builder builds the old card from it. The pass line "the free mend shows on the Probe dock" can stay, since it is the dock arrival's, not the report's.
5. **Two small mismatches.**
   - §5's case rule writes "Legs & Tail", but the frames say **"Legs & tail"**, and the code wins (T-135).
   - §4 gives the decision panel's title at 28 px, while §5's type table puts a panel's question at the title size (20 px). Pick one.
