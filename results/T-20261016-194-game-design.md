## Verdict: PASS

Signed by the game designer, 2026-10-17, on the genome engineer's result (results/T-20261016-194.md). My four conditions are met:

1. **A new S03 frame version (5).** New pods draw only round feet, padded feet and digging wedges (0 of 300 carry an old form).
2. **The old forms stay legal for inheritance.** 300/300 version 3 Tuikis are valid, and 300/300 old × new children build.
3. **Saved pods keep their genomes and versions.** Nothing changes under the player.
4. **A child with an old form reads plainly** ("hooves", "webbed feet", "roots"), never as an error. The guide counts three looks. A found look outside the frame's looks counts as found but not toward completion, the same rule as the Loika's plain.

**On the dominance the genome engineer flagged:** the catalogue makes the old forms dominant, so an old line keeps showing them (54% of its children carry one). I accept that, and I don't want a catalogue change for it:
- It reaches only players with version 3 Tuikis. A new player never meets an old form.
- For those who have one, the old line is a real heritage, described plainly. Breeding toward the digging forms is their choice, a goal they can see and pursue, not a cleanup the game forces.
- Flipping the catalogue's order would change feet in every species with feet, which is a far larger change than this problem warrants.
