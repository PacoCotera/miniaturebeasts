## What changed
`design/art-direction.md` is expanded in place, with no new document. Branch `style/art-direction-general` is cut from station-gate at its current head.

A new section, **"The look and feel"**, goes above the existing ones. It is principle-led and has no pixel values or sizes:
- **The feel:** a small, well-made field kit for keeping living things; the life is the point, and the device is quiet and practical.
- **Textures and materials:** the life is soft and tactile (fur, waxy leaves, damp moss, stone, water). The device is handled gear: matte sage and sand polymer, dark rubber, screws, engraving, canvas and braided hose, a little wear. The screens are dark matte. Paper appears only as things that never change.
- **Light:** one warm daylight from the top left, grounding everything. The device's own light is small and purposeful, cast onto what it lights.
- **Colour mood:** bright life on a calm ground. The device speaks in colour only when it means something. If the panels make a screen colourful, the mood is wrong.
- **Information density** and **Tone of voice:** headed and marked as the game designer's to write.
- **Motion character:** the life moves all the time; the device moves only when something happens, short and eased. Reveals take their time, and nothing blinks for attention.
- **What it is not:** childish, a sci-fi HUD, a sterile lab or cabinet, a cottage with no equipment, brown on brown or cream panels, a wall of text, equipment for show.

It also adds:
- **The one line the ticket asks for:** the screens are prototypes and keep changing; newer direction overrides any rule on the page; the style guide sits below as the current implementation reference, with the values.
- **Five reference images, all our own:**
  - station-research-hands, for finish and light; its blue and orange are captioned as not the rule;
  - companion-resident-home, for warmth;
  - the vivarium day master, for light;
  - Pip in the richer treatment, for form lighting;
  - companion-storm, for motion and weather.

  Home's capture is left out because its panes are still brown.

The existing sections (Miniature Lives, Rules, From genome to creature, Not designed yet) stay below, unchanged.
