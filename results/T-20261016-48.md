## Departures from the script (specs/dock-moments.md), and why
- **The arrival enters with the frame's 180 ms dither, not a 300 ms slide.** A full-stage slide redraws 534,528 px a frame, over the face's 153,600 animation ceiling, and the dither is how every screen arrives on this face.
- **The choice is a strip under the bay, not the full panel.** The full panel would cover the crates it is about. It needs a `form: "strip"` in the panel component (T-49), added by the build if missing.
- **Who came home shows as faces in a band on Cargo's stage,** not as a mibi walking into the vivarium panel. Cargo's stage has no vivarium panel.
