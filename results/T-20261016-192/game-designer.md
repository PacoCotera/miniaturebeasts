# T-192: which state brings a module forward (the game designer's call)

## The call: one module forward, the one ✓ acts on

The column has three tiers. A module's tier comes from return signals' rules (specs/station-return-signals.md), so nothing new has to be decided at build time.

| Tier | When | How it looks (the art director paints it) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Forward** | **At most one module:** the one whose thing the bottom line's ✓ acts on now, the first `needKey` that holds (§3's order). Examples: crates in the bay; the bud ready; a portrait come; a chapter cleared; new pods | its subject lit and larger in its well, its amber lamp |
| **Live** | Any other module with its own amber lamp, or with a running wait (the bud growing with its ring; the portrait crate's ring while it is painted) | its picture at rest, at normal light; the lamp or ring shows the state |
| **Quiet** | Nothing waits and nothing runs | dimmed to its object in its well, still readable |

## Why one, and why the ✓ target

- **"What needs you reads first, in two seconds"** (the gate) holds only if one thing is first. Two modules forward is a split eye and a second decision before the first press.
- **It is the same choice the line already makes.** `needKey` orders "what you can act on now, then what is new to see, then what is waiting". Bringing that module forward makes the column and the bottom line say the same thing, once each: picture and words.
- **The others are not hidden.** A second amber lamp still shows. A player who wants the other thing moves focus there; it doesn't need to shout.

## Edge cases

- **Nothing in the column is the ✓ target:** for example, the line's first key is "meet" (the vivarium), "watch a resident today" or "set out with the Companion". Then no module comes forward, and the living window leads, which is right because the life is the subject.
- **The Probe:** never forward by itself, since nothing on the Station waits on it. Its plates stay readable when quiet, so a player can check them before a trip.
- **The Library:** forward only for a portrait come (key 4); quiet otherwise.
- **When the target changes** (the player acts and the next key holds), the forward state moves on the next screen settle, not mid-carry, so nothing jumps under the player's eye.
