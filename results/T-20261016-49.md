## What changed
One commit on arch/dock-sensing, from station-gate 54bde362. Two files.

design/devices.md
- Family table: the Caddy's reference hardware now names a hall sensor and an RGB lamp per cradle.
- New section "The cradles: seat sensing and lamps". **Sensing: a magnet in each handheld and a DRV5033 omnipolar hall switch under each cradle.** I compared it with the charging-coil status and with a contact. I chose the magnet because it reads true whatever the battery or charger is doing, it is a level so it is right again at boot, it reacts in under 1 ms, and it cannot be assembled the wrong way round. The coil status depends on a charger chip nobody has chosen, and it drops out when the battery is full or flat or the charger faults, so it is kept only as a second reading for diagnosis. A contact breaks the decided rule "everything is wireless, with no connectors".
- **Lamp: one 1.6 × 1.6 mm common-anode RGB LED per cradle behind a frosted window.** Each channel is sunk by an LEDC PWM pin at 5 mA or less, at about 5 kHz, using 6 of the 8 LEDC channels. I chose RGB so the art direction keeps control of colour; I did not pick a colour. Power: about 50 mW per lamp at full white, and sensing plus lamps stay under 0.15 W.
- Sound and haptics are listed as deferred, with nothing reserved on a board. The "not measured" list notes what gates 2–4 must test.
- New table "Reference bill of parts", by device, with quantities and no prices.

design/proposals/technical-architecture.md
- New §4.4 "The dock: seated and lifted". It adds two routes to the Caddy service: `GET /caddy-api/v1/dock` (a snapshot) and `/dock/events` (a server-sent event stream with a 5 s heartbeat).
- Payload fields: v, caddy, boot, seq, cradle, state, seats, ms, charging, snapshot. The event is a state, not a toggle, so a repeat never plays a second arrival.
- Timing: debounce is 150 ms for a seat and 300 ms for a lift. The lamp and e-paper change locally before the network send. The total from seat to the Station reacting is budgeted under 0.5 s.
- What the Station does: a seat runs `dockKey` and the frame's `dock` intent. A seat never means "no crates"; the summary comes separately.
- Offline behaviour: after 15 s without a heartbeat the Station marks the Caddy unreachable and backs off before reconnecting. It never invents a seat or a lift. On reconnect it reconciles by state plus `seats`, so a lift and reseat it missed are both applied. It also covers a Station restart, a Caddy restart and a Caddy with no Station.
- The play page's Dock control (D / Shift) stands in. It builds the same event object and feeds the same entry point.
- Deferred: sound and haptics.
- The "four routes" mentions in §3, §4.2 and §7 now include the dock's two routes. "Not designed yet" gains the summary relay, and what the Station shows when the summary is late.
