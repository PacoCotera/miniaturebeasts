  - No genome change.

  - Applied in both `lookOf` and `traitLooks`, so no raw id ever shows.

  - Nothing in any genome, pool or save moves. The stamp snapshot is unchanged.
- **The narrowing of S03's pool** to the three digging forms is filed separately, with its conditions (T-20261016-194):
  - a new S03 frame version;
  - the old forms legal for inheritance;
  - unopened pods keep their version;
