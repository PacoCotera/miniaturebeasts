## The structure: the screen is the bay, and it reads left to right

The whole stage, 992 × 506, is one recess with one surface: the bay's matte slate deck, lit from the top left. There is no wall-and-floor split, and no seams. Three places sit on it, in the order things move:

1. **Left: the dock hatch,** a recess cut into the deck. It is where the Companion's crates come in, and where the ones still waiting sit, stacked from its floor. There is room above them for a third, since the bay carries three. When none wait, its shutter is down.
2. **Centre: the open crate,** at full size under the bay's light, its contents in it or stood before it. It is the subject, and it sits where the eye lands.
3. **Right: the Pods case's edge, let into the bay.** These are the same six round, ringed wells as the Pods room's case, so a shell lands in the very well the player will find it in.

**The fourth destination is the top bar.** The materials' counters there are Home's own, and they tick.

Every place is a thing in the device that does a job: where crates come in, where one is opened, where pods go. Nothing is a panel for its own sake. The deck is filled edge to edge; its only open floor is the space in front of the crate, where the cells stand.

## The flight, without a line

Each content's destination answers it, and the same object is seen at both ends:

| Content | Before | After | What answers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pods | in the crate's sockets | in the case's free wells, the same shells | the well lights: a mint ring and a pool from below, the arrival colour |
| Energy, Data, Essence | three cells stood on the deck | gone from the deck | the top bar's counters tick, with +n in mint |
| A pod with no free well | in its socket | still in its socket, the crate open | nothing lights; the bottom line says "a pod waits for a well". It is a plain wait, with no coral and no mark |

The empty sockets, with the moss pressed where the pods sat, show that something left. The lit wells holding the same three shells show where it went. Between frames the motion is UI/UX's: each object travels toward its destination, and no path is drawn.

## Nothing negative

- Every content is a gain, so nothing is coral, red or crossed.
- The waiting pod stays in plain view in its crate. It is not shown as refused.
- The emptied crate closes and goes back through the hatch. It is never left as a bare box.
