## What changed
One commit on batch/B-20261016-4, rebased onto 6b5e8b71 before the push.

- **The rule** (`design/proposals/lvgl-switch.md` §2.1, beside the hold rule): an event may carry `skip: true`. Its hold is kept apart from every other event's hold. While it runs, ✓ and only ✓:
  - cuts each playing `skip` event to its end, which says `done`;
  - ends that hold only;
  - is then said as the intent `skip` on the event's target.
  Other keys act as in any hold. ✓ in a hold without `skip` is dropped, as before. The `event` and `intent` rows of the wire table carry `skip`.
- **The face:**
  - `anim.c`/`anim.h`: an event's `skip` flag, a separate skip hold (`g_skip_until`), `anim_skippable()` and `anim_skip()`;
  - `wire.c`: parses `skip` as a boolean, and refuses anything else;
  - `screens.c` `screens_key`: ✓ in a skippable hold sends `skip`.
- **The host** (`host.mjs`):
  - a separate `skipUntil`;
  - `at(ms, fn, skip)` tags a step as belonging to the skippable event;
  - `h.skip()` ends the skip hold and runs the tagged steps in order on that frame, as reduced motion does with hold 0;
  - a `skip` intent is handled before every other rule, so it is never the hatch's cutting key, and a late one does nothing;
  - `UI.skip` marks the hold for the line and is cleared when the hold runs out.
  Kept room and Dock keys still act at the end, because the frame loop dispatches them once the hold is over.
- **The two events only:**
  - `cargo.json events.opening` gets `"skip": true`, and every step `openBay` schedules is tagged;
  - `incubator.json events.hatch` and `hatch.moving` get it, and the hatch's hand-off is tagged.
  growNow, the install and the mend are untouched.
- **The line:** `strings.skip` "Skip" is added in `cargo.json` and `incubator.json`. The opening's and the hatch's lines show ✓ Skip only while the hold runs.
