## Verdict: FAIL, on two things the general layer must say and show

Signed by the creative director, 2026-10-17. Judged: design/art-direction.md on `style/art-direction-general`, beside the device sheet (`results/T-20261016-175/device-sheet-1x.png`).

**The test:** would an artist new to the project, holding only this page, land on the look of Home, the one accepted screen, and on one device rather than five?

**What it does well, and should keep:**
- **The feel:** "a naturalist's instrument made by someone who loves animals". It is a proposal with its own reasons, as the owner's 10:29 rule asks, not a list of the owner's lines.
- **Colour mood:** the test "if a screen looks colourful because of its panels, frames or bars rather than its life, the mood is wrong". It catches brown-on-brown and the colour bars at their root.
- **Density and tone** (the game designer's): one subject, depth folded, a glance before a read, warm never cute, never punish absence. Strong and specific.
- **Motion:** the life always moves; the device moves only when something happens.

## Blocking

1. **The one-machine principle is missing.**
   - The owner's signal at 06:41 was "nobody looking at the integrated experience". The finding under the three rejections was that the Station had become five devices (`specs/station-experience-review.md` §1).
   - The general layer never says that every screen is the same machine. It needs:
     - one ground, one frame, one pane;
     - the same objects (the pod, the stamp) drawn the same everywhere;
     - things carried across the hand-offs, not re-drawn;
     - a screen that is right alone but foreign beside its neighbours is wrong.
   - Write it in your own words and reasons. It is the principle the style guide's values serve, and it belongs here, above them.
2. **No picture shows the device as it should be.**
   - The only image of the device is station-research-hands. It is used twice (The feel, Information density), and its caption disowns two of its colours.
   - An artist who looks at the pictures, not the captions, learns a blue display and orange corners.
   - Use the style guide's plate 01 "do" (Home with its panes in slate, a diagram) as the general layer's picture of the target: one lit life, a quiet slate machine around it.
   - Replace the second station-research-hands with it in Information density, where "one subject, calm everywhere else" is exactly what it shows.

- The page mixes "color" and "colour". Pick the repository's spelling.
