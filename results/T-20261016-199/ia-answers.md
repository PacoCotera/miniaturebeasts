# Home: the game designer's answers to the converger's three questions

Game designer, 2026-10-17. These answer the questions asked through the creative director on the converged Home (v1). The picture itself is in another repository, which this session can't reach, so the gate on the picture is not given here.

## (a) The carriers rule: "where the missing piece is"

The rule as worded ("every pod and resident whose genome shows the wish's unlit look") is **not true to the rules unless it is limited to what the player has read.** Showing a carrier the player hasn't read reveals hidden genome content: it would answer, for free, the question that reading pays for.

The rule:
- **A resident or pod is listed** only if, **on a chapter the player has read**, it shows the look, or carries it as a read copy (reading a chapter shows both copies).
- **Excluded:**
  - unread chapters, even when the genome carries the look;
  - sealed chapters;
  - unidentified pods.
- **A pod in the case counts** as a carrier, because it can be grown and then crossed. It is listed after the residents, since it is one step further from a cross.
- **If no read carrier exists,** the tile names no one. It points the player to read the species' unread chapters of that trait ("not found yet: read a Coat"). That is a truthful "maybe here", never a promise.

That is fair to both motivations. For the feel, the tile is a short path to the dream. For the mechanics, reading has a clear payoff: a carrier appears.

## (b) The no-wish fallback

- **Confirmed:** with no wish pinned, the pursuit shows the species' field guide nearest complete.
- **"Nearest"** means the fewest looks still unfound.
- **Ties** go to the species the player touched most recently (read, grew or crossed).
- **A species with no looks found at all** is never chosen.
- **The guide counts only what completion counts.** For the Loika that is 14 looks, with plain excluded unless seen (T-200).

## (c) The ▲ "first" mark on Home

- **The register keeps its three marks** (the gold glint for new here, the wish star, the first triangle), as agreed in T-173.
- **On Home, ▲ earns no mark of its own.** When a look new to the guide lands, the pursuit's frame fills with that look, painted. That filling *is* the news, and it plays once, as an event. A standing triangle on top would be a second way of saying it, and it would sit on the picture at rest, which is the noise the creative director has already flagged.
- **▲ stays where the register defines it:** on a gene or chapter (a pod or a mibi up close), where it says "this one taught you something".
