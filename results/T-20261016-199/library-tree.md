# Home's Library module: which tree, what fills, what the loop animates (the game designer)

Game designer, 2026-10-17. This answers the owner's 16:09 direction: the Library on the right shows genome trees in a calm, looped animation of progress. It stays within 14:49 ("trees for the mechanics player") and 14:52 (one aggregate at the top level, not a trait grid).

## Which tree: the family tree of the pursuit's species

There is one tree, and it is real data: the recorded lineage (each mibi's parents are in the save). It is of one species, the pursuit's: the pinned wish's species, or else the guide nearest complete (ia.md).

- **The roots** are its founders: the pods grown, with their found place.
- **Each node** is a resident or an ancestor, as its small portrait. A released one stays as a faded node, because the line remembers it.
- **The branches** are the crosses: two parents join and a child grows up from the join.
- **The newest node** is the bud in the Incubator if it is of this species, forming at the tip.

## What fills (the progress)

- **The tree grows upward** with each generation the player breeds. Height is the visible measure of the mechanics player's work.
- **The crown is the goal.**
  - **With a wish pinned:** the crown is the wished mibi's silhouette, with its pieces. A piece lights when a node in the tree shows that look, read only.
  - **With no wish:** the crown is the species' field guide as a canopy of leaves (not a ring: rings belong to the Incubator), one for each look counted toward completion (Loika: 14). Each found leaf is painted, and the rest are empty frames.
- **Nothing is a number.** Progress is height, lit pieces and painted leaves.

## What the loop animates (calm and rare: one eased pass every 20–30 seconds, the creative director's timing)

- A soft light rises from the roots through the branches to the newest node, then into the crown, where it lights the held pieces or leaves in turn and fades.
- It is the inheritance flowing, the copies passed down: the one idea the tree teaches.
- **On a new event** (a child born, a piece newly held, a leaf found), the next pass stops at it, and the new thing settles in once. Then the loop goes back to rest.
- There is no motion between passes. The pass runs only while Home is open, and the tree never blinks to ask for attention.

## Why this tree, and not the others

- **The guide alone** would be a grid of looks, which is the trait grid ruled out at 14:52. Here the guide is a ring of leaves inside the tree: an aggregate.
- **A wish-only tree** is empty for a player with no wish. The family tree always has something, because it starts at the first founder.
- **The family tree is where both motivations meet.**
  - For the feel, it is *their* mibis, by name and face, a family.
  - For the mechanics, it is the pedigree and the path to the wish.
- **One press down** (✓ on it) opens the full lineage and genome trees in the Library, the depth the owner wants kept.

## Truth rules (for the genome engineer's check)

- Pieces and leaves light only from read chapters, never from hidden copies.
- A node is a real recorded mibi or a released ancestor, and the crown is never a forecast.
