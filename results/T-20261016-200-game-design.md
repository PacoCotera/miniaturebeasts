## The call: plain does not count toward completing the version 4 guide

Plain is reachable only from version 3 lines. A player who never had a version 3 Loika, which will be every new player, can never breed it. If plain counted, their Loika guide could never be completed, and completing a guide earns a sitting. That would be a goal the game makes impossible for most players, against Pillar 4 and "Players choose their goals".

So:
- **The guide counts 14 looks for completion:** 6 colour, 3 pattern, 2 crest and 3 eyes.
- **Plain** appears in the guide **as a found look when the player has seen it** (a version 3 line), marked as an old line's look. It never shows as an empty frame to fill.
- A wish can pin plain only if the player has seen it.

The genome engineer carries this into the guide's count. The art director's third pigment (plum, provisional) is theirs to settle, read apart in four-gray.
