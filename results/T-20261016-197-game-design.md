## Verdict on UI/UX's Cargo structure (Home's Bay opened): PASS on truth, with three fixes

Signed by the game designer, 2026-10-17, against specs/game-design-gate.md. This is a wireframe, so the §7 composition checks (specs/gate-recalibration.md §7) are judged on the painted stills.

The parts that pass:
- pods travel to the wells, and materials go to the counters;
- the Pods hatch with its six wells;
- the portrait crate carries its mibi's face.

## The four questions

1. **Berths.** Walk crates are three at most (design/architecture.md). Sitting crates do not count against those three (design/proposals/the-portrait.md §8). **Fix:** the bay needs room for a portrait crate beside three walk crates. Use a fourth berth, or a place of its own, for example the flat crate behind the door.
2. **Place badges.** A crate records no place today. The trip records keep every place the walk entered, in a list (`sv.trips[].places`), and no "main" place exists. One badge per crate would invent a rule. **Fix:** no badge for now. If the crate is given its trip's places (a build question for the architect), show each place entered. Do not show a chosen one.
3. **A pod with no free well** waits sealed in the bay and lands when a well frees, oldest first (design/architecture.md). It can outlast the crate it came in, and can wait across trips. **Fix:** keep waiting pods on one shared floor spot, not in front of a berth whose crate may already be gone. It is a plain wait: no coral and no "full".
4. **The find strip.** A find goes beside the sealed page it opens (results/T-20261016-197/game-designer.md). A species with no pod has no page in the Pods room yet, so that case is not designed. Draw only the designed case, and the gap stays parked with the creative director.
