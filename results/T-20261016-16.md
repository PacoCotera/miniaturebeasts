## The conflict, measured (incubator-growing at bc866c71, linear light, Y = .2126/.7152/.0722)
- The look multiplies the master by (0.200, 0.186, 0.160). A master at pre-look white (1.0) therefore composes to at most **luminance 0.186**. That ceiling is where today's flat plate already sits (plate now: p50 0.157, p95 0.186).
- The machinery the plate must match, R zone (792..936, 104..216): p50 0.110, **p95 0.315, max 0.988**. The vent box face: p50 0.173, p95 0.257, max 0.679. The small box face: p95 0.369, max 0.980.
- Pre-look values those tones would need: vent box p95 (1.15, 1.45, 1.28); small box (1.76, 2.04, 1.81). All above 1.0, so they can't be painted.
- So after the look the plate's whole range lies under the box faces' **median** (0.173 lum). It can't carry the lit edge or highlight that makes the boxes read as rendered. The review fails exactly that ("looks flat").

## What the ticket writer needs to choose (not mine to choose)
1. **Exempt the plate from the look:** apply K x WARM to the stamp glyph only, and place the plate master 1:1 at machinery tones. Its p95 would be about 0.26 to 0.32 (the boxes' range), under 0.34. That changes `stamplook.mjs` labelPlate and `frame.json regions.stampSlot.look`, which is code and spec: the build ticket and the UI/UX owner.
2. **Keep the look and accept a dark plate:** the plate becomes a recessed, shadowed panel (like A), not a box of the machinery. That drops step 1's "same light, material and edge"; the review's check 3 would need re-judging.
3. **Raise K on the Incubator only:** for the plate's lit edge to reach the vent box p95 (0.257), K must be at least 0.26 on R and 0.29 on G, so about 0.30 overall. That is a signed look value (art director, masters-pods 001bcd51) and the brightness rule in `frame.json regions.stampSlot.brightness`, so it needs a re-sign.

Once one is chosen, the master is about an hour's work with the kit (plate-device-120x120 plus a recess at 8..112 for the up-to-104 glyph, corners filled opaque; the kit's rounded corners leave alpha holes that labelPlate would show).
