## The finding
- Spec section 2 asks for `shieldLine` at **14 px** ("each plate takes one strike" / "no plates: the trip ends", weight 400) and for the two dial captions `reachWord`, `deepWord` at **12 px**. Step 3 of the ticket says to draw them "with the existing text word".
- The face has exactly three Inter sizes: `prototypes/face/src/prim/prim.c:65`, `font_of(px)` returns face_inter_16, face_inter_20, face_inter_28 and NULL for any other size; `prototypes/face/CMakeLists.txt:16` builds only those three font files; `vocab.c v_cap(px)` knows 16, 20 and 28; frame.json `type` lists 16, 20 and 28 (pitch 20, 28, 36). The face-check requires every string to be Inter at 16, 20 or 28.
- So the 14 px line and the 12 px captions cannot be drawn by the existing text word. Fixing that means baking two new fonts (or a different size in the spec); I did not choose.
## Measurements to decide with (real measurer, dist/metrics.json)
Width in px at 16 / 20 / 28: "Shield" 46 / 59 / 84; "each plate takes one strike" 198 / 256 / 367; "no plates: the trip ends" 168 / 217 / 314; "reach" 42 / 55 / 77; "deep" 38 / 48 / 70.
